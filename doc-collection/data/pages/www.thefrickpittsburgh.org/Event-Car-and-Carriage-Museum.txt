Car and Carriage Museum
Event information
October 27, 2026
10:00 AM - 5:00 PM
Description:
Travel back to the time of horse-drawn carriages, see some of the first horseless carriages to have an impact on Pittsburgh, and learn about Pittsburgh’s role in the developing automobile industry at the turn of the 20th century. The Car and Carriage Museum is always free to view.
