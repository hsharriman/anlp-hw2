PERSONIFICATION
Personification is when something that is not human,
like a lamp or a pencil, is given the characteristics
of a human. It is used in writing and stories to share
more vivid details about something.
For example you could describe the wind as being loud.
With personification you might say, the wind stood up and
gave a shout. He whistled on his two fingers.
