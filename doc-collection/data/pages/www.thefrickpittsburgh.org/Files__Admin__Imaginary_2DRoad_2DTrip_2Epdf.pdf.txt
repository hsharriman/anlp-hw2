Take an Imaginary Road Trip
Even if you can't go on a real trip right now, you can still dream up a fun adventure. Write a story
about an imaginary road trip that you would like to take. It can be to a real place, or somewhere in
your imagination. Use the photographs and questions below to get your story started, but be creative!
Choose Your Vehicle
Every great road trip starts with a car! Choose one of the historic cars below, which are part of the
collection at The Frick Pittsburgh. In your story, be sure to describe what your car looks like.
1931 American Austin Coupe 1909 Keystone Six-Sixty Roadster 1914 Model T
Choose How You'll Get There
Describe the amazing things you see as you drive. Tell your readers about the landscape, the animals,
the road, and the weather. If you plan to stop along the way, describe what you see and do there.
Choose Your Destination
Tell your reader where you're headed on your road trip. Why do you want to go there? Are you visiting
someone special, or visiting a place you've never been? Describe what you'll do once you get there!
