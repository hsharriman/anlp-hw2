Support the Campaign for Clayton
Clayton is the only remaining intact Gilded Age mansion from Pittsburgh’s Millionaire's Row. Built in 1870 and recognized on the National Register of Historic Places, this 154-year-old treasure is showing its age. From crumbling bricks and deteriorating mortar to warped wood and 20 layers of old paint — Clayton is in desperate need of some tender loving care.
Please join us and help preserve this vital part of Pittsburgh’s history by contributing to the Campaign for Clayton today!
