Fill your holiday season with cheer when you visit The Frick Pittsburgh Museums & Gardens for a Home for the Holidays at Clayton tour. See the historic house of Henry Clay Frick festively decorated and learn about how Pittsburghers in the Gilded Age celebrated all the winter holidays. Whether taking a tour, sipping on a warm drink at The Café at the Frick, or joining one of our seasonal programs, we are your one-stop destination for family fun and ringing in the most wonderful time of the year.

Scenic Gardens & Greenhouse
