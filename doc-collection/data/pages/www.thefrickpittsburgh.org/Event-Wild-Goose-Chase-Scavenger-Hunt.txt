Wild Goose Chase Scavenger Hunt
Wild Goose Chase Scavenger Hunt
Event information
October 23, 2026
10:00 AM - 5:00 PM
Location
The Frick Pittsburgh
Cost:
Free and open to all
Description:
Go on a wild goose chase with Gail the goose! Explore the Frick on a free scavenger hunt as you reunite Gail with her flock. Can you find all of the geese across the site?
Grab a Scavenger Hunt Sheet at the Grable Visitor Center. Once visitors have found all of the hiding geese, they can return their completed sheet to a staff member at the front desk in the Visitor Center to claim their prize. Free and open to all.
Please check in at the Grable Visitor Center (the modern building at the end of the parking lot) upon arrival.
Featuring Gail the Goose!
