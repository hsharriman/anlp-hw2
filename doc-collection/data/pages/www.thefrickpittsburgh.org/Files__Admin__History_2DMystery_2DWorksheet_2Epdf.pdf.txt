SOAP SAVER SOAP
SOAP SAVER: This tool helped you use every last piece of soap, instead of
throwing them away, You would put the soap scraps into the cage, and then
swish the soap saver around in the dish water.
GLOVE STRETCHER LEATHER GLOVE
GLOVE STRETCHER: Leather gloves shrink when they get wet. After inserting
the closed end of the glove stretcher into one of the glove's fingers, you
squeezed the handle. This caused the tool to open and stretch the leather out
to its original size.
PILLOW FLUFFER PILLOW
PILLOW FLUFFER: Instead of fluffing a pillow with your hands, you would hit the
pillow with this tool to keep the pillow fluffy.
