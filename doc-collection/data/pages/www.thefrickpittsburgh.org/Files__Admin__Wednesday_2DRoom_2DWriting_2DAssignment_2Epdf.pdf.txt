A Description of
My Bedroom
Helen Clay Frick grew up in Pittsburgh over 100 years ago. She
was educated in her home, which was common for girls of her
age and social standing at that time. Helen was taught by a
governess named Marika Ogiz, who Helen called Mademoiselle.
A governess is like a teacher and nanny in one, and Helen and
Mademoiselle were very close. In 1898, Mademoiselle Ogiz
assigned a writing assignment, in which Helen described her
bedroom and what she liked about it. You can read what Helen
wrote below.
"My bed-room has three beds in it. One is
Mademoiselle’s, the other is mine, no one
sleeps in the other. There is a bureau,
three chairs, and a sofa in it and a wash
stand. There are three windows, a big one
and two little ones. The big window looks
out on our garden, and one of the little
ones also, the other one to the stables. It
