Be patient! Lay the filters flat and let the water

Step 5
Once your filters look like the ones on the left you can open them up. The
water will have transferred the color to the whole filter. These will still be wet
so give them some time to dry while they are open.
Step 6
Get creative! You can use the dried filters for just about anything. Based on the
materials you have at home, you can make the dried filters into something new. Pipe
cleaners are great for getting creative. Try to make a flower or a butterfly!
