Cocktails and Conversations: Decking Clayton's Halls
Cocktails and Conversations: Decking Clayton's Halls
Event information
December 9, 2026
6:00 PM - 7:30 PM
Location
The Frick Art Museum Auditorium
Cost:
$15 Member
$20 Adult
$20 Senior (65+)
$10 College Student (w/ ID)
Members of The Frick Societies receive complimentary admission
Description:
Join Collections & Exhibitions Assistant Bella Hanley to learn about building a tour from a wide-ranging collection, decorating a historic home to be both accurate and exciting, and the props that make it all come to life. Attendees will enjoy seasonal cocktails and the opportunity to ask questions about how the Frick's annual Home for the Holidays at Clayton tour comes to life.
