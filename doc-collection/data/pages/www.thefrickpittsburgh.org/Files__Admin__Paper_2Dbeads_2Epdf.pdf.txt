PAPER BEADS
MATERIALS
markers
paper
scissors
glue
string, ribbon, or yarn
01 02 03 04
01
If you are using plain paper, draw your design using many different colors.
02
Next, cut out the triangles. You will want at least 5 for a necklace.
03
Using a straw or pencil you can roll your beads. Start with the wide end
wrapped around the pencil. Then place some glue on the paper all the way
to the point. Roll up your paper. Repeat until you have enough beads.
04
Cut a piece of string. Add your beads to your string. If you have other
beads at home you can add those too. Try making a pattern!
