Artist trading cards
Artist trading cards are mini pieces of artwork that are small enough to fit in
your pocket. It’s fun to make them with friends so that you can trade with each
other and start your own collections of artwork. You can also give your cards
as gifts or keep them for yourself.
Materials: pencil, paper (preferably
card stock), ruler, scissors
Optional materials: colored paper,
glue stick, paints, markers, crayons,
aluminum foil, or any of your favorite
materials!
Using the card stock, measure
and cut your artist trading
cards. These cards are 2 1/2” x
3 1/2”. Make at least four so
that you will have a small
collection to trade.
Design your cards! Fill
them with as much
art as you want.

When you’re finished designing your cards,
make sure to put your artist signature on each
of them. It’s fun to hide your signature so that
others have to look very closely to find it.
tips for getting started
If you’re not sure where to start, think about things you love. What’s your favorite
animal? Vacation? Food? You may also want to explore the three traditional
styles of visual art: landscapes, portraits, and still lifes. See below for more help!
