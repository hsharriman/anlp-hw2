Letter Writing
There was a time when writing a letter was the only way to communicate with someone you couldn't see in
person. These days, we have computers and cellphones to help us stay connected virtually, but there's still
something exciting about receiving a letter in the mail! Think about someone you haven't been able to see
lately. Write them a letter telling them about what you've been up to, and letting them know how much you
miss them!
