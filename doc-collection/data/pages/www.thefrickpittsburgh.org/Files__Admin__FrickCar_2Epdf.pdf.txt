Car of the future
Ever wish you could design your own car? Here’s your chance! You can use the
attached template or do it all on your own.
Supplies
o Paper
o Pencil
o Markers
o Glue
o Scissors
o Anything else you have! (tissue paper, scrap
book paper, pompoms, paper straws)
Steps
1. Print the attached template or draw a car.
2. Design your car using your collage materials. You can lay things out and move them around
before you glue them down.
3. Glue down anything you want to add to your car. Then add a background, where is your
car going?

You can be even more creative and make up a story about your car. Where would you drive in
this car? What sort of fuel does it use (cotton candy, ice cream, broccoli)? How do you turn it
on? Can it do anything special like go under water or fly? Share your cars and your stories with
the Frick on social media.
