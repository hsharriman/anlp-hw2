Event information

Location

Description:
Frick Pittsburgh members are invited to enjoy early access to the Clayton holiday tour before the house opens to the general public on November 20. Space is limited; advanced reservation recommended.
Planning to join us for a preview tour? Because these tours are exclusively available to members, you must be logged into your member account to view available preview tour times and reserve your spot. If you have any trouble accessing your account or seeing the member-only availability, please reach out to us at members@thefrickpittsburgh.org or (412) 342-4086.

Member Preview Tours run Nov. 14-19*
About The Tour
Take in the cheerful atmosphere of a home decked for the holidays on this specialty tour of Clayton. This year, peek into the Frick family and staff's Gilded Age celebrations, from Helen's social debut to winter trips to the White House.
Plan Your Visit:
Clayton tours include the first and second floors. The first floor of the house is wheelchair accessible. Click for more information about accessibility.
All Clayton tours begin at the Grable Visitor Center. Advance reservations are highly recommended.
*The Frick Pittsburgh is closed on Monday, November 16
