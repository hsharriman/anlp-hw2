FAQ
How do I become a member?
You can join, renew, or give a gift of membership online, by calling 412-342-4086 or by visiting the Grable Visitor Center or The Frick Art Museum.
When will I receive my membership card?
You will receive a physical copy of your membership card and acknowledgement letter approximately 1-2 weeks after your purchase.
You will also receive a link to download a digital membership card within one week of your purchase. Digital cards are optional, and you will still receive a physical card if you download it!
If it has been more than two weeks since your payment, please email or call the Membership Department at 412-342-4086 to verify your address and to request a replacement card.
Is my membership tax-deductible?
Your membership contribution is tax-deductible as allowed by law. Memberships at the 1882 Society level and below are fully tax deductible. Your mailed acknowledgement letter will contain any relevant tax information for your gift.
Can I get two membership cards?
If you join at the Dual ($90) level and above you will receive two membership cards.
Do you have a membership for people with disabilities?
The ADA ($30) level admits one person with a seen or unseen disability and an aide.
What if I don’t have my membership card and I want to visit?
If you’ve misplaced your card or haven’t received it by mail yet, don’t worry — just stop by the Grable Visitor Center or The Frick Art Museum, and our Museum Experience Associates can verify your membership.
You can also access your digital membership card right from your smartphone! If you need help downloading it, our staff will be happy to assist you.
Can I bring guests with me?
If you join at the Patron level ($250) you may bring up to two guests. Members at the Benefactor ($500) level and above may bring up to four guests.
Can another person use my membership if I am not present?
At least one named cardholder must be present in order to use your membership.
Is my membership transferable or refundable?
Our memberships are non-transferable and non-refundable. Benefits are subject to change.
Why does the Museum request my email address?
By providing your email address, you will receive our e-newsletter, have the ability to make online reservations for timed tickets and programs, and download a digital membership card. If you have trouble logging in to your online profile, please call 412-342-4086.
Do members need to reserve timed tickets?
Frick members are required to reserve timed exhibition and standard tour tickets when visiting the Frick. Timed-tickets for exhibitions and standard tours are free for members and can be reserved online or at the Grable Visitor Center when you arrive at the Frick.
When is the Café open?
For Café hours, menus, and more information, click here. Reservations are no longer accepted.
Can I use my membership to visit other museums?
Members at the Family ($100) level and above receive reciprocal membership benefits to more than 1,000 museums through the North American Reciprocal Museum Association (NARM), the Reciprocal Organization of Associated Museums (ROAM) and the Art Museum Reciprocal Network (AMRN). Reciprocal benefits vary by organization. For more information and a listing of reciprocal institutions, click here.
Are you the same museum as The Frick Collection in New York? If I am a member here, am I a member there?
Although both institutions are properties that were owned by the Frick family, The Frick Pittsburgh and The Frick Collection are two separate institutions and therefore have separate membership programs. However, if you become a member at the Family ($100) level or above, you can receive free admission to The Frick Collection through our reciprocal museum programs.
Can I give a membership as a gift?
Absolutely! You may purchase a gift of membership here by selecting Gift on a membership level of your choosing. You may also call the Membership Department at 412-342-4086 or purchase a gift membership onsite.
Can I make a contribution in honor or in memory of someone?
You may make an honorary or memorial donation online and include your loved one’s name and contact information in the comments section once you reach the billing page. For memorial gifts, we are pleased to notify the family of your gift if their contact information is provided. For more information, please email or call the Membership Department at 412-342-4086.
How can I find out if my company matches the contributions that I make to the Frick?
Many companies will match charitable contributions made by employees and retirees, doubling or tripling the impact of your gift. Contact your employer’s Human Resources department to find out if it has a matching gift program. Complete and mail the matching gift form along with your contribution to:
The Frick Pittsburgh
Advancement & Engagement Office
7227 Reynolds Street
Pittsburgh, PA 15208
Does the Museum share my information with anyone else?
We will never share your email address. From time to time, the Frick does exchange our members' mailing addresses with other local cultural organizations. If you prefer this information not be shared, please email or call the Membership Department at 412-342-4086.
