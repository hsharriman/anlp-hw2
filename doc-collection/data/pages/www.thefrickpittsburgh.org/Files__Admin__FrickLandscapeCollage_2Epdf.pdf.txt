Landscape collage
A landscape is a type or artwork where nature is the most important thing.
You can think about a place you know or imagine somewhere new.
Supplies
o Paper
o Pencil
o Markers
o Glue
o Scissors
o Anything else you have! (tissue
paper, scrap book paper, pompoms,
paper straws, wrapping paper)
Steps
1. Start with your background. Landscapes should have a foreground (the space
closet to you), middle ground (the space between the foreground and
background), and background (the space farthest away from you).
2. Add some details. Think about what kind of day you want to show (rainy or
sunny). Think about the season you want to create. Is it the city or country? Have
fun with it and use your imagination! But don’t forget to make it all about nature.
3. Share your art with your family! Tell them all about what you made.
Fun tips: straws make for great flower stems. You can flatten them between your
fingers so they will more easily be glued down. Add some texture to your art work with
tissue paper. Tear or cut it into small squares then pinch it together before you glue the
pieces down.
We love to see all your hard work. Share with the Frick your creations.
You may even be growing your at home gallery. We would love to see it!
