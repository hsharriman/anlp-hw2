Windsocks
Windsocks are often used to tell how windy
it is outside. They can also be used to
celebrate something special.
The windsock you make will be a wonderful
way to brighten up your home!
MATERIALS
paper
markers, crayons, or pencils
(something to draw with)
scissors
glue
string or ribbon
tissue paper or streamers
hole punch
Step 1
Create a design. Add your
design to your paper with it
horizontal. You can use these
pictures as a reference. Try to
leave a little space on one side
for when you shape the
windsock.

Step 2
Once you have completed your
design, add your streamers. If
you don't have streamers you
can add strips of tissue paper
or even string. This detail will
help your windsock dance in
the wind.
Step 3
It's time to put it all together!
Once all the decorations you
added have dried, add a strip of
glue to the blank edge. Next roll
the paper and bring the edges
together. You may need some
paper clips to hold it in place until
it dries.
Step 4
The last step is to punch
two holes on opposite sides
of your windsock. Then you
can add your string so you
can hang it up.
Share your art with us!

You can hang it outside or by a window so
