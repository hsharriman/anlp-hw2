paper collage sculptures
Materials
Paper in a variety of colors
glue stick
scissors
pencil
makers
Step 1
Select a piece of paper for the
background. Cut that paper in half.
This way you can make lots of
sculptures. If you have cardstock
that will make an even sturdier base
Step 2
Cut the rest of the colorful paper
into strips. Try to cut them into
different thicknesses. You can
also cut them different lengths
Step 3
Next you can add lots of fun
designs to your sculpture. Here
are some techniques to try.

For this technique, use a thick strip of paper. Cut slits into the paper
about halfway. Then fold over the slits going in the opposite
direction for every other one. Then add glue to the tabs and place
them on your base in a fun curved line
Take a piece of paper. Curl it
around a pencil. Uncurl the
paper slightly. You can glue this
piece down flat or standing
straight up. Try both ways!
You can make a zig zag piece by
folding a strip of paper back and
forth until you get to the end. Then
stretch it out to make it stand tall.
Overlapping the pieces of paper will
create an interesting deisgn.
Add as many pieces as you
want! Try to make some tall
and some short. You can
even glue some pieces to
each other. You can add
texture to your paper by
cutting areas out or adding
lines or shapes with a marker.
