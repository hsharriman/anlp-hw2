Plant Office Hours
Event information
November 4, 2026
11:00 AM - 1:00 PM
Location
The Greenhouse
Cost:
Free and open to all
Description:
The Plant Professor is in! The Frick's Manager of Horticulture and Grounds will be holding open "office hours" to answer questions on the care and condition of plants. Bring photos and questions to the Greenhouse to learn how to best care for your plants, both indoor and outdoor.
Plant Office Hours will be held each Wednesday in November.
Please do not bring leaves, roots, or full plants to the Frick.
