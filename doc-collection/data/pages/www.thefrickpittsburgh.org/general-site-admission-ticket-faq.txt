General Site Admission Ticket FAQ
At the Frick, we are always striving to make our visitors feel welcomed and heard on their visits. For us to enhance your experience and the offerings you're interested in, we are asking visitors to acquire a free general site admission ticket to explore our free offerings, like the Car & Carriage Museum, the Greenhouse, and the permanent collection at The Frick Art Museum, and our paid offerings, like our signature tour of Clayton and temporary exhibitions.
Whether you get a general site admission ticket online or come onto the site without one, we are asking all visitors and Frick members to start their visit at The Grable Visitor Center. Once there, you can check in, get your designated wristband and learn about all the offerings of the day.
Please refer to this FAQ page to get answers to some questions you may have about this new requirement. If you can’t find what you’re looking for here, please direct additional questions to info@thefrickpittsburgh.
What is a general site admission ticket?
A free general site admission ticket is now required to explore our free offerings, like the Car & Carriage Museum, the Greenhouse, and the permanent collection at The Frick Art Museum, and our paid offerings, like our signature tour of Clayton and temporary exhibitions. Paid tickets are still required for our Gilded, Not Golden tour of Clayton, temporary exhibitions, and many of the Frick’s programs.
Why is a general site admission ticket now required?
We are always looking to improve. Over time, this new ticket will allow us to better tailor your visit and the available offerings for you.
By having a better picture of who our visitors are, and where they come from, you will be assisting us in critical fundraising efforts.
By having a more accurate count of the visitors on our site at any given point, we can better ensure the safety and security of all our campus.
How can I get a general site admission ticket?
You can add a free general site admission ticket to your cart while buying a ticket to our Gilded, Not Golden tour and/or an upcoming exhibition. Whether you get a general site admission ticket online ahead of your visit or come onto the site without one, we encourage all members and guests to start their visit at The Grable Visitor Center. Visitors will check in to get their designated wristband.
Do I have to pay for the general site admission ticket?
No, the general site admission ticket is free.
How will Frick staff know I have checked in at The Grable Visitor Center and have received my free general site admission ticket?
You will receive a wristband once you check in indicating that you have your general site admission ticket.
What areas of the Frick require a general site admission ticket?
A free general site admission ticket is required for anyone interested in visiting our free offerings, like the Car & Carriage Museum, the Greenhouse, and the permanent collection at The Frick Art Museum, and/or our paid offerings, like our signature tour of Clayton and temporary exhibitions.
Do I need a separate ticket for each museum or attraction (The Frick Art Museum, The Car & Carriage Museum, Greenhouse, Clayton)?
No, you do not need separate tickets for each. The general site admission ticket will cover you for all the aforementioned. With that said, paid tickets are still required for our Gilded, Not Golden tour of Clayton, temporary exhibitions, and many of the Frick’s programs.
Do I need a ticket for every member of my party, including children?
Yes, every member of your party is required to have a general site admission ticket.
I already got my general site admission ticket online, or I need to get one once I am on site. Where should I start my visit to the Frick?
Whether you have bought tickets online or you are visiting without tickets, all guests should start their visit at The Grable Visitor Center. Our staff is ready to help you have the best experience possible, and would be happy to get you set up with a general site admission ticket.
