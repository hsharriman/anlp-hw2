O Holiday Night: Clayton Tour at Your Own Pace
O Holiday Night: Clayton Tour at Your Own Pace
Event information
December 11, 2026
5:30 PM - 8:00 PM
Location
Check in at the Grable Visitor Center
Cost:
$10 Member
$15 Adult
$15 Senior (65+)
$10 Youth (3-17)
$10 College Student (w/ ID)
Members of The Frick Societies receive complimentary admission
Description:
See the magic of Clayton at night when the house is decorated for the holidays! Spend time in each room on the first floor as you tour at your own pace. Enjoy complimentary holiday snacks and drinks while admiring the outdoors lights on the Frick grounds, before and after your time in Clayton.
Please note: only the first floor of Clayton will be open this evening.
