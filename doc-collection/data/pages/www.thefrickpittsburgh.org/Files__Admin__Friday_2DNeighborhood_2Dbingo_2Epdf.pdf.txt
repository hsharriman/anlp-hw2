NEIGHBORHOOD BINGO
Grab an adult to go on a walk with you. As you safely explore
your neighborhood, try to find as many of these items as you can.
You can walk until you find them all or try to complete one row
on each walk you take.
Don't forget to be kind to your neighbors!
Take pictures as you go and share them with us #frickpghkids!
