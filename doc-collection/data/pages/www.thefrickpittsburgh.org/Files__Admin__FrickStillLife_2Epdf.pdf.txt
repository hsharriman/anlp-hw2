A Still Life of your own
A still life is a type of artwork
where objects are the most
important thing. The objects are
often common objects that the
artist has at home and he or she
places in an arrangement called a
composition.
Here is a painting from the
Frick’s collection. The artist
wanted to fool the viewers to
thinking the objects were real.
What are some things you
see?
William Michael Harnett (American, born Ireland, 1848–1892).
Still Life, 1890. Oil on panel, Frick Art & Historical Center.
Your turn!
Walk around your house and gather 5-10 of your favorite things.
Then find a flat surface to be the base of your still life. If you have a
blanket or sheet available you can drape this over the table as a
uniform bottom layer. Then take turns with your family members
adding to your still life. Be thoughtful with where you place the items!
When you are done take a picture or try to sketch your still life.
