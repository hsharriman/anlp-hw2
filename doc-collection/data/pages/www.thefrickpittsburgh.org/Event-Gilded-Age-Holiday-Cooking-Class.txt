Gilded Age Holiday Cooking Class
Gilded Age Holiday Cooking Class
Event information
November 14, 2026
2:00 PM - 3:30 PM
Location
The Community Room
Cost:
$25 Member
$30 Adult
$30 Senior (65+)
$20 College Student (w/ ID)
Description:
Come together this holiday season to learn about typical Gilded Age holiday treats that have stood the test of time! In this workshop, attendees will make, taste, and take-home sugar plums, festive popcorn balls, and chocolate wine. This workshop is ages 18+.
Please note: This workshop will include almonds. Please abstain from participation if you have a severe nut allergy.
Full ingredient list:
Dried figs, dates, dried apricots, almonds, powdered sugar, granulated sugar, cornstarch, popcorn, light corn syrup, butter, water, marshmallows, vanilla, salt, sherry, brandy, unsweetened chocolate
