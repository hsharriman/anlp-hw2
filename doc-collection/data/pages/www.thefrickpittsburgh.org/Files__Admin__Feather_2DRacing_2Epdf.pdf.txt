Feather Racing
Feather racing was a popular parlor game during the 19th century. You can
learn to play using the instructions below and items you have in your house!

How to play:
You'll need at least two people to play. Divide the players into
two teams.
On a table or countertop, mark your goal lines with the tape or
string. Team 1's goal line should be at one end of the playing
surface, and team 2's goal line should be on the opposite end. If
you don't have a table big enough, you can also play on the floor.
Place the feather directly in the middle, between the two goal
lines.
One player from each team gets a fan. When someone says,
"go," each player tries to fan the feather across the opposite
team's goal line. Whoever succeeds in fanning the feather across
the line wins that round.
The game can have has many rounds as necessary for every
player to have a turn fanning the feather. Whichever team has
the most points after everyone has had a turn, wins!
