Description:
There’s nothing quite like the sound of handbells at the holidays! Beginning their 10th anniversary season, Ring Pittsburgh returns to the Frick with an afternoon of festive favorites, musical surprises, and the dazzling precision of one of the region’s premier handbell ensembles.
Blending the warmth of familiar holiday music with the unexpected versatility of handbells, Ring Pittsburgh brings this unique musical art form to life in a lively concert that is sure to put you in the holiday spirit. Come hear the bells ring, celebrate the season, and experience a holiday tradition with a distinctly musical twist.
Advance registration and pre-payment recommended. Walk-ups accommodated as space permits.

About the Performers
Founded in 2016, Ring Pittsburgh is an auditioned community ensemble of experienced handbell musicians dedicated to expanding appreciation for the art of handbell ringing. The ensemble takes a musical form traditionally associated with church settings and brings it into the concert setting, performing everything from classical and hymn adaptations to pop, rock, original compositions, and seasonal favorites. Ring Pittsburgh educates and entertains audiences throughout southwestern Pennsylvania with performances that showcase the artistry, precision, and versatility of handbell music.
