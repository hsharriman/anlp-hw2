Sport, Fashion, and Gender Equity: Conversations with Women and Girls Foundation
In conjuction with Sporting Fashion: Outdoor Girls 1800 to 1960, the Frick was proud to partner with the Pittsburgh-based Women and Girls Foundation for three equity-minded panel discussions surrounding women's sport and fashion. Listen to the conversations below, and don't miss Sporting Fashion at The Frick Art Musuem through Sunday, September 26, 2021.
