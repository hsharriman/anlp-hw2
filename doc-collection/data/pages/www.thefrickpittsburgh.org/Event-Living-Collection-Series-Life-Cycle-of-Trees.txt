Description:
Just because the leaves are falling doesn’t mean there’s any activity stopping for the trees at the Frick! Take a tour of the historic grounds and learn about the seasonal cycles trees go through annually and over the course of their lives. This tour will include a .5 mile walk.
Two tour times are available, one at 10:30 AM and one at 2:00 PM. Each tour will last approximately 1 hour.

About the Living Collection Series
Explore the the natural world with the Frick's ongoing Living Collection Series. Through walks, tours, lectures, and more, the Living Collection Series uses the Frick's grounds as the backdrop for learning and connection. As an accredited arboretum, the site features dozens of varieties of trees and shrubs, in addition to decorative and vegetable gardens and a historic greenhouse.
