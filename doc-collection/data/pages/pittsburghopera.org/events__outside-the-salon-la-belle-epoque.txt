Indulge in the romance of the Belle Epoque with wine, French cuisine, and captivating melodies from the golden age of Paris.
Outside the Salon is a new event series that brings the power and intimacy of opera out of
traditional venues and into unexpected spaces across Pittsburgh. Each performance takes
place in a different neighborhood, offering audiences a close-up, immersive experience with extraordinary voices in settings where culture and community intersect.
