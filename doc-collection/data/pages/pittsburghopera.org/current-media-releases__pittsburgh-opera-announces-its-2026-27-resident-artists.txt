Pittsburgh Opera announces its 2026-27 Resident Artists
Pittsburgh Opera is proud to announce its stellar class of 2026-27 Resident Artists:
Drew Comer, bass
Maya Davis, mezzo-soprano
Ayanna Delk-Lewis, soprano
Olivia Gacka, stage director
Léa Nayak, soprano
Erik Nordstrom, baritone
Joseph Stevens, pianist
Logan Wagner, tenor
Pittsburgh Opera’s Resident Artist Program is one of the country’s leading training programs for young singers. Over 800 applicants from around the world regularly vie for just a handful of openings.
After completing advanced education such as graduate degrees, Pittsburgh Opera’s Resident Artists further their careers under the guidance of the opera world’s leaders and innovators. They study languages, diction, movement, and acting, while developing their vocal skills, expanding their repertoire, and performing on stage in Pittsburgh Opera productions.
In addition to singers, Pittsburgh Opera’s Resident Artist program includes a Stage Director position and a pianist position.
Pittsburgh Opera’s Resident Artists regularly go on to headline at the world’s most prestigious opera houses, including the Metropolitan Opera. Both Emily Richter (2024) and Shannon Crowley (2026) were Grand Finals Winners of the Metropolitan Opera Eric and Dominique Laffont Competition while they were Pittsburgh Opera Resident Artists.
