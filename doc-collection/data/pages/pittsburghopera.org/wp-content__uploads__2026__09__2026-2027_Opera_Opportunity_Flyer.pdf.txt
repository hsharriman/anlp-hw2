2026–2027 Pittsburgh Opera
Opera Opportunity Program for Students
Pittsburgh Opera is pleased to offer
Opera Opportunity as a way to
include more students at opera
performances. This program allows
students and teachers from eligible
middle and high schools to
purchase reduced-price tickets.
One or more teachers must attend
the prerequisite Workshop for
Educators.
“Students loved attending the
opera and wanted to know when
the next performance was. It has
become a tradition to attend the
opera with families and friends!”
Requirements for participation
• Complete application form, including the signature of the building principal.
• Submit requests for up to 30 tickets (total number of students and adult chaperones).
Requests for number of tickets and preference for date of performance will be honored
whenever possible, but are not guaranteed.
• Arrange for payment no later than one week prior to the performance.
• Attend the corresponding, prerequisite Workshop for Educators.
• Prepare the students before the opera to the fullest extent possible.
• Attend the opera and supervise students. Include one adult for every 8–15 students,
2 adults for 16–23 students, and 3 adults for 24–30 students.
