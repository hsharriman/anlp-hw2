Corporate Giving
Pittsburgh Opera is internationally recognized for its productions, community engagement, education programming, and its prestigious Resident Artist program. Join companies across over a dozen industries that support one of Pittsburgh’s premier performing arts organizations.
Our audiences can be one of your most valuable resources. Performances, concerts, broadcasts, and live and virtual programming reach potential customers and clients locally and nationally.
To explore a potential partnership that fits your company, contact Torrance Gricks, Manager of Corporate Development & Event Fundraising, at 412-281-0912 ext. 225.
Stay connected with Pittsburgh Opera, visit us on LinkedIn!
Benefits of being a Corporate Partner
Partnering with Pittsburgh Opera unlocks exclusive opportunities for your company:
Complimentary tickets to Pittsburgh Opera performances, cast parties, and pre- & post-performance events
Recognition for your company to include (but not limited to):
Your company’s listing in the Opera’s program books
Recognition on Pittsburgh Opera’s website and the Opera’s social media platforms
Recognition in the supertitles at performances
Access to unique behind-the-scenes events
Mingle at the Galaxy Lounge, our exclusive donor lounge at the Benedum Center
Enjoy client entertainment opportunities
Receive a rental discount for your events at the Bitz Opera Factory.
