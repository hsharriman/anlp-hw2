Allegheny County Summer Concert Series
Photo by David Bachman Photgraphy
Pittsburgh Opera often performs at either the South Park Amphitheater or the Hartwood Acres Amphitheater each summer as part of the Allegheny County Summer Concert Series.
These fun, free 90-minute programs typically feature many of opera’s greatest hits, as well as favorite selections from the American songbook. The singers are a mix of current and former Pittsburgh Opera Resident Artists and guest artists.
