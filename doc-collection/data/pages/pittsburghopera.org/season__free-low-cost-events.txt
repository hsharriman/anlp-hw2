Free & Low Cost Events
Resident Artists Ashley Fabian and Andy Berry in a Song Shop concert. Photo credit: David Bachman
Pittsburgh Opera strives to make opera financially and intellectually accessible to all. Join us this season for free and low-cost events such as Song Shop concerts, our Rising Stars concert, outdoor park concerts, and more.
Click the links below for a complete listing of all our public events, free or otherwise, or contact us at 412-281-0912 or via e-mail.
As always, dates, times, locations, and performers are subject to change. Please check our website calendar listings or our Facebook page for the most current information.
