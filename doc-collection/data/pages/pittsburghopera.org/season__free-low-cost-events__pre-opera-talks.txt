Pre-Opera Talks
Kristin Gatch at The Barber of Seville (photo credit: David Bachman)
Ticketholders are invited to attend a Pre-Opera Talk one hour before each performance’s
curtain at the Benedum Center and CAPA Theater, and 45-minutes before curtain at the Byham Theater this season.
Learn about the composer, the story, and some juicy details about the opera! These talks are free to all ticketholders.
The Pre-Opera Talks are given by Kristin Gatch, Assistant to the General Director and Development Research Associate.
