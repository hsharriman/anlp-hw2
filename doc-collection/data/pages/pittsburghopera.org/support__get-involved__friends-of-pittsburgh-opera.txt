Join FRIENDS of Pittsburgh Opera
FRIENDS of Pittsburgh Opera is a vibrant community of supporters who share a passion for opera and help ensure it thrives in our region.
Membership is included with an annual gift of $150 or more, or when you begin a recurring monthly gift of $10 or more. Your generosity provides vital support for Pittsburgh Opera while connecting you to a welcoming community of fellow opera enthusiasts through exclusive events, volunteer opportunities, and special benefits.
FPO membership benefits include:
Monthly newsletters with Pittsburgh Opera updates and insider news
Invitations to community gatherings throughout the season, including our Speaker Series
Early invitations and special pricing for favorite FPO events, including the Holiday Party and Garden Parties
Opportunities to volunteer, connect with fellow opera enthusiasts, and deepen your involvement with Pittsburgh Opera
