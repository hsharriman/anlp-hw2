Outside the Salon “Sip & Sing”
Friday, October 23, 2026, 6:00-9:00PM
Bitz Opera Factory Hahn Rehearsal Hall and Costume Shop
Like wine, opera is a language of region and craft, carried across borders by voice and body alike. Sip & Sing celebrates where the two meet.
Join Pittsburgh Opera’s Resident Artists for an evening of live performances, wine pairings, plus a rare backstage look at Pittsburgh Opera’s creation of our production of Orpheus & Eurydice! Watch our set take shape, create a costume piece, make a community art piece, and go behind the curtain like never before.
Your $50 ticket includes wine tasting, light bites, live entertainment, and behind-the-scenes activities.
Sip & Sing is hosted at the Bitz Opera Factory’s Hahn Rehearsal Hall – not usually open to the public! Get Tickets.
