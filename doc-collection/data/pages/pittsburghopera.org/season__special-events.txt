Special Events
As a principal in the city’s cultural community since 1939, Pittsburgh Opera and its events have developed a reputation for elegance and excitement.
OUTSIDE THE SALON
Multiple Dates
Unexpected Venues, Exceptional Experiences
Outside the Salon is a *NEW* event series that brings the power and intimacy of opera out of traditional venues and into unexpected spaces across Pittsburgh. Each performance takes place in a different neighborhood, offering audiences a close-up, immersive experience with extraordinary voices in settings where culture and community intersect.
FASHION SHOW
Monday, March 15, 2027
Bitz Opera Factory
2425 Liberty Avenue
Don’t miss our iconic fashion event, which combines couture and opera in a dazzling runway presentation. The 2026-27 season’s fashion show theme will be in celebration of International Women’s Month.
