Lesson Plan Stems in the English Language Arts and Mathematics
sections are Common Core Friendly, and are marked: CCF
Arts and Humanities
State Standards: 9.1, 9.2, 9.3, 9.4
What do you see in your mind when you close your eyes? What do you think PreK
the woman in the painting, Woman with Eyes Closed, is seeing? Draw what
you imagine she is seeing in her mind.
Opera orchestras usually include flute, clarinet, horn, trumpet, trombone, PreK
violin, viola, cello, and string bass. Learn the names of the orchestra
instruments and match them to photos of the instruments. What do you think
an orchestra would sound like if it included a bongo, slapstick, guiro, or sizzle
cymbal?
Draw a picture of a friend with his/her eyes closed and then another picture PreK
with his/her eyes open. What details have you included? Which do you like
better—eyes open or closed? Why?
How does a composer decide to write an opera? What comes first? The PreK/ES
story or the music? Woman with Eyes Closed is an opera is about a stolen
painting. What kinds of stories do you think would make a good opera? What
experiences, ideas, stories, or objects inspire you to create?
Woman with Eyes Closed is an opera in eleven scenes. How are operas with ES
no intermissions different from grand operas with overtures, acts, and
pauses?
Jennifer Higdon wrote the opera Woman with Eyes Closed for a chamber ES
orchestra, but she wrote her opera Cold Mountain for a full orchestra. What
are the differences? What instruments are usually included in operas?

Lesson Plan Stems
Sometimes painters create art that looks so real it could be a photograph, ES
and other times painters capture their “feelings” (impressions) on canvas.
Look at some paintings to determine if they represent realism or
impressionism.
The lead character in Woman with Eyes Closed is the mother, Mona Lack, ES/MS
whose part was written for a contralto. How does that voice range compare
to other operatic voice ranges? How would you describe how a contralto
voice sounds?
In this opera, the museum curator is described as intelligent, dedicated to ES/MS
her work, and an aesthete—a person with a special appreciation of art and
beauty. How would this character express those qualities on stage? How
should she be costumed to reflect these qualities? Design a costume and
make-up for this character that projects these characteristics.
Look over this instrumentation for Woman with Eyes Closed, which includes ES/MS
some unusual percussion instruments.

How can two players play all these instruments? How can they pre-set the

What happens to people’s voices as we age? Do opera singers’ voices drop ES/MS
to lower ranges as they age? Do rock singers’ voices drop to lower ranges
as they age? Name some popular singers whose voices have changed over
time—how would you describe what their voices sound like now? Can opera
singers continue to perform? If they are “past their prime” as singers, what
other careers can they pursue in the arts?
Jennifer Higdon, the composer of Woman with Eyes Closed, is a female ES/MS
opera composer. What other female composers are creating new work
today? What female composers wrote opera or classical music in the past?
Jennifer Higdon has won three Grammy Awards for her compositions. These ES/MS
awards are regarded as the most prestigious and significant awards in the
music industry worldwide. What categories are included? What are the six
standard awards? What are the 11 genre-specific fields? What other opera
or classical composers have also won Grammy awards?
Woman with Eyes Closed is an opera about visual art. Explore the artistic ES/MS

Lesson Plan Stems
What does depicting someone with closed eyes symbolize in art? How does ES/MS
depicting a subject with eyes closed (as opposed to eyes open) affect how
an audience might receive and interpret a painting?
In 1987, Lucian Freud was invited to choose 29 paintings for “The Artist’s ES/MS
Eye” exhibition series at the National Gallery in London. He chose some Old
Masters and wrote in the catalogue that he expects a painting to “astonish,
disturb, seduce, and convince” its audience. Which paintings did he choose
for this show? In your opinion, does Freud’s own work meet what he asks of
painting? Which paintings would YOU choose for such an exhibition?
Drawing with your eyes closed, also known as blind drawing, is an intriguing ES/MS
art activity that allows you to tap into your intuition and subconscious mind.
Create several drawings with your eyes closed. What have you discovered
about yourself?
What is an opera premiere? Why is a premiere important? Who commissions ES/MS
operas for a premiere, and what’s involved in commissioning an opera?
Opera Philadelphia commissioned Woman with Eyes Closed while David
Devan was the company’s leader. What new operas premiered at Opera
Philadelphia during General Director David Devan’s leadership? Why was
the premiere of Woman with Eyes Closed delayed, allowing this opera’s
premiere to be scheduled at Pittsburgh Opera?
Jennifer Higdon uses unusual instrumental colors in her compositions, such MS/HS
as alto flute, as well as using unusual percussion instruments. Listen to
some of her music and identify some unusual colors, or timbres. Could you
say that her compositional techniques and sound colors are unique?
Art theft as the basis for stories is not uncommon: Woman in Gold is a 2015 MS/HS
biographical drama film based on the true story of Maria Altmann, whose
aunt, Adele Bloch-Bauer, was painted by Gustav Klimt. The painting was
stolen from Altmann’s relatives by the Nazis just before World War II began.
How was Klimt’s painting style different from Picasso, Monet, Matisse,
Gauguin, and Lucian Freud?
How are musicians influenced by their own cultural environments? Jennifer MS/HS
Higdon commented that her musical style grew out of exposure to popular
music such as the Beatles, the Rolling Stones, and Simon & Garfunkel,
rather than to classical music. As a result, she describes her compositional
process as "intuitive" and "instinctive," rather than adhering to classical forms
and structures. Analyze and compare the form and style of her compositions
to other opera composers, such as Mozart, Puccini, and Verdi. How might
these classical composers themselves have been influenced by the music of
their own cultures?
Why do contemporary arts organizations, such as symphonies and operas, MS/HS
need support beyond ticket sales? What percentage of a performing arts
organization’s budget is supported by ticket sales?

Lesson Plan Stems
Composer Jennifer Higdon was struck by the original art heist in 2012, and MS/HS
its aftermath wherein police captured the thieves, but learned that the
paintings themselves may have been destroyed by a relative in an effort to
protect the thieves. In this opera, she asks: What is the value of art to an
individual? Does that value supersede the value of family, even if that family
is criminal? What do YOU think?
In Woman with Eyes Closed, Higdon uses extended techniques, which push MS/HS
the musicians to play their instruments in nontraditional ways. Explain what
the composer means when she says, “The pianist reaches inside the piano
and puts their hand on the strings. It’s a more contemporary feel and gives
me a wider color palette.” How can playing an instrument in a new way
create a novel sound? What other contemporary musicians are known for
playing their instruments differently from how most musicians would play the
same instrument?
The instrumentation for Woman with Eyes Closed includes a C Flute HS
doubling on Alto Flute in G. How are the instruments different? How can one
instrumentalist play both parts? How common is Alto Flute in opera?
Many of Jennifer Higdon's pieces are considered neoromantic, but her music HS
also includes non-traditional harmonic progressions and intricate rhythms.
What is meant by the term “neoromantic”? Listen to her music from Woman
with Eyes Closed. Why might it be called neoromantic? In the score for
Woman with Eyes Closed, find some examples of parallel fifths, rhythmic
ostinato, minimalism, and motivic ambiguity.
Pulitzer Prize and three-time Grammy-winning composer Jennifer Higdon HS
taught herself to play flute at the age of 15 and began formal musical studies
at 18, with an even later start in composition at the age of 21. Why are these
ages considered unusual? If someone already knows how to read music, is it
easier to learn additional instruments?

Lesson Plan Stems

Jennifer Higdon lacked formal music training as a child, so in college she MS/HS
struggled to catch up with students who were far more advanced,
acknowledging that she didn’t know any of the basics: no music theory, no
understanding of chords, intervals, and no keyboard skills. Despite these
challenges, she established herself as hard working and resilient, even when
faced with discouragement from some professors. How important is it to learn
skills for an intended career before college? What does it mean to be a hard
worker and resilient student?
Opera Philadelphia commissioned Woman with Eyes Closed while David MS/HS

Devan’s successor at Opera Philadelphia as General Director is Anthony Roth

Investigate the career trajectory for a theater design student. What training and MS/HS

Composer Jennifer Higdon's list of commissioners is extensive. She has MS/HS

Lesson Plan Stems
The crux of Woman with Eyes Closed is the art heist—the stealing of ES/MS

the etymology of the word “steel”? List some phrases that use one of these

particular historical event would make good opera material.
When painting portraits of real people, Lucian Freud required his models to ES/MS
have extended sittings, which means they were holding poses for very long
periods of time. Write a “diary entry” of the model’s thoughts and feelings
experienced during extended sittings for the painting Woman with Eyes
Closed.
Artists are trained to observe, to try to really “see” their subjects. Lucian ES/MS
Freud once said, “The longer you look at an object, the more abstract it
becomes, and, ironically, the more real.” What do you think he meant? What
is the difference between seeing, gazing, and looking; what do each of these

Librettist Jerre Dye is both a playwright and an opera librettist. What do both ES/MS

elements needed to write both. Pick a scene from your favorite book or
movie and rewrite it as both a scene from a play and a scene from an opera.
Following the robbery on which Woman with Eyes Closed is based, the ES/MS
Kunsthal Museum was criticized for its allegedly lax security measures.
Consider both the denotation and connotation of the word “lax”: what does
the term “lax” mean, and what does it imply? If a museum’s security system
is publicly called lax, what might be the consequences of this label for the
museum and all those who work for it and on its behalf?
When commenting on her opera Woman with Eyes Closed, Jennifer Higdon MS/HS
has stated: “Opera is interesting if it is about people. It’s not about the pieces

Character Themes for Woman with Eyes Closed
Eleanore N. Childs, a Pittsburgh attorney and child advocate, worked with teachers for
more than a decade to research character education and develop the Heartwood
Ethics Curriculum for Children. Heartwood’s life lessons through literature promote
understanding and practice of seven universal character attributes, which constitute the
foundation of community among all people.
The Heartwood Institute defined seven universal character attributes:
 Courage is not only mastering fear; it is the ability to face difficulty or risk with integrity and
honor.
 Loyalty is being faithful to a person, group, or ideal based on understanding that we are all part
of something greater than ourselves.
 Justice is being guided by a balance of truth, reason, and fairness to all.
 Respect is regard for all life on earth. It calls us to value all people and their contributions,
viewpoints, and differences, such as culture, religion, age, race, and ability.
 Hope guides our future through faith and aspiration, helping us to rise above selfishness and
despair.
 Honesty is being truthful and honorable, the foundation for friendship and community.
 Love is deep care and concern for others, encompassing all the attributes. The more love we
give, the richer we become.
Discuss two attributes—LOYALTY and COURAGE—after reading the synopsis of
Woman with Eyes Closed.
1. When the Inspector questions Mona, she lies to him to buy her son Thomas
some time, even though she knows Thomas is guilty of theft. Is Mona’s act of
lying to protect a loved one a form of loyalty, or is it something else? Consider
who Mona may be protecting with her lies: her son? Herself? Her mother’s
memory?
2. In Mona’s case, does her ability to face the truth of her son’s actions and still
attempt to protect Thomas—knowing he is guilty—translate into a kind of
courage, or do her subsequent actions indicate that she lacks courage?
3. Three different endings allow the audience to experience different aspects of
Mona’s character. Which ending might speak to loyalty? Which to courage?
Why?
4. The three different endings also allow the audience to consider different aspects
of courage, in that each requires a sacrifice of some sort. Is the willingness to
give up something—the idea of a price to be paid—a necessary element of
courage? Which ending requires the most courage? Why?
