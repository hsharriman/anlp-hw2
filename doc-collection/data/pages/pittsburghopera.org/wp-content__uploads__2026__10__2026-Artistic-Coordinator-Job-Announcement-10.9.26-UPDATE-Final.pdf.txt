Artistic Coordinator
Job Announcement
Position Summary
Pittsburgh Opera, Inc., a Level II member of Opera America and a is seeking a full time Artistic
Coordinator to support the Artistic Department and administer the company’s renowned
Resident Artist Program.
The Artistic Coordinator reports to and supports the Director of Artistic Operations and is an
integral member of the company’s Artistic Department. Also works closely with Manager of
Artistic Administration, Director of Production, and Production Coordinator.
Key Responsibilities:
Artist Services: Arranges travel, housing, and ground transportation for all visiting artists and
serves as the primary point of contact for all relevant logistics. Maintains financial paperwork for
visiting artists, establishes artist fee and per diem schedule for Finance Department and
distributes fee, per diem checks and reimbursements to all Principal and Resident Artists.
Responsible for obtaining all necessary paperwork and guiding the process for necessary work
visas for foreign artists. Works with preferred properties to establish seasonal hotel rate
agreements. Liaises with Artist Hospitality Committee.
Resident Artist Program: Responsible for administering all Resident Artist administrative
functions, including but not limited to contracts, stipend and expenses reimbursements, music
distribution, and facilitation of release requests. Serves a primary point of contact for Resident
Artist engagement logistics. Oversees the Resident Artist audition process and manages all
scheduling, communications, venue relations, and staff travel/accommodations. Serves as
monitor for all Resident Artist auditions. Manages interdepartmental requests, including artist
interviews, bio/headshot updates, teacher workshop appearances, etc.
Artistic Department General: Supports and manages the numerous activities of the Artistic
Department. Coordinates artist interviews and social media appearances and proofreads artistic
content for marketing materials. Processes Artistic department expense reporting. Responsible
for drafting, collating and arranging artist agreements and conveying to American Guild of
Musical Artist representatives as necessary. Manages and fulfills comp ticket requests for
visiting artists and Resident Artists.
Artistic Department Activities: Attends all Sitzprobes, technical rehearsals, dress rehearsals
and performances as well as other Opera events throughout the season. Manages supertitles for
all Pittsburgh Opera productions. Serves as Artistic Department representative for run-out
engagements as necessary. Coordinates Principal Artist auditions. Works with Production
Coordinator on Daily Schedule logistics and distribution. Responsible for Artist wellness checks
on performance days.

Qualifications:
 Ability to read music.
 Minimum of 2 years of experience working in an opera company, festival or training
program; demonstrated success in managing artists, contracts and schedules.
 Experience working with collective bargaining agreements preferred.
 Excellent communication skills, both written and oral; ability to influence and engage a
wide range of artists and outside organizations and build long-term relationships.
 Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite preferred.
 Strong organizational and time management skills with exceptional attention to detail.
 A professional, flexible, and resourceful style; the ability to work independently and as a
team player, to take initiative, and to manage multiple tasks and projects at a time.
 Must be available and willing to work nights and weekends.
 High energy, passion and commitment for Pittsburgh Opera’s mission, values and ethical
standards.
 Ability to lift and carry material 25-50 lbs and work evenings and weekends.
 Undergraduate degree in a relevant field, or equivalent combination of educational and
professional experience.
 Reliable transportation and the ability to travel independently to assigned work locations
as required.
This is full-time exempt position that requires a flexible schedule, including evenings and
weekends.
Salary range for this position is $45,000-49,000 annually commensurate with experience and
demonstrated skills.
Benefits include medical, and vision; paid vacation and holidays.
To apply, please respond by email only to Kristin Gatch at kgatch@pittsburghopera.org. Your
email should include a cover letter and resume. Please indicate “ARTISTIC COORDINATOR
Application” in the subject line.
Candidates must be legally authorized to work in the United States.
The Pittsburgh Opera is a tax exempt 501(c)(3) organization and is an Equal Opportunity
Employer.
