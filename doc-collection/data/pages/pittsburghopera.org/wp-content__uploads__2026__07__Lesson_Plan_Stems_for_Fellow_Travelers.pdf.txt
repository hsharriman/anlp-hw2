Lesson Plan Stems for Fellow Travelers
How does the opera Fellow Travelers by Gregory Spears and Greg Pierce
intersect with all academic content areas?
Begin with a lesson plan stem and connect the opera to classroom curricula.
NOTES: Suggested grade levels are listed to the right of each lesson plan stem.
PreK=Pre-Kindergarten, ES=elementary school, MS=middle school, HS=high school
Arts and Humanities
State Standards: 9.1, 9.2, 9.3, 9.4
Opera composers and librettists write the music and words as collaborators. PreK
What does it mean to collaborate? Tell your classmates about a time you
may have collaborated with someone: What did you do together? Why did
you choose to work this way? Did you enjoy working with others? Practice
collaborating by creating a drawing with another student.
Listen to a few different pieces of music from an opera: a chorus number, a PreK
small number of singers such as a duet or quartet, and then to an aria. Can
your ears “hear” how many people are singing at the same time? How can
you tell when there are different numbers of people singing? What do you
listen for in these songs that helps you decide if it’s one, two, or more people
singing?
Fellow Travelers was written so that several singers in this opera sing ES
multiple roles. Why do you think some performers sing multiple roles? How
can the opera be staged so that the audience understands when one singer
is singing a different character? How might that affect production costs?
Listen to some sections from Fellow Travelers, which is sung in English, ES/MS
then listen to some selections from a different opera that is sung in a
different language. Do you think it is easier to understand an opera sung in
English? Why or why not?
Fellow Travelers has nine singers and no chorus listed on the American ES/MS
Guild of Musical Artist website. In this opera, some singers sing multiple
roles. What is a swing singer on Broadway? Is that the same as opera
singers who sing multiple roles?
Pittsburgh Opera Education 2

Lesson Plan Stems
Gregory Spears, the composer of Fellow Travelers, studied in the United ES/MS
States and Denmark. What kind of music is popular among young people in
Denmark today? How does it compare to the music that you and your
friends enjoy?
Greg Pierce, the librettist of Fellow Travelers, also writes plays, fiction, and ES/MS
lyrics. Is writing a play the same as writing an opera libretto? How does
writing a play, a libretto, or lyrics differ from writing prose fiction? Try
rewriting a section of one of your favorite stories as a scene from an opera.
What did you have to change or add to retell the story in this different form?
Which style do you prefer: prose writing, screen writing, play writing, or
libretto writing? Why do you prefer your chosen style?
Since 2016, Fellow Travelers has been produced by at least a dozen other ES/MS
opera companies: Cincinnati Opera, Prototype Festival (New York City),
Lyric Opera of Chicago, Minnesota Opera, Des Moines Metro Opera,
Virginia Opera, Arizona Opera, Boston Lyric Opera, Florida Grand Opera,
Opera Columbus, Madison Opera, and Opera Parallèlle. Are all these opera
companies the same size? How does Pittsburgh Opera compare in size to
these other companies? How does the size of an opera company relate to
its repertoire and productions?
Look at some production photos of Fellow Travelers. The opera has 16 ES/MS
scenes, so choices for scenery, props, and costumes must match the time
period and story. Therefore, time is limited for scene changes. What choices
have the designers made in the productions? For example, why would a
Fordham pennant be seen in one scene?
Fellow Travelers has 16 scenes in two parts. Do the sets determine the MS
staging decisions and passage of time? Or is it the other way around? Or a
little of both? How do stage directors make decisions?
When a new show becomes popular and seems to be the kind that will be MS
performed for many years, it is said to have “legs”. Fellow Travelers was
praised as “a near-perfect example of fast-flowing musical drama” by The
New York Times and is already widely considered a contemporary classic.
How unusual is this review? Research other contemporary operas to
determine how many have “legs”.
Thematically, color plays a significant role in Fellow Travelers (Red scare, MS/HS
Lavendar scare). What is a pastel color, and how does it compare to a
primary or secondary color? How is the idea of “pastel color” used in this
opera?
Gregory Spears, composer of Fellow Travelers, studied in the United States MS/HS
and Denmark. Listen to some music from the opera. How would you
describe his musical style? Does his music remind you of other kinds of
classical music? Can you identify different elements that seem to comprise
his style, such as romanticism, minimalism, and early music?
Pittsburgh Opera Education 3

Lesson Plan Stems
Fellow Travelers is published by Schott Music, a publishing company that MS/HS
was founded in 1770, and is still in business today. The website lists the
orchestra instrumentation this way: fl.ob.cl(bcl)-2tbn-pno-str(min 4.4.3.3.2).
Which instruments are in the orchestra? Why does Schott use this system to
describe orchestral instrumentation?
One writer described the music in Fellow Travelers as “subtly beautiful, with MS/HS
an unusual orchestration and tonal harmony that includes pastel
dissonances.” What audience of readers do you think this person was
writing for? What do you think is meant by the descriptions “subtly beautiful”
and “pastel dissonances”? Listen to an excerpt of the opera on the
Pittsburgh Opera website and write your own description. Consider who you
might be writing for: Your friends? Teachers? Social media? How does
addressing a specific audience help shape your description of this music?
What makes a work of art a “classic”? Think of another classic that you have MS/HS
read or seen and compare it to Fellow Travelers. What qualities do they
share?
Fellow Travelers has 16 scenes in two parts, which means scene changes MS/HS
have to happen quickly and the places themselves have to be easily
recognized by the audience. What is the “iconography” of different places
that make them instantly recognizable or understood by an audience? One
opera reviewer noted that the Fellow Travelers production maintained
tension through swift scene changes. How can quick scene changes such
as these impact the drama of a story? (Consider how “quick edits” are used
in films.) Select a story that takes place in many locations and design
scenery that would work for multiple scenes.
Both composer Gregory Spears and librettist Greg Pierce have lived and MS/HS
worked at Yaddo, an artists’ community in Saratoga Springs, New York.
Yaddo is the leading nonprofit retreat for artists and writers, who come from
all nations and backgrounds to live and work in a supportive community.
How would collaborating with other artists benefit opera creators?
Gregory Spears’s vocal music has been described as “charmingly HS
idiosyncratic, alert to the revealing irregularities of speech in dialogue and, in
arias, animated with squiggly ornaments reminiscent of the early Baroque.”
Listen to several arias and identify when these ornaments occur. Why are
they included? How does having this kind of ornamentation impact the
story?

Career Education and Work
State Standards: 13.1, 13.2, 13.3, 13.4
Composer Gregory Spears studied in Denmark as a Fulbright Scholar. What PreK
is a scholar? When you think about what you might want to be when you
grow up, how important is getting a good education for that job?
Gregory Spears is the composer of Fellow Travelers. What is a composer? PreK
Can you name any famous composers, like Mozart or Beethoven? How do
you think they learned music? What do you imagine they were like as
children?
The composer of this opera, Gregory Spears, won a Fulbright scholarship to ES
study music in Denmark. What is a scholarship? Why might a scholarship be
important to earn? Do you think getting a scholarship will be important for
you to reach your career goals? Why or why not?
One of the real people depicted in Fellow Travelers was a United States ES/MS
Senator. How does someone get involved in politics and get elected to a
city, state, or national office? What do you think attracts people to careers in
politics? What would you enjoy most about being a politician? Least?
One of the historical persons depicted in Fellow Travelers was a United ES/MS
States Senator, which is an elected political position, However, not all
political positions are acquired by election; some political careers are begun
through an appointment. What is meant by the term “political appointment”?
How are people appointed to political positions? How does that career differ
from an elected official’s career? Research political positions that are
appointed.
Composer Gregory Spears earned multiple degrees in composition: a B.M. ES/MS
from The Eastman School of Music, an M.M. from the Yale School of Music,
and a Ph.D. from Princeton University. How do composers and librettists
learn their skills today? Is it necessary to attend college? Is earning
advanced degrees an unusual career path for a composer or musician?
One of the characters in Fellow Travelers is a journalist, someone who MS
gathers and disseminates information to the public. What skills are needed
to be a journalist? What education is required? What media formats would
journalists have used in the 1950s, and what formats are used today? How
has the career of journalism changed since the 1950s? How do journalists
ensure accuracy nowadays? How important is journalism in a democracy?
Both composer Gregory Spears and librettist Greg Pierce have lived and MS
worked at Yaddo, an artists’ community in Saratoga Springs, New York.
How do Yaddo and other supportive communities for artists function when
compared to the patronage systems of wealthy individuals that supported
artists in the past? What do you think are the advantages and disadvantages
of each?
Pittsburgh Opera Education 5

Lesson Plan Stems
Some of the historical people depicted as characters in Fellow Travelers are MS/HS
a United States Senator and an aide in a government office. Research
Joseph McCarthy, Roy Cohn, G. David Schine, and Charles E. Potter. Trace
their careers and compare their historical portrayals to how they are
characterized in the opera.
As of 2024, more than three million people were employed by the United MS/HS
States’ Federal government, which accounts for less than 2% of all
employment in the U.S. Research government careers in the 1950s and
today. What are the benefits of working for the federal government? How
difficult is it to get a job with the government? What are the advantages and
disadvantages of a career in government agencies? What opportunities
have changed since the time in which this opera is set? How has the size of
the federal workforce changed since then, when compared to the overall
workforce population? Is working for the government lucrative? Why would
someone want to work as a public servant?
One of the characters in Fellow Travelers is an aide in a senator’s office. MS/HS
What does an aide do and how does one apply for this job? What
clearances are required? What are the benefits and drawbacks of being a
senators’ aides: do they have access to confidential information, sit in on
senate meetings and hearings, or have the ability to influence the opinions
of a senator? Are these jobs considered steppingstones for other careers?
One of the characters in Fellow Travelers is a journalist. In the 1950s, there MS/HS
were no cell phones, personal computers, internet, or social media, so news
and information would have been disseminated through fewer platforms and
at a slower pace than we experience today. What are some advantages of
having a lag in reporting time? What are some of the disadvantages? How
does today’s expectations of instantaneous access impact how journalists
work and how they build their careers?
Gregory Spears spent a year as a Fulbright Scholar at the Royal Danish HS
Academy of Music, where he studied early Danish minimalism and
orchestration with composers Hans Abrahamsen and Per Nørgård. What is
a Fulbright scholarship? What does it allow a recipient to do? How does
someone become a Fulbright Scholar? Is the program prestigious?
In Fellow Travelers, Hawkins betrays his friend and lover Timothy by HS
informing the government investigating committee that Timothy is gay. One
of the motivating factors that drives Hawkins to this betrayal is the need to
preserve his own career with the government. In what ways can the
perceived or actual needs of a career come into conflict with a person’s
personal life, desires, beliefs, and ethics? Describe your reaction to
Hawkin’s decision. If he hadn’t informed on Timothy, what consequences to
his career might Hawkins have faced? Can you think of a situation when it
would be appropriate to betray someone’s secret for the sake of a career?

Lesson Plan Stems
Civics and Government
State Standards: 5.1, 5.2, 5.3, 5.4
One of the ideas this opera explores is censorship, the term we use when PreK
certain words or ideas aren’t allowed. Do you know any words that you
aren’t allowed to say? Why might some words be ok to say and others are
not? Imagine if a common word such as “and” was censored. Try telling a
short story without using the word “and”. How difficult was it to tell the story
this way?
Fellow Travelers reminds us that the 1950s was a time of fear for many PreK
people. What does the word “fear” mean? What are some things grown-up
people might fear? What are some things you might fear? Why are you
fearful of these things?
One of the purposes of the House of Representatives and the Senate is to PreK/ES
debate ideas to create laws and govern the country. A debate is a
conversation where different points of views about ideas are discussed
(think: pros and cons.) Pick an idea to debate with a friend, such as
chocolate vs. vanilla, school uniforms, or longer recess time. How will you
support your ideas about your topic? How can you convince your friend
that your ideas are correct?
Color figures prominently in this opera because colors can be symbols that PreK/ES
stand for other meanings. What does a white wedding gown mean? What
does a red rose mean? What do red-white-and-blue mean to Americans?
What colors are in other nations’ flags, and what do those colors mean?
In Fellow Travelers, some of the characters are afraid that personal details ES
would be shared with powerful people who could hurt them. How do you
keep personal details safe?
In Fellow Travelers, Timothy, in agony over his fraught relationship, tells ES
Hawkins he’s decided to enlist in the Army. Where are Army recruitment
offices in Pittsburgh? What are the rules for enlisting? What are the
requirements to be eligible to enlist?
Politicians are elected to office to represent all the people who live in their ES
districts. How do elected officials know what their constituents want? How
do constituents communicate with their representatives? What can you do
if you disagree with the decisions your elected officials are making? What
are some of the things that others have done to make change?
One significant term associated with 1950s politics and the opera Fellow ES
Travelers is the concept of blacklisting. What is a blacklist? What does it
mean if someone is on a blacklist? Investigate some famous people who
were blacklisted in the 1950s. Why were they blacklisted? How did that
impact their lives and careers?

Read the Declaration of Independence, particularly the “We hold these ES/MS
truths to be self-evident…” sections. What rights does this document seem
to establish, and for whom? What rights were not guaranteed to all of its
citizens in the United States’ Declaration of Independence? [You can start

already enumerated? Are there any current amendments yet to be ratified
that also focus on rights? If you were to propose an amendment, what
would you want to change or add?
As is seen in the opera Fellow Travelers, during the 1950s some MS/HS
government workers kept details about their personal lives secret. This is
called self-censoring. What’s the difference between government
censorship, societal censorship, and self-censorship? How does today’s
access to social media impact what is public and what is private
information? What are some people fearful of other people knowing today?
Parts of Washington, D.C. were built on drained and reclaimed wetlands or MS/HS
marshy areas, so that some parts of it are naturally prone to flooding and
poor drainage. However, the political phrase “the swamp” is often used to
describe Washington D.C., despite the fact that it is NOT built on a swamp.
What do the political phrases “the swamp” and “drain the swamp” imply
today? What does the Washington D.C. neighborhood called “Foggy
Bottom” imply? How did that neighborhood earn its name?
As with the opera Fellow Travelers, several popular films also depict the MS/HS
Red Scare and the McCarthy era: Julie & Julia, Oppenheimer, and Bridge

some films or historical novels make changes to historical events? What
are your feelings about that: should historical films allow artistic license
with historical events? Are films a good way to teach people about history?
What do you think is the best way to learn about our country’s history?
One of the characters in Fellow Travelers is a journalist. What do you think HS
the main goal of news journalism should be? What is meant by biased
reporting? Do you think that the news in the United States is unbiased?
What media is used to disseminate news reporting? Where should people
get their news from? Does the United States government support news
journalism? Do you think the news in the United States is controlled by the
government? Should there be an official government news source? Why or
why not?
Pittsburgh Opera Education 8

Lesson Plan Stems
Pittsburgh Opera marketing for Fellow Travelers includes the phrase “the HS
Red Scare, tinted lavender”. What was the Red Scare in the 1950s? How
did the U.S. government respond to the Red Scare? What role did the U.S.
Senate play in addressing the Red Scare? What role did the House of
Representatives, the Supreme Court, and the President play? What does
the phrase “tinted lavender” imply? How did the U.S. federal government
conduct investigations during the 1950s, and is the process the same
today, or has it changed? Are there any parallels to the Red Scare today?
Do current news stories about immigration draw parallels to the 1950s Red
Scare?
During the 1950s, it was common practice for police, the Central HS
Intelligence Agency (CIA), and the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) to
release the names of people arrested or questioned for committing acts of
“moral perversion” to local news journalists, who were then asked to
publish the names, addresses, and places of work for those accused. This
practice was seen as a necessary step to “protect the public”. The alleged
acts of moral perversion included any same-sex interaction between two
individuals determined to be intimate: holding hands, walking arm-in-arm,
hugging, dancing, etc. What might the government’s motive have been in
publishing the names of its employees being investigated of
homosexuality? What is this practice of public outing called today? What
do you think of this practice? Are there other examples of personal actions
that the government might seek to make publicly known?
Investigate how politics has historically used the concept of “otherism”— HS
defining and treating people as fundamentally different and inferior. What
political movements were successful in using this concept? How did these
movements spread their ideas? How has this concept been used in United
States’ political history? What are the outcomes of otherism and its
propaganda, an “us vs. them” focus in societies? Compare otherism to the
foundational statement in the United States Declaration of Independence
that "all men are created equal" and explore the cognitive dissonance
created when political movements claim both ideas.

Lesson Plan Stems

In Fellow Travelers, several scenes occur in Timothy’s apartment. Research ES/MS

Fellow Travelers takes place in the 1950s, close to the U.S. involvement MS/HS

Pittsburgh Opera Education 12

Lesson Plan Stems
Summaries are intended to be brief but still address the pertinent ideas in a MS
text, which can be a difficult task to do. Writing flash fiction is a good way to
practice this kind of succinct writing. In only 100 words, retell the story of
Fellow Travelers in a flash fiction format. Keep in mind you must include only
the essential elements of character and plot development to have a sense of
the story’s beginning, middle, and end. What will you include from this story?
What will you leave out?
The opera Fellow Travelers is based on a book of the same name by HS
Thomas Mallon. It’s described as “a searing historical novel set in 1950s
Washington, D.C.—a world of dominated by personalities like Nixon, Lyndon
Johnson, and Joe McCarthy—and infused with political drama, unexpected
humor, and heartbreak.” Read the summary of the opera and consider if this

Character Themes for Fellow Travelers
Eleanore N. Childs, a Pittsburgh attorney and child advocate, worked with
teachers for more than a decade to research character education and develop the
Heartwood Ethics Curriculum for Children. Heartwood’s life lessons through
literature promote understanding and practice of seven universal character
attributes, which constitute the foundation of community among all people.
The Heartwood Institute defined seven universal character attributes:
• Courage is not only mastering fear; it is the ability to face difficulty or risk with integrity and
honor.
• Loyalty is being faithful to a person, group, or ideal based on understanding that we are all
part of something greater than ourselves.
• Justice is being guided by a balance of truth, reason, and fairness to all.
• Respect is regard for all life on earth. It calls us to value all people and their contributions,
viewpoints, and differences, such as culture, religion, age, race, and ability.
• Hope guides our future through faith and aspiration, helping us to rise above selfishness
and despair.
• Honesty is being truthful and honorable, the foundation for friendship and community.
• Love is deep care and concern for others, encompassing all the attributes. The more love
we give, the richer we become.
