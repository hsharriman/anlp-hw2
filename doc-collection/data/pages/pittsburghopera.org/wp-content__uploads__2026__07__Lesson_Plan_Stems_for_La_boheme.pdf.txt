La bohème
How does the opera La bohème by Giacomo Puccini, Luigi Illica,
and Giuseppe Giacosa intersect with all academic content areas?
Begin with a lesson plan stem and connect the opera to classroom curricula.
NOTES: Suggested grade levels are listed to the right of each lesson plan stem.
PreK=Pre-Kindergarten, ES=elementary school, MS=middle school, HS=high school
Arts and Humanities
State Standards: 9.1, 9.2, 9.3, 9.4
At the end of an opera, all the performers appear together on stage for a PreK
“curtain call,” which is when the audience shows its appreciation for the
show. How do audiences show appreciation for performers? How do artists
thank audiences during a curtain call? How would you organize a curtain
call for this opera? Which characters will go first? Last? Why? Practice
giving a curtain call with your classmates.
One very famous song in La bohème is Musetta’s Waltz, which has three PreK
beats in each group. Practice clapping a series of STRONG-weak-weak
beats. Listen to music in a meter of three and sway with the music.
Puccini wrote ten operas, including one called Il trittico, which includes PreK
three short operas. Find examples of items in groups of three.
In the first scene of La bohème, there is a fireplace in the bohemians' PreK
apartment, but real fire cannot be used on stage. What colors would you
use to paint a fire?
Conductors lead an orchestra by showing them when and how to play the PreK/ES
music together. How does a conductor show the musicians what tempo to
play? Imagine you are conducting an orchestra for an opera. How would
you keep the orchestra on tempo? Show how you might guide an orchestra
to change from one tempo to another.
For La bohème, compare a traditional set and costumes from the late ES
1800s with a production set in the present day. Why might a director
choose one type of setting and costume over another?

Lesson Plan Stems
The music for Musetta’s Waltz “Quando me’n vo” in Act II and has become ES

What are some other opera earworms?
Listen to Musetta’s Waltz, one of the most famous songs from opera. Can ES

Puccini also wrote an opera called Il trittico. A “trittico” or “triptych” is a ES/MS
work of art with three parts. Why would artists create art in three parts? Do
triptychs exist in music and visual art? What are some famous examples?
Design and create costumes for La bohème set in our time. How will the ES/MS
different groups of people dress? What do we consider to be a “bohemian”
look in 2025?
In many operas important action occurs offstage. Where does action occur ES/MS
off-stage in La bohème? How does the audience know what happened?
What is a caricature? How are caricatures drawn? What does a caricature ES/MS
artist look for when creating a caricature of a subject? Draw caricatures of
the characters in La bohème, based on their personalities and your
imagination.
The music for “Quando me’n vo” in Act II is also called Musetta’s Waltz. ES/MS
What is a waltz? How does waltz music sound? When was the waltz first
invented, and how did it differ from other dances at that time? What
different kinds of waltz forms are there? Are there any current popular
songs that use a waltz tempo? Learn how to dance a basic waltz.
There are many sound effects that are used in operas, plays, musicals, ES/MS
and movies. How are these effects produced? Are they made in the same
manner for both stage and screen? What sound effects should be included
in La bohème? How would you create these sound effects for this opera?
What are the plot and musical characteristics of verismo, and how does ES/MS
this style apply to La bohème? How are singers for a verismo opera
different from singers for a Mozart opera? How are verismo arias different
from arias by Mozart and arias by Verdi?
At one point, there were two versions of La bohème. There are also two ES/MS
versions of Phantom of the Opera. What are the reasons for more than one
form of a work? Compare the number of times Puccini’s La bohème was
performed at the Met during the run of RENT—a modern take on La
bohème—both on and off Broadway. Was RENT the longest-running show
in Broadway history?
What is meant by motive (or motif) in music? Explore Puccini’s use of MS/HS
motive as well as other composers. Create your own characters; give each
a motive that represents class, ideology, or disposition.

Civics and Government
State Standards: 5.1, 5.2, 5.3, 5.4
If the Christmas Eve party in La bohème had gotten too loud, could PreK
someone at the Café Momus have called the police? How do you call the
police in Pittsburgh? What is the phone number for calling in emergencies?
In the 1830s, when La bohème was set, France was ruled by King Louis- PreK
Philippe I. What is a king? Did the United States have a king in the 1830s?
Who is head of government in the United States? How is that person
chosen to be the head of government?
In Puccini’s lifetime, Italy unified and became the Kingdom of Italy, with PreK/ES
Victor Emannuel II presiding as its first monarch. What other countries
have kings or queens? How is a king different from a president?
As a 17-year-old student at the Milan Conservatory, Puccini was already a PreK/ES
heavy smoker who was so poor that he couldn’t afford tobacco. Unlike the
character in La bohème who sells his coat to buy medicine, though, young
Puccini found money to buy his cigarettes by secretly selling off pipes from
the organ that he was hired to play as the church’s organist. What might
this opera be like if Puccini had been caught stealing these pipes and sent
to jail?

Lesson Plan Stems
During the 1830s, the time period in which La bohème is set, there was ES/MS
extensive political unrest in Paris and throughout France. Does Puccini
include any references to those political times in the opera?
Describe the type of government in power in France in 1830. Was it a MS/HS
monarchy, dictatorship, republic? Chart the different kinds of governments
in France from 1790 to the present.
How has France’s system of government changed from the 1830s to the MS/HS
present? How has membership in the European Union affected the
government of France and other countries that are member-states? Create
a graphic organizer to show this change.
Puccini loved fast automobiles, and was an early adopter of this new MS/HS
technology, long before owning an automobile was commonplace. When
did driver licensing become required to operate a vehicle? Are autos the
only vehicles for which formal operator’s licenses are required? What kinds
of skills did early drivers need to demonstrate to obtain a license? How has
the licensing process changed over time?
In 1830, the year that Puccini set La bohème, the July Revolution MS/HS
succeeded in overthrowing Charles X and the Bourbon monarchy in favor
of Charles’ cousin Louis-Philippe I, who ascended the throne as a
constitutional monarch. What led Parisians to revolt against the Bourbon
monarchy? What is the difference between an absolute monarch and a
constitutional monarch? How did this change reflect larger social and
political shifts of that time and influence French government in the modern
era? Did Puccini include any reference to this governmental change in the
opera?
Even though Puccini has been dead for more than 100 years, his operas MS/HS
are still frequently performed. How do opera companies gain permission to
perform older operas? What does it mean when music rights are expired?
When do literature copyrights expire? How much does it cost to copyright
material? How much does it cost to pay royalties for an opera? How much
does it cost to rent orchestra scores? Why would an opera company rent
instead of purchasing scores? What happens if someone uses copyrighted
music or literature without gaining permission first? How might an artist or
an estate obtain recourse or recompense for “stolen” intellectual property?
La bohème’s story, musical themes, and individual songs have been MS/HS
adapted as pop songs, jazz pieces, a short story, and even a Broadway
musical (RENT). How does an artist obtain permission to create a

La bohème is based on French author Henri Murger’s stories and play MS/HS
Scènes de la vie de bohème. Murger used his own experiences as an
impoverished writer in Paris as the basis for this episodic series of
vignettes set in 1830s Paris. He later converted the vignettes (Scènes de
la vie de bohème) into a successful play that became the inspiration for
Puccini’s La bohème. What is “episodic prose”? What other literary works
use this style? What other important literary movements were taking place
in the mid-late 1800s? How does Puccini's La bohème fit into these new
approaches to narrative?

Character Themes for La bohème
Eleanore N. Childs, a Pittsburgh attorney and child advocate, worked with teachers
for more than a decade to research character education and develop the Heartwood
Ethics Curriculum for Children. Heartwood’s life lessons through literature promote
understanding and practice of seven universal character attributes, which constitute
the foundation of community among all people.
The Heartwood Institute defined seven universal character attributes:
 Courage is not only mastering fear; it is the ability to face difficulty or risk with integrity and
honor.
 Loyalty is being faithful to a person, group, or ideal based on understanding that we are all
part of something greater than ourselves.
 Justice is being guided by a balance of truth, reason, and fairness to all.
 Respect is regard for all life on earth. It calls us to value all people and their contributions,
viewpoints, and differences, such as culture, religion, age, race, and ability.
 Hope guides our future through faith and aspiration, helping us to rise above selfishness and
despair.
 Honesty is being truthful and honorable, the foundation for friendship and community.
 Love is deep care and concern for others, encompassing all the attributes. The more love we
give, the richer we become.
Discuss three attributes—loyalty, hope, and respect—after reading the synopsis of
La bohème.
1. How do Rodolfo, Mimì, Marcello, and Musetta demonstrate their loyalty to and
respect for each other? How does each character demonstrate respect for
himself or herself?
2. What purpose can hope serve during trying times in our lives? Is it difficult to
choose hopeful action in the face of abject poverty?
3. How does Colline demonstrate hope for Mimì when he sells his coat? Do you
think he knows his action won’t have any impact? If so, then, why does he do
it?
4. What are some actions taken by the four friends that show they are part of
something greater than themselves individually?
5. Explore the respect that is evident in the complicated relationship between
Puccini and the two librettists for La bohème.
