February Song Shop Concert
Saturday, February 6, 2027, 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM
Bitz Opera Factory
Pittsburgh Opera’s free, informal Song Shop concerts feature the Resident Artists of
Pittsburgh Opera. Beginning at 12:00 PM on selected Saturdays, these one-hour
performances are free, fun, and open to everyone. Guests are encouraged to bring a friend and bring a lunch. FRIENDS of Pittsburgh Opera provide a reception after each Song Shop concert. Themes vary from concert to concert.
