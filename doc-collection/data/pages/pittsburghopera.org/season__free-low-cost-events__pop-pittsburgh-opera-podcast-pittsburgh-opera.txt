POP – Pittsburgh Opera Podcast
Go behind the scenes with the people that bring Pittsburgh Opera’s productions to life.
The POP – Pittsburgh Opera Podcast – features engaging conversations with Pittsburgh Opera’s singers, stage directors, conductors, and other talented professionals dedicated to putting the highest-quality opera on stage for our audiences.
The POP will drop for every Pittsburgh Opera 2025-26 production. Each episode will be available on our YouTube channel’s POP Playlist.
