Pittsburgh Opera New Guard
Join the Pittsburgh Opera New Guard and experience everything Pittsburgh Opera has to offer! The New Guard is a community for people ages 21-45 who have a passion for Pittsburgh Opera. Don’t forget to add yourself to the New Guard Facebook Group.
Want in on all of our New Guard perks and events? You’ll be invited to exclusive events, get great ticketing and other discounts, and more!
Purchase your membership online today.

Benefits
Membership Benefits
Whether you choose a Standard or Deluxe membership, you'll enjoy exciting perks designed to connect you with the art and community you love:
Complimentary Tickets
Enjoy 2 free tickets with a Standard membership or 4 free tickets with a Deluxe membership to any upcoming Pittsburgh Opera performance of your choice—best available seating, subject to availability.
(Please allow a few days for processing after you select your performance.)
Discounts on Additional Tickets
After using your free tickets, you can save either 20% (with a Standard membership) or 30% (with Deluxe) on additional tickets all season long
Discounted Access to Special Events
Receive exclusive member pricing for Pittsburgh Opera special events throughout the season.
Invitations to Collaborative Events
Invitations and special access to fun and engaging events co-hosted with other Pittsburgh professional groups.
Networking Opportunities
Connect with fellow members, arts supporters, and creative professionals in a welcoming, social setting.
New Guard E-Newsletter
Stay in the know with our New Guard newsletter—your source for upcoming events, insider updates, and more.
