Galaxy Society
Lead. Belong. Sustain.
The Galaxy Society is Pittsburgh Opera’s premier leadership giving society, and the closest seat to the stage. Members enjoy unforgettable experiences and meaningful connections while helping sustain world-class opera, arts education, and the next generation of artists.
Galaxy Society membership begins at $10,000, but New Guard members can join for $2,000. Benefits begin immediately for monthly donors whose annual commitment reaches the Galaxy Society level.
Galaxy Society Benefits ($10,000+)
As a Galaxy Society member, you'll enjoy:
Dedicated ticket concierge service and access to the best available seats
Complimentary VIP parking for Benedum Center performances
Hospitality in the Galaxy Lounge before performances and during intermissions, including complimentary drinks and light bites
Invitation to the annual Galaxy Society Dinner, celebrating our community of leadership supporters
Invitations to exclusive receptions, meet-and-greets, Sitzprobes, Dress Rehearsals, and behind-the-scenes experiences with Pittsburgh Opera artists and leadership*
Opportunity to donate ten tickets each season to the school or nonprofit of your choice
Exclusive conversations and insider updates and events with General Director Bill Powers and Pittsburgh Opera leadership
Opportunities to learn more about philanthropy, leadership giving, and legacy planning
Recognition in Pittsburgh Opera program books
*Subject to availability
Directors' Circle ($25,000–$49,999)
In addition to all Galaxy Society benefits, Directors' Circle members enjoy enhanced stewardship, personalized engagement opportunities, and curated experiences designed around their interests.
Chairman's Circle ($50,000+)
Our highest level of annual leadership giving. Chairman's Circle members receive all Galaxy Society and Directors' Circle benefits, along with bespoke experiences and exclusive opportunities created in recognition of their extraordinary philanthropic partnership.
