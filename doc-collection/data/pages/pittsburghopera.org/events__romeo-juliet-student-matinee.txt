Student Matinee for Grades 3–12: Romeo & Juliet
Tuesday, April 20, 2027, 10:15 AM at the Benedum Center
Sung in French, with English supertitles projected above the stage
The powerful families of Verona—the Montagues and the Capulets—have been feuding for centuries. So when Romeo (a Montague) and Juliet (a Capulet) meet and become hopelessly infatuated with each other, their young love starts down an inevitable path towards tragedy.
Students will enjoy a complete mainstage performance of Verdi’s comedy with full orchestra, top-quality singers, and colorful production elements. Falstaff provides rich learning opportunities about music, history, and Shakespeare.
Attend the prerequisite workshop to receive extensive education materials, including a Study Guide, Lesson Ideas, PowerPoints, and an Arts Discovery Packet.
