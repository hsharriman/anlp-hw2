Community Concerts
Community concerts are another great opportunity for an informal introduction to opera, and Pittsburgh Opera takes every opportunity to sing for the community.
Past free concerts have included:
Allegheny County Summer Concert series
Market Square Farmers Market Lunchtime Concerts
Children’s Hospital Holiday Concerts
Retirement Community Concerts
Watch our website calendar and social media for information about upcoming concerts.
