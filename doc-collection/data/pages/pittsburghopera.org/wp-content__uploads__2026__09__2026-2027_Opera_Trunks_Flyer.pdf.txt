Opera Trunks
Suggested for Grades PreK–12
Opera Trunks are large, colorful steamer trunks on wheels
(25” square by 44” high) filled with books, costumes, music,
and lesson activities—all the materials you need to introduce opera
in your classroom, camp, or library. Trunk package includes
orientation, transportation, one month trunk residency, and
a presentation by teaching artists at your location.
If students can’t come to the opera, we take the opera to them!
Trunk, Transportation, Orientation,
and On-Site Presentation included in $500 Fee
The On-Site Presentation engages students in creating an “Opera Improv” on the spot.
Additional back-to-back presentation is $300.
• Madama Butterfly Trunk features the Japanese culture and Puccini’s music.
• Opera Improv Trunk features well-loved operas for students to explore.
• Carmen Trunk features the Roma culture and Bizet’s music.
• Bravi Tutti Trunk features behind-the-scenes and opera production elements.
• Tot Trunk features child-friendly operas and activities for PreK–Grade 3 students.
For more information, email megan@pittsburghopera.org
