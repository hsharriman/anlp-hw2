Production Sponsorship
Bring the magic of opera to life — become a Production Sponsor with Pittsburgh Opera. Sponsorship offers a unique opportunity to support extraordinary performances while enjoying exclusive benefits and behind-the-scenes experiences tailored to you!
Sponsorship Levels:
Lead Sponsor ($50,000)
Co-Sponsor ($25,000)
Associate ($10,000)
Sponsor a Production:
Rigoletto
Orpheus and Eurydice
Partenope
Working for the Macbeths
Romeo and Juliet
As a sponsor, you’ll receive premium recognition in our program books, on-site signage, and on our website. You’ll also enjoy prime seating for Opening Night and other performances, exclusive access to special rehearsals and backstage tours, and personal acknowledgments during performances.
Plus, we offer personalized experiences tailored to your interests—such as a private dinner with a designer, a backstage view during dress rehearsal, a tour of the costume shop, or even the rare opportunity to appear onstage as a supernumerary!*
For more information or to create your custom sponsorship package, please contact:
