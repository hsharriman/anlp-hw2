NOTES: Suggested grade levels are listed to the right of each lesson plan stem.
PreK = Pre-Kindergarten, ES = elementary school, MS = middle school, HS = high school
Lesson Plan Stems in the English Language Arts and Mathematics
sections are Common Core Friendly, and are marked: CCF
Arts and Humanities
State Standards: 9.1, 9.2, 9.3, 9.4
Cio-Cio-San hums a lullaby to Sorrow. What is a lullaby? When and why is PreK
a lullaby usually sung? Do you know any lullabies? Sing a lullaby to your
classmates.
Color a picture of a kimono for Cio-Cio-San. What colors will you use? PreK
What do butterflies look like and how do they move? Make a butterfly PreK
costume. What materials and colors will you use?
Music, emotion, and movement are interconnected. Listen to the PreK/ES
“Humming Chorus” from Madama Butterfly. How does the music make you
feel? Practice humming a song that you know. What is the difference
between humming, singing, and talking? Cover some flashlights with
different colors of gels or cellophane. Listen to the “Humming Chorus”
again, and project your flashlights toward the ceiling to show how you feel
the music moves. What do you see? How would you describe the
movement of your lights?
Look at some photos of special instruments used in Madama Butterfly: PreK/ES
Japanese gong, Japanese bells, and bird whistles. Can you match the
sounds of these instruments to their photos?
What is the Japanese art form known as origami? Practice folding paper ES
into easy shapes and animals. Read a book about a young Japanese girl
who aimed to create a thousand origami cranes: Sadako and the
Thousand Paper Cranes. How does this story make you feel?

Lesson Plan Stems
Cio-Cio-San hums a lullaby to Sorrow. What is a lullaby? What is the ES/MS

Research Japanese pantomime. Create a dance based on this art form. ES/MS
Explore a variety of Japanese music styles. What are some traditional ES/MS

What is a pentatonic scale? Do you think music created with a five-note ES/MS
scale sounds Japanese? Listen for and identify some instances when you

Geisha such as Cio-Cio-San were often practitioners of many different fine ES/MS
arts, sometimes including calligraphy, the art of beautiful handwriting. What

lettering? Practice writing with a pencil, pen, and ink brush. What color ink
can be used for calligraphy?
In Japan, colors often have symbolic meaning. For example, white is used ES/MS
for purity, but it also symbolizes death. Sometimes brides wear white, but
what is the traditional color for a Japanese bride? Red means peace and
prosperity and is often used for festival decorations, but red is also used to
ward off evil spirits. Look at a Japanese flag. What colors are there? Why
might those colors have been chosen for a nation’s flag? What do colors
symbolize in the United States? What do the colors of our flag represent?
Giacomo Puccini came from a long line of family musicians. What other ES/MS
composers had musical ancestry? Name some composers who didn’t have
musical ancestry. Can you draw any conclusions based on your research?
Opera America, the national service organization for opera, lists on its ES/MS
website the top 10 most performed operas of each season. What other

in 2024–2025? Why?

Character Themes for Madama Butterfly
Eleanore N. Childs, a Pittsburgh attorney and child advocate, worked with teachers for
more than a decade to research character education and develop the Heartwood
Ethics Curriculum for Children. Heartwood’s life lessons through literature promote
understanding and practice of seven universal character attributes, which constitute the
foundation of community among all people.
The Heartwood Institute defined seven universal character attributes:
 Courage is not only mastering fear; it is the ability to face difficulty or risk with integrity and
honor.
 Loyalty is being faithful to a person, group, or ideal based on understanding that we are all part
of something greater than ourselves.
 Justice is being guided by a balance of truth, reason, and fairness to all.
 Respect is regard for all life on earth. It calls us to value all people and their contributions,
viewpoints, and differences, such as culture, religion, age, race, and ability.
 Hope guides our future through faith and aspiration, helping us to rise above selfishness and
despair.
 Honesty is being truthful and honorable, the foundation for friendship and community.
 Love is deep care and concern for others, encompassing all the attributes. The more love we
give, the richer we become.
Discuss two attributes—LOYALTY and LOVE—after reading the synopsis of Madama
Butterfly.
1. What problems do both Cio-Cio-San and Suzuki face in the story? What do they do
to solve their problems? How do they show loyalty to each other? How can you
demonstrate loyalty to a friend?
2. Which characters in Madama Butterfly discuss loyalty? Was Cio-Cio-San loyal to her
family and her religion? Was Pinkerton loyal to Butterfly? Brainstorm characters in the
opera who truly demonstrate loyalty.
3. Who expresses love in Madama Butterfly? Does any character express deep care
and concern for another character? How is that love expressed? How do you show love
in your family?
4. Would the ending of Madama Butterfly have turned out differently if characters had
shown loyalty and love? Would the opera have been as strong?
