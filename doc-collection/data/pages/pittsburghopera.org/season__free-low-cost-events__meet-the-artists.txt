Meet The Artists
Ticketholders for each Friday opera performance are invited to join General Director Bill Powers immediately following the performance to enjoy lively repartee, as he interviews the stars of the production.
This event is free to all opera ticketholders, and is held in the Lower Lobby for Benedum performances, the Fulton Mini Room for Byham performances, the Black Box for Pittsburgh CAPA Theater performances, and in the performance space at the Bitz Opera Factory.
