Room Rates and Capacities
Pittsburgh Opera has two primary rental spaces, which can be rented separately or together. We do not rent them separately to two different parties at the same time.
The larger of the spaces is the George R. White Opera Studio (the “GRW”). The smaller is the Founders’ Room. You can learn more about each of them below.

Rental rates: George R. White Opera Studio

The George R. White Opera Studio
Rates include the Opera Studio and Founders' Room
Uses: Presentations, productions, wedding ceremonies and wedding receptions, other receptions, or dinner parties
Capacity: 400 for a cocktail reception; 350 in theater-style seating; 200 for a dinner reception.
Square Feet: 5,000 (approx. 116' x 48')
Rental Rates: ​​​​
2026 and 2027: $1,450 for 4 hours; $4,750 daily (12 hours) rate
Available for additional costs: stage, piano
All rentals will be charged a $450 staffing fee and a $150 cleaning fee.
