Fiddlesticks Musical Exploration
Introduce your child to a lifelong love of music.
Since 1991, generations of Pittsburgh families have trusted Fiddlesticks to introduce kids ages 3-8 to music through these fun and imaginative musical adventures.
These Saturday concerts in Heinz Hall are filled with musical fun in a relaxed, welcoming, and interactive environment and are recommended for families with children ages three to eight. Fiddlesticks, your musical ambassador, and guest vocalist Corrie Stallings will guide you through these musical adventures! Pre-concert activities begin at 10:00 A.M.
Fiddlesticks concerts promote curiosity, togetherness, and music-making in a fun environment.
What kind of ambassador is seven feet tall, covered in fur, and surprisingly cuddly? It’s Fiddlesticks — a musical cat who, for over 35 amazing years, has been introducing children to the magic of music and the orchestra. Fiddlesticks loves meeting his fans after each performance in the Grand Lobby of Heinz Hall. He especially enjoys hearing about how fun the concerts are, with their audience participation elements, the “just-right” length (only about 45 minutes), and the warm, welcoming atmosphere created by everyone involved! And of course, he loves hearing how much they enjoyed the music!

26/27 Fiddlesticks Concerts
Once Upon a Melody
Nov 14, 2026 · Sat 11:15 AM
Fiddlesticks loves a good fairytale and has a big question. Can music tell a story all by itself? With help from his orchestra friends, he invites young listeners to help build a brand new fairy tale through sound. Hear how music brings to life heroes, villains, magical moments and, of course, happily ever after in this imagination-sparking concert.

Playful Pianos
Feb 27, 2027 · Sat 11:15 AM
88 keys, endless possibilities. Fiddlesticks invites young listeners on a playful musical journey to discover just how many sounds a piano can make. From soft and shimmering to bold and booming, this lively concert brings the instrument to life in unexpected ways. It’s a high-energy concert made for moving, laughing, and having fun together.

Fidd-tastic Friends
Apr 10, 2027 · Sat 11:15 AM
Fiddlesticks is hosting a musical playdate with all his favorite animal friends. Join the fun in a concert that celebrates friendship, learning to listen, and discovering what makes everyone special. From telling jokes and exploring new places to bouncing, playing, and making music together, this warm and joyful performance shows what it means to be a good friend and that everyone belongs in the orchestra.

Your Concert Day
Let’s explore music together!
10:00 a.m.
Activities
Our musical exploration begins with activities all around Heinz Hall. Come anytime between 10:00 and 11:00 a.m. and choose what you want to do.
11:15 a.m.
Concert
Meet Fiddlesticks inside the auditorium at 11:15 a.m. for an orchestra concert with the Pittsburgh Symphony and his friend, Corrie Stallings.
12:00 p.m.
Meet and Greet
Join Fiddlesticks, Corrie, and our other artist friends in the lobby after the concert for pictures, autographs, and more!
Pre-Concert Activities
Come to Heinz Hall starting at 10:00 a.m. for fun, hands-on activities with special guests! Move at your own pace as you:
Discover instruments of the orchestra with PSO musicians
Get creative with a music-inspired craft to take home
Express yourself with dance and movement
Take a break in the Quiet Room with books, fidgets, and more
Practice singing the Fiddlesticks theme song with vocalist Corrie Stallings
Fiddlesticks wants to make sure everyone feels welcome at these concerts!
