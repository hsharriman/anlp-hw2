Your Impact
The Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra enriches lives throughout Western Pennsylvania and beyond, reaching over 250,000 people annually through performances, education programs, and community partnerships. From our world-class concerts at Heinz Hall to free neighborhood performances and youth education initiatives, we believe music has the power to inspire, unite, and transform.
