Welcome Military!
The Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra is incredibly grateful for your service. In thanks, military members can take advantage of discounted concert tickets throughout the season. Browse below to see your discounted ticket offers. Remember to check back frequently as new concerts are added throughout the season. We can’t wait to host you at Heinz Hall!
