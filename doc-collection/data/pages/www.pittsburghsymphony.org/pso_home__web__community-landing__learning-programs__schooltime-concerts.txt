Schooltime @ Heinz Hall
Led by Associate Conductor Jacob Joyce  Meet Jacob
For generations, teachers have brought their students to Heinz Hall to experience live orchestral music. This season, Schooltime unites music with reading, inviting classrooms to discover how orchestral music brings some of literature's best-loved stories to life. Each unit features a PSO concert and curriculum-aligned classroom resources created by our Learning & Engagement team.

2026–27 Schooltime Concerts
Once Upon a Book
Grades K–2 November 18 & 19, 2026 | 10:30 a.m. Grace Lin, narrator
How can music help tell a story? In Grace Lin and Kate Messner's beautiful book Once Upon a Book, a young girl escapes the winter doldrums by immersing herself in stories set in faraway places. Award-winning author and illustrator Grace Lin joins the PSO on stage to read Once Upon a Book, with illustrations projected above the stage and full orchestral accompaniment. Magical adventures come to life through the combined power of music and storytelling in this vibrant performance.
Once Upon a Book by Grace Lin and Kate Messner, copyright © 2023. Reprinted by permission of Little, Brown and Company, an imprint of Hachette Book Group, Inc.

Romeo and Juliet
Grades 7–12 January 20 & 21, 2027 | 10:30 a.m.
How do artistic choices influence our understanding of a story? William Shakespeare and Sergei Prokofiev are just two of the many artists who have taken on the telling of Romeo and Juliet. Take a deep dive into one of literature's most influential stories as we explore Shakespeare's and Prokofiev's creative decisions. Music and spoken word combine in this exciting look at a classic tale.
Program Note: The story of Romeo and Juliet includes violent conflict. The concert will include discussion of how Prokofiev depicts these conflicts musically, and his artistic choices on how to handle the deaths of Romeo and Juliet.
Romeo and Juliet, Op. 64. Music by Sergei Prokofiev, Libretto by Sergei Radlov, Adrian Piotrovsky, Leonid Lavrovsky and Sergei Prokofiev. Presented under license from G. Schirmer, Inc., copyright owners.

Myths & Legends
Grades 4–6 March 9, 10 & 12, 2027 | 10:30 a.m.
Why have myths endured? Across time, people around the globe have used storytelling to make sense of the world around them. In this dynamic performance, the PSO explores myths and legends through a wide range of orchestral music. From origin stories to cautionary tales, join this musical globetrot to better understand the role myths play in teaching us about ourselves and our place in the world.

Meet the Orchestra
Grades 2–4 April 29 & 30, May 4, 5, 6 & 7, 2027 | 10:30 a.m. May 6 is reserved for PPS schools only.
How do instruments make sound? Explore how sound is created as you build the Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra! Meet the instrument families of the orchestra, learn how each instrument produces sound, hear how those sounds work together to make music, and discover what role the conductor plays in bringing orchestral music to life.

Important Schooltime Policies
Weapons are not permitted in Heinz Hall or public spaces surrounding the building.
All bags will be searched upon entry.
All adults must wear a PSO-issued nametag or school-issued ID to enter Heinz Hall. This includes teachers, staff, bus drivers, parents, and all other chaperones. PSO nametags will be sent in your registration packet one month before the concert.
Outside food and beverages, including water bottles, are not permitted. Exceptions will be made for medical reasons.
Photography without flash is permitted during the concert. Recordings of any kind are strictly prohibited.
Groups are not permitted to take photos while entering or exiting Heinz Hall.
Schooltime concerts are for children ages 5 and up.
