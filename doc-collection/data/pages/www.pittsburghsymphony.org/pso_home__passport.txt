How it works
It’s as easy as 1-2-3
1
Visit the PSO Passport page and pick your concert
Browse over 80 eligible concerts throughout the season. Whether you want to plan weeks in advance or grab a last-minute seat on the day of the show, the choice is yours.
2
Claim your $10 ticket
After you choose the concert and date you want (and add discounted parking if you desire), you'll be prompted to log in (if you're not already).
PSO Passport pricing is applied automatically during the last part of the checkout process, so don't worry if you see a different price initially in your cart.
3
Enjoy and repeat
Go to as many performances as you want — with one $10 ticket per concert. There's no cap on how many different concerts you can experience. If your plans change down the road, you can cancel your membership at any time (after the initial 3-month minimum). Future tickets reserved through your membership will be cancelled when membership ends.

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What does "unlimited concerts" actually mean?
As an active member, you can attend as many eligible Pittsburgh Symphony performances as you'd like — with one $10 ticket per concert. Your $10 ticket price applies across our BNY Classics, PNC Pops, Movies, and select special concerts.
How many tickets can I buy with my membership?
Each PSO Passport membership allows you to reserve one $10 ticket per concert. If you usually attend with a partner or friend, we recommend signing up for two memberships so you can secure seats together!
Can I really cancel anytime?
Yes! We want this program to fit your lifestyle. After an initial 3-month period, there's no long-term contract or cancellation penalty. If you try it and decide it's not for you, you can stop your monthly renewal whenever you choose. Please note: if you cancel your membership, any future tickets you have reserved will also be cancelled.
How does the pass pay for itself in just 4 visits?
Single tickets to the symphony can add up quickly. Because your membership unlocks premium seating options for a flat $10 rate, the amount you save on just four individual concerts easily covers the cost of an entire year of your monthly membership fee!
