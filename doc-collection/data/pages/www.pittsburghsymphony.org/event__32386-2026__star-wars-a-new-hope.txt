A Galaxy of Sound, Live
For 50 years, Star Wars has brought generations of fans together. On May 25 at 7pm, celebrate the saga that started it all as the Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra presents Star Wars: A New Hope in Concert at PPG Paints Arena.
Experience the legendary film like never before—on the massive screen as the Pittsburgh Symphony performs John Williams’ iconic, Oscar-winning score live. From the opening fanfare to the final battle, every unforgettable moment of A New Hope comes to life with the power of a full symphony orchestra.
The orchestra will be positioned beneath the main scoreboard, facing the Michelob ULTRA Club. The performance will be professionally amplified and broadcast throughout the arena via the venue's PA system, ensuring clear audio for all attendees. Additionally, because the scoreboard features a 360-degree display, the accompanying video content will be visible from every seat in the arena.
A 50th Anniversary Celebration
This is more than a movie. It's the Pittsburgh Star Wars 50th anniversary celebration.
Arrive early for themed activities throughout the arena and join thousands of fellow fans celebrating a galaxy far, far away. Break out your Star Wars gear, come as your favorite character, or simply bring your love of the saga. Then take your seat, watch those famous words appear on the screen and hear John Williams’ legendary score fill the arena.
One film. One orchestra. Five decades of Star Wars.
Celebrate it all on May 25, 7pm, at PPG Paints Arena.
