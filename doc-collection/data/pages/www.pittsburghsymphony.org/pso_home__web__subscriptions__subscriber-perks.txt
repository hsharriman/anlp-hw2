♦ Subscriber Perks
You’re not just a ticketholder. You’re a part of something Bold. Passionate. Unapologetically Pittsburgh.
Subscriber Benefits
Free, Flexible Ticket Exchanges
Subscribers can exchange their tickets for free across BNY Classics, PNC Pops, Movies with the PSO, and select specials. A $5 mailing fee applies to mailed tickets.
Discounts on Additional Tickets
Save 15% on additional tickets to BNY Classics, PNC Pops, Highmark Holiday Pops, and Fiddlesticks Family Concerts. Save 10% on most special performances.
Discounted Prepaid Parking
Purchase parking at an exclusive subscriber rate of just $18 at the Sixth Avenue & Penn Garage (540 Penn Ave, Pittsburgh, PA 15222). Add prepaid parking to your ticket order by calling 412-392-4900, or see directions, parking, and prepaid instructions.
Pre-Sale Access
Receive pre-sale access to special events and concerts. (Make sure your email is up to date!)
Discounts on Dining
Use your Subscriber Perks Card to receive discounts when you dine out with our partners before the concert.
The Best Seats and the Best Prices
Subscribers already enjoy the best savings over single ticket prices, before they go on sale to the public.
Questions about your subscription? Contact Patron Services at 412-392-4900 or visit the Heinz Hall Box Office.
Dining Partners
Subscribers to the Pittsburgh Symphony enjoy great benefits in addition to their incredible concert seats, including discounts and special offers at downtown Pittsburgh Dining Partner venues!
The PSO is proud to partner with downtown restaurants to offer exclusive discounts for our subscribers year-round. PSO subscribers enjoy discounts and special offers from our downtown Dining Partners by presenting their Perks Card (which are mailed to subscribers with their season tickets) at the establishments listed below.
Offers are valid every day of the week (unless otherwise noted in offer) from September 1, 2025–August 31, 2026.
