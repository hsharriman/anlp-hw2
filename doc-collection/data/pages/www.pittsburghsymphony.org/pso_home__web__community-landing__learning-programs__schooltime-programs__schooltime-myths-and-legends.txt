Learning Objectives
Learning Objective #1: Students will be able to (SWBAT) describe different types of myths and the role they play in society.
Learning Objective #2: SWBAT demonstrate an understanding that myths are created all over the world by all peoples.
Learning Objective #3: SWBAT demonstrate an understanding of how myths are relevant in the present day.
Learning Objective #4: SWBAT explain how different musical elements are used to show key details of a myth.
