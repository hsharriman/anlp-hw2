Learning Objectives
Learning Objective #1: Students will be able to (SWBAT) identify the elements of a story.
Learning Objective #2: SWBAT understand how music can be used to interpret elements of a story.
Learning Objective #3: SWBAT develop skills to increase attention, focus, and the ability to notice detail.
Learning Objective #4: SWBAT understand that reading and music help us imagine.
Classroom Resources for Once Upon a Book align with National Core Arts Standards MU:Re8.1.Ka, MU:Pr4.3.2a and PA Academic Standards CC.1.2.1. B, CC.1.3.K.C, CC.1.3.1.C, CC.1.3.1. F, CC.1.5.1. B, CC.1.5.1. D, 9.1.3 E, 9.4.3 D
