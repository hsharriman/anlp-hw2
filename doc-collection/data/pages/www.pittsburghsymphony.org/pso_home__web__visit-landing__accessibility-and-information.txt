Accessibility
The Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra and Heinz Hall are committed to providing an environment that is inclusive and welcoming to all patrons.
Sensory friendly performances are musical experiences customized especially for patrons with autism spectrum disorders or other disabilities that create sensory sensitivities. Designed in consultation with leading occupational therapy experts, sensory friendly performances provide an opportunity for patrons of all ages to engage in multisensory pre-concert activities and enjoy a symphony concert together with family and friends in a relaxed, inclusive environment. Sensory-friendly performances are scheduled occasionally. Click here to learn more about our sensory friendly initiatives.
For information about our accessible services, contact the box office at 412-392-4900. Doormen and ushers are also available for assistance at every performance.

Special Services
Concierge Service
Patrons attending Pittsburgh Symphony concerts and all other Heinz Hall events can take advantage of our Concierge Service. A Concierge will be on duty in the Entrance Lobby of Heinz Hall at all performances. The Concierge is happy to assist with information about Heinz Hall, the Cultural District and area attractions, while assisting with dining, hotel, entertainment and transportation concerns.
Our concierge is well-versed in the many opportunities available to patrons either visiting or residing in the Pittsburgh area. Concierge Service begins one hour prior to the concert and is available until after the performance. Call 412-906-1166 during any performance to speak to a concierge.
Lost and Found
Call 412-392-0340.
Physician’s Register Book
Located in the entrance lobby at the Concierge Desk. Refer emergency calls to 412-906-1166 during performances only. Please turn off beepers and cellular phones during the performance and notify your contact of the emergency number.
Latecomers Gallery
Latecomers will still enjoy the entire concert in the Heinz Hall Latecomers Gallery. There will be no seating during the performance of any piece. Patrons who must leave during a performance will not be re-seated until the completion of the piece. Also, as a courtesy to the performers and fellow concert-goers, we ask that you remain in your seat until the concert has concluded.
Guided Backstage Tours
Guided Backstage Tours are available to groups of eight or more by advance appointment for only $2 per adult; $1 per child, age 14 and younger. To arrange a tour, please contact the Heinz Hall Management Office at 412-392-4850.
Smoking Policy
Smoking is prohibited in Heinz Hall.
