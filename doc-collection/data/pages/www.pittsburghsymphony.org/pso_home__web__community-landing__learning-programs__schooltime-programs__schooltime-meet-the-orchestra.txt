Learning Objectives
Learning Objective #1: Students will be able to (SWBAT) understand how sound is created.
Learning Objective #2: SWBAT identify instrument families based on how they make sound.
Learning Objective #3: SWBAT describe the relationship between pitch and the size of an instrument.
Learning Objective #4: SWBAT understand how the conductor contributes to the quality of the sounds that instruments make.
Learning Objective #5: SWBAT articulate an opinion about a musical performance and provide supporting evidence.
Classroom resources for Meet the Orchestra align with National Core Arts standards MU:Cr3.1.2a, MU:Cr2.1.2a, MU:Pr4.2.4c, MU:Pr4.3.2a, MU:Pr5.1.3b, MU:Re9.1.2a and PA Academic Standards for Arts and Humanities 9.1.3.C, 9.1.5.E, 9.2.5.L, 9.1.5.A, 9.2.3.G, 9.2.3.D, 9.1.3.G, 9.2.3.A, 9.1.3.H
