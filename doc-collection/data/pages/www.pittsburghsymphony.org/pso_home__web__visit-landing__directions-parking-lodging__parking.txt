Directions, Parking & Lodging
Parking
Heinz Hall is located within walking distance of more than 6,500 parking spaces in the Cultural District! Season ticket holders have the first opportunity to purchase pre-paid guaranteed parking in the Sixth Avenue and Penn garage, with its entrance at 540 Penn Avenue, for just $20 per concert. Add parking to your ticket order by calling the PSO Box Office at 412-392-4900.
Parking can also be added as an option directly in your cart when you purchase tickets online.
Sixth Avenue & Penn Garage 540 Penn Ave
Pittsburgh, PA 15222
26/27 Parking Vouchers
For the 26/27 season, prepaid parking vouchers look like concert tickets instead of the QR code vouchers from previous years. Discard any parking receipt that states "THIS IS NOT A PARKING VOUCHER". Valid vouchers say "THIS IS YOUR PARKING VOUCHER":
How to use your voucher
These vouchers are date-specific. Parking vouchers will only work for the date printed on them. Be sure to bring the correct voucher to the correct concert.
When you enter the parking garage, do not pull a ticket from the kiosk. Instead, use the voucher you received in the mail to scan both in and out of the garage.
If you chose e-ticket delivery, the electronic parking voucher will be used to scan both in and out of the garage.
For additional questions, please contact your Patron Services Representative.

Directions
The Port Authority website includes a lot of helpful information on using public transit to get around Pittsburgh, including rider alerts, fees, schedules and maps, park and ride lots, a trip planner and more.
Heinz Hall is an easy walk from both the Gateway Center and Wood Street stations of the T light rail system. Several bus lines also stop within easy walking distance of Heinz Hall.
