Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra Announces
Jason Yoder as Principal Percussion
and Maria Semes as Section Viola
PITTSBURGH – Today, the Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra (PSO) announced the appointments
of Jason Yoder as Principal Percussion, beginning in September 2026, and Maria Semes to
the viola section, who joined the orchestra in June 2026.
A native of Squirrel Hill and graduate of Pittsburgh CAPA 6-12 and Carnegie Mellon
University, Yoder returns home from Seoul, South Korea, where he has served as Principal
Percussion and Assistant Timpani of the KBS Symphony Orchestra since 2023. He fills the chair
previously held by Andrew Reamer, who retired in September 2024. Yoder will hold the Albert
H. Eckert Chair.
Equally at home in percussion and timpani, Yoder has served as acting Principal Timpani of the
KBS Symphony Orchestra and Principal Timpani of the Daejeon Philharmonic Orchestra, and
has held percussion positions with the Fort Wayne Philharmonic, Illinois Symphony Orchestra,
and Civic Orchestra of Chicago. As a guest, he has performed with The Philadelphia Orchestra,
Seoul Philharmonic Orchestra, Fort Worth Symphony Orchestra, Virginia Symphony Orchestra,
and Deutsche Radio Philharmonie Saarbrücken Kaiserslautern. He has appeared as a soloist with
the Daejeon Philharmonic Orchestra and the National Repertory Orchestra, performed a duet
with Yo-Yo Ma at the G-20 summit in Pittsburgh, and performed with Alisa Weilerstein for

Classical Music Appreciation Day at the White House. Yoder also holds degrees from Temple
University and DePaul University, and was awarded the Charles Owen Fellowship at the Aspen
Music Festival and School.
Semes comes to the PSO from the Baltimore Symphony Orchestra, and previously performed
with the Louisville Orchestra. Her first season with the orchestra began on the road: she
performed on the PSO’s 2026 European Tour, which included the orchestra’s appearance at the
Salzburg Festival. She holds Bachelor and Master of Music degrees from The Juilliard School,
where she studied with Naoko Tanaka and David Chan, and performs each summer at the Grand
Teton Music Festival. She has also participated in the Tanglewood Music Center and the Aspen
Music Festival and School. In coming to the PSO she joins her husband, Associate Principal
Trumpet Conrad Jones, and her sister, Carolyn Semes, a member of the second violin section.
This weekend's BNY Classics concerts mark the final concerts for Anne Martindale Williams,
who joined the Symphony in 1976 and became Principal Cello in 1979. Joen Vasquez, Assistant
Principal Viola has retired effective with the start of the season and will be honored on stage this
weekend as well. It was also announced that Philip Pandolfi, bassoon, will retire from the PSO
on March 1, 2027. Auditions for the viola and bassoon positions are now open for applications,
and Principal Cello auditions will be announced in late fall.
