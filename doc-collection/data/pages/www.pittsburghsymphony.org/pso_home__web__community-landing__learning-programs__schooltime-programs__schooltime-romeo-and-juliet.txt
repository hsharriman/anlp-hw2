Learning Objectives
Learning Objective #1: Students will be able to (SWBAT) understand how artistic choices are made.
Learning Objective #2: SWBAT identify factors that influence artistic choice.
Learning Objective #3: SWBAT demonstrate knowledge of Prokofiev and the historical time and place in which he lived.
