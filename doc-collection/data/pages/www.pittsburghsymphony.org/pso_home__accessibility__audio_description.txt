Pittsburgh's Cultural District is committed to making our performances and facilities available to all of our patrons. If you should have further questions, please call the box office. Audio Description is a service designed for use by the blind and visually impaired. A trained describer details the action, set, props, and costumes seen on stage for the benefit of the audience members. In other words, the describer interprets the visual elements that may be important to someone who cannot see them.
Audio Description is available at select shows:
Select Pittsburgh Ballet Theatre performances
One performance of each PNC Broadway Across America Broadway show
Pittsburgh Opera, Tuesday night performances at the Benedum Center and All Performances at CAPA Black Box Theater
Other District Shows may be available upon request
