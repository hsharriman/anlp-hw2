Sidney Crosby's Little Penguins Learn to Play Hockey
About
Sidney Crosby's Little Penguins Learn to Play Hockey is an introductory program for children ages 5 to 9 to learn the fundamental skills of hockey in a fun and safe environment. The program consists of eight to ten sessions of on-ice instruction by certified coaches. First-time Little Penguins participants receive head-to-toe hockey equipment, along with a monthly newsletter with exclusive content, discounts on Penguins tickets, exclusive program rewards at Penguins games, special event opportunities, as well as a welcome packet and gift. The Little Penguins program is presented by the NHL and NHLPA with the support of Crosby, DICK'S Sporting Goods and USA Hockey. The Learn to Play program began in Pittsburgh in 2008 and has since become a league-wide initiative for the NHL.

Which Little Penguins Program Is Right for My Child?
New to Little Penguins?
Little Penguins Learn to Play is designed for first-time participants ages 5–9 and includes head-to-toe hockey equipment and 8–10 on-ice sessions.
Already completed Little Penguins?
Little Penguins Two is designed for Little Penguins graduates ages 5–9 who are continuing into the next available session. Participants receive an additional 8–10 on-ice sessions focused on continued skill development. Equipment is not included, and full hockey equipment is required.
Registration Information
The registration information below applies to first-time Little Penguins Learn to Play participants. Little Penguins Two participants will register through a separate registration link and will not select equipment sizes or receive equipment.

Little Penguins Eligibility
Please note the following program eligibility requirements:
Participants must be between the ages of 5 and 9 years old.
Little Penguins Learn to Play is offered to new, first-time participants only. Participants must not have been previously registered with USA Hockey or participated in an ice hockey league/program.
It is strongly recommended that participants complete a Learn to Skate class through their local ice rink prior to enrolling in Little Penguins.
Please note some host rinks have additional requirements, which are outlined on their registration page.
Little Penguins Two is available exclusively to children ages 5–9 who have completed the Little Penguins Learn to Play program and are continuing into the next available session. *If the host facility becomes aware that a registered child has previous hockey league/program experience, the child will be removed from the Little Penguins program with no refund.

Registration
remove   add  I registered my child for Little Penguins or Little Penguins Two, but my child is no longer able to participate. Can I cancel the registration?
We understand that things change and ask that you contact us as soon as possible with any refund requests. Please understand that Little Penguins and Little Penguins Two registrations cannot be refunded for participants who:
do not meet the program eligibility requirements;
register for multiple locations or sessions; or
cancel after registration preparations have begun, including, for Little Penguins, once the participant's equipment has been kitted and/or picked up.
This policy is in place because once registration fees are collected, preparations for an individual's participation begin quickly by the Penguins and host sites, including planning ice time, coaches and other fixed costs. These expenses cannot be recouped if a registrant cancels.
We care about your customer experience and will work to accommodate each request individually. Please email [email protected] with any cancellation questions.
remove   add  I want to register my child at our local rink, but the Little Penguins sessions are full. What do I do?
We have waitlists for all Little Penguins programs. If you are interested in a session that is full, please register for the waitlist. We will do our best to contact participants on the waitlist to move them onto a roster should a spot become available.
remove   add  I have two children. Will they be in the same session?
If you successfully registered your children for the same session, then yes. All participants will be on the ice at the same time. Coaches may split the class into groups for drills throughout the program.
remove   add  What is USA Hockey and why is my child registered with them?
USA Hockey is the national governing body of ice hockey in the United States (https://www.usahockey.com/). USA Hockey provides a number of resources for the Learn to Play program, including age-appropriate training modules and practice plans, coaching certification, background screening and insurance for Learn to Play participants.
Each first-time Little Penguins participant receives a USA Hockey player membership for the hockey season in which they complete the program. This membership remains valid for the entire hockey season and does not need to be renewed when registering for Little Penguins Two.
Little Penguins Two participants may be asked to provide their child's existing USA Hockey membership information during registration. A new USA Hockey membership is not provided with Little Penguins Two registration.
On-Ice Sessions
remove   add  How early do we need to arrive for the first on-ice session?
It is strongly recommended that participants arrive 30-60 minutes early to the first on-ice session. Please arrive in enough time to ensure that your child is able to get checked in, dressed, and on the ice on time. Please be aware that it can take longer than expected to get your child dressed, especially before the first few sessions.
remove   add  My child cannot make one of the classes. What should we do?
Please make sure to let your Coach know that you will not be attending a certain class.
Equipment
remove   add  What equipment is provided or required for the Little Penguins program?
First-time Little Penguins participants receive head-to-toe equipment, including a helmet (with a cage), neck guard, shoulder pads, elbow pads, gloves, hockey pants, shin guards, skates, jersey, hockey socks, hockey stick and equipment bag.
Little Penguins Two participants do not receive equipment with registration and must have full hockey equipment to participate.
We strongly encourage participants to purchase their own athletic supporter (jock/jill), hockey stick tape and a mouth guard. All required equipment must be worn during on-ice sessions. First-time Little Penguins participants keep the equipment they receive upon completing the program.
Next Steps/Continue to Play
remove   add  What is Little Penguins Two?
Little Penguins Two is an opportunity for children ages 5–9 who have just completed the Little Penguins Learn to Play program to continue developing their hockey skills through an additional 8–10 on-ice sessions focused on skating, stickhandling, passing and shooting. Equipment is not included with Little Penguins Two registration, and participants must have full hockey equipment to participate. Little Penguins Two is intended to be the next step following completion of the Little Penguins Learn to Play program. Program pricing and registration details may vary by host location.
remove   add  What is the next step after the Little Penguins Learn to Play program?
The Pittsburgh Penguins encourage all Little Penguins participants to continue playing hockey! Depending on availability, Little Penguins Two provides returning participants with an opportunity to continue developing their skills through additional on-ice instruction. Participants and families should also talk to their Coach or rink's Hockey Director about other next-step programs available in their area.
remove   add  Can my child participate in Little Penguins more than once?
The Little Penguins Learn to Play program is designed for new, first-time participants, and children may only participate in the introductory Little Penguins program once. Children who have completed Little Penguins may be eligible to register for Little Penguins Two, when offered, for additional 8-10 on-ice skill development. Host facilities/organizations may also provide information about other transitional or next-step hockey programs.
remove   add  Are there any fees associated with the Little Penguins Learn to Play Program?
Little Penguins Learn to Play participation fees vary by facility. Please visit the registration page for your selected host location for current pricing and program details.
