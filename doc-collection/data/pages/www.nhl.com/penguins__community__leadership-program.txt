Since 2024, the Pavement Group and the Pittsburgh Penguins have partnered to create the Locker Room Leadership program! This program invited students in 9-12th grade to learn leadership skills from great leaders.

Locker Room Leadership is a five-week program with weekly videos and assignments for students to follow along. Videos contained insight into different aspects of leadership, as well as first-hand stories and advice from local business leaders and hockey operations staff.
Weekly assignments are submitted online, and the top-ranking assignments each week earned students tickets, autographed merchandise, Locker Room Leadership merch, and more! The top-performing student each week receives a custom jersey!
The top 25 students at the end of the program are invited to a pregame reception to network and hear from local leaders, as well as watch the Penguins take on the Dallas Stars on March 28, 2026. The top student is announced at this reception, and they receive a custom prize, including a VIP Penguins experience!
