The Theatre
About
Within the beautifully repurposed former 18th century Catholic church lives Mr. Smalls Theatre, Pittsburgh’s premiere music venue and the anchor of the Millvale entertainment hub. The ambiance and spacious interior make it the ideal place for local and national acts to perform year-round. There are multiple bars throughout Mr. Smalls specializing in wine and liquor, as well as draft, craft and imported beers.
Local and national artists alike have played to sold-out crowds in the theatre – Jack White, Snoop Dogg, Yo La Tengo, Interpol, Smashing Pumpkins, Arctic Monkeys, Spiritualized and Animal Collective are just a few of the amazing talents who have graced the stage. Sold out shows by Pittsburgh artists include Wiz Khalifa, Rusted Root, The Commonheart and more. When USA Today/Esquire magazine named Pittsburgh the best place to rock, it followed by saying, “Mr. Small’s reputation is growing as quickly as its fans are. It hosted Ryan Adams for a month of rehearsal before his tour with the Rolling Stones, and rapper 50 Cent and the Black Eyed Peas have recorded new work in its studio…” Mr. Smalls Theatre has not only carved a deep niche as the best music venue of its size in Pittsburgh, but as one of the finest in the country.
* Mr. Smalls Theatre is generally a standing room only venue, except for special events where specified. Arrangements can be made for the needs of those with disabilities and the like by emailing info@mrsmalls.com in advance.
Details
The Mr. Smalls Theatre Stage
The 800-guest capacity theatre boasts a spacious yet intimate design featuring 40ft high ceilings, marble floors, custom acoustic reinforcements for superb sound, a spacious monitor room/staging area for artists, a 25x26x3 stage as well as professional standard PA and lighting systems.
The Balcony Experience
To enhance the show experience, the VIP Balcony here at Mr. Smalls Theatre offers a little slice of heaven. Standing above the rest of the venue, it provides a different kind of concert experience where patrons can enjoy the show with a unique birds-eye view of all the action. Reservations can be made in advance for individual seats, sections or private balcony rooms, which are great for special occasions or large groups of friends. All reservation fees are applied to a bar tab which lets patrons enjoy food, drink, wait service and guaranteed seating. Arrangements can also be made for special menu items, bottle service and private room rental.

Multiple Bars and Good Food
There are multiple bars throughout Mr. Smalls specializing in wine and liquor, as well as draft, craft, and imported beers. Connected to the venue is The Funhouse Restaurant, an eatery offering snacks, appetizers, burgers and chicken to hungry concertgoers. The restaurant is open during all shows at Mr. Smalls Theatre or The Funhouse @ Mr. Smalls, making it the only place in Millvale serving up delicious late night food at a great price!
