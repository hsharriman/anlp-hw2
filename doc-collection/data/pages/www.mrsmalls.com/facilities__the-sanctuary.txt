The Sanctuary
About
The Sanctuary is the newest edition to the Mr. Smalls family, located in the former 18th century Methodist church across the street from Mr. Smalls Theatre. It houses a music venue and full servince bar, and is the new location of the Mr. Smalls Recording Studio. The Sanctuary is also home to the Creative.Life.Support Center, bringing technology, art, youth, and the community together in one location. Here students will learn audio recording and video production, working with the local and national acts that perform on The Sanctuary’s stage.
Details
The Sanctuary Stage
With a capacity of 350, the former alter will serve as the stage, hosting performances from local, regional and national musicians, as well as Creative.Life.Support students.
The Recording Studio
Mr. Smalls’ state-of-the-art recording studio offers the area’s largest and best sounding tracking room, as well as a highly skilled team of producers, engineers, and musicians, all available at an affordable price. Mr. Smalls services include recording, mixing, mastering, production, voiceovers, studio musicians, live recording, format transfers, original music and beats, and commercial ad production. For more information visit: mrsmallsrecording.com
Creative.Life.Support Center
A music and technology center offers a full recording studio and video production facility, where students will experience and learn audio recording and video production. Lessons and classes are offered in many aspects of musicianship and the music industry. Chief among these programs is a year-round multi-media education seminar that operates in conjunction with The Sanctuary’s show schedule. All aspects of these shows will offer opportunities for students to shadow, and learn each position within show production.
Full Service Bar
The Sanctuary includes a full service bar, featuring delicious cocktails and a wide selection of craft beers and other beverages.
