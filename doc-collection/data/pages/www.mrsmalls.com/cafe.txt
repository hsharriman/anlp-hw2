Mr. Smalls Café
About
Just below Mr. Smalls Theatre lies Mr. Smalls Café, a newly revived space for pre and post-show hangs, along with plenty of weekly programming in between! Featuring a variety of snacks and a full array of coffees, teas and spirits, Mr. Smalls Café offers a lively alternative atmosphere with local art, a turntable and record station, and an updated PA and sound system. The Café entrance can be found on Butler Street just past the Mr. Smalls Theatre steps.
Details
Beverage Emporium
From a vast array of coffees to teas to spirits, we've got you covered. We serve a full menu featuring Rothrock Coffee, so stop in for your Cappuccino, Mocha or Espresso. Spike it up with our Irish Coffee or our Millvale Bulldog. Relax with your favorite import or craft beer. Refresh yourself with Millvale's own Abeille Voyante Teas "Mr. Smalls" blend, "Calm & Focused" yerba mate blend, or their "Jaggoff" black tea blend with a kick!
Pre and Post-Show Hangs
Going to a show in the Theatre? Skip the line for a pre-show beverage or snack in the Mr. Smalls Café and first entry to the Theatre! And after the show, pop down for post-show hangs as you wait for your rideshare. Many Theatre shows feature free afterparties with DJ sets and more! Details listed on the Theatre event pages.
Weekly Events
We now host a variety of weekly events in the Mr. Smalls Café on non-Theatre show nights! These include open mic comedy nights, bluegrass jam nights, singer/songwriter events, karaoke and more! The best part - most of the Café events are FREE. See the Mr. Smalls Café event page to see what's all coming up!
