Mr. Smalls Theatre
With an 800-guest capacity, Mr. Smalls Theatre offers a spacious, yet intimate, design featuring 40ft-high ceilings, marble floors, custom acoustic reinforcements for superb sound, a spacious monitor room/staging area for artists, a 25x26x3 stage and professional standard PA and lighting systems.
Whether it be concerts, weddings, corporate gatherings or private parties of any kind, the staff at Mr. Smalls can provide you with everything needed to make sure the event you’re having is a success! Mr. Smalls also offers catering done by the talented chefs in The Funhouse Restaurant.
SPECIAL EVENTS: Weddings
Recently touted as “the best church to have your wedding in that’s not a church”, Mr. Smalls Theatre is a wedding venue that offers it all; from the ceremony space to the cocktail area to the reception room, the staff at Mr. Smalls can completely plan your special day down to the silverware. The bride and groom will fall in love with the bridal suite and groom’s den while the guests will enjoy the outdoor courtyard and various bars. The venue takes on a majestic ambiance during the ceremony and then transforms seamlessly into an elegant grand reception hall. Mr. Smalls also offers a custom catering menu that can be designed with your tastes in mind.
To request more information email weddinginquiries@mrsmalls.com
SPECIAL EVENTS: Corporate Gatherings and Private Events
Whether planning a Bar/ Bat Mitzvah, graduation party, sweet 16 party, high school prom or class reunion– consider Mr. Smalls for your next event. And Mr. Smalls Theatre offers the perfect space for corporate gatherings of any kind: holiday parties, business conferences, award dinners, private events or anything else one can imagine. Our team can assist with booking local and national talent, DJs or a variety of other entertainment options. Friendly staff is on hand to accommodate specific needs and help with the planning process.

The Funhouse At Mr. Smalls
The Funhouse at Mr. Smalls is a model performance space for up-and-coming artists looking for a stepping-stone to even larger venues, as well as the perfect spot for regional and national artists to perform in a warm and intimate environment. Located on the top level of Mr. Smalls, The Funhouse is equipped with a full bar, an intimate setting, and the ability to house 200 patrons.
For national and local booking & information, contact: booking@mrsmallspresents.com
The Funhouse Stage
With a capacity of 200, The Funhouse stage provides an intimate performance space for musical performances, special events such as film screenings, and more.
The Courtyard Stage
This outdoor courtyard features a permanent stage with a beautiful ivy covered backdrop, perfect for a band or DJ, and multi-level areas with a partially covered upper deck. With market lights strung up high and plenty of dancing room below, this space is ideal for crisp fall evenings or cool summer night dance parties.
The Satellite Stage
The Satellite Stage is located in the Great Hall, nestled between the Mr. Smalls Theatre Balcony and the Funhouse Restaurant. This is a great place for side stage acts during bigger music showcases, providing a festival-like feel to any event.
The Funhouse Restaurant
The Funhouse Restaurant is a full-service restaurant featuring a menu that includes savory, local free-range beef and chicken, sandwiches, appetizers and much more! Mr. Smalls also offers catering done by the talented chefs in The Funhouse Restaurant. Whether it be weddings, corporate gatherings or a party, the staff at Mr. Smalls can provide you with everything needed to make sure the event you’re having is a success!
The Sanctuary At Mr Smalls
COMING SOON: The newest edition to the Mr. Smalls family will be a restaurant, coffee shop and music venue- uniting roots music with quality Italian fare. It will also house the Creative.Life.Support Center, an area created to bring technology, art, youth and the community together in one location. The Sanctuary At Mr. Smalls will begin booking once it has opened to the public. Stay tuned for more information.
The Sanctuary Stage
The 25ft x 20ft former altar will serve as The Sanctuary stage to host performances from local, regional and national musicians. The space features spectacular stained glass from the early 1900’s, detailed wooden pews and gorgeous original architecture. This venue is sure to offer an uplifting and inspiring atmosphere.
Creative.Life.Support Center
The Creative.Life.Support Center will be a music and technology complex offering a full recording studio and video production facility where students will be taught audio and video production in conjunction with The Sanctuary’s show schedule. All aspects of these shows will provide opportunities for students to learn each position within event production, while allowing the performers access to quality audio and video material.
To inquire about booking special events such as workshops, school field trips or listening parties at the Creative.Life.Support Center, contact: info@creativelifesupport.com
