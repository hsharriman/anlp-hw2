Sunday Reset is part of a community wellness series dedicated to welcoming participants into a third space to relax and refresh for the week ahead. AWAACC will facilitate access to yoga led by Black owned Yoga Studio Thinkwell Yoga. The goal here is to provided fellow wellness enthusiasts to connect, foster business for our resident cafe, and encourage gallery tours and membership.
