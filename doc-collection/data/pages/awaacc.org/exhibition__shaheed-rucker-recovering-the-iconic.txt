Shaheed Rucker: (re)Covering the Iconic
April 22 – November 1, 2026
Yvonne and Christine Cook & Family Regional Changing Gallery, Jack and JoAnne Burley Regional Changing Gallery, 1st Floor
Timed Tickets
Shaheed Rucker: (re)Covering the Iconic reimagines the visual language of Jet Magazine and Wheaties cereal boxes through bold, contemporary design that celebrate Black cultural icons from Pennsylvania and beyond. Drawing inspiration from Rucker’s experience working at Jet, the exhibition highlights figures such as August Wilson, Patti LaBelle, and D.J. Jazzy Jeff, while honoring the magazine’s legacy as a vital platform for Black storytelling and representation. Through striking reinterpretations of historic figures, Rucker connects past and present, underscoring the enduring impact of Black achievement in the arts, sports, media, and more.
Curated by Dr. Michael K. Wilson of the African American Museum in Philadelphia and organized in collaboration with Kimberly Jacobs for AWAACC.
Artist Bio
Shaheed Rucker is a visual artist and Brand Strategist whose work explores the intersection of memorabilia, advertising and pop culture. His mixed-media collage practice draws from vintage publications, history, and graphic design to create culturally resonant visual narratives. Rucker was a finalist in Citizens Bank’s Small Business Pitch Competition and previously exhibited at the African American Museum in Philadelphia. He’s also worked with the LeBron James Foundation, Stacey Abrams, Viacom & Atlanta Braves.
