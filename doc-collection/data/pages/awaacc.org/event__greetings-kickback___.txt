Step inside the Greetings exhibition for an evening where community, culture, and play come together.
Whether you’re pulling up a chair for a game of spades, challenging someone to classic video games, listening to music, or simply enjoying good conversation, the Greetings Kickback is designed to recreate the spirit of the gatherings that have brought people together across generations.
Throughout the evening, you’ll have the opportunity to experience the exhibition in a unique way by building your own digital archive. Curate a collection by uploading images from your own life alongside Greetings exhibition artifacts that resonate with you. As you explore, you’ll preserve the stories, objects, and memories that matter most, creating a personalized archive to take home and revisit long after your visit.
