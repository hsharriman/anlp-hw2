Pittsburgh’s magician Lee Terbosic returns to the skies for Houdini 100 – The Great Firetruck Ladder Escape, presented by 1team media. On Halloween night, marking 100 years since Harry Houdini’s passing and a decade since Lee’s 2016 Houdini 100 escape above Downtown Pittsburgh, Lee will attempt an upside-down straitjacket escape suspended 100 feet above Arts Landing from a Pittsburgh Fire Department ladder truck—with a burning lifeline creating a dramatic race against time.
Join us for a free evening featuring DJ Midas and DJ Bonics from 7–8:30 p.m., followed by the stunt program at 8:30 p.m. and Lee’s escape at 8:45 p.m. Come in costume and be part of an unforgettable Pittsburgh moment. Finish trick-or-treating, then head Downtown to catch the performance.
More information: Houdini100.com and LeeTerbosic.com.
