Educators & Schools
Trust Arts Education is committed to serving educators of all levels and their students with a variety of resources, in-school residency programs, and Cultural District experiences meant to provide unique opportunities for engaging with the arts.
School Day Matinee Series
The Pittsburgh Cultural Trust is proud to present matinees appropriate for all grade levels. These shows feature theater, dance, and music performances from numerous Pittsburgh Cultural Trust series.
Early Childhood Programs
The Pittsburgh Cultural Trust is dedicated to providing hands-on and interactive programming to young children.
K-12 Programs
The Pittsburgh Cultural Trust offers comprehensive and diverse programming to young artists and audiences seeking professional and personal growth.
Professional Development
Professional development is a key component to the Pittsburgh Cultural Trust Arts Education Department and programming.
Tix for Teachers
In appreciation for all you do, Tix for Teachers is a unique program that provides educators with discounted tickets for select performances presented by the Pittsburgh Cultural Trust.
