Visual Arts
The Pittsburgh Cultural Trust is proud to present a wide range of visual art through our downtown galleries and public art initiatives. Explore our wide array of current, upcoming, and past offerings below.
Current
Discover what’s on view now at our various galleries throughout the Cultural District, featuring a dynamic lineup of artists. These thoughtfully curated shows invite you to engage with fresh perspectives and contemporary conversations in the visual arts.
Upcoming
Get a glimpse of what’s ahead with our upcoming exhibitions, showcasing bold new projects and thought-provoking works. Stay tuned for an exciting season of creativity, innovation, and inspiring visual experiences.
Past
Explore the rich history of our galleries through a comprehensive archive of past exhibitions dating back to 1997.
Gallery Crawls
The Gallery Crawl is a free, multi-venue "open house" showcasing Pittsburgh’s dynamic art and entertainment scene.
Long-Term Projects
Experience art beyond the gallery walls through our public art initiatives, bringing thought-provoking and accessible works to shared community spaces.
Visit
Pittsburgh's 14-block Cultural District is home to art galleries, public art, and special events all year long. Discover art from around the world and around the corner, only in the Cultural District.
