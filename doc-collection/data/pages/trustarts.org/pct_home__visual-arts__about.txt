About
The Pittsburgh Cultural Trust presents a wide range of visual art through its downtown galleries and public art initiatives. Featuring contemporary artists from Pittsburgh and around the world, the Trust’s five exhibition spaces—including the historic Wood Street Galleries, dedicated to new and emerging media—offer rotating exhibitions throughout the year. Beyond the gallery walls, temporary and long-term public artworks are installed throughout the Cultural District, activating streets, parks, rooftops, and civic spaces with thoughtfully selected works that engage broad audiences.
The Galleries at the Pittsburgh Cultural Trust are FREE and open to the public.
