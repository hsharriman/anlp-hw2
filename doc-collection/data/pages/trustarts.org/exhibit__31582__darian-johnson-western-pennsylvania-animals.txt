Visual Arts
For Arts Landing, Johnson presents Western Pennsylvania Animals, three sculptures inspired by native wildlife, extending his long-standing interest in the physical environments of the region. Known for his intricate painted cityscapes, whimsical “I Spy” compositions, and expressive animal figures, Johnson invites viewers into a world that rewards close looking. This project, a continuation of a collaboration between the Pittsburgh Cultural Trust and VaultArt Studio, affirms the conviction that public space must reflect the full spectrum of human experience, including the perspective of artists with disabilities.
About the Artist
Darian Johnson is a Pittsburgh-based painter, sculptor, and illustrator whose artistic practice is rooted in attentive observation, imaginative world-building, and a deep connection to place. A resident artist at VaultArt Studio—a nationally recognized platform dedicated to elevating the work of artists with disabilities—Johnson brings a distinct and vital perspective to the region’s contemporary art landscape.
Western Pennsylvania Animals by Darian Johnson is commissioned by the Pittsburgh Cultural Trust for Arts Landing. This project is in collaboration with VaultArt Studio, an organization that supports and promotes the inclusion of artists with disabilities in contemporary art. VaultArt Studio is a project of Achieva.
