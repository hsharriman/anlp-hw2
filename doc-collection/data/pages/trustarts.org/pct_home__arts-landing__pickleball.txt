Pickleball at Arts Landing
Open daily 7 am - 10 pm
The pickleball courts are now open! Be the first to experience these one-of-a-kind courts that also serve (pun intended) as a public art ground mural, designed by visual artist Sharmistha Ray.
Free Play
The pickleball courts are open to the public daily and are first come, first served.
Pickleball with PUMP
Thursdays nights in will be reserved for Pickleball with PUMP.
Rules & Regulations
Please help keep the courts safe, clean and welcoming.
Play at your own risk.
Pickleball play only.
Use courts only during Arts Landing operating hours.
Wear proper court shoes.
No food, glass, alcohol, pets, bikes, scooters, skateboards, or skates on the courts.
Do not play on wet courts.
Share the courts.
If courts are full, place your paddle in the rack to join the queue and rotate.
Be respectful of other players, spectators, and equipment.
