Festivals
To complement its mainstage presentations, The Pittsburgh Cultural Trust produces a number of festivals to celebrate the performing arts in its many forms, often in unique places and spaces. From the Dollar Bank Three Rivers Arts Festival, to Highmark First Night Pittsburgh, the Trust is committed to showcasing local arts and artists while bringing the very best the world has to offer to our region.
