How Do Subscriptions Work?
By becoming a PNC Broadway in Pittsburgh subscriber, you're guaranteed entertaining and memorable experiences year round! Subscribers get early access to eight amazing Broadway titles, with the ability to add or swap into three additional titles (later this June). In addition to getting first access to all things Broadway, subscribers get year-round added benefits.
Split your payment up! If you purchase your subscription by May 8, 2026, you can split your payment in half. Pay the first installment on the date of purchase and your second payment on May 13, 2026, interest-free. It's our way of making Broadway in Pittsburgh even more affordable!
Subscriber Benefits
Swap-a-show - Subscribers are permitted to swap tickets from one of the season shows into another season show or one of the season options (starting in June). Limited to one swap per season.
Exclusive exchange privilege - Can't make your normal subscription night? No problem! As a subscriber, enjoy unlimited ticket exchanges within the same event.
Select seat locations - Receive the best seats available to the hottest shows touring Broadway can offer in Pittsburgh at the very best price!
Priority ticket offers - Get your friends and family early access to single tickets to all Broadway shows. Subscribers can order tickets to season options as well as additional tickets to all season shows before they go on sale to the public.
Dining discounts - Enjoy exclusive discounts to downtown restaurants before and after shows. Check out this season's restaurant partners.
Guaranteed season parking - Purchase parking at Theater Square Garage along with your subscription and never worry about finding a parking space all season long!
Guaranteed seats for next year - Subscriptions are automatically renewed each season RISK FREE. Your seats remain secure year after year. If you are not interested in the applicable season, not to worry, we offer a full money-back guarantee if you cancel your subscription within 30 days after our season announcement.
Additional Content - Throughout the year, we invite subscribers to join us for additional events and provide exclusive insider content. Plus, join us for Talk Backs to get the inside scoop on Broadway performances.
Interest-free payment plan - If you purchase your subscription by May 8, 2026, you can split your payment in half. Pay the first installment on the date of purchase and your second payment on May 13, 2026, interest-free.

2026-2027 SUBSCRIPTION POLICIES
AUTOMATIC RENEWAL: All subscribers will be automatically renewed each season. Your credit card will be charged the then-current subscription price annually or in installments (if you’re on a plan). You have 30 days from the date of the season announcement to cancel the renewal via the cancellation link in the email or calling/emailing us. If you cancel before the charge date from the email, you won’t be charged. After that date, we’ll refund you if you make the request within 30 days following the season announcement. A valid credit card, email and Pittsburgh Cultural Trust account are required. Please make sure your email address on file is up to date.
RENEWAL DEADLINE: If we are unable to automatically renew your season subscription for the 2026-2027 season, you must log in to your Pittsburgh Cultural Trust account and pay your renewal invoice no later than 11:59pm on April 6, 2026 to keep your seats. Any requests for adjustments to the standard subscription benefits or show offerings must be completed by May 28, 2025 and are limited to one request per season ticket package account per season. After the deadline, only the benefits associated with the season subscription will be offered.
UPGRADES, SWAPS & EXCHANGES: Seating upgrades and ticket exchanges are subject to availability. The ability to swap or exchange individual shows will begin shortly after the upgrade window. Swaps or exchanges must be completed no later than 48 hours prior to your performance or by 5 pm on Friday for weekend performances. In some cases, a show may require exchanges to be completed at least 72 hours prior to your ticketed performance. The difference in price is due at the time of the swap, exchange or upgrade if moving into a higher priced performance or seating location. Refunds cannot be given for the difference when exchanging tickets from a higher- to a lower-priced performance.
PACKAGE PRICING: The price of each subscription package reflects the face value of your tickets (inclusive of all applicable season subscriber discounts), $58 in handling fees, $40 in facility fees, $12 in district fees, and all applicable taxes.
SEASON OPTIONS: Season Options are not included in any subscription package. Additional purchase is required, and all seating is subject to availability at the time of purchase. All Season Options must be paid in full at the time of purchase and are not eligible for the Payment Plan. Ticket prices for the Season Options include a $5.00 per ticket facility fee and a $1.50 per ticket district fee.
ACCESSIBLE SEATING AND OTHER SPECIAL ACCOMODATIONS: The Pittsburgh Cultural Trust strives to make it easy to purchase accessible seating. Accessible seating areas are solely for people with disabilities and their companions. ADA services are available upon request. For more information, please visit TrustArts.org/TheaterAccessibility.
For more information about accessible seating or other special accommodations, please visit the Pittsburgh Cultural Trust’s Accessibility page or call 412-456-6666 Monday through Saturday 10 am - 8 pm, Sunday noon - 8 pm.
ADDITIONAL TICKETS: Orders that exceed the ticket limit of nine will be canceled without notice, including multiple orders with the same account, billing address or credit card. Additional tickets are subject to availability.
RESALE: We do not support the reselling of tickets above face value. Doing so will constitute a breach of these Subscription Terms and Conditions and Account Holders who violate this rule may be subject to revocation of their purchasing status and seats and be subject to penalties outlined in Pennsylvania State Law (4 P.S. § 201, et seq.).
TERMS & CONDITIONS: Season subscriptions and all ticket privileges are subject to Pittsburgh Cultural Trust Terms and Conditions, the Broadway Across America Standard Season Subscription Terms and Conditions, and the 2026-2027 PNC Broadway in Pittsburgh Offer Details posted on this page. By purchasing a Season Subscription, you agree to such Terms and Conditions.
AUDIENCE ADVISORY: The PNC Broadway in Pittsburgh series offers a diverse selection of arts and entertainment. Not all productions may be appropriate for all ages. Patrons are encouraged to familiarize themselves with shows offered to make informed decisions prior to purchasing a season subscription.

Venue or each event may unilaterally implement health and safety protocols based on updated public health guidance and applicable law. Updates will be sent to ticketholders before each event or posted at TrustArts.org/Welcome. All ticket holders are required to comply. Events are planned at full capacity.
