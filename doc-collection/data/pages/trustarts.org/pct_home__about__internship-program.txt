Internship Program
The Internship Program at the Pittsburgh Cultural Trust is designed to elevate and inspire the next generation of leaders in the arts and non-profit fields. At the Trust, students gain experience in arts leadership through a semester and summer program that provides fundamental workplace skills as well as specialized training in specific disciplines.
