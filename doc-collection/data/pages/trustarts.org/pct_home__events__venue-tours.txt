Venue Tours
Your Tour Experience
Embark on an immersive journey through theatrical history with our venue tours of the Benedum Center and Byham Theater.
Led by passionate guides, each tour is a unique experience enhanced by personal stories and exclusive insights. Explore every facet of these cultural landmarks, from the mechanics backstage to the grandeur of the stages themselves.
What to Expect
Tours will be offered on select weekdays and weekends, determined by venue availability.
Tours will last approximately 1 hour.
The rest is up to you! The experience will be yours to customize with your tour guide.
Prepare to be enchanted by the magic of the theater and book your tour today!
