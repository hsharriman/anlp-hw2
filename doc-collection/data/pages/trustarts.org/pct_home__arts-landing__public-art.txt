Public Art
Public art is woven throughout Arts Landing, inviting visitors to slow down, look closer, and discover something new with every visit. Six distinct artworks are integrated across the site, transforming paths, gathering places, and open views into moments of reflection, play, and connection. Whether you encounter a piece by chance or seek them out intentionally, these works reward curiosity and encourage exploration.
