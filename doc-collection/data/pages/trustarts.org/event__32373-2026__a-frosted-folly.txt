Opening night of A Christmas Carol has arrived, the audience is in their seats, and disaster strikes: the entire cast and director are nowhere to be found. With the curtain minutes away, canceling isn't an option. Seizing control of the chaos, the exhausted stage manager takes the helm and forces the backstage crew into the spotlight. Armed only with the prompt book, makeshift props, and whatever spare costumes they can grab from the racks, this ragtag crew must step on stage and play the parts. It will take a true Christmas miracle—and a lot of frantic improvising—to pull off the performance of a lifetime before a live crowd.
