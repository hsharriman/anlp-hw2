Public Transportation
There are many ways to use public transportation to get to the Cultural District including Pittsburgh Regional Transit buses, "T" light-rail service and Pittsburgh's famous inclines. For schedules, rates and general information about these services, visit Pittsburgh Regional Transit's online Trip Planner or call 412-442-2000. Please note that the "T" is always free downtown. (Buses are not free downtown.)
