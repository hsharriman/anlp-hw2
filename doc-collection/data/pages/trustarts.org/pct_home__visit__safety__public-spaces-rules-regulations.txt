Public Spaces Rules & Regulations
Be Respectful of Others
Visitors are expected to:
Use respectful language and behavior toward all people
Avoid conduct and speech that is disruptive, threatening, discriminatory, or unsafe
Supervise children at all times
Keep entrances, exits, and walkways clear and accessible
Help Keep Public Spaces Safe
For everyone’s safety and recreation, the following items and activities are prohibited:
Weapons and firearms
Fighting, throwing objects, and disorderly conduct of any kind
Fireworks, drones, laser pointers, and similar items
Open flames, including grills, candles, or fire pits
Smoking, including vaping and e-cigarettes
Possession or use of drugs and illegal substances
Alcohol, unless purchased at the space and consumed in designated areas
Camping, including tents, sleeping bags, and similar items
Coolers, duffle bags, or luggage of any kind
Sleeping or loitering
Panhandling or soliciting
Nudity or indecent exposure. Shirts, athletic tops, and full coverage bottoms are required. Undergarments must remain covered.
Transportation & Recreation
Bicycles, scooters (motorized or electric), skateboards, rollerblades and skates, hoverboards, shopping carts, and similar devices are generally not permitted, except if used to travel through Arts Landing on concrete or other hard-surfaced pathways. These devices may not be ridden, used, or parked on lawns, planted areas, playground surfaces, or other non-paved areas.
Care for Arts Landing
Arts Landing’s landscape and public art are here for everyone to enjoy. Please follow these guidelines:
Don’t litter! Dispose of trash properly and help to keep Arts Landing clean.
Stay on designated paths and out of plant beds. Do not pick or remove flowers or plants.
Do not climb on or deface artwork, trees, or structures.
Do not dump liquids, ice, or other materials in plant beds or common areas.
Do not attach items, post signs, or affix materials to Arts Landing features.
Unless directed otherwise, you may touch the public art! However, do not climb, hang on, or otherwise act in a way that could damage any art works.
Confetti, glitter, balloon or lantern releases, bounce houses, or similar materials are prohibited.
Arts Landing Playground
The Cultural Trust is proud to host Downtown’s first playground!
Play equipment is limited to children ages 5-12 (small Step Cluster by Woodland Animals limited to ages 2-5). Other rules for the playground area include:
Play at your own risk.
Adults must supervise children at all times.
Use equipment safely.
Pets, other than service animals, are not permitted.
Follow Arts Landing rules.
Ball playing is not permitted.
Pets & Wildlife
Pets are generally welcome but must be leashed (no longer than six feet). For certain events and/or in designated spaces, the Trust may prohibit pets (other than service animals).
Pet owners must clean up after their pet and use designated relief areas outside of Arts Landing.
Help protect wildlife by not feeding or disturbing animals.
Activities Requiring Permission
To ensure appropriate use of the space, prior approval from the Trust is required for the following activities. Stay tuned for more information on renting Arts Landing!
Events, gatherings, parties, or organized activities involving more than 20 people
Performances, amplified sound, or busking
Organized sports
Commercial activity, vending, solicitation, or distribution of materials
Any photography, filming, or video recording requiring equipment, staging, crew, or exclusive use of an area
Chalking or other non-permanent marking materials
Use of third‑party vendors, décor, equipment, or structures
