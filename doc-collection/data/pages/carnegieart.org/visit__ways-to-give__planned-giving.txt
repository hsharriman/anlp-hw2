Planned Giving
Planned giving offers a way to make an impact on future generations. Consider naming Carnegie Museum of Art as a beneficiary in your will or retirement account or making a gift of stock or another unique asset. Your donation supports the importance of creativity within our society and makes possible future programs, exhibitions, and research.
In appreciation of your planned gift, you will be welcomed into the Cornice Society. Cornice Society members are invited to special events and, unless anonymity is preferred, listed in select museum publications.
Additionally, you can enjoy a lasting legacy at Carnegie Museum of Art through making a gift to one of our established endowments. For further information, email our advancement team.
