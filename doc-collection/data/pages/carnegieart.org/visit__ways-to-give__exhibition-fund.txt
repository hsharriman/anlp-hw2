Exhibition Fund
Driven by curiosity, Carnegie Museum of Art makes space for expansive inquiry into the art and ideas that shape our time. The exhibition program serves as the primary platform for the museum to create experiences that connect people to art, ideas, and one another. Each year, through our dynamic exhibitions, we reignite our commitment to engaging locally and globally with artists, performers, partners, and visitors who seek to experience museum joy. Our collection reinstallations and exhibitions explore contemporary social issues by combining and juxtaposing local and global perspectives.
The Carnegie Museum of Art Exhibition Fund not only provides support for our internationally recognized exhibitions and commissions, but also impacts the museum’s diverse audiences and artists working to advance the region. Gifts to the Exhibition Fund help to champion creativity within the museum’s walls and beyond. Support to the Fund demonstrates the importance of creativity within our society, making possible exhibitions that welcome, inspire, challenge, and inform. Supporting the museum through the Exhibition Fund is a celebration of its far-reaching impact and an investment in advancing it for future generations.
To learn more about supporting the Carnegie Museum of Art Exhibition Fund, please call +1 412.353.4831 or email our advancement team.
