Co-presented with International Poetry Forum
On November 28, 1979, Mary Oliver visited Carnegie Lecture Hall to participate in Pittsburgh’s International Poetry Forum reading series. Nearly half a century later, International Poetry Forum and Carnegie Museum of Art invite you to join us for a free screening of Mary Oliver: Saved By the Beauty of the World, which spans her early days as a poet through her passing in 2019.
Introduction by Jake Grefenstette
President & Executive Director, International Poetry Forum
