Talking with Kids About Art!
Tours Jan. 10, 2025–Dec. 18, 2026
Photo: Bryan Conley
When
Every Friday, 11:30 a.m.–noon
Where
Scaife Galleries
Tickets
Free, museum admission required
We welcome you and your kids in the galleries! During this tour, museum educators facilitate a playful and thoughtful relationship with art for children ages 3 through 10 and their families with hands-on activities and interactive conversations. This 30-minute experience is free with museum admission.
This program meets in the Scaife Lobby before visiting the Scaife Galleries.
