Experience the 59th Carnegie International through movement.
Become part of a work of art by moving through the 59th Carnegie International on this unique tour experience.
We each have different reactions to works of art, but what about a work of art might make you move to it? What might that movement be? What might you be feeling in that moment? 59th Carnegie International artist Brooke O’Harra worked closely with Carnegie Museum of Art docents to create a tour that invites you to respond to works of art through limited physical movement.

59th Carnegie International
Organized every four years by Carnegie Museum of Art, the Carnegie International is the longest-running exhibition of international art in North America. The 59th edition is the most collaborative and far-reaching to date—a clear expression of the museum’s founding commitment to the art and artists of our time, at once grounded in our locality and extending globally.
