When We’re Open and
How to Find Us
Carnegie Museum of Art is conveniently located in Oakland, a bustling neighborhood in the east end of the city of Pittsburgh. From the museum, you can easily access Carnegie Library, Carnegie Mellon University, the University of Pittsburgh, and the Phipps Conservatory and Botanical Gardens.
Our multilevel parking garage accommodates visitors with accessible spaces, elevators, and bike racks. Enter our museum at the front Fountain Plaza entrance on the corner of Forbes Avenue and South Craig Street with ramps and automatic doors, at the Sculpture Court near the parking garage with automatic doors and elevator access, or at the Carriage Drive entrance on Forbes Avenue.

Prepare for Your Visit
Are You Ready to Buy Your Tickets?
Buy your tickets online in advance of your visit to skip the lines and get into the galleries faster!
Are You Visiting with a Group or Bringing a Class?
Groups of 10 or more can experience the museum with a guided tour led by one of our friendly and knowledgeable docents or on their own with a self-guided option. For K–12 students and teachers, we offer school group visits during the academic year. Learn how to co-create a museum experience for you and your students.
Are You Parking at the Museum?
Our six-level parking garage is located just behind the museum. Access the parking garage by driving south on South Craig Street, past our Fountain Plaza. Parking rates are dependent on the duration of your visit. Cash is not an accepted form of payment for parking.

Parking is at the vehicle owner’s risk. Carnegie Museums is not responsible for fire, theft, collision, damage to, or loss of the vehicle or any articles left therein.
Are You Taking Public Transit or Bicycling?
We’re located at the South Craig Street on Forbes Avenue bus stop, which services eight bus lines, and are two blocks away from additional convenient stops on South Craig Street at Fifth Avenue. If you need more information, please visit Port Authority of Allegheny County for more details. If you’re biking, please feel free to use our bicycle racks in the parking garage!
Want to Experience the Museum in Your Home Language?
We’re working to make our museum accessible across languages! We offer additional programming and tours in many languages for our visitors. Please enjoy our museum guides offered in an array of different languages that are available for download. If you have questions, please email our Visitor and Museum Services team or call us at +1 412.622.3131.
What Do You Need to Know Before Visiting?
We want to make sure all our visitors have a safe and enjoyable experience while also protecting the art. Please be sure to keep in mind our primary policies below during your visit.
Do not touch or lean on any of the artwork or display cases.
Do not bring food or drink into the galleries.
Do not bring weapons or firearms into the galleries.
Please wear backpacks on the front of your person or store them in our visitor lockers.
Do not use any flash photography.
Please take a moment to review our in-depth visitor conduct policy for more information.
Looking for Lodging?
Coming in from out of town and need a place to stay? We suggest checking out our official hotel partner, which is down the street from the museum, The Oaklander!
Save on Admission
We know how important it is to save money, and we’re happy to offer discounts for those who qualify. And don’t forget—Carnegie Museums of Pittsburgh members get in for free. Kids ages 2 and under are always welcome at no cost. Visitors ages 13 through 18 receive free admission with a Carnegie Museums of Pittsburgh Free Teen Membership.
Just a heads up: These discounts can’t be combined with other coupons, discounts, or admission to paid programs at Carnegie Museum of Art and Carnegie Museum of Natural History. If you want to take advantage of these discounts but have questions, give us a call at +1 412.622.3131 or email our Visitor and Museum Services team in advance of your visit. Don’t forget to bring your ID when you come, too!
Below are the many ways to save big when you visit:
All Visitors: Late Afternoon Discount
Enjoy $12 admission on adult tickets and half off all other admission after 3 p.m. on weekdays. Use promotional code AFTER3 during your checkout.
RAD (Allegheny Regional Asset District)
The RAD Pass provides Allegheny County library cardholders ages 18 and up with access to free or discounted passes to cultural attractions in our region with a library card from any Allegheny County library. Learn more about the RAD Pass on RAD’s website.
AAA Members: Save $1
Member of AAA? If so, bring your membership card when you visit for $1 off admission for up to six people.
Active US Military and Veterans: 50% Off
If you’re an active member of the US military, receive 50% off admission. This discount extends to up to five family members per military ID.
As part of the Blue Star Museums Program, active US military personnel and their families get free admission from Armed Forces Day (the third Saturday of May) through Labor Day.
Are you a US veteran? If so, receive 50% off admission with your veteran ID. Plus, on Veterans Day, you and up to three guests can get 50% off admission. It’s our way of thanking you for your service.
Bank of America Customers and Employees: Free Admission, First Saturday and Sunday of the Month
On the first consecutive Saturday and Sunday of every month, get one free admission. Simply present your valid Bank of America credit card, debit card, or proof of employment along with a photo ID. This offer is part of the Museums on Us program, which gives Bank of America customers and employees access to free admission at participating museums and cultural institutions.
Colleges and University Students: Free Admission
If you’re a student at one of the following universities—University of Pittsburgh (fall and spring semesters only), Carnegie Mellon University, Point Park University, Chatham University, Carlow University, or Duquesne University Bayer School of Natural and Environmental Sciences or Honors College—visit us for free with a valid student ID.
If you don’t attend one of the schools listed above, don’t worry—you can still get a discounted admission for $15 with a valid student ID.
Access/Electronic Benefit Transfer (EBT)/SNAP Card Holders: Admission for $1
If you have an Access, Electronic Benefit Transfer (EBT), or SNAP card from any state (like a Pennsylvania Access card, Ohio Direction card, or West Virginia Mountain State card), get general admission for up to four people at just $1 each by presenting your card along with a matching photo ID. This program is supported by BNY Mellon.
Educator Preview
Carnegie Museums of Art and Natural History invite PreK-12 educators to explore the museums prior to field trips. Educators who teach PreK-12 in Pennsylvania, Ohio, West Virginia, Maryland, or New York, are eligible for one free admission, once a year. We hope that this visit inspires you and sparks future connections we can make with your students.
To take advantage of the Educator Preview, bring your school educator ID to the admissions desk on the day that you plan to preview.
Please note: Valid for general admission only. Not valid for group visits, field trips or programs that require individual registration. Valid only for PreK-12 educators from schools located in the following states: Pennsylvania, Ohio, West Virginia, New York, and Maryland. No cash value. Not transferable.
Merrill Lynch Customers and Employees: Free Admission, First Saturday and Sunday of the Month
If you’re a customer or employee of Merrill Lynch, get one free admission on the first consecutive Saturday and Sunday of every month. Simply present your valid Merrill Lynch credit card, debit card, or proof of employment along with a photo ID.
Personal Care Attendants (PCA)
Personal care attendants (PCA) who are accompanying visitors with disabilities are eligible to receive free admission to the museum. If you are a PCA, please identify yourself as a PCA while purchasing tickets onsite to receive free admission. Visitors with a PCA wishing to purchase tickets before their visit can call +1 412.622.3131.
Port Authority Connect Card Holders: $1 Off Admission
If you have a Port Authority Connect Card, be sure to bring it with you when you visit for $1 off admission.
