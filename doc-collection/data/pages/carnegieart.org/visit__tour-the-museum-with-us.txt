Organizing a group to come visit?
Self-guided group tours offer admission to Carnegie Museums of Art and Natural History while guided, conversation-based tours are 60 minutes in length and are designed for groups of 10 to 15 adults (ages 16+). We offer docent- and curator-guided tours. If you’d like to explore the museum on your own, we offer group admission discounts as well. Please be sure to fill out our visitor request form for the self-guided group tour option. We’re happy to offer custom tours to meet the interests and needs of your group!
Accessibility Needs
As part of our offerings, we lead verbal description tours for individuals who are blind and tours in American Sign Language, available upon request. We work to accommodate the needs of our visitors: For any accessibility needs or questions, please make note when you make your group visit requests.
School and University Visits
K–12 students and teachers, we offer school group visits during the academic year. Learn how to co-create a museum experience for you and your students. University students and faculty, we welcome you to use the museum as your campus and resource during the semester. We offer group visits that will support your class and your students.
Interested in bringing a group? Here’s what you need to know:
Please reach out to book your tour four weeks prior to your desired tour date.
We aim to accommodate your requested date and time within our staff and spatial capacities of the exhibition. Please provide alternate dates and times in case your first option isn’t available.
Tours may be scheduled from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Wednesday through Monday (the museums are closed on Tuesdays between Labor Day and Memorial Day) and Thursdays 10 a.m. to 7 p.m.
Each tour is designed for a minimum of 10 people and maximum of 15 people.
Please note, Curator-Guided tours are subject to availability and only offered on weekdays, (Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.).
One week prior to your visit, we will send you confirmation and final payment information. We kindly ask that you to send final attendance numbers and payment one week in advance.
