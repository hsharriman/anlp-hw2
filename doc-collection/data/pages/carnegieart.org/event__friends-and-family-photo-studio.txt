For a limited time each year, Carnegie Museums of Art and Natural History become the setting for Friends and Family Photo Studio. Gather family, friends, loved ones—or simply yourself—for a professional portrait session in some of the museums’ most beautiful spaces. Choose from a variety of distinctive backdrops, including seasonal settings during Carnegie Trees, and work with one of Pittsburgh’s talented photographers to create photographs in a place unlike any other.
Now in its sixth season, Friends and Family Photo Studio has become a cherished annual tradition for many Pittsburgh families and visitors. Whether returning year after year or joining for the first time, participants are invited to experience the museums in a new way and leave with photographs that capture a moment in time.
This season welcomes photographers Cameron Brown, Beth Kukucka, Karlie Malone, Darieus Veltri, Jaye Frances, and new this year, Dymond Jewell.

Beth Kukucka is a photographer and filmmaker in Pittsburgh. Karlie Malone
Karlie Malone has spent the last decade mastering the art of capturing the human experience through her camera’s lens. With a Bachelor’s degree in Photography from Edinboro University, Karlie has honed her craft as a portrait photographer. Over the years, she has developed her signature style that combines true to life colors with contemporary perspectives, focusing on candidness and intimacy in each frame. Her portraits are celebrated for their authenticity and depth, showcasing her knack for making people feel both comfortable and seen in front of the camera.

Darieus Veltri
Darieus Veltri is a freelance photographer born and raised in New Kensington, PA. From a young age, he has had a natural love for photography and creative expression, which has only grown over time. Since purchasing his first camera in 2015, Darieus’s passion has taken him on countless adventures, allowing him to connect with diverse individuals while refining his skills.
Darieus specializes in event and portrait photography, but also enjoys capturing daily life, travel, and city scenes in his spare time. He has had the privilege of photographing notable figures including Patrick Mahomes, Terrell Owens, Damar Hamlin, and Skyy Moore. He has worked with organizations such as Carnegie Museum of Art, PNC Bank, The International Free Expression Project, and many other great Pittsburgh-based businesses.
