Forum Series
Located in our museum lobby, our Forum Gallery presents the work of living artists in an ongoing series that invites them to expand their practice through a commission or new presentation of existing works. Initiated in 1990, the Forum series is an opportunity for artists to deepen their relationship to and understanding of the museum by using the institutional resources, including the collection, as a springboard for inspiration, research, and creative exploration.
