Give Your Time
If you’re looking for ways to donate your time, we offer a number of rewarding opportunities to support the museum as a volunteer. Some examples include helping out with one-time special events, conducting ongoing research, or becoming one of our dedicated and knowledgeable docents. Our volunteer program works with your schedule and interests.
By becoming a volunteer, you get amazing perks, including:
Free admission to the four Carnegie Museums of Pittsburgh
Discounts for parking, classes, food services, and merchandise at our store
Regularly scheduled enrichment programs that include lectures and conversations with the museum’s curatorial and research staff
Invitations to receptions and special events
Work experience and letters of recommendation
To learn more, visit volunteer with art or email our Volunteer Office.
