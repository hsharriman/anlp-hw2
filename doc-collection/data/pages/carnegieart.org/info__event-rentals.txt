Event Rentals
Photo: Chris Uhren
Carnegie Museums of Pittsburgh offers more than 20 impressive spaces within its four museums for weddings, special functions, and corporate events. To learn more, please contact Carnegie Museums of Pittsburgh’s special events department, which oversees event rentals, at +1 412.622.3241 or visit the following page on Carnegie Museums of Pittsburgh’s website.
