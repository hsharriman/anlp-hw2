Memorial and Tribute Gifts
If you’ve ever wanted to honor your loved ones for special life events such as weddings, anniversaries, and birthdays, consider a tribute gift. Remember a loved one, friend, family member, or colleague by donating in their name.
Interested to know more? We look forward to working with you through the process. Email our advancement team.
