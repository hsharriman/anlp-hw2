A Place for Youth Creativity
Photo: Bryan Conley
We have an abundance of opportunities for young people to engage with their museum!
Young learners can grow and learn with Mini Makers, school aged children can gather afterschool for Museum Makers or register for a full day Summer Camp, and older children and teens can hone their skills in the Art Connection and Youth Art Studio. Read on to learn more about how to enroll in one or more of these programs!
A Place for...
Early Learners
Art Starts
Newborn–18-month
Spend the morning at the museum while deepening your emotional connection with your child, growing visual development, and encouraging early communication through art viewing and making! Infants and toddlers alongside their caregivers will explore a monthly theme through studio and gallery exploration in this program specifically designed for the earliest learners.
Monthly Program
Mini Makers
Ages 1–5
Visit the museum with your young children to explore engaging sensory, imaginary play, art-making, art viewing, and movement activities. We’ll keep your little artist engaged by moving between stations that will invite each child and guardian to create in a way that is interesting and developmentally appropriate
Weekly Program
Elementary and Middle School
Museum Makers
Ages 6–10
Young artists will explore art making, discussion, and sensory experiences in this weekly afterschool program for early elementary-aged children.
Weekly Program
Day Off at the Museum
Ages 6–10
During Day Off at the Museum, kids will find inspiration in the galleries and create original artworks through painting, printing, sculpting, and mixed media.
Day-long Program
The Art Connection
Grades 5–8
Young artists in grades 5–8 are challenged to create unique artworks inspired by the work in our galleries, culminating in an exhibition of their own at Carnegie Museum of Art.
Weekly Program
High School
Youth Art Studio
Grades 9–12
Students in grades 9–12 will gather weekly to learn artistic skills to develop their personal art practices and portfolios as well as gather information regarding continued education and professions in the creative field.
Weekly Program
Summer Camps
Kids are invited to play, explore and make in the museum on their days off from school. Whether it’s for one day or a whole week, we have you covered with engaging activities for select school holidays and summer vacation.
