Our Future
For the first time in over 20 years, Carnegie Museum of Art is embarking on a comprehensive campaign, focused on increasing authentic engagement with our communities, embracing innovations that will ensure the sustainability and relevance of our programs and exhibitions, and creating new opportunities for learners of all ages.
Carnegie Museum of Art’s vision of the future centers on expanding the imaginative capacity of museum work, catalyzing creative agency, and enriching our locality’s artistic life to bolster our reputation and influence both at home and on the world stage.

Opportunities
Our Building
Carnegie Museum of Art is embarking on a transformative effort to revitalize its building and grounds—redefining how visitors experience art, connect with one another, and engage with Pittsburgh’s vibrant cultural life.
Our Program
Carnegie Museum of Art’s artistic program is a dynamic engine for innovation—bringing together exhibitions, collection presentation, and education and public programming to expand the imaginative capacity of museum practice.
Our Sustainability
Endowment giving provides a renewable, sustained source of support—ensuring that Carnegie Museum of Art can continue to serve its audiences, steward its resources, and adapt to the evolving needs of our time.
