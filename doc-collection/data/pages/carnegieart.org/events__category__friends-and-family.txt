A Place for Friends and Family
Visitors contemplate Frank Stella’s Delaware Crossing, 1967, at Carnegie Museum of Art; photo: Bryan Conley
Meeting up with your bestie, loved one, friends, family, colleagues, or a first date?
Make the museum your go-to destination for any occasion! Depending on the day, you can see a film, watch a performance, go on a tour, join a dance party, make art, or engage in conversations about art and the world around us.
