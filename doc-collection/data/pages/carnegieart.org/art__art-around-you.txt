Art Around You
Did you know that art is all around you when you visit the museum? While you can find art in our galleries, there is also art to experience in other spaces, including the architecture of our building, art in our lobby, and outdoor spaces like the Sculpture Court and Fountain Plaza. Learn about our many different art spaces below!
Hall of Architecture
Opened in 1907, the Hall of Architecture is one of only three architectural cast courts in the world.
Hall of Sculpture
Built after the Greek Parthenon, the Hall of Sculpture is a must-visit for all sculpture and architecture enthusiasts.
Grand Staircase
A soaring three-story historic staircase located at the heart of the museum was added to the original Carnegie Institute building in 1907.
Sculpture Court
Relax among the sculptures from our collection in an open-air setting.
Fountain Plaza
At the museum’s entrance on Forbes Avenue, you can find some of our most impressive sculptures.
Scaife Lobby
Take a moment to explore the immersive and colorful artworks in our museum lobby.
Gallery of Miniatures
Did you know that the museum’s teeniest, tiniest rooms are tucked between two of the biggest?
