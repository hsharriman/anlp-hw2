Dine with Us
Want to fuel up before your visit? Feeling hungry afterward? Stop by Café Carnegie!
We offer seasonal cuisine made with responsibly sourced ingredients in a comfortable, family-friendly, full-service dining area and a walk-up espresso and wine bar. Walk-ins are always welcome.
Discuss your favorite galleries over lunch or a carafe of wine after your visit, or fuel up with some expertly crafted coffee before you take on the art, all without ever leaving the museum. Hungry enough for a full meal? Enjoy our chef-forward dining menu with curated eats. Just need a little pick-me-up? Grab a cookie or tea in between your explorations of the museum!
A friendly reminder that there is no food or drink allowed in the galleries.
