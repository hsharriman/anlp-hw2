Advisory Board
Carnegie Museum of Art’s Advisory Board is a dedicated group of volunteers who are instrumental in supporting the museum’s advancement of its mission. The group consists of leaders within the Pittsburgh region and beyond, across sectors including business, education, government, and arts and culture. The Advisory Board supports the director of the museum to realize the museum’s strategic framework, while ensuring that the museum’s annual programmatic, fundraising, and financial goals are met. Learn more about Carnegie Museums of Pittsburgh’s Board of Trustees.
