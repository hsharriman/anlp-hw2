Endowment Gifts
Enjoy a lasting legacy at Carnegie Museum of Art through making a gift to one of our established endowments, which provide a stable financial foundation for the museum now and years to come. These permanent, self-sustaining sources of funding allow the museum to meet our needs each year.
Currently, we maintain and rely on the following endowments:
Carnegie Museum of Art Endowment
Carnegie International Endowment
Carnegie Museum of Art Exhibition Fund
For more information on making a gift to one of these endowments or establishing your own named endowment fund at the museum, please contact us at +1 412.353.4831 or email our advancement team.
