Corporate Sponsorship
Strong arts and culture institutions are essential drivers in thriving communities. It is up to us, together, to steward this museum into the future so that we can be curatorially ambitious and responsive to our audiences. Many of our corporate donors are Premier Partners.
The benefits of being a Premier Partner include:
Corporate Family Day for employees
Discount on the rental of museums facilities
Advertising discount in Carnegie Magazine
Complimentary museum one-day membership passes
Complimentary employee Memberships
Employee recruiting, client, or prospect tours
Use of historic Founder’s Room for board meetings, retreats, company dinners
Join us in making an impact in the arts!
For more information about becoming a corporate partner, email our advancement team.
