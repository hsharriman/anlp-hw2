Vendor Inquiries
Vendor applications for the 2026 festival are now open. Apply now on our vendor page
If you are interested in vending at a future event but are unsure if this is the right opportunity for you, please connect with our team by completing our Vendor Interest Form. (Please note that this form is not a formal application and does not guarantee acceptance as a vendor.)
NOTICE: All vendor-related communications will come directly from Pittsburgh Downtown Partnership (PDP). Please be aware of potential scams and fraudulent solicitations. Vendor fees will not be charged until vendors have been officially notified by PDP that their application has been accepted. All accepted vendors will receive a secure invoice directly from PDP for payment. PDP does not accept vendor fees through cash-sharing payment apps (such as Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, or similar services).
