Li’l Gherkins KidsPlay Activity Area | Picklesburgh
Li’l Gherkins KidsPlay Activity Area
Kids can get active and try their luck at pickle-themed carnival games. It’s a perfect spot for pint-sized pickle lovers to play and create!
Hours of Operation
Thursday, July 16 | 12:00 pm – 10:00 pm
Friday, July 17 | 12:00 pm – 10:00 pm
Saturday, July 18 | 12:00 pm – 10:00 pm
Sunday, July 19 | 12:00 pm – 6:00 pm
Location
Arts Landing (Towards Penn Ave)
Carnival Games
Carnival games are $2 per play. Tickets can be purchased onsite, no reservation necessary.
Pickle Jar Toss
Pickle Duck Pond
Pickle Bean Bag Toss
Pickle Derby Race
Entry & Guidelines
Li’l gherkins must be accompanied by an adult and adults must be accompanied by a li’l gherkin.
Arts Landing Playground Rules and Regulations
Play equipment is limited to children ages 5-12.
Small Step Cluster by Woodland Animals limited to ages 2-5.
Play at your own risk.
Adults must supervise children at all times.
Use equipment safely.
Pets, other than service animals, are not permitted.
Follow Arts Landing rules.
Ball playing is not permitted.
