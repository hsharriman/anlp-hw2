Pickleball
Picklesburgh is July 16-19 and this year’s festival is turning out to be pickle-est of all! We are bringing yet another attraction to the briniest bash of the year.
Pickleball will join the festival lineup at Arts Landing, the Pittsburgh Cultural Trust’s four-acre outdoor space located in the heart of Downtown. The brand-new painted Pickleball courts will feature tournaments, beginner-friendly clinics, demonstrations, and learn-to-play sessions presented by the Pickleball Warehouse offered at select times every day during the festival.

Join Us for Pickleball Action!
Whether you’re a seasoned fan or just discovering the game, there’s something fun for everyone.
Spectators Welcome
Come cheer on the competitors and enjoy the excitement of our tournament events: Special Competitions, Beat the Pro, Kings Court, Men’s Singles Moneyball, and Men’s Doubles Moneyball.
Admission is free, and spectators are encouraged to stop by, watch the action, and experience the fun atmosphere.
Want to Play? Members of the public are invited to participate, too! Here’s how:
Sign up for a Pickleball Clinic  – Perfect for beginners and players looking to improve their skills. Spots for clinics are limited, so act fast!
Sign up: clinicS
Create an account to access the full list of clinics. Be sure to choose a “Picklesburgh Clinic” with your preferred date and time.
Open Play Sessions – Join other players at Arts Landing for casual games and meet fellow pickleball enthusiasts. Open play sessions are first-come, first-served.
Come watch, learn, compete, and have fun—we look forward to seeing you on the courts!
