Public Transit
Pittsburgh Regional Transit has nearly a hundred bus lines that connect downtown. Find more information on how to ride PRT on their website, or visit PDP’s transit webpage. Riders can expect minor service disruptions during Picklesburgh. Route detours will be announced here closer to the date(s) of Picklesburgh.

Parking
See parkpgh.org for downtown parking garage locations and rates. Note: Private garages charge event pricing, whereas Pittsburgh Parking Authority garages do not, with the exception of Sixth & Ft. Duquesne Garage. All garages are open and accessible during the event.
