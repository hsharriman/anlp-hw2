Zoo Information - Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium
Skip to content
Guests visiting the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium will experience a living park with live animals in a primarily outdoor setting, subject to all weather conditions. Due to changing conditions including health, safety, operational, or weather concerns, certain animals may be unavailable for viewing, and areas of the park may close unexpectedly.
Please observe the following policies for the health and safety of your group, our animals, and staff members. Guests may also like to review our Accessibility features, or check our Operations schedule for any daily modifications.
Ticketing
General Ticketing, Admissions, & Refundsexpand_more
Ticketing policies are subject to change at any time at the discretion of the Pittsburgh Zoo.
The Zoo is a rain-or-shine facility. Provided the Zoo is able to open and maintain basic operation, the Pittsburgh Zoo adheres to a no-refund, no-credit, no-substitution, no-rain check policy.
The Zoo retains the right to close for general admission or cancel an event. Guests with pre-purchased tickets for admission on this day or for a specific event will be offered a reschedule or refund opportunity, at the discretion of the Zoo.
All rescheduling requests are evaluated at the discretion of the Pittsburgh Zoo. Rescheduling requests are not guaranteed, as they depend on availability and are likely to incur additional fees, as outlined below or for specific events.
The Zoo offers discounts and periodic free admission for qualifying guests; please check our Discounts page and Events page to see standing and upcoming offers. Unless otherwise noted, special offers must be redeemed at admission gates. Guests who qualify for discounts but purchase advanced online tickets will not receive price adjustments or refunds after purchase.
Only tickets obtained from the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium or parties authorized by the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium to sell/distribute tickets will be honored. To verify authenticity, contact the Zoo at 412-665-3640, ext. 0. Any imitation or altered version of an official Zoo ticket—digital or physical—will be refused entry without exception. Our scanning system validates all tickets, and fraudulent submissions will result in denied admission and possible removal from Zoo grounds. We appreciate your cooperation in ensuring fair access for all guests.
Zoo tickets purchased and not used will not be refunded.
By purchasing tickets, you agree to the Zoo’s visitation policies.
Daytime Admission Reschedulingexpand_more
Expanding on the above general ticketing policy, “General Daytime Admission” includes daytime entry to the Zoo between the approximate hours of 9:30 a.m. and 3 p.m. (seasonally dependent, with additional hours available during peak season) and any self-guided experiences during your visit.
Daytime Admission tickets include valid use details directly on the ticket. Tickets may include a valid date, expiration date, or other limitations. Admission tickets may only be used in manners listed on the ticket.
Flex tickets, or any tickets received as part of a sponsorship, contest, donation, gift, or media trade are subject to limitations and expiration dates listed on the ticket and cannot be rescheduled.
General Daytime Admission Single Day Tickets must be used on the date listed on the ticket. Learn more about Single Day tickets here.
Guests wishing to reschedule their pre-purchased general admission Single Day tickets to an alternate available date may call the Zoo’s main line at 412-665-3640 or email [email protected] 48 hours in advance to request a reschedule for $5 per ticket. As a rain-or-shine facility, requests to reschedule Zoo tickets due to weather conditions will be denied.
Daytime Admission Group Policyexpand_more
Single day online daytime admission purchases that include 15 or more general admission tickets of any age range automatically receive a flat rate discount on daytime admission tickets in their shopping cart. Prices subject to change. Group discount only applies to Single Day general daytime admission tickets. Learn more here.
The single QR code on the bulk ticket receipt is valid for all admission tickets purchased on the existing bulk order. The number of attendees arriving from the group will be recorded each time the code is scanned at gates. The order receipt and QR code can be printed/forwarded to all members of the group and the same QR code will be scanned to record the appropriate number of guest arrivals as they arrive.
The Zoo is unable to add additional tickets to existing discounted orders. The Zoo will not maintain a list of uniquely identifiable attendees, so please be sure your QR code is only offered to your approved group attendees. Additional guests beyond the original discounted order will need to purchase tickets at the going admission gate rate.
Per above general ticketing policy, tickets purchased but not used by group attendees will not be refunded.
Schools, colleges, daycares, scouts, camps, and other educational institutions can apply to receive additional discounts and options for additional classes here.
Daytime Re-entryexpand_more
Guests may leave the park and return on the same day during daytime operation. Any guest wishing to do so must have their original receipt to be granted re-entry. Re-entry will only be granted on the same day of purchase as the original admission.
Event Refund/Reschedule Policyexpand_more
This includes educational classes, on-grounds ticketed meals, evening experiences, behind-the-scenes tours, and more.
Unless otherwise specified for a specific event, all cancelations must be made at least two weeks prior to the start of your event. In the event of a cancelation or change, the Zoo will retain a transaction fee and processing fee.

Safety & Conduct
Guest Safetyexpand_more
Guests are to stay on public pathways only. Barriers between you and the animals such as rails, moats, rocks, and fencing are there for the safety and protection of both you and the animals. Please do not lift children over rails or barriers, allow them to play on rocks, rest them on railings, or stick fingers or arms through fences. Do not enter non-public areas and stay on the main pathway and in guest areas at all times. Violators will be prosecuted.
Code of Conductexpand_more
The Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium respects the wildlife we care for, the communities we serve, and our team. The Zoo prohibits behavior that is intentionally disruptive to any fellow guest, volunteer, vendor, member or staff, or wildlife. Such behavior can include, but is not limited to, harassment, intimidation, and/or discrimination of any kind, including physical, verbal, or non-verbal abuse. Violation of this policy can result in removal from the premises without refund, and/or permanent ban from the grounds.
Animal Safety & Pet Policyexpand_more
Please do not feed any animals at the Zoo. Resident Zoo animals are not domesticated pets and all of our animals’ diets are closely monitored by our vet and keeper staff. Please observe all safety requirements.
Personal pets are not permitted on Zoo grounds, in the parking lot, or unattended in cars. Guests must leave pets at home or arrange for off-grounds pet care services. Guests with service animals are welcome and may reference our Accessibility page for our Service Animal policy.
The Zoo cannot assist with or accept found/injured wild animals from the general public. The Zoo works with official local, regional, and international networks to assist in such cases.
Certain items are dangerous for resident animals and are not permitted on grounds. Please see “Prohibited Items” below on this page.
Dress Code & Maskingexpand_more
For the health, safety, and comfort of all guests, proper attire must be worn at all times. Guests must wear shirts at all times, and guests must wear shoes while walking in the Zoo.
Provocative, revealing, or offensive clothing is prohibited. Disguises, costumes, or articles of clothing that conceal a guest’s identity are prohibited. There are specific costume guidelines for special events such as ZooBoo; please refer to the policy on the website’s event page.
Masks and other face coverings are acceptable for personal health or religious reasons.
Guests participating in special programming such as events, educational classes, or animal encounters may be required to wear a mask or, in some situations, show proof of COVID vaccination. Guests participating in any special programming will be alerted to requirements prior to involvement in the special event.
The Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium reserves the right to refuse admission to anyone who refuses to comply with the dress code.
Personal Wheeled Devices & Mobility Assistanceexpand_more
Guests are welcome to visit the Zoo in personal wheelchairs or electric mobility scooters. Guests are also welcome to bring strollers or personal wheeled devices like wagons so long as they are under the control of an adult.
Please remain on the pathway throughout your visit. All permitted wheeled devices must use the elevator located next to the escalator when entering the park.
Prohibited wheeled devices are listed below under the “Weapons & Prohibited Items” heading.
Visit our Accessibility page to learn more about mobility assistance, including use and rental of wheelchairs and seated electric scooters.
Bag Inspection Policyexpand_more
Guests may bring portable items such as bags and coolers to the Zoo, though we reserve the right to inspect them. While clear bags are not required, they will speed the admission process. Guests must keep items with them at all times. Prohibited items are listed below on this page.
Outside Food and Drinkexpand_more
Food and drinks are available for sale on grounds. Guests can bring their own bagged lunch-style food or leave food or belongings in their vehicles (at their own risk) and return to the car to get them throughout the day. If the guest chooses to do this, a receipt is required for re-entry to the Zoo. We do not offer storage lockers or places to store coolers/picnic lunches. We reserve the right to inspect any bags or coolers brought onto Zoo grounds.
There are also picnic areas within the park and, as long as they are not reserved for the day (noted with a sign on the pavilion), guests are welcome to utilize these areas for eating outside food or consumables purchased in the park. Anything left in a picnic area unattended is at the guest’s own risk and is not recommended.
Guests cannot bring alcohol, glass containers, anything that needs to be cooked, or grills of any kind into the park.
Fully catered lunches are not permitted to be brought or delivered on grounds by general daytime guests. The Zoo’s exclusive food and beverage partner, SSA Group, offers catering services for rental clients; guests renting a space at the Zoo for a Private Event should check with the Events team regarding contracted catering requirements.
Severe Weather & Emergenciesexpand_more
The Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium is a rain-or-shine facility; closures or event cancelations due to weather conditions such as rain, snow, heat, and cold are rare. Rain checks and refunds will not be granted provided the Zoo is able to continue basic daily operations. Guests are encouraged to dress appropriately for weather conditions and plan to stay warm, cool, or dry, depending on needs. Guests are always encouraged to stay hydrated. In the unusual event of a Zoo closure due to extreme weather, the Zoo will contact ticket holders and place a messaging alert on our website.
Due to changing conditions including health, safety, operational, or weather concerns, certain animals may be unavailable for viewing, and areas of the park may close unexpectedly.
The Zoo is recognized by the National Weather Services as a StormReady community. In the event of severe weather during normal daily operations, guests may be refused entry into the park, or if already in the park, guests may be asked to relocate to indoor facilities until the weather passes.
Zoo staff members prepare for a variety of potential emergency situations with routine drills and can assist guests. The Zoo will make on-grounds announcements and/or staff members will contact guests in the event of emergencies. Guests must comply with staff requests in the event of an emergency situation.

The use of tobacco products, as well as all smoking and vaping, is prohibited on Zoo grounds. Guests must leave Zoo grounds to use these products.

Weapons & Prohibited Itemsexpand_more
The following items are prohibited on Zoo grounds:
Weapons of any kind.
Outside alcohol. See our Food and Retail page for alternative options.
Tobacco, smoking, or vaping products.
Non-mobility wheeled devices: heelys, skateboards, rollerblades, bicycles, standing scooters, hoverboards, roller skates, and personal go-carts. Unauthorized motorized vehicles other than wheelchairs or mobility scooters are not permitted on the Zoo paths. See our Accessibility page for additional information.
Balloons, disposable straws, loose glitter and confetti, glass containers, laser pointers, and any objects intended for throwing (balls, Frisbees, paddleballs, etc.), which can be very dangerous to animals.
Drones are not permitted to be brought onto or fly above Zoo grounds.
Any animal or pet that is not a service animal. See our Accessibility page for additional information. Please note animals may not be left in cars; pets and non-service animals should not be brought to the Zoo.
Any item not permitted must be disposed of by a guest, returned to vehicles, or removed from grounds in the case of non-service animals. Prohibited items may not be left in the care of the Zoo; the Zoo will not be responsible for the safe-keeping of any prohibited item.

Media
Consent for Media Acquired by the Zoo and Zoo Partnersexpand_more
As a venue attended by the public, please be aware that cameras and recording devices will exist in public areas of Zoo grounds during your visit.
The Zoo utilizes cameras for business purposes including safety and promotion. Zoo employees frequently capture media of our grounds for promotional use, including photos and videos. Your visit to the Zoo constitutes implied consent for you and your party members to be captured on media for Zoo promotional and informative purposes. Media may be published in any format, in whole or in part, in print and digital materials such as, but not limited to, the Zoo’s website, social media, email communications, video/commercials, radio/podcasts, and a variety of publications such as advertisements, newsletters, flyers, and billboards.
Third party commercial media may not be collected on grounds without approval from the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium. The Zoo may partner with third parties, including but not limited to sponsors, vendors, private rental clients, and media production companies, to which we may provide Zoo-captured media, or which may also acquire their own media. Participation in activities on Zoo grounds related to these third parties implies consent to have media captured for use by these third parties. On days when the Zoo is hosting Zoo-wide third-party commercial-use media shoots, notice will be posted near the area of filming or at the entrance of the park.
Personal Mediaexpand_more
We welcome making memories here at the Zoo! General admission guests are always welcome to capture media in standard public areas for personal use, provided guests follow the Code of Conduct (listed above here on the Zoo Information page). This media may be posted on personal, non-commercial social media pages. Guests may tag us on social platforms @pghzoo; at the discretion of the Zoo, material shared with us via tagging may be shared publicly on official Zoo socials with credit to the posting account.
Guests may generally photograph any of our resident animals visible on habitat, provided equipment does not become a barricade for other guests. Please be aware of other guests during daytime operation and minimize disruption. General guest photos should be taken from publicly accessible spaces, and you and your equipment should remain over the public pathway. Do not reach anything over fences or barricades and do not place equipment in/on habitats. Our staff does reserve the right to request no photography, or request a guest move photography/equipment should a need arise for the well-being of animals and fellow guests.
Flash photography is often permitted on Zoo grounds, though certain areas will have signage posted where flash photography is prohibited. Please follow guidance provided on signage. During certain nighttime events, like the Jack O’Lantern Extravaganza, animal habitats are not lit for public view, and flash photography or lighting of animal habitats is prohibited for nighttime animal comfort.
Guest media capture may be limited in areas when guests are escorted by staff to exclusive behind-the-scenes areas (such as during a Wild Encounter). Zoo staff will advise while on tour. Livestreaming or filming the entirety of a Wild Encounter/behind-the-scenes experience is prohibited; guests must remain alert to their surroundings at all times during a tour.
Guests most frequently capture media with cell phones or personal cameras, which are welcome. We request guests with carted equipment or tripods contact the Marketing department at [email protected] for additional guidance. Drones are strictly prohibited from being brought onto or flying above Zoo grounds without express permission from the Marketing department.
Guests looking to commercialize personal material acquired at the Zoo must first contact the Marketing department for approval at [email protected].
