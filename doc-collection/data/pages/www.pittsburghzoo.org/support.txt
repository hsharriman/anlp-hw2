Support the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium
Your generosity makes a world of difference to the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium, helping us protect wildlife, provide exceptional care for animals, and inspire future generations to take action for conservation. We offer a variety of ways to give so that you can choose the option that works best for you.

Other Ways to Give
There are many impactful ways to support the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium. These options not only help us continue our mission but may also offer you valuable tax benefits.
Join the Grow Wild Society
This exclusive group is for those who generously donate $1,000 or more to our Annual Campaign. Donors enjoy VIP benefits, including invitations to behind-the-scenes experiences, exclusive preview events, and other special opportunities.
Grow Wild Society
Smart Giving
These tax-efficient options include Donor-Advised Funds (DAFs), stocks or securities, real estate donations, Qualified Charitable Distribution (QCDs), and cryptocurrency!
Smart Giving
Legacy Gifts
By including the Zoo in your estate plans, whether through a bequest or as a beneficiary of your retirement account, your legacy gift ensures that our conservation and educational efforts continue for generations to come.
Forever Wild Society
Workplace Giving
These are powerful ways employees can create a lasting impact in our community—and for the animals you care about. Through programs such as matching gifts, volunteer grants, and payroll deductions, your generosity can go even further.
Workplace Giving
Adopt an Animal
Show your love for your favorite Zoo animals from alligators to zebras! By symbolically adopting one of our animals, you help us provide the best care possible, as well as support our education and conservation programs.
Adopt an Animal
Become a Corporate Partner
By becoming a corporate partner, your company can make a significant impact on wildlife conservation efforts while also gaining valuable exposure to approximately one million annual visitors.
Corporate Partnerships
Become a Member
Support the animals and visit as much as you like. A Zoo Membership gives you unlimited free visits and exciting benefits all year!
Membership
Camp Scholarships
Your generosity can make a life-changing difference for children by supporting our Campership Scholarship Fund, making Zoo Camp more accessible to campers who otherwise could not afford to attend.
Camp Scholarships
Donate to the Annual Fund
Your support of the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium is a vote of hope for the future of wildlife and wild places and enables us to continue our vital conservation programs in ways that truly matter.
Annual Fund
Honorary & Memorial Gifts
Make a donation in honor or in memory of a friend, family member, or someone important to you that has shared your passion for wildlife and the wild places they live.
Honorary & Memorial Gifts
