The Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium’s Forever Wild Society is a special group of donors who have made the extraordinary commitment to include the Zoo in their estate plans. By joining the Forever Wild Society, you help ensure the Zoo’s ability to protect wildlife, inspire future generations, and advance conservation and education for years to come. Your legacy gift reflects your dedication to creating a thriving future for people, animals, and the planet.

Ways to Leave a Legacy Gift
Bequests
A bequest is a simple and flexible way to leave a gift to the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium in your will or trust. You can designate a specific amount, a percentage of your estate, or the remainder after other distributions.
Beneficiary Designations
You can name the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium as a beneficiary of your retirement plan, life insurance policy, or financial account. This option allows you to support the Zoo without changing your will or trust.
Charitable Gift Annuities
Create a gift that gives back. With a charitable gift annuity, you can provide future support to the Zoo while receiving a fixed income for life and potential tax benefits.
Charitable Remainder Trusts
Establishing a charitable remainder trust allows you to provide income to yourself or your loved ones for a set period, with the remaining assets benefiting the Zoo. This option can help reduce taxes while making a meaningful gift.
Gifts of Real Estate
Donating real estate, such as a home or property, is a powerful way to support the Zoo’s mission. Real estate gifts can provide significant tax advantages while contributing to wildlife conservation.
Join the Forever Wild Society
When you include the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium in your estate plans, you’ll join the Forever Wild Society, a community of dedicated supporters committed to the future of wildlife and conservation. Members enjoy exclusive benefits, such as invitations to special events, recognition in our publications, and updates on the impact of their legacy gifts.
