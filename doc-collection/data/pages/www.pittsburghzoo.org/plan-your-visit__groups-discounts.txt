General Group Discounts
Getting a great group rate on daytime admission to the Zoo is easy! Add 15 or more single day general daytime admission tickets of any age range to your shopping cart and automatically receive a $23 flat rate per ticket (effective January 1, 2026). Single QR code confirmation can be shared with all guests in your group for easy entry at gates. Just pick what day you’d like to visit the Zoo and get started here.

RAD Pass
RAD Pass provides Allegheny County library card holders with access to reservations where they can receive free or discounted passes to cultural attractions in our region. The program now runs year-round! A pass is valid for general admission during daytime operating hours only.

OpporZOOnity Program
Guests who reside in one of 10 counties around the Zoo and receive food assistance from the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) can gain general daytime Zoo admission any day for $5 each for groups up to 4 people who qualify for the OpporZOOnity Program. This discount program does not apply to any special programming or events at the Zoo. The program only applies to general daytime admission. You must apply in advance of your visit.
OpporZOOnity Program
Military Admission
All active duty, reservists, and veterans of the United States military receive $5 off general daytime admission with proper identification (i.e. military ID, DD-214) on all operating days of the year.
The Zoo offers FREE admission to active duty, reservists, and veterans with proper identification on select days: the full week between Armed Forces Day (May 16, 2026) through Memorial Day (May 25, 2026), as well as the specific days of Independence Day (July 4, 2026) and Veterans Day (November 11, 2026). This discount does not apply to family members or dependents of military personnel/veterans, nor to special ticketed Zoo events. Offer may only be redeemed at admission gates.
Paws for a Cause
The Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium is pleased to offer free daytime admission to nonprofit organizations that serve individuals with developmental, intellectual, neurological, or physical disabilities. Because demand for this program is high, and daily capacity is limited, reservations are required, and not all requests can be accommodated. Requests are reviewed in the order they are received and must meet all program guidelines.
