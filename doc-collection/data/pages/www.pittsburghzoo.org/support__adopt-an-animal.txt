Adopt an Animal FAQ
Do I get to take the animal home with me?expand_more
No. All adopted animals remain in the care of the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium. We hope you will visit your adopted animal often!
Do I get to name my adopted animal?expand_more
No. All Zoo residents already have names. If there is a specific animal you would like to adopt, we are happy to put the animal’s name on your adoption certificate. Please email [email protected] to make this request.
How long will it take to get my adoption packet?expand_more
Adoption orders are generally filled 1-2 weeks after the order date.
Where can I see my name for recognition?expand_more
Your name will be published on the Adopt an Animal board located between the Safari Gift Shop and the carousel on Zoo grounds. The board is updated once a year, in the Fall, with all adopter’s names from the previous year (August-July).
If you adopt as a gift for someone else, the name of the gift recipient will be the name published on the recognition board.
Will other people adopt the same animal I adopted?expand_more
Yes. Animal adoptions are available to anyone at any time.
How long does my adoption last?expand_more
Adoptions are good for 1 year and may be renewed annually.
