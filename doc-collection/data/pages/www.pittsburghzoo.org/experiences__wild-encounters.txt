Connect to Wildlife
The Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium is home to more than 8,000 animals representing over 600 species. This includes over 30 species of concern, which means their numbers are threatened, endangered, or even critically endangered. When you participate in a Wild Encounter, you not only get up close and personal with these amazing creatures, but you also help the Zoo support our animals. Go behind the scenes and learn about the personalities that make them unique, see how they live, and how we care for them. Admission to the Zoo is now included in this experience. Zoo Members will receive an applicable discount based upon membership level.

Get ready to embark on a wild and wondrous journey with our family fun animal adventure! You’ll meet a delightful variety of reptiles and small animals, including slithering snakes and adorable armadillos. Our expert handlers will guide you through their fascinating world, sharing all about their unique behaviors and habitats. But here’s the twist—some of these incredible creatures are animal ambassadors! These special animals play a key role in helping us teach the public about the importance of wildlife conservation. While they may not be able to live in the wild, they’re here to connect with you and share their story. It’s a fun-filled adventure where you’ll learn, laugh, and make memories with furry, scaly, and fantastic friends, all while discovering how you can help protect the animal kingdom.
