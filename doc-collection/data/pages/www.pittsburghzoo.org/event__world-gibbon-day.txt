Celebrate the small, acrobatic apes known for their unique vocalizations and agility. Learn how we can help these incredible animals, many of whom are critically endangered.
Touch, feel, and explore biofacts with our docents; plus, other fun and educational activities. All ages welcome!
Event Schedule
10:30 a.m. – 3 p.m.: Gadgets for Gibbons Donation Table
The Gadgets for Gibbons program collects old laptops and tablets to be broken down into parts and recycled with a portion of the proceeds donated to Saving Animals from Extinction, or SAFE Gibbon.
10:30 a.m. – 3 p.m.: Gibbon Exploration Table at the Tropical Forest Complex
11:30 a.m.: Keeper Chat at the Gibbon Habitat at the Tropical Forest Complex
