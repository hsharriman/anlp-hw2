Parents’ Night Out
Saturday, October 24 from 5:30–8:30 p.m.
Saturday, November 21 from 5:30–8:30 p.m.
Looking for more than just a babysitter? Plan a special evening while your kids enjoy a fun-filled experience at the Zoo! Activities include a pizza dinner followed by animal encounters, tours, games, and a craft – all before settling down to a movie.
Parents’ Day Out
Saturday, December 12 from 12-3 p.m.
Plan some “me” time in the afternoon while we hang out with your kids at the Zoo. Activities include a pizza lunch followed by animal encounters, crafts, and a Zoo adventure.
