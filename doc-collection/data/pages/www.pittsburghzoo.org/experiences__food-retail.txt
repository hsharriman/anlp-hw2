Food & Retail - Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium
Skip to content
Eat, drink, and find the perfect souvenir! From quick snacks to filling meals, the Zoo offers a wide variety of dining opportunities, including healthy and allergen-friendly options. After a great day in the park, take home the perfect gift for a loved one. Here’s a taste of what we have to offer! Take a look at our Map to find your way around.
The Zoo also offers water bottle filling stations throughout the park to support sustainability efforts.
The Zoo’s food and retail service is operated and managed by our partners at SSA Group and offer a diverse array of options. All locations accept Visa, MasterCard, Discover, and American Express.

One Wild Brew, conveniently located at the top of the escalators near the Zoo entrance, offers a variety of food, beverages, snacks, and seasonal offerings.
Food items include sweet and savory shareables, seasonal breakfast sandwiches, and other snacks. Beverages include seasonal coffees, Icees, fountain drinks, beers and seltzers. One Wild Brew offers an option to support guests on vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or dairy-free diets.

African Savanna: Savanna Stopexpand_more
Offering a range of drinks, light snacks, and small novelties, Savanna Stop will keep you moving on your trek onward to ostriches, nyala, and elephants. Open seasonally and weather dependent.

The African-themed Jambo Grill is located at the mid-point of the Zoo, making it a great place to take a break during your safari. Jambo Grill offers a fresh, made-to-order menu with the widest offerings, plus classics like burgers and fries, chicken tenders, sandwiches, pizza, and salads. Safari Plaza: Safari Ice Creamexpand_more
Safari Ice Cream is a great stop to pick up a cool treat on a hot day. Open seasonally.
Safari offers ice cream cups and cones, ice cream floats, sundaes with optional toppings, cotton candy, popcorn, soft drinks, and water.
Safari Plaza: The Beer Denexpand_more
Pittsburgh Zoo history fans recall when bears used to reside in the area that is now The Beer Den. 21+ guests can stop here to reminisce and enjoy an adult beverage, and guests of all ages can grab a snack. Open seasonally and weather dependent.
Options include a variety of domestic and craft beers and hard seltzers, as well as water, soft drinks, popcorn, and cotton candy.
Water's Edge Plaza: Dippin Dotsexpand_more
Among the most popular offerings at the Zoo, this kiosk offering the ice cream of the future reigns supreme near the Water’s Edge Pavilion. Open seasonally and weather dependent.
Kids Kingdom: Hops Cafeexpand_more
Located across from the sheep and goat yard and in front of the Animal Motions Playgrounds, Hops offers a quick pick-me-up on adventures through Kids Kingdom. Open seasonally.
Hops offers regular and specialty flavored soft pretzels, dairy-free soft serve, Icees, soft drinks, water, domestic and craft beer, and hard seltzer.

The Village: Village Outfittersexpand_more
The first building guests see when exiting our iconic escalator, Village Outfitters is one of two gift shops located in The Village. This open-air shop features a wide selection of children’s toys, plush animals, T-shirts and more. This shop is for the young and young at heart. It is a favorite shop for school groups and young families.
Open daily, April through September, weather permitting.
The Village: Safari Outpost (open year-round)expand_more

The Village: Safari Wheelsexpand_more
Located in The Village at the main entrance to the Zoo, this location primarily offers electric scooter, wheelchair, and stroller rentals.

Aquarium: Current Gift Shop (open year-round)expand_more
The Current Gift Shop is located in the Aquarium. This spectacular gift shop features an array of unique aquatic plush creatures, apparel, gifts, books, jewelry and toys.
Open year-round.
Water's Edge Plaza: Northwest Trading Companyexpand_more
Located in the plaza between Water’s Edge and Kids Kingdom. Whether it’s a plush animal from the Water’s Edge exhibit, or gear for a Northwest expedition, you’ll find it here.
Open daily, April through September, weather permitting.
