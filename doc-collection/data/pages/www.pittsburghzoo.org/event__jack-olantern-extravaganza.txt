It’s Gourd to be Here!
Thousands of artistically hand-carved pumpkins glow in the night during the walk-through Jack O’Lantern Extravaganza, presented by Peoples. The gallery of pumpkins features all-new designs, including an array of beloved Zoo animals, famous Pittsburghers, Cryptids, familiar characters from “a galaxy far, far away,” and recognizable faces from the worlds of animation, music, sports, and more – presented with sound, light, and other special effects.
The premier family-friendly nighttime event in Pittsburgh is the perfect combination of nighttime fun, the spirit of autumn, and artistry. Over 4,200 Jack O’Lanterns illuminate The Islands and Jungle Odyssey in a dazzling spectacle, including differently themed areas with a Pittsburgh connection and our showstopping pumpkin trail finale, the Living Wall. All pumpkins and decorations are provided by local partner Soergel Orchards.
In addition to pumpkins of all shapes, sizes, and designs, the Jack O’Lantern Extravaganza will feature activities for children, gently spooky seasonal displays, 21+ experiences in the Garden Tent, and specialty seasonal food items. The evening Jack O’Lantern Extravaganza adds a fall family tradition that will delight guests of all ages.
Ticket Options
The time on your ticket indicates the range of time when you will enter the Zoo but does not require a specific exit time. Once admitted to the Zoo at the listed time for the event, you may stay in the park as long as preferred, or until park close. Single Day tickets must be used on the selected evening and are not transferrable to other nights. Flex tickets must be used by the expiration date listed on the ticket.

Single Day Tickets
Standard non-Member adult tickets are $24 for weekdays Monday-Thursday, and $26 for weekends Friday-Sunday. Child tickets, ages 2-13, cost $6 less: $18 for weekdays and $20 for weekends.

Member Discounts
Members save $2 on all Single Day tickets. Discount will automatically be applied to all Single Day Jack O’Lantern Extravaganza tickets in the cart once Members log in. Number of tickets discounted is limited to the level of Membership held by the guest.

Group Discount
Groups of 5+ can automatically receive a $23 flat rate on all Single Day adult tickets. Discount will automatically be applied to all applicable Jack O’Lantern Extravaganza tickets in the cart. All child tickets will receive listed rates lower than $23. Groups must select a date and time that works for everyone in the party. Note that Member discount does not apply to group sales.
Most Choice: Flex Ticketsexpand_more
Guests looking for the flexibility of visiting Jack O’Lantern Extravaganza during any night or time of the festival may purchase a Flex ticket. Flex tickets are single-use, flat-rate tickets that are good for any operating night at any time 6:30 – 9:30 p.m. These premium-priced $28 tickets offer options for guests with evolving plans.
No discounts are available for Flex tickets. Ticket price applies as a flat rate to guests of all ages over 24 months.

Arriving at the Zoo & Sunset Showtime
Children under 24 months old do not require tickets.
Guests arriving at the Zoo without Jack O’Lantern Extravaganza tickets may buy tickets on grounds if tickets remain available. Guests are encouraged to purchase tickets before arriving at the Zoo to guarantee availability.
The event will take place rain or shine. Tickets are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged. Only discounts listed on this page apply; no additional discounts are available.
Main admission gates will open nightly 30-45 minutes before the Jack O’Lantern trail opens at the top of the park. For best experience, showtime for the Jack O’Lantern trail begins nightly at sundown. Early arriving guests may experience wait times. Guests will experience attractions, displays, and activities along the Savanna path to and from the trail.
Experience & Getting Around
Animals will not be visible in their public habitat during evening hours. To see all Zoo animals, you may also plan a daytime visit. Please note that the Zoo will fully close for one hour between daytime operation and nighttime Jack O’Lantern Extravaganza.
While our basic dress code applies, guests are welcome to wear costumes to the Jack O’Lantern Extravaganza if they prefer. Zoo staff may ask guests to modify costumes for safety. Costumes are encouraged to be family-friendly and should enable safe movement through the Zoo. Teens and adults may not wear a costume that fully covers their face. For additional costume fun at the Zoo with trick-or-treating, check out our daytime ZooBoo.
The Jack O’Lantern trail itself is at the top of the park in The Islands and Jungle Odyssey and is approximately 1/3 mile. Guests will both enter and exit the trail from the Savanna path. The entire event loop is approximately two miles and includes uphill portions. Tram will not operate during this evening event. Guests looking to rent a wheelchair or electric scooter are highly advised to place an advanced reservation. Quantities are limited. Without a reservation, rentals are on a first-come, first-served basis and there is no guarantee of availability. To make a mobility assistance reservation, please call 412-365-6020.
The Jack O’Lantern Extravaganza is a highly sensory experience that includes colored lights and ambient sounds throughout the park. The Living Wall, the finale of the Jack O’Lantern Trail, features quickly flashing lights and loud music. A sign is posted before entry to this section. Guests with sensitivities may like to check out a sensory bag at our admission gates.
