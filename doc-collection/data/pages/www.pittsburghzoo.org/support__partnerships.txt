How does EITC impact our community?expand_more
The Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium has been a cherished institution in our region for more than 126 years, offering not only a space for families to connect with wildlife but also serving as a powerful educational resource. Our programs are designed to inspire a love for nature and wildlife conservation among visitors of all ages. However, we cannot achieve our goals alone – we rely on the generosity of organizations like yours to help us continue providing these valuable educational opportunities.
The EITC program provides a unique opportunity for businesses to allocate a portion of their tax dollars directly to support educational initiatives in our community. By designating your EITC contribution to the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium, you would be investing in the future of our region’s children and the sustainability of our environment. Your contribution will enable us to enhance our educational programs, create interactive exhibits, and provide valuable resources to schools and teachers, all while aligning with your corporate social responsibility goals.
How does the tax credit work?expand_more
The Educational Improvement Tax Credit (EITC) program, administered by Pennsylvania’s Department of Community and Economic Development (DCED), encourages strategic philanthropy by allowing companies to distribute their tax dollars to organizations like the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium support the important work of our schools through our programming.
Participating in the EITC program allows your company to potentially receive a Pennsylvania tax credit of up to 90% of your contribution to the Pittsburgh Zoo. When factoring in the impact on federal taxes, your contribution can result in nearly a 100% benefit for your company.
It’s important to note that the EITC program offers a tax credit, not just a tax deduction, to businesses approved by EITC. To qualify, you need to provide proof of a contribution to the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium equivalent to 75% of the total amount donated during the tax year. Furthermore, a tax credit equal to 90% of the total amount contributed is granted if you commit in writing to contribute the same amount for two consecutive years.
Getting started is easy!expand_more
If you are a Pennsylvania business, the Educational Improvement Tax Credit (EITC) program could be beneficial to you. By participating in the program, you would receive a substantial tax credit and impact the community through your support of the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium.
