The Tropical Forest and Aquarium will be closed for this event. Water's Edge will remain open for access to restrooms.
Price
$45
Enjoy a night of music in the park! Rock the night away at this special preview of our new concert series. Stroll through the park and enjoy the cool, late autumn air while you are entertained by a variety of musicians, bands, and performers. We’ll also feature autumnal delights like specialty cocktails, spiked hot beverages, and cozy firepits perfect for roasting s’mores.
Guests are welcome to bring a blanket or camp chairs for cozy fall vibes as you watch the musical acts. Most acts are outdoors, so bundle up for a night under the stars!
Entertainment for the evening will include:
Three main acts playing in The Village, Jambo Plaza, and Water’s Edge Pavilion
Additional acts stationed around the Zoo
Fall-themed activities including a Rock ‘n’ Roar music trivia scavenger hunt
S’mores-making stations and fire pits
Autumnal beverage bar with specialty cocktails and hot drink spiking options available for purchase
Autumnal menu items in our cafés available for purchase
Please note that animals will not be visible in their public habitat during evening hours.
All guests at this event must be 21+. Please have your ID ready when you arrive.
