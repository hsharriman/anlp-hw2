THANK YOU, STEELERS NATION!
On behalf of the Pittsburgh Steelers, thank you to everyone who helped make the 2026 NFL Draft a safe, accessible, enjoyable, and record-breaking event. From our partners at Visit PITSBURGH and the NFL to our elected officials, public, private, non-profit and community partners, this was truly a team effort.
Most of all, thank you to the fans—from Pittsburgh and beyond—whose passion and energy made this event unforgettable, setting a new NFL Draft attendance record with 805,000 people over three days!
The Draft showcased our city at its very best. We were honored to share Pittsburgh with the world.
