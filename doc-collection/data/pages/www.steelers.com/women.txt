Pittsburgh Steelers Women | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com
Women of Steel represents the powerful and passionate female fans who make up the heart of Steelers Nation. As part of the NFL's largest female fanbase, these women celebrate their love for the Black and Gold through every game, every moment, and every generation.
With special events, tailored content, and shared traditions, Women of Steel reflects the strength, spirit, and loyalty of the women who support the Pittsburgh Steelers.

PNC SMALL BUSINESS SPOTLIGHT
In Pittsburgh, small businesses are the backbone of our region, representing the determination we have to offer.
In partnership with PNC, the Women of Steel Small Business Spotlight program will receive support from executives at PNC and the Pittsburgh Steelers, providing mentorship to women-owned small businesses while utilizing the Steelers platform to bring awareness to them.​
Both organizations believe in a world where women transform companies and push society forward. This program is finding ways to support females, drive them to the top, and keep them climbing.​
2026 Honorees
Mercearia Brasil
Owner: Keyla Nogueira
Keyla Nogueira is a self-described "chefpreneur" — chef plus entrepreneur — because, as she puts it, "I work just as hard on the business as the food, and there isn't one without the other." She was the proud co-owner and head chef of the beloved Casa Brasil, where she spent years sharing the authentic flavors of her home with the city.
Her journey continued by founding Mercearia Brasil, a Brazilian kitchen and market that has grown into a vibrant space where customers can discover traditional groceries, enjoy Keyla's cooking, and connect with the culture. Today, from its home in Allison Park, the store serves both the Brazilian community and the wider Pittsburgh region through food, catering, and cultural events.
Through every chapter of her work, Keyla has remained committed to preserving her roots while creating something that belongs to the community she now calls home. Her entrepreneurial journey reflects resilience, reinvention, and her belief that a business can be more than a place of commerce—it can be a place where people feel seen, welcomed, and connected.
At the heart of everything she creates is what Keyla simply calls Brazilian Soul.
Burgh Bits and Bites Food Tour
Owner & Founder: Sylvia McCoy
Sylvia McCoy is a proud first-generation Pittsburgher with roots in Switzerland and Germany. Fluent in English, German, and Swiss-German, she spent nearly a decade living and working in Switzerland.
Upon returning to Pittsburgh, Sylvia brought her international perspective back home, working as Assistant Director for Business Development at the University of Pittsburgh's Katz Graduate School of Business and volunteering as a tour guide with the Pittsburgh History & Landmarks Foundation. Inspired by her passion for local history, culture, and cuisine, she founded 'Burgh Bits & Bites Food Tours in 2008. Burgh Bits and Bites has been ranked as a TripAdvisor Travelers' Choice and is among their top 10% of listings. Most recently, Burgh Bits and Bites was awarded as a top ten food tour in the US by USA Today.
Today, Sylvia lives just outside the city with her husband and two children. She remains deeply involved in the community, serving on the boards of the Swiss American Society of Pittsburgh, Swiss Room at the Cathedral of Learning as well as former committee member for Destination Greater Pittsburgh. Her work has earned her recognition as a Pittsburgh Magazine "40 Under 40" honoree, a "2011 Legends of the Strip" awardee, a "2015 Sheraton Celebration of Champions" recipient and a two time Butler County tourism grant awardee.
Sylvia believes in taking nothing for granted—only with gratitude.
Franklin on Penn Weddings
Founder & Creative Director: Melissa Crawford
Melissa Crawford is the founder and creative director of Devoted to You and Franklin on Penn, two brands redefining the wedding and event experience through a modern and distinctly non-traditional lens. She began her career with nothing more than a dream, a single Craigslist post and an unwavering belief in her ability to curate events that challenge convention and elevate authenticity. With no formal training, she built her expertise through hands-on experience and relentless determination and passion.
Melissa is recognized for her non-traditional aesthetic, meticulous attention to detail and a bold approach going against the grain. Her work centers on creating inclusive, intentional spaces where every couple feels seen, respected and celebrated. She has been featured on countless wedding blogs as well as in local and national magazines for her work in the wedding industry. She has also led workshops, developed a mentorship program for aspiring wedding planners and Empower YOU, which is a retreat for women owned business within the wedding industry.
Her leadership blends professionalism with an intuitive, detail orientated style, guiding teams and clients with clarity, confidence and purpose. As she continues to grow her companies and her team, Melissa remains dedicated to making a meaningful difference in the wedding industry-crafting refined, memorable events that honor individuality, embrace innovation and celebrate love in all forms.
Dance on the Go, LLC
Founder & Owner: Carol A. Fisher
Carol A. Fisher is the Founder and Owner of Dance on the Go, LLC, a Pittsburgh-area mobile soul line dance and wellness company that brings engaging dance experiences directly to businesses, senior communities, community organizations, private clients, and special events.
With more than 11 years of experience as a line dancer and approximately 8 years of teaching experience, Carol has developed a reputation for providing patient, encouraging, and easy-to-follow instruction. Her teaching philosophy is centered on creating a welcoming, judgment-free environment where participants can learn, move, socialize, and have fun—regardless of their previous dance experience.
Carol's journey into line dancing began more than a decade ago. After attending two line dance classes and initially deciding that line dancing was not for her, she later explored urban ballroom and Chicago steppin'. When she returned to line dancing, it became a passion. That experience helped shape her belief that people often need encouragement, repetition, and a supportive instructor—not prior dance experience—to discover the joy of dancing.
Through Dance on the Go, Carol combines movement, music, wellness, confidence-building, and community connection. Her programs are designed for a variety of settings, including corporate and employee wellness programs, senior communities, community events, private parties, festivals, and group activities.
Carol is also recognized for her community involvement. She has provided instruction through senior centers and community organizations and has participated in numerous events throughout the Pittsburgh region. The City of Pittsburgh's Homewood Senior Center has recognized her as a community leader and described her as a dance instructor who promotes healthy living and joyful movement through line dance.
Carol's mission is simple: bring the dance floor to you. Through Dance on the Go, she helps people become more active, confident, connected, and joyful—one step at a time.
2025 Honorees
Knotzland
Founder/Lead Desginer: Nisha Blackwell
In August of 2014, we were founded by Nisha Blackwell, who was let go during the downsizing of a local coffee shop and decided to brew her own ideas. Spurred by a passion, the world around her, and building an environmentally friendly business - Knotzland became a reality. Some of our favorite partners and places to source and rescue high-quality pieces are The Pittsburgh Center for Creative Reuse, T's Upholstery, Ultra Fabrics, Drape Companies, Other designers in our Sewical Network, and the Minnesota and Pittsburgh Operas. The fabric is truly the first step in creating circular products. Knotzland has grown a powerhouse up-cycling business with goals of cultivating ecosystems that support people and the environment, all while using items that already exist in the Universe- all over the world. Knotzland is dedicated to sourcing and rescuing high-quality materials, and we enjoy the process of repurposing our found treasure into creative pieces that are unique to our customers' styles. Our loving approach to design emphasizes quality over quantity as we carefully locate, repurpose, and create locally-crafted custom pieces.

Kelli Burns DJ Entertainment
Founder: Kelli Burns
Burns is more than a DJ and entertainer — she is a creator of experiences, transforming ideas into unforgettable celebrations. From luxurious weddings and milestone mitzvahs to movie wrap parties and celebrity gatherings, Kelli brings a personal touch, collaborating closely with clients and planners to ensure each event is seamless, stress-free, and memorable.
Inspired by growing demand, Kelli expanded her vision into Kelli Burns Entertainment (KBE) — a powerhouse team of talented DJs who share her passion for precision, creativity, and entertainment magic. Known for producing events that pulse with energy and leave lasting impressions, KBE is the trusted choice for Pittsburgh Steeler legends, including 2024 Hall of Famer Alan Faneca and other greats like Justin Strzelczyk, as well as top corporations and families seeking polished, heartfelt celebrations.
At the core of Kelli's work is her genuine love for people. She listens closely, customizes each event, and goes the extra mile to make clients feel celebrated. Whether producing multi-year events like Girls Night Out with nationally recognized female recording artists or setting the perfect vibe at Seven Springs and Nemacolin Woodlands, Kelli creates experiences that are exciting, meaningful, and fun.
Deeply committed to her community, Kelli mentors up-and-coming DJs and event professionals while supporting nonprofits such as the Humane Animal Rescue League, UPMC Magee-Women's Hospital, P.O.W.E.R., A Glimmer of Hope, The Mario Lemieux Foundation, and Phipps Conservatory.
Guided by joy, connection, and celebration, Kelli ensures that every event — through her personal touch or KBE's signature team — leaves guests with memories they carry long after the final note.
2024 Honorees
Stories Like Me
Helen Campbell / Co-Owner and CEO
Helen Campbell (she/her) and Imogen Campbell Hendricks (she/her) - co-owners, business partners, and mom and daughter team - are thrilled to be selected as Women of Steel.
Stories Like Me is an indie bookstore for children through young adults, a community hub, and a safe space where all people can find themselves represented in books. To build their business, Helen and Imogen drew on their love of stories, along with their skills as project management professionals and musicians (musicians are very detail-oriented!). Before opening their brick-and-mortar store in Greenfield in November 2022, they did small pop-ups all throughout Pittsburgh to bring diverse stories into the community.
Helen, a native of England began her career in film and TV, after which she worked as a project manager, brings all her Jedi/Mary Poppins-type super powers to making sure all kids and families can see themselves in stories. Imogen, named after a character from Shakespeare, reads everything voraciously, writes, organizes, and loves trying out boutique gins!
They find their joy and passion through finding stories for all of our kids and families.
Fierce Knockouts
Karin Christoff / Owner
Karin, a proud mother of two teenage boys and married to a fellow veteran, brings a unique journey to her business. Originally from Peru, she arrived in the U.S. with no English skills but, through much sacrifice, learned by attending night school. She served as a U.S. Army soldier and was deployed to Afghanistan. She earned her bachelor's in information technology and MBA after she was honorably discharged from active duty.
Inspired by her personal experiences during the pandemic, Karin founded Fierce Knockouts to address a gap in workout apparel for women. She recognized that many women gain weight around their midsection, yet most clothes are designed for an ideal body shape. By creating beautiful, confidence-boosting workout clothes, Karin aims to make busy women feel amazing whether they're working out, running errands, or doing chores at home. Her mission is to empower and uplift women, one outfit at a time.
Mele’s Gifts & Party Planning
Jarmele E. Fairclaugh / Gift Creator & Owner
Founded in 2019 by Gift Creator and Owner Jarmele E. Fairclaugh, Mele's Gifts & Party Planning emerged from a deeply personal journey. What began as a heartfelt tradition of creating custom gifts for Jarmele's beloved grandmother, who resided in a nursing facility, has grown into a thriving business dedicated to celebrating life's special moments.
Jarmele's mission started with simple acts of love—gift-wrapping chocolates, clothing, and shoes to bring a smile to her grandmother's face. Her grandmother, a cherished beacon of joy and strength in the family following the passing of Jarmele's mother, inspired the creation of Mele's Gifts & Party Planning.
Following her grandmother's passing, Jarmele's family—her son Michael and husband Mark—encouraged her to turn her passion into a business. They established a website and business cards, and with their support, Mele's Gifts & Party Planning was born.
Today, Mele's Gifts & Party Planning is proud to be part of a vibrant community of woman-owned businesses and is honored to be recognized as a Woman of Steel by the Pittsburgh Steelers and PNC Bank. We specialize in custom-created gifts and exceptional event planning, dedicated to turning your ideas into reality and making every occasion memorable.
Celebrate with us, and let's bring your vision to life!
The Colombian Spot
Karen Perdomo / Owner
Karen Perdomo, a Colombian and New Jersey native, with a background in school business administration, opened The Colombian Spot, a family business, in the former Pittsburgh Public Market in October of 2015 introducing Arepas in the city of Pittsburgh for the first time. Starting as a small business with a limited space and a limited menu, it grew into a full service restaurant in the South Side neighborhood and ever since, this has been the Latin spot for many families in a time where Latin American restaurants were non-existent in the city . Later in 2022, a second location opened in the Oakland area near Pitt campus offering take out options for students and professionals.
While Empanadas and Arepas are the signature items on the menu, The Colombian Spot offers a diverse Latin American fusion menu, and catering services. Karen's leadership, strategy, organization, discipline, and vision for the business, has developed many growing opportunities, including catering services for corporate and professional baseball teams, as well as community affiliations with Latin organizations and other local neighborhood organizations.

2023 Honorees
Makeshift Bikes
Aryn Gaslowitz | Founder
Makeshift Bikes is a small, scrappy bike repair shop and community space in the Northside that was founded by Aryn Gaslowitz in 2017. In addition to selling and repairing bicycles and promoting their use, Makeshift is focused on sustainability, creative reuse, and hands-on teaching.
While this is their first year operating as a full service brick and mortar shop, Makeshift has existed in various capacities. Beginning in a garage in Perry Hilltop and then as a pop-up in the Allegheny Commons Park, Makeshift has, at every stage, been dedicated to serving the Northside and helping anyone interested in learning more about bike maintenance--especially those who have been underrepresented in the typically male-dominated spaces of cycling and mechanics.

Open Space Counseling, Consulting & Wellness​
Samantha Goldberg, MSW, LCSW | Founder & Executive Director​ ​
Founded in 2018 by Executive Director Samantha Goldberg, Open Space Counseling, Consulting and Wellness is a LGBTQIA+ affirming and racial justice-oriented mental health care group providing services across the state of PA, with a mission to help people move from merely surviving to truly thriving. Open Space CCW specializes in serving those that are part of marginalized communities or feel "other," and creating an affirming, safe space for both their clients and their employees.​
When Samantha, a social worker by trade, began seeing first-hand the serious lack of LGBTQIA+ competent and affirming mental health services available to the LGBTQIA+ community, and witnessed more and more colleagues experiencing inequity and burnout, the idea to transform Open Space CCW from her solo practice into a group practice was born.​
