Welcome to the home of Steelers Nation, where every touchdown, every season, and every tradition tells a story. Built on history, dedication and unyielding fan passion, this page is dedicated to our fans, no matter where you wave your Terrible Towel.
STEELERS NATION
The Terrible Towel
The Terrible Towel is a symbol of Steelers Nation, created by Myron Cope, recognized around the world. Since 1975, waving the iconic gold towel has united fans in support of the team, turning every game into a historic experience.
Renegade
Renegade, the iconic Styx song, is the ultimate game day hype anthem for Steelers fans since the early 2000's. Played at crucial moments in the stadium, it gets the crowd on their feet, uniting everyone at Acrisure Stadium, creating an electrifying atmosphere.
Steely McBeam
Meet Steely McBeam, the Pittsburgh Steelers mascot! Making his debut in 2007, Steely embodies the Pittsburgh and Irish culture with his namesake. He creates fun, passion, and spirit of Steelers Nation everywhere.
