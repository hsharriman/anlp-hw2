Steelers Donation Requests
The Pittsburgh Steelers are committed to supporting a wide range of charities in our community. Our goal is to accommodate as many requests as possible. Please keep in mind that this platform is used to evaluate charitable donation requests only.
Please be sure to read the guidelines below before submitting your request.
Please submit requests at least 6 (six) weeks prior to the event date to be considered.
Requests for game ticket donations will not be accepted.
We do not accept unsolicited items and will not assume responsibility for items mailed to the facility.
Employee recognition events, company picnics, and family reunions will not be accepted.
Organizations are only eligible to receive one (1) donation per calendar year.
We do not donate to individual fundraisers for national campaigns (i.e. United Way, American Cancer Society, Relay for Life, etc.) because we contribute to those charities directly.
Submission of this application does not guarantee a donation. If your donation request is approved, you will receive an email regarding the status of your request (due to the large number of requests, we do not accept phone calls or emails regarding the status of a particular request).
