Website Accessibility Statement
Updated: May 30, 2025
The Pittsburgh Steelers (Club) has an ongoing commitment to achieving the highest levels of satisfaction for all aspects of its fans' online experiences. As part of these efforts, the Club is dedicated to offering a quality online experience at is website https://www.steelers.com (the "Website") to fans with disabilities that is full and equal to that provided to fans without disabilities.
To assist in achieving the aforementioned accessibility goals with respect to the Website, the Club has committed to digital assets being designed, developed, and operated in substantial conformance with generally-recognized and accepted guidelines and/or standards for online accessibility (the "Standards"). While these Standards may change and/or evolve over time, they are currently the World Wide Web Consortium's Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2 at Levels A and AA ("WCAG 2.2").
As part of these efforts, the National Football League has engaged experienced accessibility consultants, including the National Center for Accessible Media at WGBH ("NCAM"). NCAM provides training to necessary individuals (including in the Club) on an ongoing basis regarding accessible website design, development, and maintenance. Furthermore, our Website will continue to be assessed on a recurring basis from both an engineering and user-experience basis (including the use of assistive technology (such as screen readers and screen magnifiers), in order to provide feedback to the Club regarding substantial conformance with the WCAG.
As the Website is constantly evolving, please be aware that our efforts are ongoing. If, at any time, you have any specific questions, feedback, or concerns about the accessibility of any particular web pages on the Website, please contact us through accessibility@nfl.com. If you do encounter an accessibility issue, please be sure to specify the Web page/URL/other detail in your email, and we will make all reasonable efforts to address your concerns.
Please be advised that the accessibility@nfl.com email address is limited to processing accessibility feedback and/or questions related to NFL digital assets only and is not able to respond to questions related to other NFL live event or team specific inquiries. Feedback and/or questions (including regarding other accessibility topics) related to specific team or live event issues should be directed to the individual team or to experience.support@nfl.com.
