All 162 regular season Pirates games can be heard on SportsRadio 93.7 THE FAN or 100.1 FM/KDKA-AM 1020 in Pittsburgh and throughout the region on the Pirates Radio Network. The Pirates flagship radio station, SportsRadio 93.7 THE FAN, will air extended local pre-game and post-game coverage before and after every broadcast.
