WALK-UP TOURS
There will be no walkup tours on Sundays or days of a weekday/Saturday game. There will also be no tours in the event of special ballpark activities (such as concerts, festivals, etc.).
Daily tour highlights may include:
Legacy Hall, Warning Track, Bullpens, Pop’s Plaza, Pittsburgh Baseball Club Level, Premium Suites and Press Box, Batting Cages, and Dugouts. All areas are subject to change without notice.
PNC Park is now cashless therefore we only accept credit cards, debit cards or gift cards (gift cards will only be accepted for tour ticket purchases.)
Please use the button below to check times for available walkup dates and to purchase tickets.
If you would like to speak with our Tour personnel, please call the PNC Park Hotline at 412-325-4700 or e-mail us at pncparktours@pirates.com.
