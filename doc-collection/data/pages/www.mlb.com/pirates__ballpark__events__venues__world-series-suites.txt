World Series Suites
PNC Park World Series Suites provide an All-Star setting for entertaining clients, colleagues or family and friends. These suites are located down the left-field line on the Pittsburgh Baseball Club Level. It's the perfect location to host a business meeting, with locations for various breakout sessions.
World Series Suites are available for 20-40 people for non-game day events.
