RE/MAX SELECT REALTY HOME PLATE CLUB
Know Before You Go Information
The RE/MAX Select Realty Home Plate Club entrance is located on West General Robinson Street.
The dining room will open two (2) hours prior to game time, and dining service will end an hour after first pitch.
Enjoy all that PNC Park has to offer, pre-game dining room tables are for 90-minute experiences.
Enjoy the Grab & Go Area, behind your seats, for in-game food and beverages.
In-seat beverage service will be offered throughout the game.
For the very best experience, please use our gameday guide for information regarding: bag policy, digital ticketing, and other important items to know before coming to PNC Park.

Dining Room Table Reservations
Each RE/MAX Select Realty Home Plate Club ticket includes a pre-game dining experience. Reservations are strongly suggested for all parties. Tickets for RE/MAX Select Realty Home Plate Club are required for dining room entry.
