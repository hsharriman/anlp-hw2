The Great American Banana Split Celebration is one of the largest and most anticipated events in our region — filled with live entertainment, family activities, vendors, and of course, the iconic banana splits. But behind every scoop, stage, and smiling guest is a team of dedicated volunteers making it all happen.
This year, we’re inviting you to join us. Whether you can give a few hours or want to be deeply involved, there’s a role for you — from guest services and activity stations to set-up, clean-up, and everything in between.
Why volunteer?
Be part of a high-energy, feel-good community tradition
Meet new people and expand your network
Support a free event that brings joy to thousands of families
Make a visible difference in your community
Volunteering at the Great American Banana Split Celebration isn’t just about helping, it’s about being part of something bigger than yourself. Spots will fill as we get closer to the festival, so we encourage you to sign up early.
