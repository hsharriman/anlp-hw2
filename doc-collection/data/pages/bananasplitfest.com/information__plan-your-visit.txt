Make it a Banana Split Weekend
Why visit for a day when you can make it a whole weekend?
Start your morning with breakfast and coffee, spend the day enjoying the Great American Banana Split Celebration, then explore everything our community has to offer. Stay local, dine local, and discover why Latrobe is more than just the birthplace of the banana split!
From unique dining experiences and local shops to family-friendly attractions, museums, and outdoor adventures, you can fill an entire weekend with fun while supporting local businesses.
Plan your getaway, book your stay, and get ready for the sweetest weekend of the year!
