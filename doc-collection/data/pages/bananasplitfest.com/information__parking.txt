Parking
Parking so easy, it's bananas!
Where to Park
Enjoy free parking at non-reserved spots throughout the city of Latrobe during the event. However, if you would like to visit Latrobe following the event, payment instructions can be found below.
Parking
Choose from the many on-street spots, surface lots, or the parking garage in downtown Latrobe.
How To Pay
All of our parking spots are equipped with coin-fed meters. Additionally, you can pay electronically through the Pango App.
Where is parking available?
If you are looking for a place to park, no need to worry! These lots will provide free parking and plenty of spaces.
300 Chestnut Street Parking
Latrobe Municipal Parking Garage (1099 Court Yard Plaza)
Latrobe Municipal Parking Lot (309 Weldon Street)
Latrobe Station (329 McKinley Avenue)
