Banana Splits Across America
Help us make history, one split at a time!
Let's Take the Banana Split to All 50 States!
Join us as we celebrate America’s 250th birthday by taking the Great American Banana Split across all 50 states.
How to Participate
1. Make a donation of $5 or more
Support the Great American Banana Split Celebration by clicking the button below.
2. Get your official banana split cutout
We’ll mail you an official Great American Banana Split Celebration cutout, ready to travel with you.
3. Take a photo on your adventure
Bring your banana split cutout to a famous landmark, hometown welcome sign, scenic overlook, favorite attraction or anywhere your travels take you.
4. Send us your photo
Email your photo to info@bananasplitfest.com with the city and state where it was taken. Bonus points for posting on social media and tagging us!
5. Follow the journey
We’ll feature submitted photos on our website and social media as we track our progress toward reaching all 50 states.
Click here to get started!
Every submitted photo will be entered into random prize drawings throughout the campaign for fun banana-themed merchandise and Great American Banana Split Celebration swag!
