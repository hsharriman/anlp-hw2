Congratulations to our 2026 Banana Split Princess and Court
Our 2026 Banana Split Princess is Leah (center) the court includes Clara (left) and Avril (right).
We’re thrilled to announce the newly crowned Banana Split Princess and her court! These outstanding young representatives captured hearts with their confidence, charm, and community spirit, and they’ll proudly serve as ambassadors for the Great American Banana Split Celebration throughout the year.
Join us in congratulating this incredible group as they take on appearances, events, and sweet responsibilities that celebrate the legacy and fun of our beloved festival. Be sure to say hello when you see them out and about—and help us support them as they represent our community with pride!
