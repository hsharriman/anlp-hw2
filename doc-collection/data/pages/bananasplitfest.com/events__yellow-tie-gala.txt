Latrobe Art Center Yellow Tie Gala
Kick off Banana Split Celebration weekend in style!
An Evening of Art, Community & Banana Splits
The Yellow Tie Gala is the official kickoff event for the Great American Banana Split Celebration weekend. Hosted by the Latrobe Art Center, this annual fundraiser brings together community members, local leaders, and supporters for an unforgettable evening filled with entertainment, food, drinks, and fun.
Guests are encouraged to wear semi-formal attire or something yellow as they enjoy a unique celebration inspired by Latrobe’s famous banana split tradition.
Registration Required by Friday, August 14th! Tickets are limited and advance registration is required.
