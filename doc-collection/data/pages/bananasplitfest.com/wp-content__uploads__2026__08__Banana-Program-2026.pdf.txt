will showcase photos from all 50 states, demonstrating just
how far one small-town invention has traveled.
As an added incentive, every submitted photo will be
entered into random prize drawings throughout the cam-
paign for banana-themed merchandise and official Great
American Banana Split Celebration swag.

Warriors Rock Veterans’ Tribute
to close the celebration with a
powerful tribute to America’s heroes
The Great American Banana Split Celebration will
conclude on a memorable and meaningful note Sunday as
Warriors Rock takes the stage as this year’s headline perfor-
mance. More than a concert, Warriors Rock is an unforget-
table patriotic production that blends live music with the
personal stories of military veterans, creating an emotional
experience that celebrates service, sacrifice, and community.
What makes Warriors Rock truly unique is its commit-
ment to honoring local veterans. In the weeks leading up to
the celebration, veterans from our own community will be
interviewed and invited to share their military experiences
and the songs that carried special meaning during their ser-
vice. During the performance, those heartfelt stories will be
shown on the big screen, followed by a live performance of
each veteran’s chosen song by Gary Racan and the acclaimed
Studio-e Band. The result is a moving tribute that connects
music, memories, and the people who served our nation.

From Fossils to Feathers:
Fun Takes Flight at
Adams Memorial Library
Step into a prehistoric adventure at Adams Memorial Library!
In keeping with the summer reading program theme, Unearth a Sto-
ry, kids can play Plinko to win fossils and minerals, roll the dice to
create their very own dinosaur name, and decorate a library card
holder to take home. The library will be open from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.
on Saturday, August 22, with special activities throughout the day.
Adding to the fun, meet Homer—a real pigeon who wears pigeon
pants and walks on a leash—true story! Homer will visit the library from
10:30–11:30 a.m., giving visitors the chance to pet him, hold him, take
photos, and even see him fly. Pigeons can be misunderstood, but they are
beautiful, funny, intelligent, and make great pets. Come learn more about
these amazing birds and enjoy a one-of-a-kind experience with Homer!
