LOGO
The logo is a playful stylization of the dessert we
celebrate. The split sits atop the event’s full name
and birthplace both of which are curved upwards on
each end, as a banana would be.
While the split in the logo isn’t the most accurate
portrayal of the classic recipe, it is instantly
recognizable to a wide audience.
Full color logo mark. There is a white keyline border that
should always be around the full color version.

DIFFERENT FLAVORS
Annual variations
Additional imagery may be added to the celebration’s logo on a year-by-year basis for
accommodation of event sponsorships, tributes, of beneficiaries or the year. These should be done in
consultation with the associated image’s owners to conform to their own brand standards.
One color options
Instances where the full color logo isn’t appropriate for use, a single color option may be used. Single
color options do not have a border. Check for the most current annual variation to stay consistent
with the current year’s celebration.

LANGUAGE
It’s hard to be sad when you’re eating ice cream. Be
fun, funny, light, and clever when writing or talking
about the Banana Split Celebration.

TERMINOLOGY
The official event name is the Great American Banana Split Celebration.
Use the term celebration should be used instead of festival whenever possible.
Website and social media handles are the exception; for length,
the handle or URL should use BananaSplitFest.
Only for internal use may the event be shortened to GABSC.
For external use, the event may be shortened to Banana Split Celebration
only after being first introduced by the official name.
Modifiers may be added annually, based on sponsorship support of the event.
The celebration is an event, with multiple activities.
Naming conventions of the event and associated activities may have
modifiers based on sponsorship, beneficiary, or tribute.
