Tournament Rules
Game Play
Teams consist of two players. Partners stand at opposite boards on the same side of the court and throw toward each other.
Each team receives four bags of the same color. All eight bags begin at one end of the court.
A coin toss will determine which team throws first (has honors) in the opening round.
The player with honors begins play by throwing a bag toward the opposite board. Players may throw from anywhere behind the front edge of their board.
Players alternate throws until all eight bags have been thrown.
Any bag that hits the ground before landing on the board is considered a foul bag and must be removed from the playing surface.
The team that has honors will begin the next round from the opposite end of the court.
Play continues until one team reaches 21 points or more.
Scoring
3 points – Bag passes through the hole.
1 point – Bag comes to rest on the playing surface.
1 point – Bag is hanging into the hole without falling through.
1 point – Bag is hanging off the edge of the board without touching the ground.
0 points – Bag is touching the ground while resting on the board.
0 points – Bag is supported by another bag that is touching the ground, unless the supporting bag can be removed without causing the scored bag to fall.
Round Scoring (Cancellation Scoring)
At the end of each round, only the team with the higher score earns points.
The winning team receives the difference between the two teams’ scores for that round.
Example: Team A scores 7 points and Team B scores 4 points. Team A earns 3 points for the round.
The team that wins the round earns honors and throws first in the next round.
If both teams score the same number of points in a round, no points are awarded and honors remain with the team that threw first in the previous round.
