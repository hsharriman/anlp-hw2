Holidays At The City-County Building
MENORAH LIGHTING
DATE & TIME: Tuesday, December 8 - 5:00 PM
PLACE: City-County Building
Join Mayor Corey O'Connor and community leaders in lighting the giant 12-foot Menorah located in front of the City-County Building. The Menorah serves as a beacon of light, symbolizing freedom and peace for all. The celebration includes live music and Chanukah refreshments of latkes and donuts.
PITTSBURGH'S OFFICIAL CHRISTMAS TREE
Pittsburgh's official Christmas Tree was first erected at the construction site of the City-County Building in 1913. For more than a century, a beautiful live evergreen (the only live holiday tree Downtown) has graced the front steps of our seat of government. Visitors enjoy hundreds of larger-than-life ornaments, including Pittsburgh’s Coat of Arms designed by Wendell August Forge, and 90 distinct ornaments, each bearing the name of Pittsburgh's beloved neighborhoods. Be sure to spot your favorite!
TREE LIGHTING ON HIGHMARK LIGHT UP NIGHT®
The City of Pittsburgh kicks off the holidays on November 21 at its official tree lighting ceremony presented by Clearview as part of Highmark Light Up Night ®. Festivities along Grant Street include live music, light show, fireworks, food trucks, select artists and makers, games, and a one-of-a-kind gingerbread house display.
