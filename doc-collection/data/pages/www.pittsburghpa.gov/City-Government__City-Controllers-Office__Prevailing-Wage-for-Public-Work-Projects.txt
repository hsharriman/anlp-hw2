Prevailing Wage for Public Work Projects
Definition of Public Work
"Public work" includes the following:
Construction
Reconstruction
Demolition
Alteration and/or repair work other than maintenance work
Public work is done under contract and paid for in whole or in part out of the funds of a public body where the estimated cost of the total contract is more than $25,000. This does not include work performed under a rehabilitation or manpower training program.
Obligations of Contractors/Subcontractors
Every contractor and subcontractor shall post for the entire period of construction

The applicable prevailing minimum wages for each craft or classification of workers performing the public work contract
A statement advising workers that if they have been paid less than the prevailing wage rate for their work classification they may notify the City Controller and request an investigation.
If you believe that the prevailing wage obligations have not been met for a public work project within the City of Pittsburgh, please contact the City Controller’s Office at city_controller@pittsburghpa.gov or 412-255-2055.
Service Worker Prevailing Wage
The City Controller helps set service worker prevailing wage rates and enforce compliance for the service worker prevailing wage act.
The Office of Equal Protection provides guidelines, provides intake, and offers investigation services related to the Service Worker Prevailing Wage Act. Please visit the Office of Equal Protection’s page to learn more.
