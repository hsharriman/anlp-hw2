Schenley Skating Rink
Announcement
The rink is an ice surface from November through March and a roller surface from May through August.
The Schenley Park Ice Skating Rink opened in 1975 and has entertained ice and roller skaters, as well as hockey enthusiasts for a generation.

Public Skate Information
We have a 300 person maximum on skate sessions. Once we reach maximum capacity, admission is closed for the session.
Parents and guardians who are not participating in skating are still required to pay admission.
Admission is good for only one session.
No re-entry privileges.
No outside food and/or drinks allowed in the rink facility besides water.
ACCESSIBILITY: Skate aids are available at all sessions except for adult only, on a first come first serve basis.
Tickets
Online tickets are available up to 30 minutes before each session ($0.90 processing fee). Tickets may be purchased at the box office, too.
