Art on Braddock
The Department of Parks & Recreation presents “Art on Braddock,” a community event celebrating local arts on Saturday, May 16, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., free to enter. The event aims to enhance visibility and financial opportunities for local artists and chefs, transforming Frick Park and Regent Square into a vibrant hub with vendor booths along Braddock Avenue. Attendees can interact with artists, enjoy culinary samples, and engage in community activities such as youth baseball and yard sales. CitiParks Director Eric Sloan emphasized the event's role in fostering creativity and community connections.
