Dollar Bank Cinema in the Park
Grab a blanket or lawn chair and head out for an unforgettable evening of free cinema under the stars! Dollar Bank Cinema in the Park entertains throughout the summer in various park settings. Movies begin at dusk.
Return to your favorite movie location at Highland Park, Riverview Park, Schenley Park Flagstaff Hill, Schenley Plaza, Arsenal Park in Lawrenceville, Banksville Park, Brookline Memorial Park, Grandview Park in Mt. Washington, McBride Park in Lincoln Place, Ormsby Park in South Side, and West End/Elliott Overlook. Or enjoy a movie at new sites: Frankie Pace Cap Park.
For additional movie information, follow CitiParks Office of Special Events on @PghEventsOffice on X, @PghEventsOffice on Facebook or @PghEventsOffice on Instagram.
The Sunday, July 26, movie at Schenley Plaza – "Film Pittsburgh Presents: ReelAbilities Summer Shorts” – will screen a number of short films selected by representatives of Pittsburgh's disabled community. This special screening will serve as a preview of the upcoming ReelAbilities Film Festival. Audio descriptive services and additional accessibility needs can be requested by filling out the following form: CLICK HERE FOR ACCESSIBILITY FORM
Please Note: Inclement weather may cause cancellations. For cancellation updates follow follow CitiParks Office of Special Events (@pgheventsoffice) on X, Facebook or Instagram.
Dollar Bank Cinema in the Park is presented by the Citiparks Office of Special Events.
