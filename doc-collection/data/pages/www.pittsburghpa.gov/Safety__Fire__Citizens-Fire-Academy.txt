Citizens Fire Academy
The first ever Citizens Fire Academy will be September 2 - October 14 at the Fire Training Academy (1395 Washington Blvd).
Registration is now closed.
Join us for an exciting hands-on experience!
Learn firefighting techniques, emergency response, and how the City of Pittsburgh Fire Bureau serves and protects your community, all at no cost.
Dates | September 2 - October 14, 20266 - 9pm (Sept, 2, 9, 16, 23, Oct. 14)
9am - 4pm (Oct. 3 and 11)
Pittsburgh Fire Training Academy, 1395 Washington BLVD, Pittsburgh, PA
This program includes hands-on physical activities. Participants should be able to lift approximately 20 - 30 pounds and will be required to complete a medical form upon registration.
REQUIREMENTS:
Must 18+
Resident of Pittsburgh. Wilkinsburg, or Ingram
SPACE IS LIMITED
