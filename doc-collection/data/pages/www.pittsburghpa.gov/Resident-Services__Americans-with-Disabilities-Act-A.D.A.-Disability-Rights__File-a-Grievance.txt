File a Grievance
The Americans with Disabilities Act is a federal civil rights law that provides people with disabilities equal access to employment (Title I), state and local government programs (Title II), and goods and services (Title III). The City of Pittsburgh is committed to making City programs, services, and facilities accessible to all people.
The City’s Grievance Procedure, established to comply with Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act (A.D.A.), may be used to allege discrimination on the basis of disability in the City of Pittsburgh’s provision of programs, services, or facilities.
A grievance should be submitted via the form below by the grievant or their designee as soon as possible, but no later than 60 calendar days after the alleged violation.
After receipt of a grievance, the City’s A.D.A. Coordinator will communicate with the grievant to discuss the complaint and possible solutions. The ADA Coordinator will respond in an accessible format, explaining the City’s position and offering options to resolve the grievance.
If the response by the A.D.A. Coordinator does not resolve the issue to grievant’s satisfaction, the grievant or their designee may appeal the decision of the A.D.A. Coordinator to the Manager of the Office of Equal Protection, who will communicate with the grievant in an attempt to resolve the complaint. Afterwards, the Manager of the Office of Equal Protection will respond in a format accessible to the grievant with a final resolution of the complaint.
The City of Pittsburgh will keep all written complaints, appeals, and Office of Equal Protection responses thereto for at least three years.
Grievance Form
This form is only for grievances pursuant to Title II - Public Services of the A.D.A. If you have previously requested and believe you have been wrongly denied accommodations under Title II, please complete the form below.
Please note:
This grievance procedure is for reporting allegations that the City of Pittsburgh government has violated Title II of the A.D.A. It is not for reporting complaints against private landlords, private businesses, other government agencies, or other non-City entities, and such requests will not be processed.
City employees seeking workplace accommodations pursuant to Title I of the A.D.A. should contact Human Resources.
If you believe that your rights have been violated under any title of the A.D.A., please contact the City’s Commission on Human Relations.
Instructions
Please fill out the form completely. Once you have done so, please hit “submit.” If a required field does not apply, please enter “N/A.” If you do not know the information under required filed, please enter “unknown.” If this form is inaccessible to you due to disability, a reasonable alternative for submission will be made available upon request to ADA@pittsburghpa.gov.
