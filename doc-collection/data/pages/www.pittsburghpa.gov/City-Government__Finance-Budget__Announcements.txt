June 5th, 2026 TREASURER'S SALE Postponed until June 26th, 2026
The City of Pittsburgh has postponed the Treasurer’s Sale previously set for June 5th, 2026.
The Treasurer’s Sale will now take place on June 26th, 2026, at 10:00 am in the City-Council Chambers on the 5th Floor of the City-County Building. Advertisements for the sale will be run in the Pittsburgh Legal Journal and the Pittsburgh New Courier on June 10th and June 17th.
