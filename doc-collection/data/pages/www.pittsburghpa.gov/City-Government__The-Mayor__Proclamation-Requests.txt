Proclamation Requests
Thank you for your interest in requesting a proclamation from the Office of Mayor Corey O’Connor. Proclamations are issued to recognize significant events, milestones, and contributions within the City of Pittsburgh.
Please read before submitting:
Requests must be submitted at least six (6) weeks in advance of the date needed. Late submissions may not be accommodated.
Please provide as much detail as possible to support your proclamation request. Incomplete submissions may result in delays or may not be processed.
This form does not accept attachments. Please copy and paste all relevant information directly into the fields below.
