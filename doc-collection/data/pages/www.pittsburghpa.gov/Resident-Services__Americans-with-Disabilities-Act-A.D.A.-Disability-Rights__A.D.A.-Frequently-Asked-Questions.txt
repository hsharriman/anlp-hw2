A.D.A. Frequently Asked Questions
What is the City of Pittsburgh A.D.A. Coordinator’s role?
The City of Pittsburgh A.D.A. Coordinator ensures that all citizens have the opportunity to participate meaningfully in the programs and services offered by the City of Pittsburgh. The A.D.A. coordinator's role bridges policy, infrastructure, and strategic planning to prevent discrimination and support the City's goals of increased inclusion and access across abilities.
I have an accessibility concern with the City of Pittsburgh.
File a Grievance
Make an Accommodations Request
I have an accessibility concern and live in Allegheny County, but not the City of Pittsburgh.
Contact the County A.D.A. Coordinator, Caylin Snyder, at:
Email: Caylin.Snyder@alleghenycounty.us.
Office Ph: 412-350-2649
I have a discrimination concern that occurred in Pittsburgh but is unrelated to the City of Pittsburgh.
Contact the Pittsburgh Commission on Human Relations, which investigates instances of discrimination and seeks resolutions for anyone who has experienced harm because of discrimination.
I would like to apply for an accessible residential parking space.
Please Dial 3-1-1 or 412-255-2621. The operator will ask a few questions to determine eligibility to apply for an accessible residential parking space.
I would like to have an accessible parking sign removed.
Either:
Submit a request, under "Parking", "Handicap Parking Signs, Removal"
Dial 3-1-1 or 412-255-2621.
