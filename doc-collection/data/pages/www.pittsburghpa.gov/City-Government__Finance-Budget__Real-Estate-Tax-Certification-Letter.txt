Real Estate Tax Certification Letter
Need a permit to do business in the City? Request a tax certification letter showing that you are up-to-date on your taxes.
Contact Us
For Real Estate Tax Certification Letters, please contact us:
412-255-2525
RealEstateTaxCerts@pittsburghpa.gov
Or visit:
City of Pittsburgh
Real Estate Division
414 Grant Street, 1st Floor
Pittsburgh, PA 15219
Tax Certificates typically take 5-7 business days to complete. Once completed, the City sends the requestor the certificates via the method specified in their initial request form. The City will not send the certificate any other way than what the requestor specified in their initial request form. If the account corresponding to the tax certificate requested has a significant number of delinquencies, liens, or any discrepancies associated with it, additional delays in completing the certificate may occur. If the City experiences a high volume of certificate requests, delays in completing the certificates within the 5-7 day timeframe may also occur.
