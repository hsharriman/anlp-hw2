Our Mission
The mission of the Pittsburgh Bureau of Fire is to safeguard lives, property, and the environment for the residents, visitors and businesses in our community. We achieve this through advanced fire suppression, emergency medical services, hazard mitigation, rescue operations, dynamic emergency management, disaster response and proactive domestic preparedness, while prioritizing diversity, equity, and inclusion in all our efforts. We are committed to leveraging the latest technologies, training, practices, and procedures to enhance response capabilities and delivery of our services.
