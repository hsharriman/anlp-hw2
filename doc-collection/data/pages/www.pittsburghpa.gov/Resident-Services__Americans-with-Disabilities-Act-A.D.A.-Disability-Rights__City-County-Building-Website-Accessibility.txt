City County Building & Website Accessibility
CCB Entrance
Accessible entrance to the City-County Building is located on the Ross Street side.
CCB Restrooms
An accessible women's restroom is available on the fifth floor, Grant Street side.
An accessible, all-genders restroom with baby-changing table is available on the 8th floor, Ross Street side.
