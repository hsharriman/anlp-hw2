Pistons in the Park Car Cruise-In
September 19, 2026, Noon - 4 p.m.
Allegheny Commons Park West, Northside (enter at Brighton Rd. and Ridge Ave.)
FREE
Rev up the end of summer with an afternoon of classic and tuner cars, trucks and street rods! Cruise into the park and enjoy car clubs, food trucks, vendors and more. FIRST 150 Arrivals recieve a free T-shirt!
2026 Trophy Categories:
WesBanco MVP (Chosen by WesBanco)
Best Accessories (Chosen by Skeptik Innovations)
Cleanest Detail (Chosen by Distinct Automotive Appearance)
Biggest Misfit (Chosen by Misfit Autowerks)
Most Likely to get a Speeding Ticket (Chosen by representative of Pittsburgh Public Safety)
Belongs in a Museum (Chosen by a representative of Pittsburgh Vintage Grand Prix)
Best Theme (Milk Truck, Army Jeeps, etc.)
Mayor's Choice (Chosen by Mayor Corey O'Connor's Office)
No registration required for awards. Winners announced during the final hour of the event.
