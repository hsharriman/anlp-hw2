What to Do If Your Tax Payment Is Returned by Mail
The United States Postal Service has made the City of Pittsburgh aware that, on occasion, United States Postal Service sorting equipment may malfunction, resulting in the USPS returning tax payments to the taxpayer.
If this occurs, please resubmit your payment by placing the original envelope—with the return label intact—inside a new envelope and remailing it.
The City will process the submission according to the postmark on the original envelope. The City will calculate and apply any applicable discounts, penalties, fees, etc. using that date.
Please note: the original envelope must be included for the City to honor the original mailing date. Without it, the claimed postmark date cannot be verified.

Real Estate Department Advisory
Taxpayers can pay their 2026 Annual Real Estate Tax Bills online at https://magic.collectorsolutions.com/magic-ui/en-US/OneTimePayment/pittsburgh
Click the link above or scan the QR code to pay by e‑Check.
It is only $0.50, which is cheaper than a stamp!!!
Taxpayers who are paying via installments should be receiving their 2026 3rd Installment Real Estate Tax bills the week of July 1st.The 3rd installment due date is July 31, 2026.
If you have any questions about your bill, please call us at (412) 255-2525 or email us at RealEstateTaxInfo@pittsburghpa.gov
During billings, our Real Estate Division may experience high call volumes and is working diligently to answer all communications. Please do not sit on hold but leave a detailed voicemail or contact us via email. Please note that a return call from the Real Estate Division may not be identified as such with Caller ID and may say "Bellevue".
Thank you for your patience!

Dog Licenses
A license is required for every dog, age three-months and older, living in the City of Pittsburgh. Dog licenses and registration tags help to assist in finding lost pets. Annual dog licenses are valid from January 1st through December 31st of each year.
