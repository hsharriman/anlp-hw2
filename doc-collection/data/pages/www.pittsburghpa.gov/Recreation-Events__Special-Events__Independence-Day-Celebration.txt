A Once in a Lifetime Celebration
In 2026, Pittsburgh joins communities across the nation in commemorating the 250th anniversary of the United States of America. Leading up to the City of Pittsburgh’s award-winning Independence Day Celebration, residents and visitors can experience a weeklong series of patriotic ceremonies, concerts, family activities, public art, and community events throughout the city thanks to our many civic partners. Explore this page for details on the City's Independence Day Celebration, the week long America 250 celebrations, and more!
Independence Day Celebration
In 2026 the City's Independence Day Celebration returns to Point State Park, Liberty Avenue, North Shore Great Lawn, and the Grandview Park Bandstand. Please note that some locations will be subject to a security screen.
Ferris Wheel
See Pittsburgh like never before July 1 - July 5 by taking a breathtaking ride above the city skyline on the Piatt Companies Salute to Service Wheel. Soar high above the festivities enjoying unforgettable views of the Golden Triangle, our rivers, and the 4th of July celebration below. With 50% of proceeds going to a veterans non-profit, tickets expected to sell quickly so secure your ride today!
Partner Events
The City is proud to partner with the Downtown Partnership, the Cultural Trust, National Flag Foundation, Heinz History Center, Ft. Pitt Museum, the County, VisitPittsburgh, the Symphony, Fort Pitt Blockhouse and America250 PA to bring you a week of programming.
Time Capsule
On June 4, 2026, Mayor O’Connor announced that a new time capsule will be sealed in the City-County Building and invited residents to share their hopes and ideas with the City Archives for what to put in the capsule for the Pittsburgh community 100 years from now. Ideas for “Pittsburgh’s Time Capsule of Tomorrow” can be submitted here from now until July 1, 2026.
Press & Awards
Named the Best July 4th Celebration in the Country by USA Today readers, Pittsburgh's Independence Day Celebration has become a nationally recognized destination for patriotic festivities. Discover the awards, media features, and stories that continue to put Pittsburgh in the national spotlight.
