Stewardship & Gardening
Adopt-A-Lot Program
This temporary land access program empowers residents to transform City-owned vacant lots into vibrant, community-serving spaces through temporary activation agreements. Vacant lot projects can provide greater access to fresh food, manage stormwater, and beautify communities.
City Farms Garden Program
City Farms facilitates land access to underutilized open space in City parks and greenways for community gardens, farms, and food forestry/orchards. This program also provides direct technical assistance and material support to urban agriculture sites within in the program.
Community Garden Plots
Gardeners of all experience levels are welcome to rent a plot and grow their own food in following Pittsburgh community gardens. City of Pittsburgh Community Gardens are available for City of Pittsburgh residents only.
Greenways Stewardship Program
Greenways are permanently conserved, primarily passive open spaces. Approved stewardship activities include: trail building, urban agriculture, tree planting/orchards, invasive species removal, litter/dump site clean-up, and more.
Help-A-Lot Program
Through Help-A-Lot, residents are able to request one-day access to City-owned vacant lots for community stewardship and maintenance activities including: mowing, weeding, mulching, removing invasive species, illegal dump site clean-up, and litter clean-up.
