Pittsburgh America 250 City Art Poster Project
Help tell Pittsburgh’s story.
The City of Pittsburgh is participating in the United States Conference of Mayors’ Freedom 250 City Art Poster Project and invites Pittsburgh artists of all ages and skill levels to create an original poster celebrating Pittsburgh’s place in the American story as the nation marks the 250th anniversary of the signing of the Declaration of Independence.
$10,000 in prizes | Submissions due Wednesday, October 14, 2026, at 5:00 p.m.
SUBMIT YOUR ARTWORK
Please review the eligibility, artwork, and project requirements below before submitting.
The Creative Challenge
Show us Pittsburgh—where we’ve been, who we are, and where we’re going.
What does Pittsburgh contribute to the American story—and what do you hope Pittsburgh will become over the next 250 years?
Submissions may explore Pittsburgh’s people, neighborhoods, history, landscape, industry, innovation, arts and culture, traditions, resilience, or any other aspect of the city through the artist’s own perspective.
Artists should not feel limited to familiar Pittsburgh landmarks or symbols. The City particularly welcomes original and unexpected interpretations of Pittsburgh’s identity and future.
Who Can Apply?
The project is open to:
Individuals who currently reside within the City of Pittsburgh; and
Groups or organizations located within the City of Pittsburgh.
Artists of all ages and skill levels are encouraged to participate. Individual and collaborative submissions are welcome.
Applicants under age 18 must submit with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.
Each individual, group, or organization may submit one work.
For a group or organizational submission, one person must serve as the primary contact. Any award associated with a group or organizational submission will be made to the organization rather than to individual participants.
Awards
$5,000 Grand Prize
One work will become Pittsburgh’s official Freedom 250 City Art Poster and represent Pittsburgh in the U.S. Conference of Mayors’ national project.
Five $1,000 Finalist Awards
Five additional works will be recognized by the City and celebrated publicly.
Awards are subject to final funding and required City approvals.
Artwork Requirements
Please review these requirements carefully before creating or submitting your poster.
The design must prominently incorporate “Pittsburgh” and “America 250.”
Artwork must be designed as a vertical 24" × 32" poster.
Artwork must be original, human-created visual art. AI-generated imagery will not be accepted.
Artwork must remain nonpartisan and may not include political messages or inflammatory language.
A broad range of original visual media is welcome, including painting, drawing/illustration, photography, printmaking, digital illustration/design, collage, mixed media, and other original visual art.
Artists working in physical media may submit a high-quality photograph or scan for initial review.
Consistent with the national project, artwork should celebrate local history, reflect community identity, and inspire hope for the future.
Final Production Specifications
Applicants do not need to submit a production-ready PDF for initial City review.
If selected as Pittsburgh’s official poster, the final artwork must meet the U.S. Conference of Mayors’ production requirements:
Finished artwork size: 24" wide × 32" high
Bleed: 0.25" on all sides
Overall artwork size with bleed: 24.5" × 32.5"
Safe area: Keep important content within 22" × 30"
Final file format: PDF
Scale: 100%
Resolution: Minimum 300 DPI
Color mode: CMYK (4-color process)
RGB images and spot colors must be converted to CMYK.
USCM will add its standardized branding bar containing the NEA, U.S. Conference of Mayors, and America 250 logos. Do not include this branding bar in your design.
For more information about the national project, visit: U.S. Conference of Mayors Freedom 250 City Art Poster Project
Use of Selected Artwork
If selected as Pittsburgh’s official submission, the artwork and related submitted materials will be provided to the U.S. Conference of Mayors and may be displayed, reproduced, published, and used in connection with the Freedom 250 City Art Poster Project. Participation in the national project includes permission for the National Endowment for the Arts to use submitted materials for promotional and educational purposes across media, in perpetuity. Additional City use/reprodction and public-records acknowledgments are included in the submission form.
What You’ll Need to Submit
The online submission form will require:
Your name or group/organization name;
Primary contact information;
City of Pittsburgh residential address for an individual applicant, or Pittsburgh address for a group/organization;
Pittsburgh neighborhood;
Artwork title and medium/materials;
A brief description of the work (maximum 150 words) explaining the story or idea behind it, its connection to Pittsburgh, and how it relates to the United States’ 250th Anniversary;
One digital image of the complete artwork; and
Required eligibility, originality, rights, content, use/reproduction, and public-records acknowledgments.
