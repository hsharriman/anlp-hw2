Letters of Support Requests
All requests for a letter of support from the Office of the Mayor are reviewed on a rolling basis by the Mayor and staff. If necessary, a follow up call may be scheduled to further discuss the project. Please allow an appropriate amount of time for approval of a letter of support in your project planning timeline.
