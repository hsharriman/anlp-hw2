Duquesne University Athletics
Jacob Davey joined the Duquesne Athletics media relations staff in March of 2023.
Davey arrived on the Bluff after serving as Sports Information Director at West Liberty University, where he was the primary contact for all 20 NCAA Division II sports. He promoted three Mountain East Conference championships and three NCAA Tournament appearances including a national finalist men's basketball team and a national champion wrestler. He also was a voting member for the NCBWA Division II Atlantic Region.
Previously, Davey was Assistant Sports Information Director (Intern) at his alma mater Pitt-Greensburg, helping promote 13 NCAA Division III sports and the school’s first women’s tennis conference title. A cum laude graduate of Pitt-Greensburg, Davey was an all-conference member of the Bobcat baseball team and gained experience in sports information throughout his collegiate career. He also is a member of the National Wiffle League Association Tournament Committee and facilitated ESPN broadcasts for the 2023 championships.
An active member of the College Sports Communicators (CSC) and the National Collegiate Acrobatics & Tumbling Association (NCATA) Awards Committee, Davey resides in Canonsburg, Pa.
