BEFORE DUQUESNE
Played 34 carer games over four seasons at three stops (Indiana 2022 & 23, Middle Tennessee 2024, Eastern Michigan 2025) ... Totaled 24 tackles and three pass breakups

Was an early enrollee in spring 2023 at Indiana ... Played two games and redshirted in 2023 - one of 15 true freshman on roster to see game action ... Played 11 games in 2023 with four tackles
