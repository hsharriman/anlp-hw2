Duquesne University Athletics
A senior in the Duquesne University Palumbo-Donahue School of Business, Louie Botti serves as an Offensive Student Assistant on the Duquesne football staff in 2026.
Botti spent the previous two seasons as a student assistant on the defensive staff, working with the defensive line and linebacker position groups.
A native of Pittsburgh, Botti is pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration degree with a double major in Finance and Business Analytics and a minor in Accounting. He is also a member of the Duquesne Asset Management Group and Phi Eta Sigma Honor Society.
