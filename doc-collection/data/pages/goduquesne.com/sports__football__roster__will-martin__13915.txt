BEFORE DUQUESNE
Standout two-way player at nearby Seton LaSalle Catholic High School
First Team All-Conference selection and Conference Defensive Player of the Year
Led Seton LaSalle to the 2025 WPIAL District Championship, scoring all three touchdowns out of the wildcat while adding an interception, four tackles, and two tackles for loss in the title game
Guided the Rebels to a PIAA State Semifinal appearance as a senior, finishing with 69 tackles, 3 sacks, 10 TFLs, 3 interceptions, 3 forced fumbles, 8 offensive touchdowns, and over 300 all-purpose yards
Committed to Duquesne over multiple FBS offers
Earned High Honors academically and was a member of the Future Business Leaders of America (FBLA)
Also competed in varsity basketball
PERSONAL
Born August 21, 2007 in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania
Son of Carrie and Alexander Martin
Youngest of three, with older sisters Alexandra and Drew
Mother, Carrie, played basketball at Davis & Elkins
