Duquesne University Athletics
Anthony Longo, who spent his first five-plus seasons as a strength and conditioning coach at Duquesne, was elevated to Director of Sports Performance in the winter of 2022.
Longo came to The Bluff after holding internships at both Robert Morris University and the University of Pittsburgh. He worked with Olympic sport athletes as well as the football and men's and women's basketball teams while at RMU.
A Steubenville native, Longo earned a bachelor of science in exercise science, wellness/physiology from Waynesburg in 2013 and a master of science in health and fitness from Pitt in 2014.
A four-year starter and three-year co-captain for Waynesburg's baseball team, he also served as a graduate student assistant in the University of Pittsburgh's Department of Health and Physical Activity.
Longo and his wife Jennifer reside in Steubenville with their daughters Yvonne and Elliana.
