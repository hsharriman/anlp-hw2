AS A JUNIOR (2025)
Legged 62 of the 65 kickoffs this season for the Dukes with 19 touchbacks (most by a Duquesne kicker in a season since 2015) and an average of 58.7 yards per kick

NEC Special Teams Player of the Week after game-winning 33-yard field goal as time expired in season finale at Robert Morris ... Also made season-long 34-yard FG earlier in the fourth quarter to tie the game, and was 2-2 on PATs in the win.
NEC Special Teams Player of the Week after win at Mercyhurst — 13 points (3-3 FG, 4-4 PAT) ... 3 FG tied for second-most in a single game in program history

17-for-20 on PATs ... Made first 13 to begin the year

BEFORE DUQUESNE

Also played soccer and volleyball in high school
