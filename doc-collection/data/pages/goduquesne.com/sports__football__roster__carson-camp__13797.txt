Biography
BEFORE DUQUESNE
Arrives at Duquesne with 40 career games played over five seasons of FCS football at three stops
Totals 29 career touchdown passes and 6,136 passing yards (153.4 per game)
Began career in 2020 at South Dakota spending three seasons at USD ... Played 4 games at Sacramento State in 2023, then most recently played the 2025 season at Southeastern Louisiana
Played 13 games, passing for 1,794 yards and 14 touchdowns, to lead Southeastern Louisiana to an FCS playoff appearance in 2025 ... Completed two touchdowns in regular-season finale win vs. Nicholls.
Started all 23 games played over three seasons at South Dakota (2020-22)
Named to the MVFC All-Newcomer Team in 2020-21 season ... Was first true freshman in program history to record season-opening start at quarterback ... Passed for 221 yards and accounted for all three touchdowns in leading the Coyotes to a 27-20 win at No. 7 Illinois State in his collegiate debut
Threw for career-best 2,252 yards and 17 touchdowns in 2021 with the Coyotes ... Completed 57-yard "Hail Mary" as time expired to defeat No. 4 South Dakota State on Nov. 13, 2021 (play was named Sports Illustrated Play of the Year in 2021) ... Ranked top-20 of FCS in multiple passing categories.
Three-time MVFC Honor Roll selection ... CSC Academic All-District in 2022
All-time career passing leader (5,115 yards and 59 touchdowns) at Normal West HS
Two-time All-State honorable mention, first team all-conference, offensive player of the year, and Big 12 Scholar-Athlete Award recipient ... IHSA All-Academic scholar athlete in 2018 ... Also played varsity basketball and baseball.
PERSONAL
Born March 6, 2002 in Bloomington, Illinois
Son of Jennifer and Jimmy Camp
Has two siblings, sister Reganne and brother Gavin
Sister Reganne was an All-Big East pitcher at Seton Hall in softball
Brother Gavin is a current college football player with stops at University of Nebraska-Kearney and Illinois State
Enjoys hunting, cooking and reading in free time
MAJOR: Intends to pursue a master's in Leadership
