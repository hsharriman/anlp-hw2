BEFORE DUQUESNE
Spent two seasons at UMass after beginning collegiate career at University of Miami for three seasons.
At UMass: Played in 19 games totaling 30 tackles and one interception ... Charted 22 tackles in 2024 over eight games and added 1.0 tackles for loss with an interception vs. Miami (Ohio).
At Miami: Appeared in eight games, mainly on special teams ... Had four tackles ... Made debut and first career tackle against Florida State in 2020 ... Posted three tackles vs. Central Connecticut State in 2021 ... Competed in 2020 Cheez-It Bowl vs. Oklahoma State.
Was a consensus four-star prospect by 247sports, ESPN and Rivals out of Champagnat Catholic HS.
Helped lead Champagnat to a state championship and finished 2019 season with 62 tackles, one interception, three passes defended and a forced fumble.
Garnered double-digit offers from Power Four programs out of HS
Also competed on HS track & field team
