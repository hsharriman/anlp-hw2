AS A REDSHIRT SOPHOMORE (2025)
Started first five games at linebacker, played in six total before missing remaining games with injury
Tied career high with 6 tackles three times (at Pitt, vs. nationally-ranked Lehigh, vs. New Haven) ... Multiple tackles in every game
Finished with 26 tackles, 2.0 TFL, 1 PBU ... Was among top-two on team in tackles throughout first six games before injury
First season at linebacker; transitioned from defensive back
AS A REDSHIRT FRESHMAN (2024)
Played in all 11 games and made one start at safety vs. West Va. Wesleyan
Four pass breakups
Charted 25 tackles (14 solos) with multiple tackles in nine of 11 games ... Season-high four tackles at Boston College
Forced two fumbles, but both were retained by the offense ... Two forced fumbles tied for second-most in the NEC
BEFORE DUQUESNE
Spent 2023 season at Purdue University ... Did not see game action and transferred with all 4 years of eligibility
Three-star prospect out of Lake Minneola HS ... 81st best safety prospect by ESPN in recruiting class
HS team was district champions in 2019, state finalist in 2020 and regional champions in 2022
Also qualified for state track meet as a member of the 4x100 team in high school
Graduated summa cum laude
