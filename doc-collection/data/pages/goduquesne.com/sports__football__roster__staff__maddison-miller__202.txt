Duquesne University Athletics
Maddison Miller joined the Duquesne University Sports Medicine staff as an athletic trainer in February 2025 and works with the football program for the Dukes. Prior to joining Duquesne, Miller worked as a clinical athletic trainer at Bucknell University with the football and baseball teams.
Miller is a 2020 graduate of West Chester University, where she earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Athletic Training. She also earned a Master of Science degree in Sports Medicine from the University of Pittsburgh in 2022. While working towards her master's degree, she was the athletic trainer with the Pittsburgh Passion from 2020-2022 and then played a year of professional football with the team. She also was an athletic training intern with the football program at the University of Pittsburgh from 2022-2023.
Miller is certified by the Board of Certification as an athletic trainer and is also licensed to practice in the state of Pennsylvania by the PA State Board of Medicine.
