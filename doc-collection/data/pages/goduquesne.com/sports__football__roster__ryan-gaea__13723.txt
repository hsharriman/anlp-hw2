BEFORE DUQUESNE
Spent one season at San Diego State and redshirtted in 2024
Three-year letterwinner at St. Mary's High in Stockton, California
Helped the Rams to the 2023 Division 2 CIF Sac-Joaquin Section finals, finishing the year 11-2 overall and 5-0 in league … Also reached the 2021 Division I CIF Sac-Joaquin Section semifinals and 2022 Division I CIF Sac-Joaquin Section quarterfinals
Named the 2023 Tri-City Athletic League Defensive Lineman of the Year
In his three seasons on varsity, the Rams went 31-6 overall and a perfect 15-0 in league play
Recorded 120 tackles, 16 tackles for loss and 12 sacks in his three varsity seasons … Recorded 92 of his tackles across his junior and senior seasons
Rated as a three-star recruit by 247sports, On3 and ESPN, and a two-star recruit by Rivals
Also played baseball and was member of Polynesian Club
