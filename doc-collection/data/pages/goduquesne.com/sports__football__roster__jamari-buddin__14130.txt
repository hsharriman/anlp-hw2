Biography
BEFORE DUQUESNE
Arrives at Duquesne for fifth collegiate season
Began career in 2021 at Penn State, spending two seasons as a Nittany Lion ... Played 13 games with 12 tackles ... 8 tackles (1.5 TFL), 1 fumble recovery and a QB hurry in nine games during 2022 season.
Played 2024 and 2025 seasons at Grand Valley State (DII) in Michigan ... Played in 19 games totaling 37 tackles with 10 TFLs and two sacks ... 2024: battled early season injury and made nine tackles (2.0 for loss) in nine appearances ... 2025: played all 10 games with 28 tackles, 8.0 TFLs, and 2.0 sacks.
Was consensus top-15 recruit out of the state of Michigan in 2020 ... Two-time first team all-conference selection and four-year letterwinner out of Belleville HS ... Was HS team captain ... Earned Detroit News All-West team, Detroit News Dream Team, and MaxPreps All-Great Lakes Region honors ... Also was an honorable mention all-conference basketball player.
