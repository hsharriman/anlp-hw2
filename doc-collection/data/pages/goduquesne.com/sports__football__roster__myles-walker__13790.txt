Biography
BEFORE DUQUESNE
Spent five seasons at Akron (FBS) playing in 25 games
Posted a career-best 20 receptions for 209 yards in 2025, including a career-high 5 catches vs. Duquesne
Totaled 28 receptions and one touchdown as a Zip
Was a three-star recruit by 247Sports out of Central Valley High School (40 minutes northeast of Duquesne)
Multiple time All-State and All-Conference honoree
Two-way standout at WR and DB named to Pittsburgh Post-Gazette Fab 22
Team captain on CVHS team that went 13-0 and won state championship in 2020
Totaled 30 touchdowns, 1,300 all-purpose yards, and six punt return touchdowns over final two seasons
Three-sport star; also played basketball and ran track
