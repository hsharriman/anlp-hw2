Biography
BEFORE DUQUESNE
Spent four seasons at University of West Georgia (FCS)
Played in 26 games with 22 starts at linebacker with 153 tackles, 13.5 TFLs, 5.5 sacks, 2 forced fumbles (both in 2025), and 1 interception in a 2024 conference win over Utah Tech ... Started all 11 games both in 2024 and 2025
Led team in tackles back-to-back seasons in 2024 (76) and 2025 (71)
Phil Steele Magazine's Third Team All-UAC in 2025
Posted three career double-digit tackle games (12 vs. Tarleton State in 2024, 11 in 2025 season opener at Samford, and 10 vs. ETSU in 2025)
Two-time Georgia state champion out of Warner Robins HS where he earned All-State recognition by Recruit Georgia
