Electric Avenue Sponsored by King Electric
The Pittsburgh Vintage Grand Prix is partnering with King Electric for our 2025 Electric Avenue at Schenley Park on the July 19/20 weekend. The display is in a prime location just below the Bob O’Connor Golf Course clubhouse and alongside Cars N Coffee.
The Pittsburgh Vintage Grand Prix represents all aspects of the automotive industry, including electric vehicles. Most of our auto sponsors bring EVs to display at Schenley Park.
King Electric is a full-service electrical contractor specializing in a wide range of services, from home upgrades to electric vehicle (EV) charging. Justin King said, “The King Electric team is excited to be a part of the Vintage Grand Prix and the Electric Avenue to showcase what is next in the automotive field and your home’s compatibility.”
Owners of Tesla and all other Electric Vehicles that register for the International Car Show will be displayed on each side of the King Electric tent on the fairway beside the Bob O’Connor Golf Course Clubhouse.
Tesla will also be there with vehicles, including a Cybertuck.
