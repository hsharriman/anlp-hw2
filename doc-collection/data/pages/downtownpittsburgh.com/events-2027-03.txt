Girls: Friday, March 5, 2027, 9:00 AM - 7:30 PM; Friday, March 5, 2027, 6:30 PM, New Hazlett Theater, 6 Allegheny Square E, Pittsburgh, PA 15212, USA
Organized by New Hazlett Theater. Repeats: On 2 selected dates.
GIRLS by Maya Anabella is an unfiltered series of confessions that pulls back the curtain on girlhood. Told from the perspectives of five young women, the production relies on the pure power of words to tackle life's heaviest, most authentic topics. By saying the quiet parts out loud, the piece navigates the raw realities of the modern female experience. Fusing sharp wit with vulnerable truths, the performance offers an uncompromising look into girlhood, confronting body image, societal double standards, and the silent struggles of youth. What begins as isolated stories transforms into a fierce declaration of strength. Up close and personal, the piece is a moving reminder that our flaws shape exactly who we are, and no matter what, we are never alone. Girlhood isn't something you can understand unless you experience it. It's time to experience GIRLS.

Miss Saigon: Sunday, March 14, 2027, 7:30 PM, Seating Chart, 101 6th Street, Pittsburgh, PA 15222
Organized by Pittsburgh Musical Theater. Repeats: Runs Mar 14 – Mar 25 (12 days).
The Iconic Love Story Live on Stage A breathtaking musical epic featuring an unforgettable score including “The Heat Is On in Saigon,” “The Movie in My Mind,” “Last Night of the World,” and “I’d Give My Life for You.” This powerful story of love, sacrifice, and survival has captivated audiences around the world for generations. In the chaotic final days of the Vietnam War, a doomed romance changes two lives forever. Kim, a resilient young Vietnamese orphan, meets Chris, a disillusioned American Marine, at a bustling Saigon nightclub. Torn apart by the sudden, historic Fall of Saigon, they embark on an epic, multi-year journey of survival. Powered by a legendary, sung-through score, Miss Saigon is a staggering portrait of a mother's fierce devotion and the enduring strength of the human spirit amidst broken places. Music by Claude-Michel Schönberg Lyrics by Alain Boublil, Richard Maltby, Jr. Miss Saigon is licensed by Music Theatre International (MTI) www.mtishows.com by arrangement with CAMERON MACKINTOSH LTD.
