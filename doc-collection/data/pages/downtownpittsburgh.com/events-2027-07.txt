Financial Aid File your Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) . Available online at FAFSA.gov: Duquesne’s Federal School Code is 003258 . When completing your FAFSA, use the IRS Data Retrieval Tool if possible. You can also create your CSS Profile — The CSS Profile provides a more complete understanding of a family’s financial picture when awarding institutional non-federal need-based financial aid. Duquesne’s CSS code is 2196 (cssprofile.collegeboard.org/). Families are encouraged to submit this in addition to the FAFSA in order to be considered for other University scholarships and grants. Commit to Duquesne University To submit your enrollment deposit and confirm your attendance at Duquesne University, log in to your Duquesne Applicant Portal and complete your Enrollment Response Form. Online payment is recommended. If you need to send a personal check or request a 529 account check, mail to: Duquesne University, Office of Admissions, 600 Forbes Avenue, Pittsburgh PA 15282 Set Up Your MultiPass Account If you haven’t already done so, set up your MultiPass account, which provides access to Duquesne Portal/DU Mobile App (Duquesne’s online student portal), email and other online resources. The Duquesne Portal is where you will be able to order books, pay bills, view your grades and more! After you confirm your enrollment and set up your MultiPass, be sure to check your new Duquesne email account on a regular basis. Student success coaches, staff and professors may send important information to you there. Other Important Dates Feb. 1 - Housing application opens; available to committed students May 1 - Final Day to Confirm Enrollment July 6-9 - First-Year Advisement Summer Transition (FAST) Program Highly encouraged one-day program to help get you prepared for your first semester. FAST is a one-day program for new first-year and transfer students that offers you and your family essential insights and resources to prepare for your academic journey at Duquesne. Each school offers two specific days for participation, and you only need to choose one that fits your schedule, making it a convenient and personalized experience. While this program is not a requirement, it is highly encouraged for all incoming students and family members to attend to ensure a smooth transition. We hope you'll join us at FAST to lay the foundation for a successful and fulfilling experience at Duquesne. We can't wait to welcome you to our community! Scouts at Work Camp: Sunday, July 11, 2027, Scouting America, Laurel Highlands Council, 1275 Bedford Ave, Pittsburgh, PA 15219
Organized by Laurel Highlands Council. Repeats: Runs Jul 11 – Jul 17 (7 days).
Scouts at Work Camp is not your traditional summer camp. This Scouts BSA resident camp program places a strong emphasis on immersive, hands-on opportunities for Scouts to experience various trade occupations, professional, and STEM careers. Scouts have an opportunity to learn from and work with professionals and experts from more than 20 different occupations and fields, including archaeology, aviation, railroading, law, digital technology, engineering, carpentry, welding, electrical installation, firefighting, robotics, photography, journalism, surveying, space exploration, and more.

Distant Worlds: Music from FINAL FANTASY – Eternal: Saturday, July 31, 2027, 7:30 PM, Heinz Hall for the Performing Arts, 600 Penn Ave, Pittsburgh, PA 15222
Organized by Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra.
FINAL FANTASY 40th Anniversary Distant Worlds: music from FINAL FANTASY – Eternal Were you there forty years ago when the “Prelude” played for the first time? Were you there thirty years ago when “Opening - Bombing Mission” first brought players to Midgar? Whether your adventures started in Cornelia, Eorzea, Valisthea, or elsewhere, we invite you and your fellow warriors of light to continue your journey with this new concert series. Our special 40th anniversary program celebrates the enduring music of Nobuo Uematsu, Masashi Hamauzu, Naoshi Mizuta, Hitoshi Sakimoto, Yoko Shimomura, and Masayoshi Soken, brought to life by a full orchestra and chorus and conducted by Maestro Arnie Roth. HD videos created by SQUARE ENIX accompany the music, celebrating four decades of FINAL FANTASY and the evolution of video game technology.
