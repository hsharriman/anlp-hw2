August Wilson: The Writer’s Landscape - AWAACC: Friday, January 1, 2027, August Wilson African American Cultural Center, 980 Liberty Ave, Pittsburgh, PA 15222, USA
Organized by August Wilson African American Cultural Center.
"August Wilson: The Writer’s Landscape" is the first-ever permanent exhibition dedicated to Pulitzer Prize-winning playwright August Wilson, exploring how his Pittsburgh upbringing profoundly shaped his acclaimed American Century Cycle. This 3,600-square-foot exhibit guides visitors through three acts: "The Coffee Shop," inspired by his early observational writing, a replica of "The Office" showcasing his personal items and manuscripts, and "The Street," a recreation of Pittsburgh’s Hill District featuring an interactive map, video, and authentic props and costumes from his Broadway productions. Attendees will gain insight into major themes from his plays and discover the timeless relevance of his artistic legacy.

Dolly Parton's Threads: My Songs In Symphony: Tuesday, January 26, 2027, 7:30 PM, Heinz Hall for the Performing Arts, 600 Penn Ave, Pittsburgh, PA 15222
Organized by Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra.
Dolly Parton's music comes to Pittsburgh in Dolly Parton's Threads: My Songs In Symphony, an innovative multimedia experience featuring Dolly on screen and taking audiences in a visual-musical journey of her songs, her life, and her stories. With a cast of phenomenal guest vocalists and musicians, the Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra performs new and innovative orchestrations by David Hamilton, including hits such as "Jolene," "Coat Of Many Colors," and "I Will Always Love You," in addition to Dolly's personal favorites and a selection from her upcoming Broadway musical. Co-produced by Dolly Parton together with Schirmer Theatrical and Sony Music Publishing. Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra has partnered with the Dollywood Foundation so that $1 per ticket goes to support Dolly Parton's Imagination Library. Learn more at Imaginationlibrary.com.
