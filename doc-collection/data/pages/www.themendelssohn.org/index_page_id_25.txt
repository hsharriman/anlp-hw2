Join Us - Mendelssohn Choir of Pittsburgh
We welcome all dedicated vocalists to audition for the Mendelssohn.
Join the Mendelssohn
The Mendelssohn Choir of Pittsburgh is the chorus of choice of the Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra. As such, we hold ourselves to the highest musical standards. All members are expected to be able to read music and sing expressively, in tune, and with a tone quality appropriate to choral music. There are two categories of membership: Volunteer and Professional Core.
The June 2026 auditions for the 2026-27 season are closed, but we may open more spots in August to fill any vacancies, if needed. Please fill out an audition interest form to be notified of our next round of auditions.

Audition Requirements
Professional Core
MCP employs a small number of professional singers to serve as leaders within the ensemble. Members of the core are expected to have excellent vocal technique, strong music reading skills, and the diligence and preparation to be performance-ready at every rehearsal. Ideal candidates for the professional core will have prior choral experience, be comfortable in a wide range of vocal music historical periods and genres, able to sing with varying amounts of vibrato, and flexible in their approach to choral singing and musical interpretation. Members of the core may be called upon to perform as soloists or in small ensembles as repertoire requires.
Professional Core Audition Requirements:
2 solos (at least one in a language other than English or Latin)
2 prepared choral excerpts from Orff’s Carmina Burana and Haydn’s Mass in Time of War
Volunteer Choir
Most members of our chorus are dedicated volunteers who give their time and talent to make MCP an ensemble of and for the people of Pittsburgh and the surrounding region. Sight-reading skills are helpful but not required to sing as a volunteer, and we provide all singers with resources to prepare repertoire at home (including reference recordings, individual practice tracks, diction recordings, etc.). Volunteer singers are expected to arrive to every rehearsal as prepared as possible so the rehearsal can be spent shaping the sound and musicality of the ensemble. Ideal volunteers possess strong pitch-matching ability, flexibility/willingness to sing a variety of musical styles and approaches, and a passion for rehearsing and performing music at the highest professional level.
Volunteer Audition Requirements:
1 solo in any style and language
2 prepared choral excerpts from Orff’s Carmina Burana and Haydn’s Mass in Time of War
