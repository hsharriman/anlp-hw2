MCP Welcomes Cameron Gunsbury-Massey as New Executive Director
The Mendelssohn Choir of Pittsburgh has welcomed Cameron Gunsbury-Massey as its new Executive Director. Cameron joins MCP after serving as Executive Director of the Pittsburgh Youth Philharmonic Orchestra, where he strengthened programming, expanded partnerships, and supported meaningful organizational growth.
As the Choir continues its tradition of artistic excellence and dynamic collaborations throughout the region, Cameron’s leadership begins an exciting new chapter for one of Pittsburgh’s most enduring cultural institutions.
