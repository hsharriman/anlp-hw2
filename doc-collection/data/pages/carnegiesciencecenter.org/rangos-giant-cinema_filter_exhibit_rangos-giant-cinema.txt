Rangos Giant Cinema
An Experience as Big as the Screen Itself!
Rangos Giant Cinema is the most technologically advanced movie experience in Pittsburgh, boasting a 71-by-39-foot Certified Giant Screen®, two industry-leading Christie® laser–illuminated 4K laser digital projectors, and premium Dolby Atmos surround sound system with 49 speakers.
From Hollywood features and classic films to educational and 3D experiences, Rangos promises to provide an immersive and exciting experience for everyone, whether you’re looking for a night just for adults or for family fun. After your stay, we want you to feel like you’ve visited the “Mother of All Theaters.”
Please arrive 15 minutes prior to the show start time. To ensure a quality experience for our guests, we do not allow late entries.
Rangos Giant Cinema is open daily.
