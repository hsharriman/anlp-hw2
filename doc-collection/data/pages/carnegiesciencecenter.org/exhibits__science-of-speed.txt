Science of Speed
Powered by Lenovo
Get ready for an all-new, high-octane experience that blends heart-pumping excitement with mind-expanding discovery. Separate ticket required.
Buhl Planetarium and Observatory
Fly to the Moon, journey to Saturn, explore the stars, and travel through distant galaxies—all without leaving Pittsburgh!
Miniature Railroad & Village®
Explore Western Pennsylvania history with this iconic exhibit. Featuring hundreds of animated scenes that capture life up to 1940. All aboard!
Sports360
The Sports360 exhibition takes the science of sports to the next level with more than 25 interactive activities to get your heart pumping.
USS Requin Submarine
Come aboard and experience what life was like inside a real Cold War-era submarine.
BNY Fab Lab
Get hands-on with 3D printing or laser cutting and other creative ways to be a maker.
Mars: The Next Giant Leap®
Discover the many facets of space exploration and find yourself 300 million miles away on the surface of the Red Planet.
Robot Hall of Fame
See a dozen replica robots as a tribute to the fictional machines that inspired the creators of real-world robots.
H2Oh!
Exhibit
Get ready to dive headfirst into all things H2Oh.
Bricksburgh
Exhibit
Play the role of architect and builder with thousands of bricks of all sizes.
Little Learner Clubhouse
Exhibit
Feel inspired—and release some energy—in this hub of hands-on activities for curious minds ages 6 and under.
BodyWorks
Exhibit
Explore the complexities of the human body and exactly what makes us—us.
NETL Energy Zone
Test your knowledge of energy consumption and learn real-world problem-solving skills.
Zeiss Model II Star Projector
This iconic, interactive feature of our SpacePlace exhibit gallery was used from 1939 to 1991 at the Buhl Planetarium.
Our Place in a Changing Climate
Step inside “Our Place in a Changing Climate” exhibit and explore how a global issue is shaping life right here in Western Pennsylvania.
