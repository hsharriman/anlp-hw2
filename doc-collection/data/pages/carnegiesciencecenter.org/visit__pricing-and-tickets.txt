2026 NFL Draft Operations Update
Due to road closures and security access related to the 2026 NFL Draft, Kamin Science Center will be temporarily closed to the general public from April 13 through May 1, 2026.
While the Science Center is closed to the general public, school and group visits will continue during the following dates: April 13–17 and April 29–May 1.
All group visits must be scheduled in advance through our Reservations Team at 412.237.3400.
Normal museum hours will resume on May 2, 2026, when the Science Center will reopen seven days a week for the summer season.

E-Gift Cards
ADD A GIFT CARD TO YOUR CART
Purchase a gift certificate online and you will receive an email with a code. Print out the code or save it on your phone.
The gift certificates can be used at all four Carnegie Museums for the purchase of admissions, camps, classes, ticketed events, store items, and memberships. Gift certificates can hold a minimum of $10 and a maximum of $500. Enter a value at checkout.
Gift certificates may not be used for Science of Speed kart racing and simulators, food service, parking, or online XPLOR Store or Membership purchases. They can be used for purchases on-site in the XPLOR Store  or to purchase Memberships at the Science Center Ticket Counter.

Our Ticket Options
Secure your tickets online before you arrive. There is no surcharge for online ticket purchases. On-site ticketing is available in the main building.
General Admission: Explore all five floors of the museum, live demonstrations, USS Requin (SS 481) submarine, and Buhl Planetarium astronomy shows. Special traveling exhibits may require a separate ticket.
USS Requin: Included with General Admission on a first-come, first-served basis, and subject to availability. Weather and river conditions permitting. Tours operate at 20-minute intervals with a limited capacity. Timed tickets are required and can be obtained on the day of your visit at USS Requin Gateway on the Ground Floor. Full mobility is required for this experience.
Rangos Giant Cinema: Separate tickets are required for movies. A General Admission ticket is not required for visitors only watching a movie. Click here for current showings. Please arrive at least 15 minutes prior to the show, as every show begins promptly at the start time.
Laser Shows in the Buhl Planetarium: Separate tickets are required for laser shows. A General Admission ticket is not required for visitors only watching a laser show. Please arrive at least 15 minutes prior to the show, as every show begins promptly at the start time. Filming and live streaming of laser shows is prohibited.
Members: Members enjoy free General Admission. Discounted tickets can be purchased for Rangos Giant Cinema, laser shows, and limited-time traveling exhibits. Click here to become a Member today . For discounted offers, learn more or apply for Community Access Memberships .
Tickets are sold up to four weeks in advance. To inquire about dates further out, please call 412.237.3400 between 9 a.m. and 5 p.m. If your preferred time slot is sold out online, a limited number of tickets may still be available at the on-site Ticket Counter on a first-come, first-served basis.
