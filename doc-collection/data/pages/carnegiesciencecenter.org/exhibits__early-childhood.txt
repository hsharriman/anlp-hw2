Little Learner Clubhouse
Exhibit
Powered by PNC Grow Up Great®
Fostering Curiosity and Imagination
Aspiring scientists ages 6 and under and their families will love the Little Learner Clubhouse, powered by PNC Grow Up Great®, a nature-inspired hub for free play and hands-on learning. With activities designed to inspire the littlest learners, it’s a must-see attraction for family fun.
Learning is always a hands-on experience:
The Clubhouse
Climb and explore a playground with great views of the exhibits below.
The Water Table
Make a splash while learning about rivers and water sources.
The Tomato Stand
Sort red and green tomatoes using conveyors and vacuum tubes.
The Cove
Role-play inside our yellow submarine or navigate through hanging sea kelp for some quiet time.
The Baby Garden
This farm-themed soft play area is designed for the youngest scientists.
The Button Wall
Explore a wall of 200 buttons that activate a variety of sights and sounds throughout the gallery.
