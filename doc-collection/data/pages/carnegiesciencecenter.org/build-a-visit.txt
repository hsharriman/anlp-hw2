About Kamin Science Center
Kamin Science Center is the ideal place for fun things to do in Pittsburgh with kids. If you’re looking for family fun, the Science Center is the most visited museum in Pittsburgh, where adults and kids can experience hands-on activities and STEM learning. Explore a variety of educational exhibits in our premier, family-friendly museum.
