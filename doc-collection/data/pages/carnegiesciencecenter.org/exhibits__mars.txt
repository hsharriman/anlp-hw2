Mars: The Next Giant Leap®
All Ages
Included with General Admission
2nd Floor, Mars: The Next Giant Leap®
Content Themes: Engineering, Environmental Science, Outer Space, Physics
Grades: 6-8, 9-12
Get Ready to Touch Down on Mars and See All the Red Planet Has to Offer.
Embark on a 300-million-mile journey to Mars and explore the future of space exploration at the award-winning science exhibition Mars: The Next Giant Leap®, presented by Howmet Aerospace Foundation and powered by the PNC Foundation. Discover what it takes to establish a society on Mars, how we sustain life, and why exploring other worlds matters for Earth.
This 7,400-square-foot permanent exhibition next to Buhl Planetarium is the most ambitious experience in Kamin Science Center’s history. With community input and a focus on inclusivity, you’ll explore how sustainability, climate change, social justice, and resource access can shape humanity’s future.
What you’ll experience:
Visualize a Mars Settlement ​
Imagine life on Mars and consider how to prioritize resources, food, and technology.
Vote on the Future
Make decisions about what to build on Mars and see how these decisions impact daily life.
Explore Climate Issues
Learn how surviving on Mars can help us address climate challenges on Earth.
Space Careers
Discover why Pittsburgh is poised to be a hub in the growing space economy.
Discover the experiential zones:
View from Mars
Experience breathtaking, large-scale images of the Martian landscape and a unique perspective of Earth.
Generously supported by Arconic Foundation.
Climatology
Explore how climates shape the conditions for life on Mars in an interactive, large-scale digital gallery.
Generously supported by The Bozzone Family Foundation.
Martian Garden
Discover sustainable living on Mars and learn how to nourish the body and mind in a Martian settlement by reimagining food production.
Generously supported by Henry L. Hillman Foundation.
Martian Living
Imagine what daily life on Mars might be like by exploring the living space of future Martian residents.
Generously supported by The Philip Chosky Charitable and Educational Foundation.
Dream Big: Space
Weigh in on key decisions for an imagined Mars settlement and see the impact of your choices unfold through both digital and hands-on experiences.
Generously supported by The Rossin Family Foundation.
Pittsburgh in Space
Imagine the thrilling future of humanity in space, driven by groundbreaking research and visionary ideas happening right now in Pittsburgh.
Generously supported by Ray and Joneen Betler.
Search for Life
Operate Martian rovers—just like the ones exploring Mars today—to search for signs of life and water while discovering how life influences climate.
