Dive Deep Into an Amazing Piece of History.
Step aboard USS Requin (SS 481), an authentic Cold War-era submarine, to discover how 80 men used skill, humor, and ingenuity during daring defense and scientific missions, some of which are still classified to this day.
Self-guided tours are included with General Admission on a first-come, first-served basis. A timed ticket is required and can be obtained on the day of your visit at USS Requin Gateway, located on the Ground Floor.
Tours operate every 20 minutes, and full mobility is required for this experience.
USS Requin closes annually, from January through mid-March, for maintenance and cleaning. Submarine hours also vary depending on the time of year.
A Uniquely Immersive Experience for All
USS Requin (SS 481) Submarine
State-of-the-art and battle ready when it set out just days before the end of World War II, USS Requin holds the distinction of being the navy’s first radar picket submarine. Come see one of the region’s most immersive historical science exhibits.
USS Requin Gateway
Located on the Ground Floor at the Science Center’s staircase and lobby, USS Requin Gateway enhances the submarine tour by offering military and Cold War history while also including submarine-based content for those unable to board the ship.
Escape USS Requin
At the height of the Cold War, a B-25 bomber crashed into the Monongahela River. Was it a mechanical error or something more sinister? Test your detective skills with puzzles and games about Pittsburgh, USS Requin, and local history. Advance tickets required; no walk-ups. Learn more here.
