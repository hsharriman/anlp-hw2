Matching Gifts
Double or Triple Your Impact with a Matching Gift
Many employers offer matching gift programs that can multiply your contribution through a corporate match. Gifts from retirees and spouses of employees may also qualify for matching. Check with your Human Resources department to determine whether your employer matches charitable donations and request a matching gift form. Fill out the form and send it to:
Carnegie Museums
4400 Forbes Avenue
Pittsburgh, PA 15213
It’s an easy way to make your gift go even further in support of Carnegie Museums!
