Corporate Sponsorship
A Gift to Carnegie Museums is a Gift to Your Community
Supporting Carnegie Museums is an investment in the cultural vibrancy of the Pittsburgh region and the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.
We Deliver Value
Enjoy prominent visibility that provides unparalleled touchpoints to local, nation, and international visitors throughout the year. From high-profile patron events to blockbuster exhibitions and enriching educational programs, sponsorship opportunities provide a way to connect with key audiences and meet your company’s philanthropic and marketing goals.
Customized Partnerships
Carnegie Museums welcomes the opportunity to develop a tailored benefits package designed to serve your company’s business needs.
