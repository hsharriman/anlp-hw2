TEEN MEMBERSHIP
Welcome to the Teen Membership program, an opportunity for teens to receive a free membership to Carnegie
Museums of Pittsburgh. Teen members receive exclusive perks: FREE unlimited general admission to all four Carnegie
Museums, access to special programming just for teens, reciprocal admission to 300+ ASTC reciprocal science centers,
and discounts in museum stores and cafes.*
To qualify, teens must be 13–18 years of age, a resident of the state of Pennsylvania, and parents or legal guardians must give
consent as outlined below. For teens under 18 years old, please provide parent or legal guardian contact information below.
Teen Memberships are not valid for school field trips, group sales, or group visits. Other restrictions may apply.
