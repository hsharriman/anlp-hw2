Family Access Membership $20.00
Carnegie Museums is helping to remove barriers to membership with a $20 Family Access Membership for individuals and families who live in Pennsylvania and are eligible to receive public assistance.
This Family Access membership includes:
Free general admission for one year for two (2) Adults and four (4) Children to Carnegie Museum of Art, Carnegie Museum of Natural History, Kamin Science Center, and The Andy Warhol Museum
Invitations to members-only events and programming
Free reciprocal admission at 300+ ASTC museums and science centers
Discounts on classes, camps, lectures, and special events
Discounts on laser shows at the Buhl Planetarium and educational films at the Rangos Giant Cinema
A 10% discount in museum stores and cafes (20% store discount during Member Shopping Days)
Free subscription to Carnegie magazine
Free reciprocal admission at an additional 275+ ROAM museums and arboretums
