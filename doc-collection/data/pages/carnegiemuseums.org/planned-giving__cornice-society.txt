About the Cornice Society
The Cornice Society recognizes and thanks our future donors during their lifetime. Donors who intend to make a gift to Carnegie Museums through their will, a beneficiary designation, or charitable gift annuity are encouraged to join this community of like-minded donors.
Cornice Society members enjoy unique museum experiences, communication, and recognition (Members may choose to remain anonymous if you prefer).
Contact us to inform us of your intentions and learn more about the Cornice Society.
We’re here to help you meet your goals!
Our team would be happy to speak with you in confidence about your giving goals, with no obligation.
