Our Mission and Impact
OUR MISSION
To preserve and expand the resources of art and science as agents of personal growth and social advancement in Pittsburgh and beyond.
EXPLORE OUR CORE VALUES
Building Community
Families sharing quality time together. School children and their teachers discovering unique sources of inspiration, knowledge, and fun. Explorers of all ages experiencing one-of-a-kind exhibitions and events. The reach of the four Carnegie Museums is far and wide and open to all.
We welcome nearly 1 million annual visitors.
200,000 schoolchildren participate in K-12 educational programming.
Inspiring Lifelong Learning
Carnegie Museums inspires, educates, and challenges. Our museums are places of thoughtful, playful discovery—where curious minds explore the most pressing issues of the day. And they bring to the region experiences only the four Carnegie Museums could deliver.
Our museums produce 100+ special exhibitions, films, theater shows, and live performances a year.
15,000 adults attend classes, lectures, and special events.
10,000+ children attend camps and classes.
Welcoming To All
Carnegie Museums strives to expand its impact and access through discounted admissions and free days, as well as our Community Access Memberships
Up to 100,000 people receive free admission each year.
30,000 schoolchildren receive free or discounted programming or transportation.
20,000 teens receive FREE teen memberships.
4,000 families receive discounted Family Access memberships.
Copies of Carnegie Museums’ Federal IRS form 990 and audited financial statements are available upon request.
