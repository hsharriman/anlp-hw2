About Us
There’s no cultural organization in the world quite like Carnegie Museums. With a legacy of creativity, exploration, and discovery dating back to 1895, we are a family of four dynamic, distinctive museums—Carnegie Museum of Art, Carnegie Museum of Natural History, Kamin Science Center, and The Andy Warhol Museum.

Our Mission and Impact
As the largest and most far-reaching cultural organization in the Pittsburgh region, each year Carnegie Museums serves up to 1.5 million people, including more than 200,000 schoolchildren.
