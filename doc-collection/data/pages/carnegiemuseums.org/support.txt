Donors Circle
A gift to Carnegie Museums is a gift to your community: to the 200,000 school kids inspired by Carnegie Museums educational programming each year; to the nearly 1 million visitors to our museums.
Patrons Circle
The Patrons of Carnegie Museums have a legacy of leadership giving. They trust the Carnegie Museums as places for learning and connection.
Giving Societies
Explore our museum-specific giving societies to make a lasting impact on the museums you love.
ALL TOGETHER Campaign
Your support of Carnegie Museums’ comprehensive campaign will expand the capacity of our four museums to transform lives and build communities for decades to come.
Corporate Membership
As a Corporate Member, you’ll be part of a network of business leaders investing in our community and engaging your employees through access to discounts and one-of-a-kind experiences.
Gift Planning
A planned gift can be one of the easiest and most impactful gifts you make. There are many ways to help sustain Carnegie Museums for future generations.
