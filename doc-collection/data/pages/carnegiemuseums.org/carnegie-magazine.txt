Big Picture
Since its launch in 2022, Mindful Museum has made Wednesday mornings at Carnegie Museum of Art the place to be for hundreds of visitors aged 55+ to enjoy yoga, artmaking, art tours, and guided meditations before the museum opens. Mindful Museum participants receive early entry into Carnegie Museum of Art at 9 a.m., discounted coffee and a pastry at Café Carnegie, and $5 parking.
