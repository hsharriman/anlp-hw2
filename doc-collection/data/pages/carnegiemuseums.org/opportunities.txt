Opportunities - Carnegie Museums
Join Our Team
Andrew Carnegie dreamed of a cultural institution that could ignite the passions of future generations of artists, scientists, and explorers. For over 100 years, Carnegie Museums has been engaging communities locally, nationally and internationally to do just that. Our team collaborates across disciplines to preserve the past and envision the future, finding new ways to bring our vast collections and resources to audiences here in Pittsburgh and across the globe.
Be part of Carnegie Museums’ long history of celebrating the great capacity of human beings to explore, discover, and create spaces of wonder for all generations.
Ways to Contribute
Experiential Engagement & Volunteering:
Throughout the organization are committed and curious individuals holding unique, non-employee roles that support Carnegie Museums’ mission to preserve and expand the resources of art and science as agents of personal growth.
These roles include Internships, Externships, Work Study, Fellowships, Job Shadowing, and Volunteers.
Employment:
Carnegie Museums has opportunities for employment that touch nearly every industry across all career stages—whether you’re stepping into your first job, returning to the workforce after a career break, or a seasoned professional looking for a new challenge.
Some Current Departments from Around the Organization Include:
Finance, IT, Stores, Marketing & Communications, Human Resources, Visitor Services, Education, Special Events, Museum Development, Membership, Telemarketing, Institutional Partnerships, Culture and Community, Public Engagement, Custodial, Campaign Initiatives, Trades Workforce, Science & Research, Curatorial, Collections & Exhibitions, Digital Engagement, Design and Publishing, Experience Design and Fabrication, Service & Operations, Workforce Development
Need assistance or an accommodation? Carnegie Museums is committed to working with and providing reasonable accommodations to applicants with disabilities. If you need assistance or have a request for a reasonable accommodation during any part of the application or hiring process due to a disability, please contact our human resources team at HRConnect@carnegiemuseums.org or 412.622.3311.
