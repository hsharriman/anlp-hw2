Become a Member
As a member of Carnegie Museums, enjoy free admission to Carnegie Museums of Art and Natural History, Carnegie Science Center, and The Andy Warhol Museum.
Young Professional Membership
The Carnegie Connectors membership is for young supporters of the four Carnegie Museums who demonstrate an interest in art, science, and natural history and a drive to create a community based on their shared interests.
Senior Membership
Seniors save year-round on individual membership to the four Carnegie Museums.
Free Teen Membership
Teen Membership is a free pass to exploration and discovery available to all teens 13–18 years old who are residents of Pennsylvania.
Community Access Memberships
Community Access Memberships represent an invitation for everyone to join us as members and enjoy the benefits of being part of the Carnegie Museums member community all year long.

We’ve compiled answers to the questions we hear most often to help you get the information you need quickly.
