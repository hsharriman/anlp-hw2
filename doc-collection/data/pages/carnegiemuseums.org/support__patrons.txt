Patrons Circle
Be a part of history! The Patrons of Carnegie Museums have a 130-year-old legacy of leadership giving. They trust the Carnegie Museums as places of unparalleled learning and connection.
All Patrons enjoy:
All standard Donors Circle benefits
Free museum admission and free unlimited educational films at Rangos Giant Cinema
Complimentary Family-level gift membership
Recognition in our annual listing and on donor plaques displayed at Carnegie Museums of Art and Natural History
Private docent-led tour for up to 12 people at Carnegie Museum of Art, Carnegie Museum of Natural History, or The Andy Warhol Museum*
Invitation to a special viewing of a Rangos Giant Cinema film
Invitations to exclusive Donor and Patrons Circle events (behind-the-scenes encounters, conversations with curators, and more)
A physical membership card, as well as the standard digital card
Personalized Services line: 412.622.5772
