Global Pass
Make a difference from a distance! With the NEW Carnegie Museums Global Pass, participants outside the tri-state area visiting Pittsburgh can celebrate their love of art, science, and natural history with $5 off general admission to our four world-class museums, free virtual programming, online store discounts, and more.
*Global Pass is available to those who live outside the states of Pennsylvania, Ohio, and West Virginia.
All Global Pass holders enjoy:
A 10% discount in museum stores and cafés (20% store discount during Member Shopping Days)
$5 off general admission for one year for an unlimited amount of guests PLUS a one-time free visit for named member
Invitations to members-only virtual programming
Free virtual subscription to the award-winning Carnegie magazine (four issues per year)
