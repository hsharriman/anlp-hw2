Mattress Factory Conduct Policy
Last updated: September 2026
Mattress Factory strives to ensure a welcoming, inclusive and safe experience for all Museum
visitors and staff.
Mattress Factory has a zero -tolerance policy for individuals who engage in behavior or
language that is abusive, harassing, discriminatory or threatening. This includes attire or
visible tattoos displaying language or designs that could pose a safety risk or create a hostile
environment. Conduct that poses any risk to the health, wellness or safety of other visitors or
to Museum staff or property is also prohibited.
Mattress Factory reserves the right to refuse or revoke the admission or membership of
any visitor whom it believes violates these guidelines.
