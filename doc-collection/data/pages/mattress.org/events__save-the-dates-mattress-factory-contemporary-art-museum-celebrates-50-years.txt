Our Mission
Mattress Factory is an artist-centered museum, international residency program and renowned producer and presenter of installation art. We say “yes” to artists, offering time and space to dream and realize projects in our hometown, Pittsburgh, PA. We invite audiences from around the world and around the corner to step inside, immerse and connect with the artistic process.

Save the Dates:
Mattress Factory Celebrates 50 Years!
In 1977, Mattress Factory was founded on curiosity: what happens when you give artists time, space, and support to make new, ambitious, risk-taking works?
49 years later, we're still making space for artists and audiences. Nearly one thousand artists and hundreds of thousands of visitors have converged in our neighborhood.
In 2027, Mattress Factory celebrates 50 years.
That's worth a party. Actually - let's make that two.
