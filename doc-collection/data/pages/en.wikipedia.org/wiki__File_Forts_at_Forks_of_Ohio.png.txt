DescriptionForts at Forks of Ohio.png
English:
The map places the forts according to the positions of reconstructed elements, such as this bastion of Ft. Duquesne.
Self-made map of the Forks of the Ohio (Monongahela, Allegheny and Ohio rivers). Shows Fort Pitt and Fort Duquesne. Outlines of the walls (curtains) of the forts traced from the original plans as published in 19th century histories.
