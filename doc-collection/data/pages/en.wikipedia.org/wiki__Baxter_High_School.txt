Baxter High School (now the Pittsburgh Student Achievement Center) is a historic high school building in the Homewood North neighborhood of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Built in 1908, is now home to an alternative school for grades 6-12. It was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1986.[1][3]
