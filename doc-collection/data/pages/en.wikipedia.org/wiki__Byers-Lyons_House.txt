The Byers-Lyons House (now Byers Hall of the Community College of Allegheny County's Allegheny Campus) in the Allegheny West neighborhood of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, is a building from 1898. It was added to the List of City of Pittsburgh historic designations on March 15, 1974,[2] the National Register of Historic Places on November 19, 1974,[1] and the List of Pittsburgh History and Landmarks Foundation Historic Landmarks in 1989.[3][4]
