Conroy Education Center provides education for children with special needs in the Pittsburgh Public Schools. The school was added to the National Register of Historic Places on September 30, 1986, as Conroy Junior High School,[1] and the List of Pittsburgh History and Landmarks Foundation Historic Landmarks in 2001.[3]
