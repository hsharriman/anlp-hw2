===2008–present: Manfred Honeck=== In May 2006, [[Manfred Honeck]] first guest-conducted the PSO. He returned for another guest appearance in November 2006. In January 2007, the PSO announced the appointment of [[Manfred Honeck]] as its ninth music director, effective with the 2008–2009 season, in a reversion of conductor leadership format to the traditional music director hierarchy.<ref>{{cite news | url=https://www.nytimes.com/2007/01/24/arts/24hone.html?ex=1327294800&en=63c573b6727e887e&ei=5088&partner=rssnyt&emc=rss | title=In Pittsburgh, a Music Director to Replace Three Conductors | work=The New York Times | author=Daniel J. Wakin | date=January 24, 2007 | access-date=2007-04-27}}</ref><ref>{{cite press release | url=http://www.pittsburghsymphony.org/honeckpr.pdf | title=Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra Announces New Music Director Manfred Honeck | publisher=Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra | format=.PDF | date=January 24, 2007 | access-date=2007-04-27}}</ref> His initial contract was for three years. In September 2009, the PSO announced the extension of Honeck's contract to the 2015–2016 season.<ref>{{cite news | author=Andrew Druckenbrod | title=Honeck to remain Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra conductor until 2015 | url=http://www.post-gazette.com/pg/09253/996931-388.stm | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121018092853/http://www.post-gazette.com/stories/ae/music/honeck-to-remain-pittsburgh-symphony-orchestra-conductor-until-2015-357197/ | url-status=dead | archive-date=October 18, 2012 | work=Pittsburgh Post-Gazette | date=September 10, 2009 | access-date=2009-09-12}}</ref> In February 2012, the PSO announced the further extension of Honeck's contract through the 2019–2020 season.<ref>{{cite news | author=Andrew Druckenbrod | title=Honeck agrees to contract extension with PSO | url=http://www.post-gazette.com/pg/12044/1209951-100.stm | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140810002039/http://www.post-gazette.com/breaking/2012/02/13/Honeck-agrees-to-contract-extension-with-PSO/stories/201202131211 | url-status=dead | archive-date=August 10, 2014 | work=Pittsburgh Post-Gazette | date=February 13, 2012 | access-date=2012-02-18}}</ref> In June 2007, the orchestra announced the appointment of American conductor [[Leonard Slatkin]] as the orchestra's principal guest conductor for the 2008–2009 season.<ref>{{cite news | url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-dyn/content/article/2007/06/14/AR2007061402486.html | title=Slatkin Also To Conduct in Pittsburgh | newspaper=Washington Post | author=Tim Page | date=June 15, 2007 | access-date=2007-07-18}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Orchestra taps Austrian maestro to lead in fall 2008 |newspaper=[[Pittsburgh Post-Gazette]] |date=January 24, 2007 |url=https://news.google.com/newspapers?nid=1129&dat=20070124&id=2edRAAAAIBAJ&pg=6850,2684605}}</ref> In November 2006, the PSO announced a pledge of $29.5 million from the Richard P. Simmons family as the start of a capital challenge for the orchestra to address long-standing financial concerns.<ref>{{cite news | url=http://www.post-gazette.com/pg/07014/753720-42.stm | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070929122057/http://www.post-gazette.com/pg/07014/753720-42.stm | url-status=dead | archive-date=September 29, 2007 | title=Dick Simmons: A 'passion' for the Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra | work=Pittsburgh Post-Gazette | author=Timothy McNulty | date=January 14, 2007 | access-date=2007-04-27}}</ref> In December 2006, the PSO announced the launch of an $80&nbsp;million capital fund-raising drive after the initial $29.5 million boost from the Simmons family.<ref>{{cite news | url=http://www.post-gazette.com/pg/06335/742583-42.stm | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070929125452/http://www.post-gazette.com/pg/06335/742583-42.stm | url-status=dead | archive-date=September 29, 2007 | title=PSO outlines goals at start of $80&nbsp;million fund drive | work=Pittsburgh Post-Gazette | author=Timothy McNulty | date=December 1, 2006 |access-date = April 27, 2007}}</ref> In March 2009, the PSO announced the discontinuation of its chamber orchestra series after the 2008–2009 season, along with staff reductions of 9 positions.<ref>{{cite news | url=http://www.post-gazette.com/pg/09086/958602-42.stm | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121015135128/http://www.post-gazette.com/stories/ae/music/pso-forced-to-end-chamber-orchestra-335565/ | url-status=dead | archive-date=October 15, 2012 | title=PSO forced to end chamber orchestra | work=Pittsburgh Post-Gazette | author=Andrew Druckenbrod | date=March 27, 2009 | access-date=2009-03-28}}</ref> In September 2009, the Pittsburgh Symphony and Honeck agreed to extend his contract through the 2015–2016 season. In February 2012, the PSO announced the further extension of Honeck's contract through the 2019–2020 season.<ref>{{cite news | author=Andrew Druckenbrod | title=Honeck agrees to contract extension with PSO | url=http://www.post-gazette.com/pg/12044/1209951-100.stm | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140810002039/http://www.post-gazette.com/breaking/2012/02/13/Honeck-agrees-to-contract-extension-with-PSO/stories/201202131211 | url-status=dead | archive-date=August 10, 2014 | newspaper=Pittsburgh Post-Gazette | date=13 February 2012 | accessdate=2024-09-20}}</ref> In September 2018, the PSO announced the further extension of Honeck's contract through the 2021–2022 season.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.post-gazette.com/ae/music/2018/09/24/Pittsburgh-Symphony-Orchestra-Manfred-Honeck-contract-new-board-chair-Tony-Bucci/stories/201809240103|title=PSO extends Manfred Honeck's contract, elects new board chair|newspaper=[[Pittsburgh Post-Gazette]]|accessdate=2024-09-20}}</ref> In September 2021, the PSO announced a further extension of Honeck's contract as music director through the 2027–2028 season.<ref>{{cite press release | url=https://pittsburghsymphony.org/pso_home/press-room/press-releases/2021-2022/manfred-honeck-extends-contract-through-the-2027-2028-season | title=Manfred Honeck Extends Contract As Music Director of the Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra Through The 2027–2028 Season | publisher=Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra | date=24 September 2021 | access-date=2024-09-20}}</ref> In June 2026, the PSO announced the newest extension of Honeck's contract as its music director through the 2032–2033 season.<ref>{{cite press release | url=https://assets.culturaldistrict.org/culturaldistrict/system/assets/20260610/honeck-contract-extension-web-46aaf81a.pdf | title=Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra Renews Music Director Manfred Honeck Through 2032/2033 Season | publisher=Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra | date=10 June 2026 | access-date=2026-06-16}}</ref>
