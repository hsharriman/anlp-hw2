Wood Street Galleries is an art gallery located in Downtown Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, operated by the Pittsburgh Cultural Trust. The gallery has built a reputation for exhibiting international new media artists due to the yearslong curation of former director and curator, Murray Horne.[1][2][3][4][5] The gallery occupies the upper floors of the Wood Street 'T Station' building.[6][7][8]
History
[edit]
The trapezoidal building that houses the gallery has been rented to the Pittsburgh Cultural Trust since 1990 by the Pittsburgh Port Authority for $1 per year.[9] Wood Street Galleries was established two years later in 1992, with former director and curator Murray Horne joining the gallery later in the 1990s.[9]
