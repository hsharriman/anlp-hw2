DescriptionNativeTowns Pittsburgh.png
English:  Native American towns and other points of interest regarding Native Americans, upper Ohio valley, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Includes Meadowcroft Rockshelter, Sawcunk, Logstown, Indian Mound, The Point, Shannopin's Town, Chartier's Town and Kittanning. Also shows Youghiogheny River, Allegheny River, Beaver River and Ohio River.
Date   19 March 2006
Source   Positions determined by descriptions found in Course of study in geographic, biographic and historic Pittsburgh, The Board of Public Education, Pittsburgh, 1921, and other sources; Background map courtesy of Demis
Author   Thomas Cool
