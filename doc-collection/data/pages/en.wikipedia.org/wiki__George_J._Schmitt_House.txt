Designated 2003[1]
George J. Schmitt House located at 7120 Ohio River Boulevard in Ben Avon, Allegheny County, Pennsylvania, was built in 1916. The house, designed by Janssen & Abbott, was added to the List of Pittsburgh History and Landmarks Foundation Historic Landmarks in 2003.[1]
