The Beltzhoover Elementary School in the Beltzhoover neighborhood of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, is a building from 1909. It was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1986.[1][4] The school was one of ten schools closed in 2004 by Pittsburgh Public Schools.[5]
