The Warhol Dinner
Since 2015, The Warhol has hosted an annual dinner in support of the museum’s exhibition and learning and public engagement programs. In keeping with Warhol’s imaginative mix of Uptown and Downtown, guests mingle with contemporary artists, restaurant scene regulars, Hollywood stars, blue chip art dealers and collectors, and museum patrons. A small art auction with works from several contemporary artists who were inspired or influenced by Warhol is another highlight of the evening. For more information, tickets, or sponsorship opportunities, please contact portraitsociety@warhol.org.
Wyndham Grand Pittsburgh Downtown is The Warhol’s official hotel sponsor.
