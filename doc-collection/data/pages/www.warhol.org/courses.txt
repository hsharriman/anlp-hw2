Courses & Workshops
Emulsion: Adult Print Class
Have you ever wondered how screen prints or graphics tees are made? Now is your chance to learn. Explore Andy Warhol’s printmaking process through the lens of the artist and the techniques that he popularized. In this four-week course you will learn about Warhol’s early blotted line and stamping techniques, his early 60s single layer print technique, and his 70s/80s multilayer print and collage techniques. During the course you will get hands-on practice at designing, producing, and printing single and multi-layer screenprints. Space is limited.
Act 48 credits available for teachers.

Teen DJ Academy
Learn to build and blend your own remixes that will get everyone on the dance floor! Presented in partnership with Tech 25, the Teen DJ Academy is a 14-week workshop series for participants ages 13–18 taught by professional DJs. Participants will get hands-on experience with pro gear and perform at Youth Invasion, LGBTQ+ Prom, and other teen events. This program is free and participants will earn a stipend for the 15 week program plus an additional stipend per performance. Space is limited. Participants are encouraged to attend all 14 workshops and commit to performing during our scheduled events.

The Warhol Academy Fellowships
The Warhol Academy’s programming includes single and multi-day training sessions for participants to learn from practicing professionals with experience in in-demand creative and technical skills as well as experts who reinforce the soft skills needed for learners to have a strong foundation upon which to develop as professionals.
