Sound Series: Fenne Lily with special guest villagerrr
Sunday, April 11, 2027, 8 p.m.
We welcome English singer/songwriter, Fenne Lily, and her band to our intimate theater. Drawing comparisons as broad and varied as Joni Mitchell and Laura Marling, Lily has crafted her unique and understated folk/pop sensibility over three records, beginning in 2018 with On Hold. She signed with Dead Oceans in 2020 and released Breach, which was produced by Brian Deck and Steve Albini. Big Picture followed in 2023, co-produced with Brad Cook and recorded in his studio in Durham, North Carolina and mixed by Jay Som’s Melina Duterte. villagerrr opens the evening.
Doors open at 7 p.m.
Co-presented with 91.3 WYEP Presents
