30th Anniversary Gala
30th Anniversary Gala
As a benefit of your membership with Carnegie Museums of Pittsburgh, you are invited to deepen your engagement with The Andy Warhol Museum by joining the Warhol Society.
Warhol Society members enjoy insider access to the museum, including exclusive opportunities to meet artists, interact with curators, and tour private collections-all while helping to sustain the museum through their contributions to our curatorial program. Programming celebrates and explores Andy Warhol’s indelible impact on contemporary visual art and culture. The Warhol Society is The Warhol’s first affinity group of closest circle of supporters, offering unique art experiences both locally and abroad.
Membership in the Warhol Society begins at $1,500 annually.
Membership Benefits Include
Invitations for two (2) guests to VIP Exhibition Opening Receptions
Reserved seating for featured discussion/ performance during exhibition openings
Invitations to studio visits with local artists
Invitations to tour local private collections
First choice ticketing to annual NYC Dinner
VIP Passes to national art fairs (available upon request)
One (1) complimentary exhibition catalogue
Membership at any level to Carnegie Museums of Pittsburgh is a prerequisite to joining the Warhol Society and comes with its own set of benefits. For more information, please click here.
For more information about Warhol Society, please reach out to us at portraitsociety@warhol.org.
The Warhol Society is graciously sponsored by RBC Wealth Management.
