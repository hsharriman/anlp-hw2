Membership
As a member of the four Carnegie Museums of Pittsburgh, you’ll have exclusive access to members-only events, enjoy store and café discounts, get free reciprocal admission, and much more.
With seven membership levels, including a monthly payment option, you can find the one designed for you.
Your membership support makes it possible for 1.4 million people to experience Carnegie Museums each year through education programs, outreach, and exhibitions, and you also help preserve the vast, treasured collections of our four museums.
