Admission Passes Donation Request Form
We donate complimentary admission passes to regional nonprofits and other community organizations to support fundraising events and raffles. Requests should be made at least four weeks prior to the date of the event. Four passes ($100 value) are offered per request on a first, come, first served basis. Passes are not guaranteed. Please limit your requests to one per organization, per year.
