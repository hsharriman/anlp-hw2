Warhol’s Index (Andy, Old Lyme, Etc.) was compiled and edited by Pat Seymour. Graphic design and layout by Maria Tarquinio. Derek Palladino and Julia Garthwaite developed the website. Matt Gray and Emily Rago provided research and archival assistance. Bryan Conley photographed archival works.
Thank you to Rachel Baron-Horn, Mario Rossero, Rick Armstrong, Charlene Bidula and the staff at The Warhol. Thank you to Anthony Flores at the Gladstone Gallery, Greg Pierce, Dagon James, Alberta Mayo with the Frank J. Thomas archive, Amy Fleming at Gagosian, Adrian Green at Kiwi Arts Group, and a very special thank you to all the contributors.
Notes on film titling:
To identify the several types of film material being referenced in this publication, a combination of italics, quotation marks, and brackets have been used in the title treatments. For the purpose of legibility and consistency, all titles, including those in quotation marks and brackets, are set in italics.
Titles that were given to a film by Warhol are set in italics. Titles in quotation marks taken from the film box, can, leader, or reel itself. Titles in brackets are titles supplied for the sake of clarity, researchers at The Andy Warhol Museum have provided a title—either because none was specified by Warhol, or because no writing or legible marking was ever found by which to identify it.
