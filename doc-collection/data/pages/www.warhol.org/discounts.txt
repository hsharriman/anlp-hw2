Discounts
The Warhol offers several discounts on museum admission. To reserve a timed ticket and redeem a discount, please call 412-237-8300.
Personal Care Attendants (PCA)
A personal care attendant (PCA) accompanying a visitor with a disability receives free General Admission during their visit. PCAs must identify themselves as a PCA at the 1st Floor Ticket Counter with the accompanied care recipient to receive free General Admission. Visitors with a PCA wishing to purchase tickets before their visit can call 412.237.8354.
AAA Members
Present your AAA membership card to receive $1 off admission for up to six people.
Electronic Benefit Transfer (EBT) Card
Present your Electronic Benefit Transfer card (EBT Card) from any state (such as, for example, Pennsylvania ACCESS card, Ohio DIRECTION card or West Virginia MOUNTAIN STATE card, etc.) to receive general admission for up to four people at $1 each. This is not valid with any other offer or group rate. This discount applies to the card issued by each state to administer government benefits payable to the cardholder. This discount does NOT apply to the Port Authority ACCESS card. Visitors must present their EBT Card, along with a matching photo ID, to receive the discount. The program is supported by BNY Mellon.
Active Military Personnel
Active U.S. Military personnel receive 50% off admission for up to four people per military ID; and from Armed Forces Day (the third Saturday of May) through Labor Day they receive free admission for the ID holder and up to five family members as part of the Blue Star Museums Program. Veterans of the U.S. Military receive 50% off admission (one person per ID) throughout the year, and 50% off admission for up to four people on Veterans Day.
Bank of America: Museums on Us
All visitors who present an active Bank of America or Merrill Lynch debit or credit card and valid photo ID, or Bank of America employee ID will receive free admission the first full weekend of each month.
Free Student Admission with Valid ID
This offer applies to individual students at the following universities only. Classes or other student groups of 10 or more seeking group discounts must pre-register for a group visit.
Carlow University
Carnegie Mellon University
Chatham University
Duquesne University (Valid only for Natural/Environmental Science and Honors College students.)
Point Park University
University of Pittsburgh (Valid only during fall and spring semesters.)
Group Visits
Discounts are offered for group visits. Submit a group visit request at least three weeks in advance of visit date.
Reciprocal Members
Carnegie Museums of Pittsburgh members enjoy free reciprocal admission to select museums around the country. Members of other museums, please check with your institution for a list of your reciprocal museums.
WQED Members
Present your WQED membership card to receive a buy one, get one free admission of equal or lesser value.
RAD Pass
The RAD Pass provides Allegheny County residents ages 18 and up with access to free or discounted passes to cultural attractions in our region with a library card from any Allegheny County library. For more information visit radpass.org.
Become a member
Carnegie Museums of Pittsburgh members receive free admission to four museums for an entire year.
