Admission and Hours
Photo by Dean Kaufman
Photo by Dean Kaufman
Museum admission provides access to seven floors of art and archives, rotating exhibitions, free daily events, and more.
Admission
Adults  $25
Seniors (65 and over)  $13
Students (with student ID)   $13
Children (ages 3–18)   $13
Members   Free
Members receive free admission all year long.
Gift cards are available to purchase online.
Tickets can be purchased at the museum or in advance online.
