Courses & Workshops
Emulsion: Adult Print Class
Have you ever wondered how screen prints or graphic tees are made? Now is your chance to learn. Explore Andy Warhol’s printmaking process through the lens of the artist and the techniques that he popularized. In this 4-week course you will learn about Warhol’s early blotted line and stamping techniques, his early 60’s single-layer print technique and his 70’s/80’s multi- layer print and collage techniques. During the course, you will get hands-on practice designing, producing, and printing single and multi- layer screen prints. Space is limited, so please register right away.
12 hours of Act 48 credits available for teachers
