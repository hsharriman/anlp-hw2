Sound Series: @ with special guest Wendy Eisenberg
Monday, January 25, 2027, 8 p.m.
Photo by Ingmar Chen
We welcome indie folk-pop duo @ (pronounced as “at”), consisting of songwriters Victoria Rose and Stone Filipczak. Their debut album Mind Palace Music (2023) on Carpark Records garnered much praise from outlets such as Pitchfork, FADER, Stereogum and Brooklyn Vegan, and NPR described the album as “a fascinating blend of acoustic instruments, folk harmonies, electronic production and a good dose of strangeness.” Their latest album Autosmile, on the iconic indie label 4AD, reflects their unique approach to the psychedelic folk tradition. Guitarist and songwriter, Wendy Eisenberg, opens the evening.
Doors open at 7 p.m.
Co-presented with Telegraph Tree
