FAQ
Here are some commonly asked questions about The Andy Warhol Museum. If you can’t find the information you’re looking for, contact us using the form below and we’ll be happy to help.
Admission Passes Donation Requests
We donate complimentary admission passes to regional nonprofits and other community organizations to support fundraising events and raffles. Requests should be made at least four weeks prior to the date of the event. Four passes ($80 value) are offered per request on a first, come, first served basis. Passes are not guaranteed. Please limit your requests to one per organization, per year.
Request passes
Artist Submissions
Artist submissions should be sent to us via the form below (attention: Curatorial Department) in a professional portfolio format. If you would like the materials returned, please provide a self-addressed stamped envelope with the appropriate amount of postage included. Our curatorial staff reviews all submissions and will reply if they are interested in learning more about the artist and work.
Researching the Collection
To request research access to The Warhol’s art and archives collections, and to view film and video material, please complete an online research request at least six months in advance of the proposed research date.
Warhol films are available to rent in 16mm from The Museum of Modern Art Circulating Film and Video Library.
Submit a research request
Rights and Reproductions and Licensing
For information about reproducing images of the museum’s collection, as well as copyright permissions around Warhol’s film and video, please contact rights and reproduction and photo services at rights@warhol.org.
Inquiries about the copyright permissions around Warhol’s paintings, prints, drawings, photographs, and sculptures should be directed to Artist Rights Society.
Donating or Selling Artwork
If you’re interested in donating or selling an Andy Warhol artwork, please send an inquiry to information@warhol.org or fill out the form below.
Does the museum authenticate artwork?
The Andy Warhol Museum does not authenticate or appraise artwork, objects, or memorabilia. For authentication, appraisal, or other sales-related inquiries, we recommend contacting your local auction house or Sotheby’s or Christies.
