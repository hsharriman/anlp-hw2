Basic Musicianship
Our Basic Musicianship courses serve those who would like to begin study of an instrument in a group setting. These are relatively large capacity enrollments filled on a 1st come 1st served basis.
Music 0120 Basic Musicianship Class Mandolin
Music 0121 Basic Musicianship Class Piano
Music 0122 Basic Musicianship Class Guitar
Music 0123 Basic Musicianship Class Voice
Note that basic musicianship credits can serve Gen Ed requirements but do not count toward the music major or minor.
