Why Donate to the Department of Music?
Gifts to the Department of Music directly impact the daily lives of students and are applied in a variety of ways.
These funds are used to supply equipment, materials, and general educational support to graduate and undergraduates in both academic and performance programs.
Making a gift to the Department of Music is quick and easy.
Steps to Donate Online:
Use the convenient online service, enter your donation amount, and select 'Choose Area(s) to Support.' Select 'Other' and indicate that you are choosing to give to the Department of Music.
Donate Online
To Donate by Check:
You can send your check, made payable to the University of Pittsburgh, to:
University of Pittsburgh
The Dietrich School of Arts and Sciences
Department of Music
Administrator
110 Music Building
4337 Fifth Avenue
Pittsburgh, PA 15260
Indicate “ Department of Music ” on the subject line of your check.
To Donate by Phone:
Call the University of Pittsburgh at 1-800-817-8943 to make your gift by phone.
Thank you very much for your generosity!
