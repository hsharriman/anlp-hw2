African Music and Dance Ensemble
Yamoussa Camara leads the African Music and Dance Ensemble (Mus 0660).
Pitt African Music and Dance Ensemble (PAMDE) introduces students the music and dance of Guinea and West Africa. The class focuses on different styles of drumming, other forms of percussion, song, and dance. Through the music and dance, students learn about the culture of West Africa. The class culminates in a performance by the ensemble.
