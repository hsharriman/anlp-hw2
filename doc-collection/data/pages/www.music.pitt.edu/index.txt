Remembering Dr. James Tare Johnson (Dr. J.)
Dr. James Tare Johnson, known to all as Dr. J. Was a part of the University of Pittsburgh music family for almost 50 years – he made his life count in Pittsburgh, and over the years thousands of lives have been improved through the study and performance of music.

Musicology Faculty Contributes to 2025 AMS Teaching Award–Honored Publication
Professor of Musicology Dr. Olivia Bloechl is among the distinguished scholars who contributed to Global Music History Course Design: A Pedagogical Toolbox with Syllabi, edited by Gavin S. Lee. The volume was honored with the 2025 American Musicological Society Teaching Award, recognizing its outstanding impact on music pedagogy. Congratulations, Dr. Bloechl!

About the Department
The Department of Music is part of the Dietrich School of Arts and Sciences of the University of Pittsburgh. The department serves the University's undergraduate student body, music majors and music minors, and graduate students, as well as the metropolitan area of Pittsburgh and the greater scholarly and musical communities.
