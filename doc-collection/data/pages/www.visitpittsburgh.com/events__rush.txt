Discover Your Persona
November 15, 2026 7:30 PM –10:30 PM
Rush is the pioneering Canadian rock band celebrated for its intricate musicianship, progressive arrangements, and enduring influence on generations of artists. Co-founders Geddy Lee and Alex Lifeson bring the group’s celebrated catalog back to the stage alongside drummer Anika Nilles. Their current Fifty Something tour honors more than five decades of Rush music while paying tribute to late drummer and lyricist Neil Peart. Expect a powerful live presentation built around ambitious compositions, soaring vocals, dynamic guitar work, and the unmistakable bass-driven energy that has defined Rush throughout its remarkable career.
