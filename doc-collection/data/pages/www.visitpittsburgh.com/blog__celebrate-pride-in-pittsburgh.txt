Pittsburgh Pride 2026
Pittsburgh Pride returns June 6 - 7, 2026
Kick off Pride month with a multi-day celebration in Pittsburgh! Pittsburgh Pride 2026 will take place June 6-7, 2026, across multiple Pittsburgh neighborhoods and featuring vendors, a community march and free concerts featuring performances by local, national and international artists.
2026 Pride events will be continuously added as they are announced
Pride Events
2026 Events
Prom
June 5, 2026 | 6-10 p.m.
Step into a night of radiance, celebration, and community at Light the Night at the Courtyard by Marriott Pittsburgh University Center—an 18+ event (21+ to drink) where you shine your brightest! Wear whatever makes you feel powerful, unstoppable, and like the royalty you are—this is your night to show up and show out. The red carpet awaits, the cameras are ready, and the vibes will be unforgettable.
Location: 100 Lytton Ave
The Decades Kiki Ball
June 5, 2026 | 11 p.m. - 2 a.m.
Blumcraft will be hosting its Kiki Ball again this year. Sign up to participate in the event's runway competition with a long list of categories, including streetwear, bizarre, OTA realness, and more. The one-of-a-kind part will also feature music from DJ Icon VJ and DJ Supreme.
Location: 460 Melwood Ave
Queer Balladeer
June 6, 2026 | 3 p.m. - 5 p.m.
Head to Harold's Haunt for a mid-afternoon sea shanty sing-along. Can't get enough? Join them every Saturday for the sing-along.
Location: 142 Grant Ave, Millvale
UNSTOPPABLE
June 6, 2026 | 7:30 p.m. - 9:30 p.m.
Point Park University’s Pittsburgh Playhouse is hosting performances from UNSTOPPABLE, a Pittsburgh queer choir. Their concert will feature music to move you. Grab your tickets before they're sold out.
Location: 350 Forbes Ave
Pittsburgh Pride March & Parade
June 7, 2026 | Line up and gathering begins at 10 a.m. on the corner of the 11th Street and 16th Street in the Strip District
All individuals are welcome to march in the Pittsburgh Pride March and Parade in support of LGBTQ+ rights. No registration is required. Come march together as a united community. Speakers will begin at 11:30 a.m. and the parade steps off at noon and ends at Allegheny Commons Park West.
Drag Brunch at the Fairmont
June 7, 2026 | 11 a.m. - 2 p.m.
This Pride Weekend, enjoy an afternoon of bold expression, vibrant energy, and indulgence at Fairmont Pittsburgh’s highly anticipated Drag Brunch. Guests are invited to the Grand Ballroom for an experience where dazzling performances meet refined brunch offerings. Tickets are $80 per person, including tax and gratuity. Each ticket includes a full brunch buffet, one mimosa, and live entertainment. A cash bar will be available.
Location: 510 Market St.
Pride Night @ the Pirates
June 11, 2026 | 6:40 p.m.
Fireworks will light up the skyline postgame after the annual Pride Night at PNC Park!
Location: 115 Federal St
Vandergrift Pride in the Park
June 14, 2026 | 12 p.m. - 6 p.m.
Vandergrift's Pride celebration, held at Kennedy Park, enters its third year with vendors, music, live performances food and more at this family-friendly event.
Location: 531 Jackson Ave
Pride Millvale
June 20, 2026 | 12 p.m. - 6 p.m.
Entering its sixth year, Pride Millvale shows the inclusivity efforts by our welcoming neighborhoods with a welcoming, educational and celebratory event. Browse lots of local vendors, find great food and more at this FREE festival organized by an all volunteer staff.
Location: 115 Federal St
Forest Hills Pride
June 28, 2026 | 4 p.m. - 9 p.m.
Forest Hills Pride is a grassroots celebration that commenced in April 2024. It is entirely organized by a group of volunteers who wish to provide a supportive, fun, inclusive, and loving event for members of the queer community and their allies. We understand that visibility, raising awareness, and creating an accepting space is crucial to many identities that have been historically marginalized.
Location: 799 Barclay Ave
Fox Chapel Pride in the Park
June 29, 2026 | 3 p.m. - 7 p.m.
Head to the Allegheny RiverTrail Park and celebrate the LGBTQIA+ community with a vendor fair, community art, crafts, hair and nail art, live music and more.
Location: 285 River Ave
