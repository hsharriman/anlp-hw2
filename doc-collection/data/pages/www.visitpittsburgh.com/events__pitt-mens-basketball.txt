Pitt men’s basketball has built a proud postseason history, highlighted by a 2009 NCAA Elite Eight run and a 2008 Big East Tournament title. The Panthers play their home games at the Petersen Events Center, a 12,508-seat arena on the University of Pittsburgh campus that opened in 2002. Catch the team in its Oakland home as it competes in NCAA Division I basketball.
