November 13, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Gym Class Heroes and Lupe Fiasco bring the Back To Basics Tour to Stage AE in a rescheduled Pittsburgh date. Gym Class Heroes are known for blending hip-hop, pop and alternative rock on hits including “Cupid’s Chokehold,” “Stereo Hearts” and “The Fighter.” Lupe Fiasco is a Grammy-winning rapper recognized for intricate, socially conscious songwriting and standout tracks such as “Kick, Push,” “Superstar” and “The Show Goes On.”
