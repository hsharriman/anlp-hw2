Duquesne Dukes men’s basketball brings Atlantic 10 action to Pittsburgh, where the team plays at UPMC Cooper Fieldhouse, named for Duquesne alumnus and basketball trailblazer Chuck Cooper. The Dukes’ history includes a 1955 NIT championship, and in 2024 they won their first Atlantic 10 tournament title before defeating BYU for their first NCAA Tournament victory since 1969. That mix of deep roots and recent March Madness success gives each home game a connection to the program’s storied past and its modern resurgence.
