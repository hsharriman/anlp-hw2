U.S. Open 2025
Join fellow golf aficionados in welcoming the U.S. Open for the 10th time in Pittsburgh, where professional and amateur players compete in one of the major golf championships of the year.
The U.S. Open Championship sets the sprawling green backdrop for unforgettable golf moments. Inviting over 150 players, nail-biting wins are decided over the lowest amount of strokes. Here, 72 holes of stroke play are played through four rounds on an 18-hole course! This multi-day championship brings together casual golf watchers and enthusiastic fans across the Pittsburgh area. Get ready to witness exciting challenges and fun activities at the U.S. Open Championship.

Dates
June 9 - 15, 2025
Location & Parking
The 2025 U.S. Open will take place at Oakmont Country Club (1233 Hulton Rd), which is 14 miles northeast of Pittsburgh. Available parking and shuttle services will be located by the country club at the red and blue lots.
Tickets
Ticket prices vary depending on selected tournament days and admission packages, including access to premium concessions, a shared outdoor space, and optimal views of the matches. Tickets range from $66.70 to $905.20. Explore select admission features, including:
Grounds - $66.70
Trophy Club - $99.70 - $369.20
Reserved Grandstand - $612.70 - $722.70
Champs Pavillion (Thursday & Friday are sold out) - $839 - $1554.70
1895 Club - Sold Out
For more information, purchase your tickets on the official website of the U.S. Open.

U.S. Open Experiences
Along with watching unbelievable rounds of golf, prepare for plenty of tee-rific highlights for all fans and ages!
Featured Premier Players - Watch the most talented golf players such as Scottie Scheffler and Rory McIlroy participate in the U.S. Open.
Golf-Centered Activities - U.S. Open fun includes activity stations such as the Ally Savings Bucket Challenge, Swing Experience by Lexus, and Junior Experiences Featuring the First Tee.
Special Merch & Memorabilia - Make your way to the Merchandise Pavillion and peruse through more than 50 different golf brands, from one-of-a-kind apparel to U.S. Open-themed accessories.
Mouthwatering Food & Drinks - Delectable options include Pittsburgh delights such as kielbasa sliders, pierogies, pepperoni rolls, and Oakmon Bakery cookies, and so much more. Enjoy diverse options from around the world like sushi and poke bowls. Hydration stations will also be available throughout the country club.
Bonus Activity: Looking for things to do before and after the U.S. Open? Schedule a visit to the Heinz History Center, where you’ll experience the USGA Experience and Museum exhibit. Learn about rare artifacts, enjoy interactive activities, and expand your trivia knowledge about the USGA.
