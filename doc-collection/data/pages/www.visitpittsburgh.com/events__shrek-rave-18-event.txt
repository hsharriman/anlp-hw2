November 5, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Shrek Rave is an 18+ dance party built around the world of the 2001 animated film. The event combines DJ sets spanning techno, house, EDM and drum & bass with Shrek-themed remixes, large-scale character visuals and live appearances inspired by the franchise. Its signature soundtrack moments include crowd singalongs to “I’m a Believer,” while themed costumes, giveaways and drinks extend the playful swamp-party concept beyond the dance floor.
