Pittsburgh World of Wheels 2027
Rev your engines.
The 66th Annual World of Wheels is rolling back into Pittsburgh for three full days of custom cars, hot rods, motorcycles, and showstopping builds. It is one of the most beloved car shows in the country and a must-see Pittsburgh car show in 2027 for fans of chrome, horsepower, and serious craftsmanship.

Location and Parking
David L. Lawrence Convention Center
1000 Fort Duquesne Boulevard
Pittsburgh, PA 15222
The DLCC features a 700-car parking garage with a 7' 8" clearance, accessible from 10th Street. More details are available through Alco Parking Corporation.
For real-time downtown parking availability, visit ParkPGH.org.
The Convention Center also offers bicycle racks in several locations both inside Level One of the garage and along the exterior on Fort Duquesne Boulevard.

What to Expect
Events & Special Appearances
World of Wheels is America’s premier custom car show series, known for bringing together the best hot rods, custom cars, vintage and classic trucks, motorcycles, and award-winning builds. Every year features new surprises, special guests, and interactive displays that make this event a favourite for car lovers of all ages.
You’ll also find unique highlights like the Mega Morph transforming robot car, WWE Hall of Famers Brother Ray and Decon, Catherine Bach and John Schneider from The Dukes of Hazzard, and Lou Gramm, co-founder and vocalist of the legendary rock band Foreigner. More celebrity appearances and activities will be announced as the event gets closer

Student Career Day | January 22
Student Career Day is a major part of the World of Wheels experience and an exciting opportunity for the next generation of automotive professionals. Launched in 2006, this program introduces students to real careers in automotive design, engineering, manufacturing, marine tech, motorcycles, welding, small engines, and more.
High school and college groups with chaperones are invited to attend. Students participate in hands-on activities and hear from industry experts who share insights, career guidance, and personal stories from across the automotive world.
Student Career Day participants receive a discounted admission price of $15 per ticket, valid only for the day of the program. Registration is required by January 12, 2027.
