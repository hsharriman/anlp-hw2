Discover Your Persona
November 12, 2026 7:30 PM –10:30 PM
Josh Gondelman brings a writer’s precision and a stand-up comic’s timing to comedy shaped by sharp observations and warmth. He spent five years writing for Last Week Tonight with John Oliver, earning four Emmy Awards with the team, then became head writer and executive producer of Desus & Mero. His stand-up specials include People Pleaser and Positive Reinforcement, and he’s also a regular voice on NPR’s Wait Wait… Don’t Tell Me.
