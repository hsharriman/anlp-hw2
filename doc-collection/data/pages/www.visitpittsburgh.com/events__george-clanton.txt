Discover Your Persona
November 6, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
George Clanton is an electronic musician, singer-songwriter and label owner known for blending synth-pop, trip-hop, alternative rock and hazy, nostalgic production into an immersive live experience. His work balances vintage textures with a distinctly futuristic sensibility, making him a compelling draw for listeners who enjoy left-field pop, dance music and underground electronic culture. In addition to his solo catalog, Clanton has collaborated with artists including Nick Hexum and TV Girl, with the latter partnership resulting in the 2024 project Fauxllennium. His performances offer a vivid, genre-fluid showcase of melodic songwriting, atmospheric electronics and DIY artistry.
