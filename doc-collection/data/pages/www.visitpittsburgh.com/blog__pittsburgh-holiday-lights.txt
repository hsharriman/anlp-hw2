Pittsburgh Holiday Lights
Experience the sparkle this 2026 holiday season in Pittsburgh!
There’s something magical about the holiday season – especially with a Christmas light show in Pittsburgh. Magnificent holiday displays spectacular decorations and, of course, glimmering luminescence add to the sparkle of the already beautiful city.
Whether you’re in the comfort of your car or bundled up outside, check out this list of places to enjoy holiday lights in and around Pittsburgh.
Pittsburgh Holiday Lights Map
Holiday Lights Outside of Pittsburgh
Downtown Pittsburgh Holiday Displays
Downtown Pittsburgh has no shortage of holiday fun, including stunning decorations and light displays. The Cultural District will host the Peoples Gas Holiday Market with construction happening in Market Square. The 65-foot-tall PPG Place Christmas Tree, located in the center of the ice rink, is a sensational site to see. Another light display sure to dazzle can be found in Mellon Square.
Stroll through the Cultural District and experience larger-than-life light projections on various Downtown buildings. Book a Walk the Burgh Holiday Lights Tour to fully enjoy Downtown’s festive ambiance while learning about the city’s history.
Phipps Conservatory and Botanical Gardens
TBA 2026
Walk through a glowing glasshouse this season at Phipps Conservatory and Botanical Gardens! Experience Phipps’ Holiday Magic! Winter Flower Show and Light Garden with marvelous poinsettias and holiday trees illuminated with sparkling ornaments and tree toppers, a dazzling, icy display in the Victoria pond room, and more.
This year, delve into the whimsical theme of "Wild Winter," where twinkling creatures and vibrant floral displays await. Marvel at more than 20 varieties of poinsettias. Wander through the Winter Light Garden and take in a botanical celebration of festive cheer. Must-see spectacles include a show-stopping orb tree shining above glowing icebergs and topiary polar bears and penguins participating in classic winter activities.
Check the Phipps official website for more information on tickets for Phipps members and for the general public.

Dustin McGrew
Dazzling Nights
TBA 2026
Pittsburgh Botanic Garden promises to immerse you in the wonder of the holidays for their Dazzling Nights event, perfect for families, friends and date nights. Have fun stepping on interactive lights, explore a forest of light and fog and journey through a tunnel of illuminated stars before taking a photo to remember at their towering tree.
If you get cold or hungry on your trek, don't worry. Toasty seasonal beverages are available along with holiday inspired nibbles. Remember to dress warm and wear good shoes, as Dazzling Nights takes place entirely outdoors.

Kennywood Holiday Lights
Fridays-Sundays | Nov. 13, 2026 - January 3, 2027
You’ve been to Kennywood for summer family fun and the spooky season Phantom Fall Fest, but have you enjoyed the park for the holidays yet?
Make your season sparkle with a visit to Kennywood during Holiday Lights! This festive event features more than one million twinkling lights, holiday-themed food and drinks and the tallest Christmas tree in the state. Visitors can stroll through the park and enjoy select rides, seasonal entertainment and a visit with Santa.
Purchase a 2026 Gold or Platinum Season Pass and receive unlimited Holiday Lights admission for this winter season.
Credit |
Jake Mysliwczyk via Kennywood
Wild Illuminations at Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium
TBA
Walk-thru the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium grounds at night for a festive holiday excursion during Wild Illuminations! Marvel as the zoo turns into a winter wonderland with dazzling lights, impressive silk-and-steel lanterns, sweet bites, and visits by Santa Claus.
Ticket sales for the public begin soon.

Holiday Lights Outside of Pittsburgh
Olgebay Festival of Lights
Nov. 5, 2026 - Jan. 3, 2027
A short drive from Pittsburgh in Wheeling, West Virginia, is Olgebay’s Winter Festival of Lights, an incredible drive-through attraction and holiday-light experience!
The Winter Festival of Lights is one of the nation’s largest holiday light shows featuring 300 acres of dazzling lights and over 100 lighted displays. Drive along the six-mile route and tune in to a variety of audio experiences for an unforgettable holiday adventure.
Pro-Tip: If you're looking to elevate your drive-through experience, we recommend trying a guided trolley tour through the Festival of Lights!

Overly’s Country Christmas in Westmoreland
TBA for 2026
In the hectic rush of the holidays, it’s time to slow down, get out of your car, and stretch your legs! This Westmoreland County tradition has been operating since 1956. Enjoy this walk-through Christmas village for good, as Overly Country Christmas exists to raise money for charitable causes.
Though the Overly Country Christmas Village may not be as extravagant as some other displays on this list, it’s still worth a stroll through, especially if you’re looking to avoid the huge crowds some of the other events on this list can draw. Like any good country Christmas, Overly’s Country Christmas Village is simple and comforting. Check back for more information about the tickets for this spectacular holiday event.
