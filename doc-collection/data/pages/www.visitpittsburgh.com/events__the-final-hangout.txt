Discover Your Persona
October 31, 2026 6:00 PM –10:00 PM
Prepare to party like there’s no tomorrow. The Plaza x ScareHouse have saved the best for last.
Step aboard The Plaza’s haunted pirate ship as it reaches its final destination. Pittsburgh’s biggest Halloween Party has docked on the North Shore and the Halloween Hangout comes to its close with one epic night. This Halloween, the ultimate spooky celebration will take to the high seas as you dance with the living and party with the dead.
Get ready for the wildest and wickedest party Pittsburgh has ever seen– it’s Halloween Night brought to you by the masterminds at Scarehouse. Mysterious mermaids, mischievous pirates, ghostly sailors, and unexpected creatures will take over the night, with surprises lurking around every corner. This larger-than-life Halloween celebration is packed with themed food and drinks, live entertainment, immersive photo moments, outrageous characters, and plenty of Halloween mayhem.
Come to SLAY. The Plaza will be awarding prizes for the night’s best costumes, get onboard with the theme or get creative and dress to kill – it’s your creativity that will count.
*Haunted food & beverages will satisfy your appetite for the supernatural and will be available separately for purchase.
