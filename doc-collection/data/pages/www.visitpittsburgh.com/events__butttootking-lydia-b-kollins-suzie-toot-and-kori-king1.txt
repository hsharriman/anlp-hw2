October 31, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
ButtTootKing featuring Lydia B Kollins, Suzie Toot, and Kori King is a high-energy drag and performance showcase known for bold personalities, comedic flair, and vibrant stage entertainment. Lydia B Kollins brings charismatic humor, theatrical style, and expressive performances that blend camp, comedy, and crowd interaction into unforgettable live moments. Suzie Toot adds a playful and energetic presence through colorful fashion, sharp wit, and dynamic performances inspired by modern drag and pop culture entertainment. Kori King rounds out the lineup with confident stage presence, high-impact choreography, and glamorous performances that celebrate creativity, individuality, and performance artistry. Together, the show creates a lively and interactive experience filled with music, comedy, dazzling visuals, and nonstop entertainment energy.
