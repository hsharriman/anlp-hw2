Pittsburgh Renaissance Festival
Huzzah! Grab your corsets and cloaks and plan your visit to the Pittsburgh Renaissance Festival
2026 Pittsburgh Renaissance Festival Information
Dates
Weekends from August 29 - October 4, 2026 & Labor Day Monday
Location
112 Renaissance Lane West Newton, PA 15089 (affectionately known as Morelandshire)
Times
10:30 a.m. - 6:30 p.m.
Admission
Adult – $27.00
Child – $12.00
Kids Under 5 Free
*Single-day admission, tickets are non-refundable
Save money by purchasing tickets online
Featured Shows
The Pittsburgh Renaissance Festivals hosts a variety of shows throughout each weekend, so you're sure to find a few you'll want to check out. Here's what's on tap for 2026:
The Knights of Noble Cause
Jousting three times daily. Join the knights for a return to the days of honor and chivalry.
The Washing Well Wenches
Wet dirty women and good clean fun.
Tohmfoolery
Daring and delicate acts of balance and skill.
The Crimson Pirates
Gorgeous melodies and tunes from the high seas.
Knotty Nauticals
Terribly clever and wickedly talented, these two will burn your socks off.
Cirqa Brava
Beauty, grace, high flying stunts and a comedic flair.
The Renaissance Men
Dynamic duo of comedy and sword fighting.
Reidiculous
Master of the whips Reid is here to shock and amaze. Four times a day on the Greek Stage.
Garry Siler
Master minstrel Garry is back at the pirates pub, final 5 weekends only.
The CRAIC Show
Medieval rock and roll. It's an electric experience for all ages.
The Witch Trial
Making Fun of Horrible History! Join famed tragedian Ned Alleyn, as he takes a stab at comedy by flipping the script on the classic Witch Trial!
Blades and Bravery
Daring deeds of dexterity and danger. Cy the sword swallower and Sarah are back for a show you won’t want to miss.
Nature's Educators
Learn about the fantastic birds of prey that inhabit our world, their care, and how to protect them.
Topolino Sisters
Interactive stories for children.
Chaste Treasure
These lovely ladies aren’t exactly what you expect at first glance. With Naughty and Bawdy tunes five times a day at the Pirates Pub stage.
The Angels
Singing, Storytelling, and Swordfighting. These Heroines in Disguise will recount tales of their exploits.
Dragonfire
Fire breathing, manipulation, and comedy. A fan favorite for many years, wilsome is back and better than ever.
Sarah Mullen - Harpist
Enjoy the heavenly sounds of harp music wafting through the trees. With an extensive repertoire of songs from around the world, you are sure to enjoy your experience. She can be found in the lanes
