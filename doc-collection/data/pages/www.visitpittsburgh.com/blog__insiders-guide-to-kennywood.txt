Insider's Guide: Kennywood
Experience everything new and beloved at Kennywood, a USA Today 10Best Theme Park celebrating 126 seasons of fun
Combining modern thrills and classic rides, complete your summer with a trip to Kennywood! Gather your friends and family for unforgettable fun.
Whether you're visiting for the first time or a lifelong fan of the park, follow our guide to what's new, special events and more. For even more foodie fun, check out our other blog on where to eat at Kennywood.
Kennywood Junction
Explore this small steel town neighborhood full of pint-sized thrills on five rides that families can experience together. Practice your firefighting skills with local fire departments, help Kenny transport cargo, take to the skies with Parker on a helicopter tour, and hop aboard the historic Olde Kennywood Railroad!
Bites and Pints Food & Drink Festival
The Bites and Pints Food & Drink Festival is back at Kennywood for another summer of tasteful treats. Join in on the fun for chef-inspired tastings of international cuisine, specialty cocktails, craft beer, and wine. As you travel around the Lagoon, you’ll discover dozens of savory and sweet food and drink offerings to enjoy. Be bold, try something new, grab a Bite Card to mix & match samples and thrill your taste buds!
NEW in 2025: sample the flavors of Cuba and Spain!
First Time Visitors Read This
As I read over this blog, I felt like maybe first-timers would need a little more of an intro to this beloved park, so I made this section specifically for you. Kennywood is Pittsburgh's theme park, with classic rides and a decidedly quirky atmosphere that makes it unlike anywhere else I've ever visited.
The rides are absolutely incredible. The Phantom's Revenge is arguably the greatest coaster on the planet and nothing beats the thrill of popping out of your seat on the dips of the Jackrabbit. There's the last Turtle ride in the world and an interactive Noah's Ark walkthrough experience that delight young and old alike.
But it's also the little things, like taking photos with the Cowboy Joe statue on a park bench or delighting in the kitsch of the pre-ride news broadcast on the Exterminator that makes everyone fall in love with this park over and over again.
There's no right way to do Kennywood and every blogger and influencer is going to have their own great recommendations. But my advice is to slow down and take it all in. Grab a corn dog, look for Laughin' Sal in the arcade, compete against your friends on the Racer, and find the reasons why you love Kennywood just like we do!
Season Passholders
Secure endless summer fun with a Season Pass! Multiple pass holder options are available, including a Gold Pass good for unlimited visits to Kennywood all year (including special events) and the Platinum Pass which allows entry to all three sister parks (Kennywood, Sandcastle and Idlewild) all year, plus additional discounts and incentives.
Plan Your Visit
Kennywood is open for the 2026 season. Visit kennywood.com for varying park hours and to purchase tickets online and view the full events calendar.
Insider’s Tips
Avoid Measurements with Height Check
Have your kids grown since last summer? Be sure to stop by one of Kennywood’s Service Centers for a height check! Located near Noah’s Ark and at the Front Gate, kids are measured and provided a color-coded wristband with their “official” height. When entering a ride, show the operator your kid’s wristband to avoid measurements for every ride.
Indulge in Iconic Potato Patch Fries
A trip to Kennywood isn’t complete without a stop at The Potato Patch! The “Patch” serves homemade fries with fresh cut Idaho potatoes. Top off your fries with melted cheddar cheese, bacon, vinegar, gravy, or seasoning salts. Eat like a local with Potato Patch Fries – food you can get Only in Pittsburgh!

Ride One of the Best Coasters on the Planet
Despite record-breaking coasters popping up across the world, the Phantom's Revenge continues to be ranked one of the top roller coasters year after year. You'll travel 85 mph through an incredible 232-ft drop as you crisscross the Thunderbolt and pass right over main areas of the park for this truly unbelievable ride! It's worth the wait for the front seat views, but the back seat provides the wildest ride.
Rain Check
Many visitors will avoid a day calling for rain, which means these days tend to be less busy. Many of the rides still run if the weather isn't too bad. If the weather takes a turn for the worse causing the park to close early, rain checks are issued for guests that present their ticket stubs. This means you can get some riding in when it isn't busy, and come back another day if the park does close!
