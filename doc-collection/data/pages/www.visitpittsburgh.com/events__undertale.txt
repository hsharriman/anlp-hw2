November 5, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Undertale – The Determination Symphony World Tour brings the acclaimed indie game’s memorable music to the concert stage in an immersive live performance. A 25-piece orchestra performs newly arranged themes while gameplay footage appears on a screen above the stage, guiding audiences through the Underground’s distinctive locations, characters, battles, and emotional moments. Created around the music of Toby Fox’s UNDERTALE, this all-ages experience offers fans and newcomers a dramatic way to encounter the game’s celebrated soundtrack. The Pittsburgh engagement is presented by Mammoth Live at the Benedum Center.
