Press Tours
VisitPITTSBURGH welcomes travel journalists to join our press tours. Experience all that this beautiful city has to offer and use it to your writer's advantage.
You're invited to visit Pittsburgh for our exciting press tours.
See firsthand why Harper's BAZAAR named Pittsburgh as one of the best places to travel in 2017, saying "we've gone as far as to dub the 'Burgh as America's Most Underrated City."
"There's never been a better time to eat in this city," says Pittsburgh Chef Brent Young for Food & Wine Magazine.
VisitPITTSBURGH'S press tours are designed to give journalists an insider's look at the city that is on everyone's hot list!
For additional information on our press tours or to RSVP, contact us.
Visitation Guidelines
The Visit Pittsburgh communications team stands ready to provide a full range of services for travel journalists like fact checking, hotels, and more.
