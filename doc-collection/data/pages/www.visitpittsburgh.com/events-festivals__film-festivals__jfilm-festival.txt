JFilm Festival
Lights, camera, action! The annual JFilm Festival is rolling into Pittsburgh.
EVENT DETAILS
EXPERIENCES
MOVIE SHOWTIMES
2026 Dates Announced
Film Pittsburgh’s JFilm Festival, the largest Jewish cultural event in the region, will return to theaters April 30–May 10, 2026. For 33 years, JFilm has showcased Jewish-themed, independent feature films from around the globe.
More information will be available in 2026.
Discover the thrill of great storytelling at the JFilm Festival! For 11 unforgettable days, Film Pittsburgh will transport you around the globe through a dazzling array of Jewish-themed independent cinema. With hilarious comedies, provocative documentaries, and dramas that will leave you breathless, this is one film festival you can't afford to miss. Begin your cinematic journey at the electrifying Opening Night Reception at Carnegie Music Hall, then dive deeper through Q&As with filmmakers, and attend Film Schmoozes to discuss the films with fellow audience members. Grab your tickets and immerse yourself in this cinematic celebration of Jewish narratives and culture.
Event Details
When & Where
Film Pittsburgh’s JFilm Festival, the largest Jewish cultural event in the region, will return to theaters April 30–May 10, 2026. For 33 years, JFilm has showcased Jewish-themed, independent feature films from around the globe.
Admission
Opening reception tickets, single showing tickets, virtual tickets, and festival passes will be available for purchase on the JFilm website. Tickets will also be available for purchase at the venue 30 minutes prior to showtime.
Accessibility
All venues are wheelchair accessible and ASL or audio description is available upon request with advance notice. Please note: The Oaks Theater does not have an accessible restroom but one is available at Fox’s Pizza, the restaurant next door.

Experiences
Opening Reception – JFilm Festival kicks off with a special opening reception of Left Alone Rhapsody: The Musical Memoir of Pianist John Bayless, followed by a live performance, Q&A with the director and main subject, and a dessert reception.
Q&As with Film Directors – After select film showings, stay for a Q&A session with the film’s director to learn more about their process and experience creating the film.
Film Shmooze –  Talk about films with fellow audience members through engaging discussions led by University of Pittsburgh, Jewish Studies Program.
Virtual Showings – Watch films from the comfort of your home with virtual showings for 6 films available throughout the duration of the festival.
