Get Ready to Line Dance with Michelle from Ballroom Connection!
Put on your dancing shoes and join us for an afternoon of fun, laughter, and fantastic music at The Hula Bar on Saturday, June 8th at 2 PM!
Dance to popular hits like Uptown Funk and the nostalgic Macarena—no experience needed, just a willingness to have a great time!
Sip on wine or enjoy our island pitcher specials to loosen up and let the rhythm take over.
Whether you're a seasoned dancer or just looking for a fun way to spend your Saturday, this is the perfect event to let loose and make new friends.
Where:The Hula Bar
When: June 8th at 2 PM
No cover—just bring your energy and excitement!
Tag your friends and let’s make it a party! See you on the dance floor!
