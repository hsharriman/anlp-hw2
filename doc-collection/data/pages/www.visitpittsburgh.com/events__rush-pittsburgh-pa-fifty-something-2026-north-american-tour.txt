November 15, 2026 7:30 PM –10:30 PM
Rush founding members Geddy Lee and Alex Lifeson have added Pittsburgh to their historic Fifty Something 2026 North American Tour.
The rare, heavily anticipated arena show celebrates 50 years of Rush's music while paying a heartfelt tribute to their late, legendary drummer Neil Peart. Taking over the drumming duties for this monumental tour is acclaimed percussionist Anika Nilles.
