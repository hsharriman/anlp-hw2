Discover Your Persona
November 2, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
GloRilla is a rapper known for her bold delivery, high-energy performances, and gritty Southern hip-hop style that blends Memphis rap traditions with modern trap production. She gained widespread attention through viral hits and unapologetic lyrics that emphasize confidence, resilience, independence, and streetwise storytelling. Her music combines booming beats, commanding vocals, and infectious hooks that have quickly established her as one of the breakout voices in contemporary hip-hop. With a fearless stage presence and rapidly growing influence in rap culture, GloRilla has become one of the most exciting rising stars in modern rap music.
