Discover Your Persona
November 8, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Donny Benét is an Australian singer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist who has cultivated a devoted international following through his distinctive blend of synth-pop, disco, funk, and soft-rock influences inspired by the sounds of the 1980s. Known for his charismatic stage persona, retro aesthetic, and tongue-in-cheek sense of humor, Benét creates music that pays homage to vintage dance grooves while maintaining a contemporary appeal. His albums, including The Don, Mr Experience, and Le Piano, showcase his talents as a bassist, producer, and songwriter, combining infectious rhythms, silky melodies, and playful storytelling. While his image often embraces irony and nostalgia, the musicianship behind his work is highly regarded, with intricate arrangements and polished production earning praise from critics and fans alike. Through energetic live performances and a catalog filled with stylish, groove-driven songs, Donny Benét has established himself as one of the most unique and entertaining figures in modern independent music.
