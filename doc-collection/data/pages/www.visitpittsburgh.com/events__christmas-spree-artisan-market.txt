Kick Off the Holiday Season at Memorial Park Church’s annual Christmas Spree! Discover thousands of unique, handcrafted treasures made by talented local artisans. Whether you're shopping for loved ones or treating yourself, you're sure to find something special for everyone on your list! Enjoy mouthwatering bites from a variety of popular food trucks and the Spree Eatery! Mark your calendar now!
