Discover Your Persona
November 11, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
little image is a Dallas-formed alternative-pop trio featuring Jackson Simmons, Brandon Walters and Troy Bruner. Their sound expands pop-rock beyond a traditional guitar-bass-drums setup with synths, electronic textures and dance-rock energy. The band’s 2023 debut, SELF TITLED, produced the No. 1 alternative-radio hit “OUT OF MY MIND.” Their sophomore album, KILL THE GHOST, released in 2026, explores transformation and resilience through tracks including “KILL THE GHOST,” “THE REAPER” and “NOVOCAINE.” little image has toured with Panic! At The Disco, Bad Suns, Joywave and Colony House, and appeared at Shaky Knees and Wonderbus.
