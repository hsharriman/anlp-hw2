Discover Your Persona
November 10, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
A Killer’s Confession is a Cleveland-based alternative metal band led by vocalist and founder Waylon Reavis, formerly of Mushroomhead. The group blends melodic hard rock with industrial textures, nu-metal riffs and emotionally charged lyrics. Its recorded work includes the debut album Unbroken (2017), Remember (2021), and the conceptual Victim 1 (2024) and Victim 2 (2025). The band has also collaborated with Korn’s Brian “Head” Welch and Mudvayne/HELLYEAH vocalist Chad Gray, including the track “Tell Your Soul.”
