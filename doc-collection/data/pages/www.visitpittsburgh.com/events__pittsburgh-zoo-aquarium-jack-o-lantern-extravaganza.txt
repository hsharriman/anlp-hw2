October 25, 2026 to November 1, 2026 6:30 PM –10:00 PM
The Jack O'Lantern Extravaganza returns for fall 2026! Join the Pittsburgh Zoo and Aquarium for a fall-tastic night of family fun! Features include last years night train, spooky scavenger hunts, seasonal food & drink, and thousands of hand carved jack o' lanterns.
21+ Add-on activations are also available on Thursday nights, join us for a mixology class, a comedy night, or our Disco's UNdead Halloween Party!
