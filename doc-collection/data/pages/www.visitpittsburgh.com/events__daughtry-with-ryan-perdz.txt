Discover Your Persona
October 26, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Daughtry is the multi-platinum rock band fronted by acclaimed singer-songwriter Chris Daughtry, whose powerful vocals and emotionally resonant songwriting have made the group a mainstay of modern rock since its formation in 2006. Emerging after Chris Daughtry's breakthrough appearance on American Idol, the band quickly achieved commercial success with hit songs such as “It's Not Over,” “Home,” “Feels Like Tonight,” and “Waiting for Superman.” Over the years, Daughtry has evolved from a radio-rock powerhouse into a more dynamic act that incorporates elements of hard rock, alternative rock, and contemporary production while maintaining its signature melodic style.
Joining the show is Ryan Perdz, a rising singer-songwriter known for blending heartfelt lyrics, engaging stage presence, and a modern approach to rock and pop-influenced music. Together, Daughtry and Ryan Perdz offer audiences a compelling mix of established chart-topping hits and emerging talent, creating a concert experience built around powerful vocals, authentic storytelling, and memorable live performances.
