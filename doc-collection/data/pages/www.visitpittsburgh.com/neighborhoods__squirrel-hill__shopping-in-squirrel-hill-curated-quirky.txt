Shopping in Squirrel Hill: Curated & Quirky
Squirrel Hill is a "walker’s paradise" for shoppers. Forget the mall, these storefronts are filled with decades of expertise and one-of-a-kind finds.
Squirrel Hill isn’t just a place to eat; it’s one of Pittsburgh’s premier destinations for independent retail. Stretching along Forbes and Murray Avenues, the business district is a "walker’s paradise" where you can find everything from rare board games and vintage vinyl to high-end footwear and globally sourced gifts. Whether you're a serious collector or just looking for the perfect Pittsburgh souvenir, you'll find it here.
The Anchors: Landmarks of Squirrel Hill
Some shops have been part of the neighborhood's fabric for nearly a century, offering a level of expertise and service you won't find at a big-box store.
Little’s Shoes | 5850 Forbes Ave.
A true neighborhood legend since 1932. Little’s has thrived for generations by offering an incredible selection from UGG and Birkenstock to New Balance, all paired with the kind of old-school customer service that makes you feel like a regular on your very first visit.
S.W. Randall Toyes & Giftes | 5856 Forbes Ave.
Step into Pittsburgh’s oldest and largest specialty toy store. It’s a literal wonderland for kids (and kids at heart), featuring thousands of treasures including classic LEGO sets, model trains, and specialized puzzles that spark the imagination the moment you walk through the door.
Hobbies, Games, & Hidden Gems
If you have a niche passion, Squirrel Hill is your unofficial headquarters.
Games Unlimited | 5876 Forbes Ave.
A cornerstone of the gaming community for over 45 years. This isn't your average toy store; they specialize in "unusual" and obscure board games, RPGs, and one of the largest puzzle selections in the country. The staff are true experts, always ready to help you find your next tabletop obsession.
Amazing Books & Records | 5858 Forbes Ave.
A book lover's dream where you might walk in for a novel and leave with a rare jazz record. They offer a unique "buy three, get one free" deal on used books and even host legendary late-night hours on Saturday nights (after sunset) with a welcoming, communal vibe.
Jerry’s Records | 1913 Murray Ave.
A world-renowned destination for vinyl collectors. With hundreds of thousands of albums in stock, flipping through the crates at Jerry’s is a rite of passage for music fans visiting Pittsburgh. It’s the kind of place where you can spend hours lost in music history.
Globally Sourced & Curated Finds
Reflecting the neighborhood's international spirit, these shops offer a window into craftsmanship and culture from around the world.
Ebisu Japanese Life Store | 5832 Forbes Ave.
This vibrant lifestyle shop transports you straight to Tokyo right in the heart of Squirrel Hill. Browse a massive selection of authentic Japanese snacks, high-end skincare, and quirky "Kawaii" gifts that make for the perfect unique souvenir.
Ten Thousand Villages | 5820 Forbes Ave.
A fair-trade retailer featuring artisan-crafted home decor, jewelry, and gifts from over 30 countries. It’s the perfect place to shop with a conscience, knowing each purchase supports makers in developing communities worldwide.
Avalon Exchange | 5858 Forbes Ave.
A curated resale shop for the fashion-forward. This is where local style-seekers go to find high-quality vintage pieces and modern designer labels at a fraction of the original price.
Riverstone Books | 5825 Forbes Ave.
A beautifully curated independent bookstore that serves as a cozy community hub. It’s an inviting spot to get lost in a new release or browse their great selection of local greeting cards and literary gifts.
A Shopper’s Planning Tip
The "Walker's" Strategy: Start at the corner of Forbes and Murray and work your way outward. Most of these shops are within a 10-minute stroll of each other.
Stop for a Rest: The neighborhood is dotted with benches, and many shops, like Riverstone Books, offer intimate spaces to browse and take a breather.
Parking & Transit: If you’re planning a big haul, there are four public parking lots within a block of the main intersection, or you can hop on any 61-series bus to land right in the center of the shopping district.
