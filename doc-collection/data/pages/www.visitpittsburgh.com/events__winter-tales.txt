November 13, 2026 to January 18, 2027
The Winter Tales immersive experience is back by popular demand, transforming the McKenna Gallery into a sensory journey through a magical winter world!
Created by award-winning OLO Creative Farm, this one-of-a-kind installation brings to life stunning works from The Westmoreland’s collection using multi-projection, surround sound, and delicate animated effects like mist, falling snow, and glistening frost. Featuring artists such as William Coventry Wall, Charles W. (Bud) Gibbons, Henry Koerner, and Ron Donoughe, the experience seamlessly blends art and technology. Visitors will feel as though they’ve stepped inside the paintings, surrounded by winter landscapes from Greensburg, Pittsburgh, the countryside, and beyond.
Select original winter works from the collection are also on view.
On view during regular Museum hours. Admission is free; no reservation required.
