Host an Event in Pittsburgh
You can help the economic recovery of Pittsburgh’s hospitality industry.
Pittsburgh is a major destination for events and meetings. But this year most of the events have been cancelled or rescheduled. So we need your help in getting our rebound started and positively impacting the local economy.
Let Us Help
Local pride plays a huge part in people selecting the city, and Pittsburgh's Business Events Team hopes that you will champion the cause of bringing meeting and convention business to Pittsburgh. We ask you to contact your employer and the organizations and associations you are affiliated with and encourage them to bring their next meeting or convention to Pittsburgh or to keep your events in Pittsburgh. You will be making an invaluable contribution, both to the local economy and to our community-wide effort, to tell the world why Pittsburgh is an ideal destination for business and pleasure.
Business Events Makes it Easy
Pittsburgh's Business Events Team is here to support your efforts. We provide complimentary services to make bringing a meeting to Pittsburgh easy! Here are some of our services:
Sending leads to the hotels and the convention center to check availability for your meeting or event.
Putting together Pittsburgh bid packets and making presentations to associations and companies to convince them to meet in Pittsburgh.
Conducting personal site inspections so meeting planners can see our facilities and what our city has to offer their group.
Working with the hospitality community to request discounted rates on transportation and other convention services.
Providing visitors guides, event listings, maps and other materials to convention delegates.
Providing housing assistance for city-wide conventions.
Providing promotional materials such as videos, power point presentations, and other online information.
Providing registration assistance for conventions when needed.
