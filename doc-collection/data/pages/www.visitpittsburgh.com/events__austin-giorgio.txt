Discover Your Persona
November 13, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Austin Giorgio is a singer-songwriter whose soulful vocals, polished musicianship, and heartfelt songwriting have earned him a growing reputation in contemporary pop and R&B. After gaining national attention through his memorable performances on The Voice, Giorgio continued to develop his artistry by blending elements of soul, pop, jazz, and acoustic music into a style that feels both timeless and refreshingly modern. His songs explore themes of love, self-discovery, relationships, and personal growth, pairing emotionally honest lyrics with smooth melodies and expressive vocal performances. Whether performing intimate acoustic sets or full-band concerts, Giorgio captivates audiences with his warm stage presence, vocal versatility, and genuine connection to every song he performs. As he continues to release new music and expand his audience, Austin Giorgio is establishing himself as an exciting emerging artist with a distinctive voice and a bright future in today's singer-songwriter landscape.
