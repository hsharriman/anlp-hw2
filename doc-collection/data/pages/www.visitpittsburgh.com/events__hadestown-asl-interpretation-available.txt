Discover Your Persona
November 1, 2026 1:00 PM –4:00 PM
In this ASL-interpreted performance of Hadestown, the myths of Orpheus and Eurydice meet the story of Hades and Persephone in a Tony- and Grammy-winning musical by singer-songwriter Anaïs Mitchell and director Rachel Chavkin. Folk, jazz and blues shape a score that follows dreamers on a journey to the underworld, setting love and hope against industry and doubt. This November 1 matinee at the Benedum Center is part of the touring production’s Pittsburgh engagement.
