November 5, 2026 7:30 PM –9:30 PM
Join us for a magical evening at the Byham Theater in Pittsburgh as we present ""Matilda - The Musical - Pittsburgh""! This enchanting production brings Roald Dahl's beloved story to life with vibrant performances, catchy songs, and a heartwarming message about the power of imagination and resilience. The Byham Theater, with its charming ambiance and stunning architecture, sets the perfect stage for this unforgettable theatrical experience.
Gather your family and friends for a night filled with laughter, inspiration, and a little bit of mischief as Matilda and her friends navigate the ups and downs of childhood. Don't miss your chance to witness this captivating show that promises to delight audiences of all ages. Secure your seats now for ""Matilda - The Musical - Pittsburgh Byham Theater"" and be part of the magic!
