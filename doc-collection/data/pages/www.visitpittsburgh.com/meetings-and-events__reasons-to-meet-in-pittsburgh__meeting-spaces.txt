Meeting Spaces
Pittsburgh has plenty of opportunities to amaze your guests.​
Find the right meeting space to make your group feel right at home in Pittsburgh. Exploring the incredible array of meeting and event spaces in Pittsburgh should be an itinerary of its own! Whether you’re hosting an intimate gathering of colleagues or entertaining thousands, Pittsburgh offers a fantastic selection of unique venues.
Convenient Locations
With venues located throughout the city, Pittsburgh makes it easy for groups to host unforgettable events. Host the event at a hotel meeting space to provide attendees the convenience of booking a room at the same location. Or, book a conference center conveniently located near a hotel. Whether your meeting is one day or several, many venues are within a short distance of incredible restaurants, shopping, and nightlife, giving guests plenty of opportunities to connect outside of meeting hours.
