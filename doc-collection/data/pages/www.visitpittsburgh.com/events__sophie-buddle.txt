Discover Your Persona
November 5, 2026 8:00 PM –7:00 PM
Sophie Buddle is a Canadian stand-up comedian and television writer whose sharp, personal comedy earned her a place among the genre’s notable voices: her debut album, Lil Bit of Buddle, won the 2020 Juno Award for Comedy Album of the Year, making her the first woman to win as a solo stand-up. She has appeared on "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon" and "The Late Late Show with James Corden", and written for CBC’s "This Hour Has 22 Minutes" and "After Midnight".
