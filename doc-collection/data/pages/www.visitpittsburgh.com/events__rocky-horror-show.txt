October 30, 2026 9:00 PM
Richard O’Brien’s The Rocky Horror Show comes to The Lamp Theatre in a production presented by Split Stage Productions. This live rock ’n’ roll musical follows newly engaged Brad and Janet after a storm strands them at the mansion of Dr. Frank-N-Furter, where they encounter Rocky, outrageous characters and iconic songs including “The Time Warp” and “Sweet Transvestite.” The Irwin engagement is scheduled for three performances on October 30–31, 2026, including late-night shows, and is presented by arrangement with Concord Theatricals.
