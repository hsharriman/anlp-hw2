October 31, 2026 8:00 PM –10:00 PM
The wildly unhinged, adult-only drag show BUTTTOOTKING will take the stage in Pittsburgh on Saturday, October 31, 2026, for a special Halloween night performance.
The show stars RuPaul’s Drag Race Season 17 favorites Lydia B Kollins, Suzie Toot, and Kori King. Fans can expect a chaotic, high-energy night of stunts, comedy, and scandal. Attendees are highly encouraged to wear costumes, as the trio hosts a "best in show" costume contest during every tour stop.
