October 25, 2026 11:00 AM –3:00 PM
Tree Pittsburgh is excited to announce the return of our Autumn Art and Maker Mart! Vendors from around Pittsburgh will showcase their handmade and vintage wares for sale – perfect for Halloween or early holiday shopping. The event will also have mexican food from La Palapa, coffee, tea, baked goods, and brunch beverages (alcoholic and n/a)!
