Beer Delivery and Takeout Services
Many local breweries are offering curbside pick-up and delivery options for their delicious brews. Kick-back, relax and enjoy a cold one from your favorite Pittsburgh brewery in the comfort of your home.
There are plenty of ways you can support local businesses. Many of your favorite breweries are offering curbside pick-up and takeout. You can even pick up some cool merch or gift cards for last minute gifts. Here are a few things happening that you can do.
Takeout, Delivery or Curbside Pick-Up
Local brew-masters are dedicated to making sure you can enjoy your favorite beer right at home! Thanks to Very Local, here is a list of breweries currently offering pickup or delivery.
Article: Pittsburgh breweries offering beer delivery
Purchase Gift Cards or Merch
While you’re relaxing at home sipping on your favorite brew, browse local restaurants and breweries offering online sales of their awesome merch! You can also grab a gift card for a future purchases.
Article: 15 of the Best Pittsburgh Restaurant & Brewery Merch To Buy Right Now
Virtual Beer Tasting
Does it get any more 2020 than this?! City Brew Tours offers unique beer-focused experiences in some of the best beer cities throughout North America and has figured out how to bring them to your home! Choose between the “Live Homebrew Experience” suitable for all ages and skill levels or the “Beer and Cheese Pairing Virtual Happy Hour” best for anyone of legal drinking age. Both experiences can be purchased as a Private Party, Corporate Event, or Gift Certificates!
Support the Craft Beer Community
First Sip Brew Box is a subscription box for craft beer lovers around the world. Support the local front line workers, the craft beer community and more with your purchase of beer gear!
Article: First Sip Brew Box fundraising to support Pittsburgh's craft brewery industry during coronavirus
Now, let’s give one big virtual “CHEERS!” to the Pittsburgh Craft Breweries! 🍻 🎉
