Romantic Dinners for Valentine's Day in Pittsburgh
Take your pick of Pittsburgh's top restaurants for a romantic dinner with your sweetheart.
Valentine's Day is right around the corner and Pittsburgh has an abundance of restaurants offering amazing dishes and great ambiance. If one of the 9 restaurants below isn't quite your cup of tea, make sure to check out tons more on our Restaurants & Culinary page.
Embrace the candlelight at these romantic eateries.
Con Alma
Downtown
The lights are low, the candles are lit on the bistro tables and jazz is wafting through the air. With an eclectic menu at Con Alma's location Downtown, there’s a variety of dishes to choose from, including New Orleans favorites like chicken and waffles and jambalaya, as well as drunken noodles and lechon asada. Cuddle up at a bistro table and enjoy some premium music after your Cultural District show.
Pusadee’s Garden
Lawrenceville
Spend a night under the stars in a garden oasis that will make you forget you’re in the middle of a city. Pusadee's Garden features elegant Thai dishes in a romantic atmosphere. Though seating in the garden itself is weather dependent, any area of the restaurant is date night worthy. Don’t forget to check out the extensive and carefully curated wine list!
Girasole
Shadyside
Pounded copper top tables and stone walls give cellar restaurant Girasole a charming twist. Their seasonal menu features homemade pastas and sauces made from locally sourced ingredients. A family operation, this tiny Italian bistro will quickly become one of your favorite date night spots. Wait times on the weekends are can be a minute, so enjoy shopping up and down Walnut Street while you wait on your table.

A post shared by Girasole Restaurant (@girasole_pgh)
Alla Famiglia
Allentown
Yes, it’s on the pricier side, but honestly, its worth every penny. Made-from-scratch Italian pastas and specialties will have you and your date leaning across the table in the candlelight to see what course is next. Plan your visit to Alla Famiglia ahead as their reservation book is often filled up well in advance.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Alla Famiglia (@allafamiglia)
The Rib Room
Downtown
Tucked next to the Meat and Potatoes kitchen in the back of the restaurant is a 16-seat room lined with vintage inspired wallpaper and red leather seating. An ode to the prime rib, the intimate Rib Room experience features old school steakhouse sides like Yorkshire puddings and the opportunity to order a true Lobster Thermidor as the perfect hors d'oeuvres to share.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Rib Room (@ribroompgh)
The Forge
Lawrenceville
This Mediterranean small-plates lounge specializes in regionally-sourced cuisine and delicious craft cocktails. Couples craving Mediterranean mezze and a sophisticated environment will fall in love with The Forge.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by The Forge (@theforgepgh)
Gaucho Parrilla Argentina
Downtown
Specializing in Argentinian-inspired sandwiches and wood-fired meat, Gaucho Parrilla Argentina is a great restaurant for couples looking to spice up their Valentine's Day. They even have a special couples dinner available on Feb. 11 (Saturday) and on Valentine's Day itself. Sparkling, white, red and rose wines are all on the menu, too.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by GAUCHO 🤠 Parrilla Argentina (@eat.gaucho)
Fig & Ash
Deutschtown
Right along the bustling blocks of Deutschtown is Fig & Ash, an American restaurant with a few modern twists. The chefs here carefully craft their dishes with local ingredients from scratch, so that every bite is truly special.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Fig & Ash (@figandashpgh)
Paris 66
East Liberty
Paris 66 is an authentic French bistro experience, right here in Pittsburgh! With a cozy atmosphere, delicious hors d’oeuvres, and a tempting list of French wines, you'll feel just like you're on a romantic date in Paris.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Paris 66 (@paris66bistro)
Baby Loves Tacos
Bloomfield
Ideal for V-Day Weekend takeout, Baby Loves Tacos is a community-focused Mexican food joint with some really awesome tacos. Feeling crafty? You and your date can buy one of their DIY taco kits, available in eight separate styles, and spend the evening perfecting your own delicious creations.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Baby Loves Tacos (@babylovestacos)
This is just a small sample of the amazing restaurants that have the perfect Valentine's Day meal planned for you. Reservations are recommended, and leave plenty of time to enjoy all courses. Happy Valentines Day!
