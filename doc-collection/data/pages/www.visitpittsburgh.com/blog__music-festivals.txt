Pittsburgh Music Festivals To Jam To
Pittsburgh hosts a number of fantastic music festivals throughout the year. Make plans to catch a few of these must-see events.
Millvale Music Festival
May 15-16, 2026
Crowned the best music fest in Pittsburgh by locals in 2024, this sprawling concert runs the entirety of the Millvale neighborhood with venues everywhere from outdoors spaces to bars, breweries, community centers and Mr. Smalls.
Download their app in advance (Google Play and App Store) which helps you personalize your music festival experience via band name, venue and/or genre.
Dollar Bank Three Rivers Arts Festival
June 5-7 & 11-14, 2026
This fun-filled event produced by the Pittsburgh Cultural Trust boasts several days of free music and art, featuring a diverse concert lineup of renowned performers every night on the Dollar Bank Main Stage.
WPA Juneteenth Homecoming Celebration
June 19-21, 2026
The City of Pittsburgh has been one of the premier locations for Black music in America, exporting great music and musicians through the country.
Allegheny County Music Festival
TBA 2026
Held in the scenic Hartwood Acres Park Amphitheater, the music goes all day while food trucks, local wineries and craft breweries keep the party going. All proceeds benefit the Allegheny County Music Festival Fund.
Northside Music Festival
July 10-12, 2026
This award-winning, free festival has become a staple event with more than 400 bands hosted over three days performing at 40+ indoor and outdoor stages in Pittsburgh’s Northside. Additionally, you’ll find family-friendly activities, more than 70 food trucks, artist markets and tons of surprises.
Pittsburgh Music Festival
July 11-12, 2026
After eight successful years supporting the community through the Pittsburgh Blues and Roots Festival organizers, Ron Esser and John Vento are excited to announce an exciting new chapter. This summer, the event will evolve into the Pittsburgh Music Festival Presented by Pittsburgh Brewing Company in Support of Band Together and Autism Pittsburgh, a two-day celebration of local music, community, and charity taking place July 11–12 on the Iron City Stages at Pittsburgh Brewing Company in Creighton.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by PGH Blues and Roots Festival (@pghbluesfestival)
Rock Reggae and Relief
August 8, 2026
Rock, Reggae and Relief is a benefit music festival that brings reggae greats, local rock legends and feel-good vibes to Downtown Pittsburgh while offering a helping hand to neighbors in need.
Iron City Riverfront Throwdown
August 8, 2026
Pittsburgh Brewing Company is turning up the volume this summer with the debut of the Iron City Riverfront Throwdown, a brand-new country music festival set for Saturday, August 8, 2026, along the scenic banks of the Allegheny River at Pittsburgh Brewing Company in Creighton, Pennsylvania.
Pittsburgh International Jazz Festival
Sept. 17-20, 2026
With Pittsburgh’s rich jazz history, the Pittsburgh International Jazz Festival (PIJF) is the city’s premier music festival. PIJF hosts live music with renowned artists from around the world at the Highmark Stadium.
FestPGH
Sept. 19, 2026
On Saturday, Sept. 19, 2026, Highmark Stadium will resound with the music of bands including the headliner Switchfoot, as well as laughter, games, family fun, service opportunities and worship during the inaugural FestPGH music and arts festival.
Four Chord Music Festival
Sept. 25-26, 2026
This all-day, multi-stage festival is a throwback to the heyday of pop punk, emo, indie rock and more music from the early 2000s. Its new home for 2026 will be at F.N.B. Stadium (formerly Highmark Stadium)!
Dormont Street & Music Festival
Oct. 10, 2026
Join us for the Dormont Street and Music Festival, one of the community’s most anticipated events of the year. Taking place rain or shine, this free, all-ages festival offers a full day of live music across three stages featuring 18 bands, along with a wide variety of food, unique arts and crafts, and activities for all. Explore more than 60 vendors, support local businesses and artisans, and enjoy the energy and spirit of the Dormont community with family and friends.
