Founded in 1982, Three Rivers Film Festival (3RFF) is the oldest and largest annual film festival in the region. Our dynamic lineup of independent features, shorts, panels, parties, and special events—including Pittsburgh Shorts, the Filmmaker Conference and Script Competition—takes place across multiple venues in Pittsburgh.
