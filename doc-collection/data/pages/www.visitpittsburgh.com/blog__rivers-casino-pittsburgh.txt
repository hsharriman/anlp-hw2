Rivers Casino Pittsburgh: All You Need to Know Before You Go
Rivers Casino Pittsburgh is turning 10 this year!
As the casino celebrates its 10-year anniversary, it is commencing construction of The Landing Hotel Pittsburgh - a seven-story, four-star property with two hundred and nineteen guestrooms which will attach to the casino on the east façade, facing the Daniel G. and Carole L. Kamin Science Center.
Feel the excitement with Pittsburgh’s first-and-only in-casino sportsbook. The Sportsbook offers betting on a wide array of sporting events including football, basketball, baseball, hockey, soccer, college sports and more. Bets include but are not limited to straight bets, parlays, totals and in-game betting. It's easy, fun, and who doesn't love the chance to cheer on your favorite team while placing some wagers?
Rivers Casino also makes a perfect location for all kinds of events. If you are looking to book meetings, conventions, weddings, or any other event, check out more info here.

Dining
Rivers offers more than just gaming, with nine signature restaurants and bars that offer a delicious variety. Here are just some of the selections:
Andrew’s Steak & Seafood
Repeat winner of Wine Spectator’s Award of Excellence, Andrew’s Steak & Seafood offers guests the opportunity to enjoy fine dining in a sophisticated atmosphere. Riverfront windows offer a breathtaking view of Downtown Pittsburgh and Mount Washington along the Ohio River.
Cíao
Perfect for a quick casual bite in a vibrant atmosphere. Enjoy a grab-and-go sandwich or salad served only as Cíao can. Open almost 24 hours a day, you can enjoy breakfast, a sandwich, salad or soup
FLIPT
FLIPT a contemporary burger-and-shake restaurant that's relatively new to Rivers Casino. It has a mouthwatering, signature burger selection and fresh-cut fries, but the delectable homemade milkshakes are the must-haves on the menu.
Pro Tip: Mondays through Wednesdays, FLIPT is offering the “Son of a FLIPT” burger meal, which includes fries and a shake, for $10.
Grand View Buffet
Hungry for something but not sure what? Then come to Grand View Buffet, where the selection is vast. The buffet serves up a variety of cuisines including Italian, Asian, barbecue and much more.
Pro Tip: Through August, each Monday and Tuesday, Grand View Buffet will offer a $10 lunch buffet.
Wheelhouse Bar & Grill
Looking for another great location to watch the game? Wheelhouse Bar & Grill at Rivers Casino is a terrific choice to watch all your favorite Pittsburgh sports teams on one of the largest high-definition TVs in Pittsburgh.
Pro Tip: Every Wednesday, Wheelhouse is offering a 2 for $20 select menu.
Keep an eye out on the Rivers gaming floor for other fun treats in honor of the 10-year anniversary

Entertainment
Enjoy live jazz, local DJs and crowd-pleasing cover bands in one of the casino's bars or lounges. Among the options are:
Drum Bar
When it comes to live entertainment in Pittsburgh, Drum Bar offers a diverse selection. Rock your weekend with live bands each Friday and Saturday night. Bands play high-energy music accompanied by synchronized lights that keep the party going.
Levels Bar
Located in the center of the casino, Levels overlooks the entire casino floor—the perfect vantage point for watching the action at the tables while enjoying one of our signature cocktails or your favorite libation. Levels features live DJs on weekends.
Event Center
Throughout the year, The Event Center at Rivers Casino will welcome live performances. The space will transform to theatre-style seating for over 2,000 and feature star-studded talent—musicians, comedians and more.

Good to Know
Rivers Casino is located along Pittsburgh’s North Shore, down the street from Acrisure Stadium and PNC Park and is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Guests of Rivers Casino must be 21 years or older to access the gaming floor.
The casino offers more than 130,000 square feet of casino floor 2,708 slot machines, 128 table games, 55 hybrid gaming seats, 30-table live poker room, and state-of-the-art High Limits gaming area
Rush Rewards members earn benefits by playing with their Rush Rewards Players Club Card—membership is free and players can redeem their points for Free Slot Play, dining and purchases from Rivers' gift shop.
