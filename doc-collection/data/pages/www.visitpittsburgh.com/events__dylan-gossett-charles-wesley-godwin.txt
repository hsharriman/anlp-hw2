Discover Your Persona
October 30, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Dylan Gossett is a Texas-born singer-songwriter whose heartfelt blend of country, Americana, folk, and Red Dirt influences has quickly earned him recognition as one of the genre's brightest rising stars. He first captured widespread attention with the breakout success of his deeply personal songs, which pair rugged vocals with honest storytelling about love, faith, family, small-town life, and perseverance. Drawing inspiration from traditional country music while embracing a modern singer-songwriter approach, Gossett creates emotionally resonant songs that connect with listeners through their authenticity and understated craftsmanship. His live performances showcase his warm stage presence and expressive musicianship, whether performing intimate acoustic ballads or full-band country anthems. With a rapidly growing fan base and an expanding catalog of acclaimed music, Dylan Gossett continues to establish himself as a compelling new voice in contemporary Americana.
Charles Wesley Godwin is an acclaimed singer-songwriter whose powerful blend of country, folk, and Appalachian Americana has earned widespread praise for its vivid storytelling and emotional authenticity. A native of West Virginia, Godwin draws inspiration from the people, landscapes, and traditions of his home state, crafting richly detailed songs that explore family, hard work, love, resilience, and the passage of time. His acclaimed albums, including Seneca, How the Mighty Fall, and Family Ties, showcase poetic lyricism, warm acoustic instrumentation, and a commanding baritone voice that brings every story to life. Onstage, he balances intimate moments with soaring full-band arrangements, creating performances that are both deeply personal and undeniably powerful. Through exceptional songwriting, heartfelt musicianship, and an unwavering commitment to honest expression, Charles Wesley Godwin has become one of the most respected and compelling artists in today's Americana and country music landscape.
