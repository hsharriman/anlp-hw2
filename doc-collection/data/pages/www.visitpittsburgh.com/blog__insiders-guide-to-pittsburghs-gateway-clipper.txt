Insider's Guide: Gateway Clipper Fleet
Pittsburgh’s Gateway Clipper Fleet is open for business and ready to welcome you aboard!
One of the best ways to spend an afternoon in Pittsburgh is by enjoying the beautiful three rivers from aboard the Gateway Clipper Fleet! Get ready to sit back, relax, and take in the gorgeous views of the city that’s unlike any other, while also learning tons of cool facts about Pittsburgh. Experiencing the city from the water is magical no matter what time of day, or season of the year. After all, the Gateway Clipper is open year-round!
Plan Your Visit
The Gateway Clipper Fleet is back to sailing the Three Rivers. The Clipper offers a variety of ways to get out on the water and take in the city views! Read on for a variety of great options to enjoy a cruise on the scenic three rivers.
Three Rivers Sightseeing Cruise
The BEST way to see the city and truly learn about her past, present and future is aboard a Three Rivers Sightseeing Cruise. Tour all three rivers while the captain and narrator present interesting facts and tales about Pittsburgh and Western Pennsylvania!
Daily from June 11-Aug. 17. See full availability here.
Sailing Times: 11:00 a.m., 12:30 p.m., 2 p.m., 3:30 p.m. 5 p.m., 6:30 p.m. and 8 p.m.
Enjoy a New “Twist” to the Evening Three Rivers Sightseeing Cruises! The 6:30 p.m. and 8:00 p.m. sailings now feature Happy Hour Cocktails
*Must be 21 years of age to purchase alcohol with proper ID.
Kids Cruises
Show your kids a great time aboard the Gateway Clipper! The variety of kids-themed cruises will keep your children on their toes with fun activities and games, story time and great music and dancing!
Kid-themed cruises offered include meet and greets with Princesses, Superheroes, silly Trolls & those fun pups on Paw Patrol!
The snack bar and full-service bar are available for purchase on kids’ cruises, serving up hot dogs, nachos, and other tasty treats, plus refreshing beverages for parents to enjoy.
Dining Cruises
The Gateway Clipper Fleet offers dining cruises sailing this summer!
Skyline Dinner Cruise & Tour
New for 2025, the Skyline Dinner Cruise & Tour is the perfect place to unwind, relax, and enjoy your weekend on the water. Sailing Friday evenings, this unforgettable experience features our delicious dinner buffet, lively DJ, and stunning skyline views along the Three Rivers. Bring your family, friends, colleagues, or that special someone for a perfect evening out.
Lock & Dam Dinner Cruise
On most Thursdays, experience the delicious Lock & Dam Dinner Cruise. Featuring the best BBQ on the three rivers, you will get to experience the process we call “locking thru” as our boat passes through a functioning lock & dam.
Wine Tasting Cruise
On select Thursdays, enjoy a Wine Tasting Cruise featuring a selection of eight wines from featured destinations across the globe, an hors d’oeuvres buffet, and fabulous city views from the Gateway Clipper riverboat.
Captain's Dinner Cruise & Tour
On Saturday, the Gateway Clipper's signature cruise, the Captain’s Dinner Cruise, sets sail. Enjoy the scrumptious Captain’s Dinner Buffet, relax with a craft cocktail and experience the perfect night out in Pittsburgh! Expert narration is provided by the captain followed by live entertainment from the house DJ playing a variety of hits! It’s a night out you don’t want to miss!
For reservations and tickets to all Gateway Clipper Cruises, visit www.gatewayclipper.com
Insider’s Tips
Here are some tips that are helpful to know so that you can be fully prepared to have the best possible experience while taking a ride on the Gateway Clipper.
Getting to the Docks
There are several great parking options in Station Square and within walking distance from the Gateway Clipper. If you are looking for public transportation options, there are busses that go to Station Square, and both the "T" and the Monongahela Incline have stops there as well. Also, Uber and Lyft drivers are very familiar with this attraction and will know exactly where to drop you off.
Gateway to Pittsburgh Murals
On your short walk to board the Gateway Clipper, you'll pass by an amazing collection of murals. These are called "Gateway to Pittsburgh" murals and stretch along both sides of the ramp leading down to the Monongahela River where the Gateway Clipper Fleet docks. The murals depict the history of this area from the late 1600's to the start of the 21st Century. Take some time to examine them as they function as a visual history of Pittsburgh through the ages.
Top Deck Tips
Looking to capture amazing photos of the city? Check out the top deck! Prime seats on the top deck fill up quickly, so be sure to arrive early to secure your perfect seat. And with no covering on the top deck, don’t forget your sunscreen.
Pittsburgh is KidsBURGH®!
Pittsburgh is the perfect destination for unforgettable family fun! Featuring many fantastic attractions, Pittsburgh has plenty of entertainment options for kids of all ages. Plan your KidsBURGH® trip today!
