Pittsburgh Fall Festivals 2026 Guide
Colorful foliage. Crisp air. Pumpkins. What's not to love about Fall?
As the temperatures start to cool down, fall festival season begins heating up! Explore a pumpkin patch at a scenic farm, celebrate at cultural festivals in the city and more with Pennsylvania's stunning fall colors as a backdrop. Happy Fall!
Halloween fans, we have a list of haunts and ghost tours in Pittsburgh for you, too.
October
November
September
Kickoff and Rib Festival
September 3-7, 2026
Acrisure Stadium | 100 Art Rooney Avenue, Pittsburgh
Western PA football kicks off right at Acrisure Stadium, home of the Pitt Panthers and Pittsburgh Steelers! Enjoy five days of football, amazing ribs, live music, the area's largest cornhole tournament and more at this free fest.
ReelAbilities Film Festival
September 3-13, 2026
Various locations in Pittsburgh
The ReelAbilities Film Festival presents award-winning films through the lens of individuals living with disabilities. In addition to impactful films, the festival also features artwork and special guests to bring the themes of community and shared human experience to the forefront. ReelAbilities is a program of Film Pittsburgh.
Pittsburgh Soul Food Festival
September 4-6, 2026
Market Square/Boulevard of the Allies between Stanwix and Wood Streets
Enjoy a Soulful Taste of the Burgh at this free festival spotlighting Black culinary, butchery, and food businesses. While the fantastic food takes center stage, a small business market, live music, BBQ contest, and cultural activities make this event an all-day affair!

Britsburgh
September 8-13, 2026
Various locations in Pittsburgh
Bringing a bit of Britain to Pittsburgh, the 2026 Britsburgh Festival is chock-a-block with events from afternoon teas and cask tapping to Shakespeare performances and even a polo match, all designed to bring people together over a shared love of all things British!
Pittsburgh Irish Festival
September 11-13, 2026
Carrie Blast Furnaces | 801 Carrie Furnace Boulevard, Pittsburgh
Guests at this year’s Pittsburgh Irish Festival can expect to see their favorite Irish international, national, and local acts in person at the event’s 34th Annual Celtic Celebration! The line-up features new bands, phenomenal solo performers, well-known favorites, local Irish dance groups, and more on four stages for non-stop fun and enjoyment.
The Original Pittsburgh Taco Festival
September 12, 2026
The Stacks at 3 Crossings | 2875 Railroad Street, Pittsburgh
Pittsburgh's top tacos and food trucks take over the Strip District! Enjoy this community-centered event, which features more than 25 food trucks and vendors, plus live music, an art market, and family fun. This festival is produced with the support of the Pittsburgh Hispanic Development Corporation and Latin American Cultural Union.

September 4-6, 2026
Market Square/Boulevard of the Allies between Stanwix and Wood Streets
Enjoy a Soulful Taste of the Burgh at this free festival spotlighting Black culinary, butchery, and food businesses. While the fantastic food takes center stage, a small business market, live music, BBQ contest, and cultural activities make this event an all-day affair!
Phantom Fall Fest
Select Dates: September 12 - November 1, 2026
Kennywood | 4800 Kennywood Boulevard, West Mifflin
Experience Kennywood's new Phantom Fall Fest! Visit the park during the day for family-friendly fun, including festive food and a unique Kennywood autumn atmosphere. After dark, the Phantom takes screams to the extreme with seven haunted houses and four scare zones.
Screenshot: Asian Film Festival
September 16 - September 23, 2026
Harris Theater and the University of Pittsburgh | 809 Liberty Ave, Pittsburgh
Harris Theater is hosting an Asian Film Festival, showcasing 12 feature films and 10 short films that highlight Asian culture and experience. Ranging from animated shorts to television episodes to modern movies, the event offers a variety of media fit for whatever you're looking to enjoy.

Pittsburgh International Jazz Festival
September 17 - 20, 2026
The August Wilson African American Cultural Center | 980 Liberty Avenue
Head to the August Wilson African American Cultural Center for our annual International Jazz Festival. This event brings classical jazz as well as modern jazz to the main stages of Pittsburgh and curates an impressive lineup of musicians ready to showcase their musical talent.
15th Annual Ukrainian Food Festival
September 18-19, 2026
St. Peter & St. Paul Ukrainian Orthodox Church | 220 Mansfield Boulevard, Carnegie
The Parish of Sts. Peter & Paul Ukrainian Orthodox Church will host their annual Ukrainian Food Festival in September this year. Shop traditional Ukrainian baked goods, grab plates of classic dishes, and enter your name to win one of the event's raffle baskets.
Panda Fest
September 18 - 20, 2026
Station Square East Lot Parking | 73 East Carson Street, Pittsburgh
Celebrate Asian culture with 80+ Asian food vendors and over 200 street vendors at the Panda Fest right here in Pittsburgh. Try dishes from China, Japan, Korea, Thailand, Vietnam, and more, then shop local artisans' goods, from art to clothing to accessories and more.
After shopping, sit in on one of the cultural programs, featuring traditional art, dancing, performances, and more live entertainment. Centering this event is the festival's mascot, pandas! Snap a picture with the many panda posters, inflatables, and themed treats.

Penn Brewery Oktoberfest
September 18-20 & 25-27, 2026
Penn Brewery | 800 Vinial Street, Pittsburgh
Pittsburgh's classic Oktoberfest is back at Penn Brewery for 2025! Join us the weekends of September 19-21 and 26-28 at Penn's Northside location for fresh beer, German food, and live oompah music!
Pittsburgh Chinese Cultural Festival
September 19, 2026
Mellon Park | 1047 Shady Avenue, Pittsburgh
Come experience the biggest Chinese community event in Pittsburgh at this free festival. Celebrate Chinese heritage, watch traditional performances, listen to beautiful music and enjoy some delicious food.
Jump Cut Pittsburgh Noir Film Festival
September 19, 2026
The Parkway Theater & Film Lounge | 644 Broadway Ave, Mckees Rocks
Be part of the inaugural Jump Cut Noir Film Festival! 2026 marks the first-ever Noir Film Festival in Pittsburgh, which is scheduled to feature indie films, filmmakers, cocktails, Q&As, workshops, and mingles for those who adore the niche film genre.
FestPGH
September 19, 2026
Highmark Stadium | 510 W Station Square Drive, Pittsburgh
On Saturday, Sept. 19, 2026, Highmark Stadium will resound with the music of bands including the headliner Switchfoot, as well as laughter, games, family fun, service opportunities and worship during the inaugural FestPGH music and arts festival.

South Park Hay Day
September 19 & 26, 2026
19th: Hartwood Acres Park | 4000 Middle Road, Allison Park
26th: South Park Fairgrounds | 1888 Brownsville Road, South Park Township
Join Allegheny County Parks for a day full of free, family-friendly fall activities. Families can enjoy hay rides, a hay maze, a pumpkin patch trip, a barnyard petting zoo, and more. Food, beverages, and snacks will be available for sale from food trucks and vendors.
Sharpsburg Summer Jam Music Festival & Bicentennial Drone Show
September 19, 2026
Kennedy Park | 1311 N Canal Street, Sharpsburg
Close out the summer with a live performance at the Sharpsburg Summer Jam Music Festival & Bicentennial Drone Show. The event will bring together incredible local performers, taking to the stage throughout the day from 1 pm to 6 pm. Dance to the music and eat from their lineup of food trucks, enjoy family-friendly activities, and drink brews from Sharpsburg's own brewmasters.
Evans City PumpkinFest
September 19 & 20, 2026
Evans City | East Main Street, Evans City
Evans City is ringing in the new season with its fifth annual PumpkinFest. Featuring live music, fall festivities, food trucks, wagon rides, car shows, free games, and more, the festival will take over East Main Street with community fun for all.
Fall Migration Festival
September 20, 2026
Stormy Oaks Nature Conservancy | 120 Forsythe Road, Valencia
Celebrate over two decades of local wildlife education and rehabilitation at Wildbird Recovery’s 15th Annual Fall Migration Festival, an annual fundraiser where supporters of all ages spend time outdoors, enjoy delicious food, and learn about different aspects of our local wildlife.

Four Chord Music Festival
September 25 - 26, 2026
F.N.B. Stadium | 510 W Station Square Drive, Pittsburgh
The 2026 lineup at the Four Chord Music Festival is sure to rock, with bands like Knocked Loose, Pierce the Veil, Mayday Parade, and Boys Like Girls. If you're into rock music or just love the music scene, reserve your tickets and get ready for a weekend-long festival of concerts, fun, and local community.
Reserve your tickets for the event on the Four Chords' website.
73 Hour Record-Breaking Infinite Cypher - Hip Hop Festival
September 25 - 28, 2026
Forest Farms | 441 Grange Hall Road, Tionesta
Feel like breaking a world record? Bring your friends and dance it out at the 73 Hour Infinite Cypher Hip Hop Festival. Looking to break the Guinness World Record for the longest rap marathon event, the Hip Hop Festival will be running for 73 straight hours (that's over 3-days long!).
This is a camping event, so be sure to pack your backpack and have your camping equipment ready to get the most out of the festival.
Butler Fall Festival
September 26, 2026
Downtown Butler, PA | Main Street
The Butler Fall Festival takes place on Main Street in Butler, PA. The event will include a car show, kids activities, artisan and craft vendors, food vendors and music!
Tropical Forest Panama Festival
September 26, 2026
Phipps Conservatory and Botanical Gardens| One Schenley Park, Pittsburgh
Celebrating its Tropical Forest Panama exhibit, the Phipps Conservatory is throwing a festival that will have educational programs and cultural celebrations. Learn more about Panamanian culture, try Latin American food from Pittsburgh's own El Sabor Latin Kitchen, and explore Phipps' latest garden.

The Pittsburgh Great Race
September 26- 27, 2026
Starts at Frick Park at Beechwood Boulevar
Lace up your running shoes and challenge yourself to the annual Great Race right here in Pittsburgh. Compete in the 5K, 10K, or the junior race for any families with track stars in their midst. For every registration, the race donates $1 to the Richard S. Caliguiri Amyloidosis Fund, which supports medical research conducted here in Pittsburgh.
Pittsburgh Silent Film Festival
September 27 - October 4, 2026
Various location in Pittsburgh
Enjoy twelve films from the silent film era screened at eight venues throughout Pittsburgh, all with live musical accompaniment.

Oktoberfest Pittsburgh
October 1-11, 2026
Downtown Pittsburgh
Taking place over 11 days, this year’s event will be held at Fort Duquesne Boulevard, utilizing the westbound roadway from Stanwix Street to 7th Street and incorporating the 6th Street Bridge. This year promises an even better celebration of culture, food, music, and community, so festivalgoers can fully immerse themselves in German-inspired activities for all ages!
Confluence PumpkinFest
October 2-4, 2026
Confluence | Along Routes 281 and 523
Participate in a Fall Foliage celebration during the Confluence PumpkinFest. The festival includes a variety of events like the Pumpkinfest Queen Pageant, a 5K run/walk, an antique and classic auto show, a parade and a largest pumpkin contest.
Reel Q Film Festival
October 2 - 10, 2026
Multiple Locations
The 41st annual Reel Q Film Festival has announced its 2026 dates. A staple of the Pittsburgh film scene, this festival highlights queer filmmakers and stories with their multi-day festival that takes over Carnegie Mellon University in October. See new film premieres, catch local short films, and even get the chance to meet young filmmakers with tickets to the Reel Q festival.
Purchase tickets on the Reel Q official website.

Jack O’Lantern Extravaganza
October 2 - November 1, 2026
Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium | 7370 Baker Street, Pittsburgh
Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium will illuminate the city with thousands of artistically carved pumpkins glowing on your night walk through this gourd-geous new display. ZooBoo trick-or-treating will also return during daytime hours, the last two weekends of October.
Leechburg Annual Fall Festival
October 3, 2026
Leechburg Riverfront Park | Kiski Avenue, Leechburg
Head to Leechburg for fall-themed activities, games, local makers, crafters, vendors, fall-inspired food and treats, music, and family-friendly Halloween festivities. The annual fall festival is free for you to attend and brings a day of festival fun to the riverfront park, which offers a great view of the water as a backdrop to the event.
Mars Applefest
October 3, 2026
145 Grand Avenue, Mars
Experience the Mars Applefest with food, fun, shopping, games, crafts, 150+ vendors and entertainment.
Festival of Combustion
October 3, 2026
Carrie Blast Furnace | 801 Carrie Furnace Boulevard, Pittsburgh
This one is sure to be lit fam (is that what the kids say?). The hottest fall festival returns to Carrie Blast Furnaces with hands-on demonstrations, tours of the property, live music, craft beer, and food trucks.

Portersville Steam Show Grounds | 1512 Perry Highway, Portersville
A vintage village, sawmill, cider press, Amish donuts, antique car rally, gas engines, oilfield equipment, farm animals, train rides, steam engines, flea market, crafts, food, kids’ pedal tractor pulling, tractor pulls, and a steam building are some of the features to enjoy.

Pumpkin Festival
Weekends in October starting October 3, 2026
Reeger's Farm | 755 Laurel Road, Shelocta
Start a new family tradition at Reeger's Farm! On Saturdays and Sundays in October, they offer hayrides, a pumpkin patch, food trucks, and more.
Freedom Farms Fall Fest
Weekends in October starting October 3, 2026
Freedom Farms | 434 Overbrook Road, Valencia
Any weekend in October, enjoy tons of fall activities like tractor rides, a corn maze, a hay mountain, a petting zoo, pick-your-own flowers, pumpkin carving and decorating, and lots more family-friendly activities.
Autumnfest
October 3-18, 2026
Seven Springs Mountain Resort | 777 Water Wheel Drive, Champion
Treat yourself to a spectacular weekend in the mountains and enjoy the best of Pennsylvania's beautiful fall foliage! Hop on the chairlift for an aerial view of the fiery colors of autumn. Shop from local artisans and crafters, enjoy live music, and take a mini getaway in the mountains during Autumnfest.
Autumn Serenade Luncheon Cruise
October 7, 14, 10 & 17, 2026
Gateway Clipper Fleet at Station Square | 350 West Station Square Drive, Pittsburgh
Enjoy a tasty autumn harvest lunch while taking in the sights of Pittsburgh's fall foliage on a riverboat tour.
Fort Ligonier Days
October 9-11, 2026
Fort Ligonier | Various locations
Fort Ligonier Days is a three-day festival that commemorates the Battle of Fort Ligonier, a key engagement of the French & Indian War. The festival features historic battle reenactments, live music, authentic crafts, delicious food and more.

Houston Pumpkin Festival
October 9-11, 2026
124 West Pike Street | Houston, PA
Features family fun for all ages, including over 175 food and craft vendors, music and entertainment, children's games and activities, a parade, and many pumpkin-related contests. Free admission.
Annual Country Fall Festival
October 9-11, 2026
Downtown Zelienople | Various locations
Enjoy two days of musical entertainment, three towers of paranormal ghost walks, pumpkin and costume contests, chainsaw carving, petting zoo, free horse-drawn wagon rides, and historic encampments and demonstrations during the Zelienople Country Fall Festival.
And while you're there, check out the Sleepy Hollow Festival in Historic Harmony! You can even take advantage of a shuttle that runs between the two towns to enjoy everything both festivals have to offer!
Pittsburgh Dragon Boat Festival
October 10, 2026
Pittsburgh Brewing Company | 150 Ferry Street
Head down to the Allegheny River to cheer on riverboat teams as they compete to win the festival's boat race. After the race, swing by some of the vendors and be sure to drop a donation or two with the festival team, as this event supports breast cancer survivors in a one-of-a-kind festival.
Fall Flower Show
Opens October 10, 2026
Phipps Conservatory & Botanical Gardens | 1 Schenley Park, Pittsburgh
With the blockbuster Summer Show, Alice in Wonderland-themed displays are here to stay through the fall! Opening Sat., Oct. 10, Fall Flower Show: Alice’s Adventures in Autumn combines the whimsy of Phipps’ unique take of Lewis Carroll’s story with our stunning chrysanthemums — and a few spooky surprises!
You'll see all your favorite characters from the book among expertly grown chrysanthemums in an endless variety of shapes, colors and sizes. Phipps has been creating chrysanthemum-themed seasonal shows since 1894, making its Fall Flower Show the longest-running in the nation and a Pittsburgh tradition that you can't experience anywhere else!

Dormont Street & Music Festival
October 10, 2026
Patomac Avenue | Dormont
Join us for the Dormont Street and Music Festival, one of the community’s most anticipated events of the year. Taking place rain or shine, this free, all-ages festival offers a full day of live music across three stages featuring 18 bands, along with a wide variety of food, unique arts and crafts, and activities for all. Explore more than 60 vendors, support local businesses and artisans, and enjoy the energy and spirit of the Dormont community with family and friends.
Pittsburgh Coffee & Chocolate Festival
October 17, 2026
Monroeville Convention Center | 209 Mall Boulevard, Monroeville
The Pittsburgh Coffee & Chocolate Festival is a celebration of all things coffee and chocolate-related… and then some! Attendees will sample a variety of coffee roasts and styles, along with a multitude of decadent chocolate-themed sweets from local and surrounding-area businesses.
Pittsburgh Monster Pumpkins Festival
October 17-18, 2026
Pittsburgh Strip District
Join in on the action during the Pittsburgh Monster Pumpkins Festival, featuring a "Costume Caper" 5K run/walk, pumpkin regatta, pumpkin pie eating contest, giant pumpkin sculptures, and, of course, record-breaking giant pumpkins. This year's event is in the Strip District, at 3 Crossings Campus along 28th & 29th Street.
Fall Fest at the Pittsburgh Playhouse
October 23 - November 1, 2026
Pittsburgh Playhouse | 350 Forbes Avenue, Pittsburgh
The award-winning Pittsburgh Playhouse at Point Park University, a multi-disciplinary arts and entertainment center located in Downtown Pittsburgh, announces the return of Downtown Pittsburgh’s second annual multi-day Fall Fest and the family-friendly hit sensation, Boo’s Halloween House Party. Visitors of all ages can enjoy food & drinks, games, local vendors and arts & crafts onsite of the Pittsburgh Playhouse, with activities on Forbes Avenue during the weekend.
Trick or Trolley
October 24-25, 2026
Pennsylvania Trolley Museum | 1 Electric Way, Washington
Come to the Pennsylvania Trolley Museum to trick-or-treat among the antique streetcars! Visit tables run by local businesses and organizations and collect all kinds of candy and holiday favors. Costumes are encouraged for all ages!
November
Three Rivers Film Festival
November 4 - 15, 2026
Multiple Locations
Mark your November calendars for the annual Three Rivers Film Festival. If you love indie filmmaking, support the local arts, or simply love a good movie, the Three Rivers Film Festival gives you the opportunity to catch brand-new films from emerging artists. The event also hosts workshops for those looking to sharpen their craft or break into the industry.
Grab your tickets on the Three Rivers Film Festival website.
Latin American and Caribbean Festival
November 7, 2026
Wesley W. Posvar Hall, 1st Floor | 230 South Bouquet Street, Pittsburgh
It's official! The Latin American and Caribbean Festival has become the largest and most significant gathering of Latin American and Latinx communities in Western Pennsylvania! Don't miss out on the event's cultural celebrations, performances, foods, and more!
