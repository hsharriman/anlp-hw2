Discover Your Persona
October 25, 2026 6:00 PM –9:00 PM
Justin Silva is a rising stand-up comedian known for high-energy performances, relatable storytelling and viral comedy content. The New Haven-born performer brings an engaging, contemporary style shaped by personal experiences and sharp observations, creating an upbeat atmosphere for comedy fans. His live work showcases a lively stage presence and accessible humor designed to connect with a broad audience. Silva’s growing profile, including appearances as part of his Sorta Rican Tour, makes him a compelling choice for visitors seeking a fresh voice in today’s comedy scene.
