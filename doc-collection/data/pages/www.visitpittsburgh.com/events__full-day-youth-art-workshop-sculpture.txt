November 3, 2026 8:30 AM –5:00 PM
In these workshops, students grades 2-5 will explore art and history through the Frick’s collections while developing their art skills across creative mediums with teaching artists. These workshops inspire curiosity and give students a safe place to connect, discover, and create. Participants can expect to leave with between two to five finished works of art depending on the workshop and pace of the artist.
Whether your child is a budding artist, working on a CAPA application, or wants to try something new, this workshop gives students the opportunity to create 3D works in a variety of ways.
