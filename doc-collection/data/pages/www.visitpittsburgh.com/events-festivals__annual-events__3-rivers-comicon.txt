3 Rivers Comicon
Celebrate the endless mediums of the entertainment universe at the annual 3 Rivers Comicon, connecting with fellow fans and meeting beloved icons.
June 6-7, 2026
Every year, the 3 Rivers Comicon brings together fans across many entertainment realms and launches an out-of-this-world event that’s filled with plenty of exceptional experiences. Don your best costume and meet the creators behind a variety of comic books. Support Pittsburgh’s arts scene by unearthing new stories and artwork from local vendors and businesses. Whether you’re planning to meet a renowned storyteller or looking to shop for one-of-a-kind merchandise, we have the ultimate guide to your comicon-centered itinerary.

Dates, Location & Admission
Dates
June 6-7, 2026
Location
David L. Lawrence Convention Center | 1000 Fort Duquesne Boulevard
Admission
Adult: $18 -$28
Children who are between the ages of nine and 14: $6 - $12
Featured Experiences
Special Guests - From comic book artists to writers, there is no shortage of featured talents at 3 Rivers Comicon. Meet the masterminds behind series such as “Batman,” “Mandalorian,” and “Generation-X.” Make your way to a panel and learn about the makings of various stories with different experts.
Cosplay Competition - Dress as your favorite characters and enter an exciting cosplay contest! Awards include $1000 in prizes and titles such as “Craftsmanship,” “Judges’ Favorites,” and “Best in Show.”
Local Vendors - Visit the Artist Alley and marvel at a wide range of striking pieces, from visual prints to handcrafted keepsakes. Revel in the ultimate shopping spree by taking home paintings, ceramic ware, books, accessories, and more.
Food & Drinks
The convention center is in close proximity to sensational culinary options for diverse palates. Look through our guide on the top places to eat in downtown Pittsburgh and grab a bite before or after the convention.
Where to Stay
Looking for hotels near the 3 Rivers Comicon? Explore flexible accommodations, budget-friendly prices, and comfortable amenities in downtown Pittsburgh, staying at a hotel that’s within walking distance to the David L. Lawrence Convention Center.
