Discover Your Persona
November 12, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Sombr is an emerging artist known for blending alternative pop, indie influences, and emotionally introspective songwriting into a moody and atmospheric sound. His music often features dreamy production, understated vocals, and reflective lyrics that explore themes of identity, relationships, and personal growth. Sombr has built attention through a distinct aesthetic that combines modern bedroom-pop textures with darker alternative undertones. With a growing online following and a unique sonic style, Sombr continues to establish himself as a rising voice in the indie and alternative music scene.
