Discover Your Persona
November 4, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Father Frank and Under The Canopy pair up for an evening of introspective songwriting, dreamy roots textures and ethereal harmonies. The California-based singer-songwriters joined forces in 2025, making this a meeting of two complementary projects rather than a solo set. Father Frank’s 2024 album SHUT UP, YOU’RE CREEPING ME OUT includes the eerie “Witch Delivery Service,” “VHS” and “the ghost of you and i,” offering a glimpse of the atmospheric, offbeat songwriting at the heart of the bill.
