Things to Do in Garfield: Arts, Culture & Creative Shopping
The energy in Garfield is fueled by a fiercely independent spirit, a collaborative "maker" culture, and a deeply rooted art scene.
Centered along the bustling Penn Avenue corridor, Garfield is a place where you can watch world-class glassblowing, browse avant-garde art, shop for eco-friendly craft supplies, and even pet adoptable cats—all on the same block. Whether you are looking for a unique handmade gift or an immersive cultural experience, Garfield offers a vibe you won't find anywhere else in the city.
Contemporary Art Galleries & Attractions
Garfield is the beating heart of Pittsburgh’s contemporary art scene. These galleries and creative hubs double as experiential landmarks where you can view, create, and buy breathtaking local work.
Pittsburgh Glass Center | 5472 Penn Ave.
More than just a studio, this is a world-renowned open-access glass art facility. Visitors can watch live glassblowing demonstrations, sign up for a hands-on workshop, or browse their stunning gallery shop featuring breathtaking, one-of-a-kind glass pieces created by local and international artists, ranging from intricate ornaments to bold sculptures.
Silver Eye Center for Photography | 4808 Penn Ave.
A cornerstone of the Penn Avenue arts scene, this gallery is an absolute must-visit for fine art lovers. Silver Eye focuses on contemporary photography through beautifully curated exhibitions that feature both internationally recognized masters and rising local talent.
VaultArt Studio | 5100 Penn Ave.
This unique gallery and workspace supports and promotes artists with disabilities. Producing some of the most vibrant, high-energy, and expressive contemporary art in the city, VaultArt is a required stop to buy original paintings, prints, and ceramics.
Irma Freeman Center for Imagination | 5006 Penn Ave.
A community-oriented art space dedicated to green practices and multimedia creativity. In addition to regular exhibits and performances, their boutique is a treasure trove of quirky, artist-made gifts, handmade ceramics, and original prints that celebrate the neighborhood's creative spirit.
Social, Specialty & Mission-Driven Makers
Shopping in Garfield means supporting local business owners who double as community builders. Here is where you can find specialty gems and interactive retail experiences.
Black Cat Market | 5135 Penn Ave.
A unique hybrid cat café and neighborhood hub. Book a session to hang out with adoptable rescue cats in a cozy lounge environment, and then browse their retail space featuring local art, zines, stickers, and vintage items.
Workshop Fiber Studio | 5131 Penn Ave.
Part DIY school and part retail shop, this is the ultimate destination for creators. Stop in to shop for modern craft supplies, indoor plants, and creative kits, or check their schedule to take an evening class on anything from sewing and woodworking to block printing.
Kraynick's Bike Shop | 5003 Penn Ave.
A legendary Pittsburgh headquarters for local bike culture since 1946. It is part shop, part community center—you can find rare vintage parts, buy a refurbished ride, or use their upstairs DIY workshop and tools to work on your own bicycle.
Experiences & Signature Events
To truly experience Garfield, you have to sync your visit with the community calendar.
The "Unblurred" First Friday Gallery Crawl
On the first Friday of every single month, Penn Avenue comes alive for the city’s premier arts crawl. Galleries, boutiques, and restaurants open their doors late, featuring new exhibit openings, live music, street performers, and pop-up makers. It is the best night to experience the neighborhood's creative energy at full volume.
Planning Tip: The Penn Avenue Stroll
The best way to experience Garfield is entirely on foot. Start your journey near the intersection of Penn Avenue and Winebiddle Street (by the Pittsburgh Glass Center) and walk westbound toward 48th Street. This allows you to easily pop in and out of the storefronts, admire the neighborhood's famous street murals, and grab a coffee or a bite along the way.
