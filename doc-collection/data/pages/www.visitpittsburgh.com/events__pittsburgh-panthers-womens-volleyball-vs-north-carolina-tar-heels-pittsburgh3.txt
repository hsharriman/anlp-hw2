November 8, 2026 1:00 PM –3:00 PM
Get ready for an electrifying showdown as the Pittsburgh Panthers Women's Volleyball takes on the North Carolina Tar Heels at the iconic Petersen Events Center in Pittsburgh! This thrilling match promises to be a highlight of the season, showcasing the incredible talent and teamwork of both squads. The atmosphere in the Petersen Events Center will be buzzing with excitement, as fans rally behind their teams in a celebration of sportsmanship and athleticism.
Join us for an unforgettable evening filled with powerful spikes, strategic plays, and the camaraderie of fellow volleyball enthusiasts. Whether you're a die-hard fan or new to the game, this is the perfect opportunity to experience the energy and passion of college volleyball. Don’t miss out on the action—secure your tickets now for the Pittsburgh Panthers Women's Volleyball vs. North Carolina Tar Heels - Pittsburgh!
