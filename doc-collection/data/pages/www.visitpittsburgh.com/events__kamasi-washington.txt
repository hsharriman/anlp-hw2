Discover Your Persona
November 6, 2026 6:00 PM –9:30 PM
Kamasi Washington is an acclaimed saxophonist, composer and bandleader whose expansive sound blends contemporary jazz with elements of funk, gospel, classical music and hip-hop. Known for his powerful live ensembles and adventurous arrangements, he first gained broad attention through his landmark album The Epic and collaborations with artists including Kendrick Lamar. His fifth studio album, Fearless Movement, explores rhythm, dance and the emotional connections between generations. Washington’s performances offer an immersive jazz experience shaped by soaring saxophone lines, dynamic instrumentation and a spirit of musical discovery.
