Bloomfield
Welcome to Pittsburgh’s "Little Italy."
Bloomfield is a welcoming, deeply rooted neighborhood where multi-generational heritage blends seamlessly with modern community charm. Hugging the bustling commercial strip of Liberty Avenue, Bloomfield is famous for its old-world Italian markets, towering classic neon signs, and friendly, independent business owners. From historic scratch bakeries and legendary hardwood grills to progressive bookstores and highly acclaimed new Scandinavian bistros, Bloomfield offers a rich, comforting experience defined by immense local pride and time-honored traditions.
5 Must-See Experiences in Bloomfield
1. Bite into a Legendary Tessaro's Burger
Pull up a chair at this iconic Pittsburgh landmark to enjoy a thick, juicy hamburger ground fresh daily by an in-house butcher and seared over a custom iron grill fueled entirely by Pennsylvania hardwood.
2. Shop the Historic Markets of Little Italy
Take a sensory stroll through multi-generational staples like Bloomfield Groceria and Merante's, where you can pick up handmade ravioli, fresh gnocchi, imported olive oils, and authentic Italian housewares.
3. Get Lost in White Whale Bookstore
Browse the beautifully curated shelves of this independent, family-owned literary haven that serves as a vital community space, complete with a cozy café serving local coffee and craft beers.
4. Catch a Bocce Game under the Bridge
Walk down toward the Bloomfield Bridge next to the public park on a warm evening to watch locals gather for lively matches on the public bocce courts, keeping a classic neighborhood tradition alive.
5. Savor an Authentic Cannoli at Paddy Cake Bakery
Satisfy your sweet tooth at this traditional scratch bakery along Liberty Avenue, a mandatory neighborhood stop famous for its fresh breads, oversized cookies, and crisp, perfectly piped cannoli.
Pro Tip: Cross the Avenue for Contemporary Arts
Bloomfield's deep historic roots make it a masterclass in local tradition, but it sits directly adjacent to one of the city's most progressive cultural districts. When you reach the western end of Liberty Avenue near West Penn Hospital, simply walk north down 48th Street for less than five minutes. You'll land right on Penn Avenue, instantly connecting you to a completely different neighborhood vibe.
Looking to experience this neighboring creative powerhouse? Head over to our Garfield Neighborhood Hub to explore local galleries and maker scene.
