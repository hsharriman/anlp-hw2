A Guide to the Pittsburgh Fringe Festival
Explore the Pittsburgh Fringe Festival with event details on performers, special activities, and more.
Pittsburgh is a thriving hub for awe-inspiring performances, and this annual event is no exception. Traverse across various entertainment realms by experiencing the Pittsburgh Fringe Festival–an all-encompassing celebration of the arts with visual and theatrical creators from around the world. From musicals and puppetry to visual art displays and spoken word poetry, this annual event offers immersive fun for all ages.

Top Things to Do During the Festival
Delve into the creative side of Pittsburgh with one-of–a-kind artistic experiences. Providing a platform for emerging artists and entertainers, the Fringe Festival showcases the works of burgeoning talents. When you step into an event venue, settle into your seat and watch stories come alive through a variety of perspectives.
Pittsburgh Fringe Festival Information
Dates - April 18 - 27, 2024
Location - The event’s venues are placed across a “Fringe Zone,” which consists of the Bloomfield, Friendship, Garfield, and East Liberty neighborhoods.
Admission - Tickets to specific Fringe events vary depending on different performances, but prices will typically range from $5 to $20 per person. Fringe Day and Weekend Passes are also available with discounted entry to the event’s shows.

2024 Entertainment Lineup
The Pittsburgh Fringe Festival spotlights a wide assortment of acts and performances, including dance shows, plays, and opera recitals. Listen to pop-infused songs from American Idol contestant and singer Sloane Simon. Tap your feet to infectious beats while the Pittsburgh-based Ray Lanich Band performs catchy rock tunes. Laugh on the edge of your seat as stand up comedian Krish Mohan lands perfect deliveries.
Top Things to Do During the Festival
Connect with Fellow Art Enthusiasts with a Fringe Button
Looking for new ways to dive into Pittsburgh’s arts community? Fringe buttons aim to spark conversations between event goers, discussing specific shows and performances during the festival. Visitors who are purchasing tickets have the option to receive a three dollar discount on admission with a button. Support the Fringe Festival by buying a button and bonding with other festival participants during the event.
Fun Fact: Start a conversation with the quirky history behind this unique celebration of the arts. Originating in 1947, several theater groups entered Scotland’s Edinburgh International Festival and were denied admission. However, these theater groups continued their performances by the “fringe” of the event’s grounds, establishing and inspiring Fringe Festivals around the globe.
Take a Culinary Side Quest with Nearby Restaurants
Indulge in mouthwatering eats and drinks between lively performances, exploring local culinary hotspots. With performance venues nestled in East End neighborhoods, the Pittsburgh Fringe Festival sets the perfect scene for snack breaks, post-show dinners, and more. Spork is an upscale spot with a seasonally driven menu, serving exquisite bites that utilize locally sourced ingredients. Don’t miss making a trip to Duo’s Taqueria, relishing authentic tacos with generous heaps of cilantro and salsa. Craving Italian food? Fish Nor Fowl is an exceptional option with scrumptious pasta and a curated wine menu.

Expand Your Arts Adventure with Museums & Galleries
Along with the lively performances from the Fringe Festival, there are other exciting ways to fully immerse yourself in the arts. Pittsburgh is brimming with some of the finest and most innovative art establishments in the country, where eye-catching installations and exhibits will beckon you into the lenses of creative geniuses. Explore legendary works at the Carnegie Museum of Art, or admire vivid displays at the Andy Warhol Museum. Make your way to a plethora of local art hubs such as 707 Penn Gallery, Pittsburgh Glass Center, and Contemporary Craft.
