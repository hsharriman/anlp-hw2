October 30, 2026 7:00 PM –6:00 PM
Marcus D. Wiley is a nationally recognized stand-up comedian, motivational speaker, and entertainer whose uplifting brand of comedy blends faith, family values, and everyday life into an engaging live performance. Known for his clean yet hilarious storytelling, Wiley connects with audiences through relatable observations about marriage, parenting, work, and the humorous moments that arise from ordinary experiences. His charismatic stage presence and conversational delivery allow him to seamlessly combine heartfelt messages with laugh-out-loud humor, creating shows that appeal to a wide range of audiences. In addition to touring extensively, he has gained recognition through television appearances, comedy specials, and viral performances that showcase his ability to entertain while leaving audiences with a positive message. Whether performing in theaters, comedy clubs, churches, or special events, Marcus D. Wiley delivers an energetic and memorable performance filled with genuine laughter, thoughtful insights, and the infectious personality that has made him one of today's standout clean comedians.
