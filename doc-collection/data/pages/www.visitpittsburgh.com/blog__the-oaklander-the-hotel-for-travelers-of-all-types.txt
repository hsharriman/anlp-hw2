The Oaklander: The Hotel for Travelers of All Types
Foodie? Fashionista? Fit right in at The Oaklander
When you travel, a hotel is way more than a place to lay down at night. Your hotel is an extension of you as a traveler and should pair with your trip to Pittsburgh as nicely as the glass of wine with dinner.
Enter The Oaklander, one of Pittsburgh's newest hotels, where luxury and elegance meets a thriving historic neighborhood. Envision yourself at The Oaklander no matter what type of traveler you are, then make plans for your next stay.
The Influencer
If your phone eats before you do, The Oaklander is for you. Everything on-site is Gram-ready from the moment you walk in. Your followers will love the panoramic views of Oakland, French brasserie-inspired restaurant and luxurious rooms packed with amenities. There's no bad angles here.
The Pamperer
Turkish cotton bedding, linens and robes. Clawfoot tubs. Nespresso coffee maker. Room service. You never need to leave the room, much less the bathtub, during your stay at The Oaklander. Soak in the views on your lush bedding or just soak in the tub and enjoy the silence with a drink and fresh berries close at hand. This is what relaxed luxury is all about.
The Hush Trip Taker
Shhhh! Your secret's safe with us. Hush Trips are the top travel trend of 2023. Take a work-ation and bring the laptop to the Oaklander where your workload will be eased by relaxing views, upscale ambiance and room service. No one except you will ever know you're working from a clawfoot tub with a glass of wine in hand.
The Socializer
Not only is the Oakland neighborhood bustling, The Oaklander itself is loaded with events to stay social! Meet a new bestie at their monthly Jazz Brunch or Born to Rum Social, jam at the Rock 'n' Oak recording studio onsite or venture out to Yoga on the Lawn. Keep the party going at Spirits & Tales with menu-driven wine, craft beer and some of the best vibes in the city.
The Fashionista
Treat yourself to a stay that looks as good as you do. The Oaklander's upscale accommodations will match your wardrobe perfectly, and your shopaholic self is just a 5-minute Uber away from the trendy boutiques in Shadyside, Squirrel Hill, Bakery Square and Lawrenceville. Just make sure you bring a suitcase large enough to get it all home.
The Bachelorette
Planning a bachelorette party or need to drop a hint to the Maid of Honor? The Oaklander will be the most-talked about moment of the weekend. It's perfectly located with great food, drinks and dancing nearby, and then party bus will drop you off in style to rest and recharge. The Presidential Suite can also connect to a King View City Room, giving tons of space for your gal pals.
The Date Night Planner
Take the stress out of planning a romantic date night or anniversary weekend and trust in the top-notch service of The Oaklander. With valet parking and expert concierge and front desk hospitality from the moment you arrive, you can focus on that special someone. For a special treat, consider their Brasserie Bonjour Package which includes French treats delivered to your room daily including wine, baguette, brie and macarons from Pittsburgh favorite Gaby et Jules.
The Pet Pamperer
Your furry friends are welcome at The Oaklander. Pets up to 50 lbs can accompany their human (two pet per room maximum) and will enjoy their walks into the bustling hospital and collegiate districts or exploring nearby Schenley Park for a fantastic hillside view overlooking the city proper.
The Scholar
Innovation thrives at The Oaklander, located in the hub of Pittsburgh higher education. Discover new ideas, finish that book you've been writing, collaborate, research and explore at your leisure; you're just steps away from the University of Pittsburgh as well as Carnegie Mellon, Carlow and Chatham universities during your stay.
