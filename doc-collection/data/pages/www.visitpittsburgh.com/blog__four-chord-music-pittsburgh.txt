Four Chord Music Festival 2026
Four Chord Music Festival is a two-day punk rock music festival held September 25–26, 2026 at F.N.B. Stadium in Pittsburgh, PA, headlined by Knocked Loose and Pierce the Veil.

Four Chord Music Festival has quickly grown into a must-visit for rock music fans around the country. Back and better than ever in 2026, the iconic festival is returns to Pittsburgh proper at F.N.B. Stadium. Join over 10,000 music fans to see Knocked Loose, Pierce the Veil, Mayday Parade, Boys Like Girls, and many more.
Get ready for the festival with this essential guide.
Know Before You Go
When & where is Four Chord Music Festival held?
The event takes place on Friday, September 25 at 12:30pm and Saturday, September 26 at 12pm. It's the first time Four Chord is being held at F.N.B. Stadium in Station Square, home to the Pittsburgh Riverhounds and Pittsburgh Riveters.
Quick Facts
Date & Time: Friday, Sept. 25, 2026 at 12:30pm and Saturday, Sept. 26, 2026 at 12pm
Location: F.N.B. Stadium (510 W Station Square Dr, Pittsburgh, PA 15219)
Price: $131-$545
Tickets: Get Four Chord Music Festival Tickets
The Experience
Four Chord has become a bucket-list Pittsburgh tradition - a chance to see world-class punk and rock bands alongside thousands of fans who love the music as much as you do. Now held at a stadium with gorgeous skyline views, the festival shows off the best of both the music and the city.
Must-See Highlights
See the headliners: Knocked Loose is headlining Friday night, while Pierce the Veil is closing out the festival on Saturday.
Get a tattoo: Sacred Form Tattoo Studio will be tattooing onsite at Four Chord! Secure your spot with a $100 deposit.

What's New for 2026
New Venue
After being held in Washington County over the last several years, Four Chord is coming home to Pittsburgh. The newly named F.N.B. Stadium, formerly Highmark Stadium, sits on the South Shore of the Monongahela River with sweeping views of the Pittsburgh skyline.
How do I get to Four Chord Music Festival?
Whether you are arriving by car or bringing your bike on public transit, getting to the Station Square is straightforward.
By Car & Drop-Off: Set your GPS to 510 W Station Square Dr, Pittsburgh, PA 15219.
Parking Recommendations: There are parking passes at F.N.B. Stadium available for purchase on Four Chord's website. There's also a plethora of parking in Station Square, from garages to street parking. You can even park Downtown and walk across the Smithfield Street Bridge if you feel like enjoying the sunshine.
By Bus or T (Pittsburgh Regional Transit): You can access Station Square using PRT bus routes 21, 24, 26, 27, and 38. You can also take the T rail system, since Station Square is the first stop outside of the designated free zone in Downtown and North Shore.
By Incline: If you're coming from Mt. Washington, you can take the Duquesne Incline or Monongahela Incline to get to Station Square while taking in a gorgeous view of Downtown.
Local Spots to Eat and Stay Nearby
Where to Eat
While festival-goers can conveniently purchase food and drinks at Four Chord, you can also explore the excellent local dining scene. Station Square is full of restaurants that taste like they've been waiting for you. Swing by Yinz Coffee for a pick-me-up before the festival, and stop by Homerun Harry's for a bite of bar food after. Then, head to The Finch for rooftop drinks and good vibes.

Frequently Asked Questions
What happens if it rains?
Four Chord Music Festival is a rain-or-shine event, however, there are several instances in which the festival may be paused or times will be altered to inclement weather, including (but not limited to): severe thunderstorms, lightning in the festival radius, excessive wind, hail, flooding, or as directed by local emergency personnel.
When does the first band go on?
Doors and set times are all subject to change. Sept 25th, 2026 (Friday) doors are scheduled to open at 1:00PM with the first band on at 2:00PM. Deluxe VIP & VIP doors are scheduled to open at 12:30PM. Sept 26th, 2026 (Saturday) doors are scheduled to open at 12:00PM with the first band on at 1:00PM. Deluxe VIP & VIP doors are scheduled to open at 11:30AM.
Is Four Chord all-ages?
Four Chord Music Fest is an all-ages event. Parents are encouraged to bring hearing and sun protection for their children. Please note that the festival features an adult environment, which may include strong language & behavior not suited for younger attendees. Parental discretion is advised. If your child is under the age of 5, they do not need a ticket.
What do I do if I have a problem with my ticket?
If you misplaced, lost, or no longer have your tickets/ticket confirmation email, please send an email with the name + email on the order to fourchordmusicfestival@gmail.com to have it retrieved.
