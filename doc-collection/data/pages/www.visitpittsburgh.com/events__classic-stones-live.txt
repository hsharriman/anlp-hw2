November 6, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Classic Stones Live is an eight-piece Rolling Stones tribute featuring Keith Call as Mick Jagger and Bernie Bollendorf as Keith Richards. The group recreates the band’s classic sound with original tunings, detailed arrangements, signature elements such as the saxophone solo in “Brown Sugar,” and backing vocals on “Gimme Shelter.” Its repertoire includes staples such as “Jumpin’ Jack Flash,” “Paint It Black,” “Brown Sugar,” and “Gimme Shelter,” presented as a recreation of the Rolling Stones in their legendary prime.
