Visit Contemporary Craft for our next Community Day: HALLOWEEN EDITION on Saturday October 31!
Join us for a day of family-friendly fun and Halloween-themed activities in our Studios! Visit for hands-on, seasonal activities led by local artists and maybe even some crafty trick-or-treating!
Community Day is FREE and open to the public. Come check out artist demos, make some art, and enjoy some light snacks. Costumes are encouraged!
Contemporary Craft is supported in part by the taxpayers of Allegheny County through a public grant from the Allegheny Regional Asset District (RAD). RAD works here.
