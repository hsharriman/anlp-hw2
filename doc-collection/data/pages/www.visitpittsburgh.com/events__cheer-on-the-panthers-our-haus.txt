Cheer on the Pitt Panthers at Our Häus all season long!
Wear your Panther gear on game days, bring the energy, and enjoy the perfect fan perk — a FREE pretzel with your visit.
Whether you’re pre‑gaming, watching with friends, or celebrating a big win, Our Häus is the spot for great bier, great food, and Panther pride.
Grab your crew, throw on the blue and gold, and make game day even better at Our Häus.
