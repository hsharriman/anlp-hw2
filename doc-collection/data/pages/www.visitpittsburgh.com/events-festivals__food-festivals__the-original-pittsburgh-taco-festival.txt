The Original Pittsburgh Taco Festival 2026
Celebrate all things tacos by attending the original Pittsburgh Taco Festival every September. Learn more about the food, fun, and entertainment.
Pittsburgh is a foodie’s haven, and our taco scene is no exception. With freshly made corn tortilla shells and tons of flavorful fillings, this annual festival in Pittsburgh is the ultimate taco adventure. Stroll through top-notch vendors, enjoy the delicious aromas filling the air, and taste the best tacos in the Burgh.

The Original Pittsburgh Taco Festival
Festival Food & Drink
From classic dishes to modern twists, The Original Pittsburgh Taco Festival features over 25 food trucks serving more than just tacos. Vendors to look forward to in 2026 include Don Polo Mexican Food, Tacos La Catedral, and Juniper Grill.
Enjoy empanadas, pupusas, arepas, and other delicious Mexican street food, as well as ice pops and fresh fruit. Vegan and gluten-free options will also be available from different vendors. Plus, there will be full bars with plenty of options for your favorite cocktail and mocktail taco pairings.
View More Taco Festival Vendors
Activities
Stroll through an art market with collections of handmade pieces. Enjoy hands-on art installations and experiences. Connect with local non-profits and groups that support Pittsburgh's Latin American community.
Many of the activities at the Pittsburgh Taco Festival are family-friendly too! Event-goers are encouraged to enjoy tacos and unforgettable fun with their little ones, including free face painting sessions and fun games.
Music & Dancing
Enjoy traditional and modern Latin music performances from local musicians, as well as high-energy DJ sets. Get into the music with on-site salsa dancing lessons and enjoy watching live performances.
Frequently Asked Questions
Do tickets include tacos?
Ticket costs go toward producing the event, including tables, tents, and entertainment. Vendors charge for their food and drink and keep 100% of the revenue they earn during the festival.
Are dogs allowed at the festival?
No. Due to the number of people and the amount of food at the festival, organizers ask that dogs stay at home.
Can I buy tickets at the gate?
This event typically sells out. If there are tickets available on the day of the event, they will be sold at the box office on a first-come, first-served basis.
Are credit cards accepted?
Payments vary from vendor to vendor. Those that only accept cash will have signage indicating so. It is always a good idea to have some extra cash on hand just in case.
Where can I park?
There are many options for both street and paid parking in lots and garages throughout Pittsburgh's Strip District.
