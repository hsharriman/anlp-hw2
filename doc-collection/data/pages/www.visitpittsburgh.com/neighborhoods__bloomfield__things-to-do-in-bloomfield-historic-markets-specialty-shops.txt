Things to Do in Bloomfield: Historic Markets & Specialty Shops
Stepping into Bloomfield means experiencing a perfect blend of old-world heritage and modern neighborhood charm.
Affectionately known as Pittsburgh’s "Little Italy," the heart of Bloomfield beats along the bustling Liberty Avenue commercial corridor. Here, towering neon signs from multi-generational family businesses sit side-by-side with progressive independent bookstores, artisan green grocers, and sweet culinary boutiques.
Visiting Bloomfield is an experience defined by friendly local owners, handmade delicacies, and unique hidden gems that preserve a tight-knit community spirit.
Landmarks of Little Italy & Gourmet Markets
For over a century, Bloomfield has been Pittsburgh’s premier destination for authentic Italian flavors, imported pantry goods, and specialty housewares. These neighborhood institutions preserve the rich culinary heritage of the community.
Bloomfield Groceria | 237 Cedarville St.
This neighborhood institution captures a classic, old-world market feel with its dedication to handmade quality. Local food lovers frequent this spot to stock up on fresh ingredients for home-cooked feasts, including their famous handmade ravioli, fresh gnocchi, and custom-cut Italian lunch meats.
Merante's Bloomfield | 4723 Liberty Ave.
Billed as the local shop with an "Italian accent," this beloved family-owned retail storefront is a staple for cooks and decorators alike. It is packed to the brim with high-quality imported Italian housewares, traditional pizzelle irons, regional cooking gear, and beautiful imported home décor. Beyond retail, they offer popular cooking classes where visitors can learn regional recipes from local experts.
Linea Verde Green Market | 4911 Liberty Ave.
A vibrant, modern artisan market that beautifully complements the historic commercial strip by prioritizing fresh, seasonal, and regional goods. Linea Verde specializes in high-quality local produce, regional dairy products, fresh flowers, and specialty Italian pantry staples like artisanal olive oils, local pastas, and hearty soups.
Independent Pages & Specialty Shops
Bloomfield is a sanctuary for book lovers, writers, and those seeking rare, intentional retail finds and specialized craftsmanship.
White Whale Bookstore | 4754 Liberty Ave.
An independent, family-owned bookstore that serves as a vibrant cultural anchor for the neighborhood. They feature beautifully curated shelves focusing on contemporary fiction, poetry, history, and titles by local Pittsburgh authors. The space includes a warm, inclusive neighborhood café that pours local coffees and custom craft beers, regularly hosting lively author readings and community book clubs.
The Big Idea Bookstore & Cafe | 4812 Liberty Ave.
Operating as a progressive, non-profit, worker-owned cooperative, this bookstore brings a deeply rooted community activism spirit to Liberty Avenue. It features a wide and unique array of radical literature, independent zines, progressive political theory, and counter-culture titles that are tough to find anywhere else, alongside a cozy café space ideal for browsing alternative media.
Best-Made Shoes | 4814 Liberty Ave.
A legendary Bloomfield landmark that represents the neighborhood’s enduring commitment to specialized, high-quality local craftsmanship. In operation since 1939, this third-generation, family-owned business is the premier full-service shoe and orthotic center in the city, specializing in custom-molded shoes and precision repairs built to last.
Experiences & Signature Events
To truly experience Bloomfield's unique neighborhood energy, try to plan your visit around these local highlights:
Little Italy Days
Every August, Liberty Avenue transforms into a massive, multi-day outdoor festival celebrating Italian-American culture. The street closes to vehicular traffic, welcoming hundreds of local artisans, food vendors, street performers, and musicians for the city's largest heritage block party.
2026 Dates: August 13-16, 2026
Bocce under the Bridge
After a day of exploring along Liberty Avenue, take a walk down toward the Bloomfield Bridge. Next to the public park, you’ll find the neighborhood's active public bocce courts, where locals gather on warm evenings for games, preserving an enduring community tradition.
Planning Tip: The Liberty Avenue Jaunt
The entire Bloomfield commercial district is highly walkable and concentrated. Start your journey near the West Penn Hospital corridor on Liberty Avenue and head eastbound toward the Bloomfield Bridge. This route keeps you completely immersed in the sights, smells, and sounds of the Italian grocers, bakeries, and independent shops that make Bloomfield unique.
