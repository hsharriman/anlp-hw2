Thank You
Thank you for submitting your request! A member of our Convention Sales team will contact you shortly to discuss your requests and provide personal assistance.
Whether you are planning a single-hotel or city-wide event, the VisitPITTSBURGH Convention Sales team stands ready to assist you throughout the entire process. From the moment you consider Pittsburgh until your last attendee departs, our expert staff, which includes our Destinations Services team, will provide you the resources you need to create the productive and welcoming experience your delegates deserve!
