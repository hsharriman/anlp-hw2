Things to Do
In Pittsburgh, there is no shortage of interesting and engaging attractions, activities and fun.
You’ll want to explore fan favorites like The Andy Warhol Museum and Carnegie Museums of Art and Natural History. A ride on one of the iconic inclines is another must. Take in the view of Pittsburgh's 90 neighborhoods from the top of Mt. Washington. After you've snapped the perfect selfie, venture beyond the city to find Frank Lloyd Wright’s Fallingwater and Kentuck Knob.
Find the quirkier side of Pittsburgh in some of the city’s weirdest attractions. Or, create your own scavenger hunt to find these hidden gems in and around town. Discover something totally unexpected with interactive and immersive theater experiences. Leave Pittsburgh with a story to tell.
