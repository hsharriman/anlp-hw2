October 26, 2026 10:00 AM –12:30 PM
The Strip District tour is a late morning/early afternoon weekday tour featuring a behind-the-counter look at a variety of eateries in Pittsburgh's historic market district.
This tour links local history with the eating establishments peppered along Penn Avenue with taste samples from around the world including Italian pastries, Mediterranean and Latin American specialties, European meats, and cinnamon bread.
Visit our website to purchase your tickets online!
https://www.burghfoodtour.com
Allergies/Food Restrictions
Vegetarian substitutions available. Please note - Substitutions for vegetarians, must be requested in advance with your ticket purchase. All food has been prepared in advance at each location, based on the information you provide to us at the time of ticket purchase. Last minute dietary changes the day of the tour will not be accommodated for any dietary restrictions. This is a carefully produced foodie adventure!
