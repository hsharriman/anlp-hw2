November 14, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Pittsburgh six-piece Sunny Daze & The Weathermen mark the release of Overcast with a hometown show featuring Sorry Face and Zinnia’s Garden. Formed in 2022, the band calls its sound “flower punk,” mixing garage rock, psych-punk and neo-psychedelic influences with 1960s-inspired rock and political messaging. Their adventurous approach extends to the stage, where members use unconventional sounds and percussion to reshape songs in the moment.
