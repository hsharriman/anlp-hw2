Discover Your Persona
November 8, 2026 6:00 PM –9:00 PM
Mark Paul is a touring stand-up comedian whose comedy draws on observations about parents, youth sports and the often-competitive world of travel baseball. He first gained attention through social-media content exploring how parents treat their children and others in youth sports, then expanded into live comedy performances across the country. His current Daddy Ball Tour brings that distinctive perspective to the stage through original material, relatable stories and commentary on the culture surrounding youth athletics. The result is an energetic comedy experience for sports families, parents and anyone familiar with the sidelines.
