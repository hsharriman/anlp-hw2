Where to Stay: Squirrel Hill
Squirrel Hill Lodging & Local Tips
While Squirrel Hill is famous for its residential charm, planning your visit is easy with plenty of nearby accommodations and seamless transit options. Here is everything you need to know to navigate the neighborhood like a pro.
Where to Stay Near Squirrel Hill
While there are no hotels directly within the neighborhood's borders, Squirrel Hill is perfectly positioned between some of Pittsburgh's most exciting districts. You’ll find upscale boutiques and budget-friendly stays just a short drive or bus ride away:
The Mansions on Fifth Avenue | Shadyside
For a touch of "relaxed luxury," this upscale option offers 22 individually decorated guestrooms and elegant wedding services.
The Oaklander, Autograph Collection | Oakland
Where modern elegance meets extraordinary service, perfect for those looking for a high-end experience near the university district.
The Inn on Negley | Shadyside
A charming boutique bed & breakfast that provides the comfort of a luxury hotel.
Hampton Inn & Courtyard by Marriott | Waterfront
Located just across the river in Homestead, these budget-friendly options offer easy access to shopping and event spaces.
Shadyside at Home Apartments | Shadyside
Ideal for longer stays, offering upscale studio to two-bedroom apartments for a more "live-like-a-local" feel.
Getting Around & Essential Services
Squirrel Hill is a "walker’s paradise" and one of the most accessible neighborhoods in the city.
Public Transit & Biking:
The neighborhood sits on several major PRT bus routes and features dedicated bike lanes for easy commuting.
Parking:
If you're driving, there are four public parking lots and ample on-street parking throughout the main business districts.
Pit Stops:
Need a break? The neighborhood is dotted with benches for resting. You can also head to the Squirrel Hill Library or the JCC, both serve as community hubs with public restrooms.
Stay Connected:
Nearly all local coffee shops, tea rooms, and bubble tea spots offer Wi-Fi for visitors.
