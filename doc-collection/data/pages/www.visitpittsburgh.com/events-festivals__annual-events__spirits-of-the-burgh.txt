Spirits of the Burgh
Spend an evening sampling the very best of Pittsburgh’s craft beverage scene.
Event Details
Featured Experiences
Where to Stay
Things to Do
Spirits of the Burgh is an annual celebration showcasing local distilleries alongside exceptional wines, ciders, and beers from craft breweries. Sample outstanding beverages, dance to live music, and grab a bite from food trucks while connecting with fellow enthusiasts and the makers behind your favorite drinks.
Event Details
Date: Saturday, February 28th, 2026
Time: 6 PM - 9 PM
Location: The Pennsylvanian
Admission: General Admission $39, Designated Driver $20

Featured Experiences
Sample Craft Spirits & Cocktails: Enjoy premium spirits and expertly crafted cocktails from the region's finest distilleries and spirit-makers.
Explore Beer, Wine, Cider & More: Choose from a diverse selection of local beer, wine, cider, and nonalcoholic options available for sampling throughout the evening.
Enjoy Light Bites: When you need to refuel between tastings, order from the food trucks.
Dance to Live Music: Take a break from sampling craft beverages and dance along to the DJ set and mingle with fellow attendees.

A post shared by BEERS OF THE BURGH (@beersoftheburgh)
Where to Stay
Turn your trip to Spirits of the Burgh into a weekend getaway or staycation. The Pennsylvanian's prime location in Downtown Pittsburgh puts you within walking distance of numerous hotels offering modern amenities and comfortable accommodations. Skip the drive home and extend your evening by booking a nearby room where you can relax and wake up ready to explore more of what Pittsburgh has to offer.
Things to Do Before & After Spirits of the Burgh
Spirits of the Burgh takes place on a Saturday night, giving you plenty of time to explore the city before the event kicks off. Spend your day visiting the city's museums and attractions, riding one of the inclines, or stroll through the unique neighborhoods that give Pittsburgh its character. If you're not ready to head back to your hotel after the event wraps up, the city is home to plenty of bars, late-night restaurants, and fun nightlife spots to keep the festivities going.
