Discover Your Persona
October 25, 2026 to December 31, 2026
Discover Pittsburgh’s Northside with the Flat Northside Friends Scavenger Hunt! Starting January 15, families can register in advance to receive their “flat” friends before traveling. Follow clues about 10 local icons, Andy Warhol and H.J. Heinz, while taking photos at historic landmarks across the Burgh! Cost per family is $10.
