Pittsburgh Opera is one of the nation's premier regional opera companies, celebrated for presenting world-class productions that combine exceptional vocal talent, innovative staging, and timeless storytelling. Founded in 1939, the company has built a distinguished reputation through performances of beloved masterpieces by composers such as Verdi, Puccini, Mozart, Rossini, and Bizet, while also championing contemporary operas and new works that expand the art form. Each production features accomplished singers, a full orchestra, striking costumes, and imaginative set designs that bring dramatic narratives to life with extraordinary musical and theatrical artistry. Beyond the stage, Pittsburgh Opera is dedicated to education and community engagement through artist training programs, outreach initiatives, and performances that introduce new audiences to the beauty and emotional power of opera. Whether presenting a classic favorite or a modern masterpiece, Pittsburgh Opera delivers unforgettable performances that showcase the enduring appeal of one of the world's most expressive performing arts traditions.
