Discover Your Persona
October 29, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Gary Vider is a New York City-based stand-up comedian known for his subtle, dry delivery and offbeat perspective. He reached the finals of Season 10 of America’s Got Talent and has appeared on CONAN and The Late Show with Stephen Colbert. Vider’s comedy has also been featured in The New York Times and Time Out New York, while his recent work includes the 2024 YouTube special *It Could Be Worse* and the limited-series podcast *#1 Dad*. His performance offers audiences an engaging evening of smart, personal comedy from a distinctive national voice.
