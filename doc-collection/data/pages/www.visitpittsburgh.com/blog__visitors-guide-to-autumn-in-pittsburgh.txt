2026 Visitor's Guide to Autumn in Pittsburgh
Forge your perfect fall getaway in Pittsburgh with beautiful colors and cozy vibes all season long.
With a mix of indoor and outdoor options, those looking to relax and explore will enjoy Pittsburgh any time of the year. Check out some ideas to plan your perfect autumn trip below:
For the Family
From outdoor family fun to engaging family-friendly museums, kids of all ages will love Pittsburgh in the fall.
Interactive Fun for Small Children
Embrace adventure at the Children's Museum of Pittsburgh with interactive exhibits like the kindness gallery, makeshop®, garage and studio. For the older youth, be sure to visit MuseumLab for cutting-edge experiences in art, tech, metalworking and learning.
Step into a Safari at the Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium
Take in the chilled air during a trip to the zoo. The Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium is tucked away into the sprawling green hills of Highland Park, and feels like it's in its own world even within the city limits. With so many animals to see - think elephants, giraffes, red pandas, and more - your time at the zoo will be a full-day adventure. Visit in October for ZooBoo and the Jack O'Lantern Extravaganza!
Take a Ride at Kennywood
Just because summer's over doesn't mean it's too late to go to the amusement park. Kennywood's rides will have your kids screaming for another go-around all season long. Perfect for thrill seekers and the little ones alike, this not-so-hidden gem outside of the city is a must-visit for families. Be on the lookout for Phantom Fall Fest, which takes over the park every October.
For the Foodie
While Pittsburgh is the destination for foodies all year, fall in Pittsburgh is a special treat. Between seasonal events and spaces that feature diverse options, foodies will obsess over the impressive possibilities.
Pumpkin spice is back in full swing
Pumpkin spice takes over the world every fall, but Pittsburgh handles the craze in a way you've never seen. Our local coffee shops and restaurants go all-in when it comes to the fan favorite flavor. Don't know where to start? We've highlighted the top spots to get your fall-flavored drinks this season.
Take in a cool day at a coffee shop
Is there much better than sipping on a delicious cup of coffee? Luckily, Pittsburgh is home to plenty of cozy coffee shops that you can escape, for the days where the weather isn't quite as warm as you want it to be.
For the Outdoor Enthusiast
Pittsburgh's unique environment makes for interesting outdoor fall activities for adventurers of any age and skill level. Whether you're looking for casual sightseeing or intense seasonal fun, there's a perfect fit for you.
Embark on a Fall Adventure
Fall is the perfect time to take in a brisk hike, and luckily Pittsburgh is teaming with trails fit for the most novice or seasoned hiker. Whether you're going along one of our rivers or venturing out into the surrounding hills and mountains, you'll find a hike that you'll remember heading into the winter months.
Book a Bike Ride
Pittsburgh is a bike-friendly city, with plenty of trails and bike lanes just waiting for you to explore. Either BYOB - bring your own bike - or rent one from POGOH before hitting the pedal to the metal.
Walk Through Incredible Gardens
Explore the beauty and wonder of nature at Phipps Conservatory and Botanical Gardens. The stunning Victorian glasshouse showcases seasonal flower shows, special exhibits and a nationally recognized orchid collection, making Phipps a must-see Pittsburgh attraction no matter the time of year.
For the Sports Fan
Cheer on the Pittsburgh Pirates at PNC Park
If you're looking for an outdoor sports adventure, the Pittsburgh Pirates season is in full swing - pun intended - during the fall months. Catch a fireworks night or a promotional giveaway while enjoying one of the best views from an MLB stadium.
Swing Your Terrible Towel at a Pittsburgh Steelers Game at Acrisure Stadium
Alexa, play Renegade! The fall means that the Pittsburgh Steelers are back in action at Acrisure Stadium in the North Shore. Grab your Terrible Towel and cheer on the Black and Gold all season long.
Watch NHL Legends Take the Ice with the Pittsburgh Penguins at PPG Paints Arena
Fall is a unique time in Pittsburgh, because all of our major sports teams are playing at the same time! The Pittsburgh Penguins begin their season at PPG Paints Arena in October, and now's the perfect time to see NHL legends - both seasoned and in the making - take the ice right before your eyes.
Celebrate Pittsburgh Sports
Celebrate the accomplishments of sports in the region with a visit to the Franco Harris Sports Museum at the Heinz History Center, hidden gem The Roberto Clemente Museum in Lawrenceville and the Steelers Hall of Honor at Acrisure Stadium.
For the Arts Admirer
Enjoy Broadway in Pittsburgh
For the theater enthusiast, don't miss the PNC Broadway in Pittsburgh series, presented by the Pittsburgh Cultural Trust. The 2026 season features Disney's the Lion King.
Connect with Art at CMOA
The visual arts are also thriving this winter with various works of arts and new exhibits on display at the Carnegie Museum of Art. And for 2026, the 59th Carnegie International invites you to take part in its vision with this rendition's theme, "If the word we."
"POP" into The Andy Warhol Museum
Explore seven floors of Warhol’s art, films and life at The Andy Warhol Museum, one of the most comprehensive single-artist museums in the world. In addition to experiencing Warhol’s work, you can create your own at The Factory, an underground studio with activities designed to bring out your inner artist. Sit for a screen test or try your hand at silkscreen or watercolors.
Explore Pittsburgh's Gilded Age at The Frick
The Frick Pittsburgh is a collection of art and history museums located in Pittsburgh’s East End neighborhood. The museum includes a collection of 19th century European art, ever-changing exhibitions and a collection of historic cars and carriages.
Release Your Creativity at the Mattress Factory
Hailed as the best facility for installation art in the United States, this contemporary art museum is an experimental lab featuring site-specific installations created by artists in residence from around the globe. Each exhibition takes you into another world that evokes a unique perspective.
For even more fall activities in Pittsburgh, check out our Outdoor Fun blog.
