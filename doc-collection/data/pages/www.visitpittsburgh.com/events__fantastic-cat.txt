November 7, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Fantastic Cat is a roots-rock supergroup of four New York City singer-songwriters: Anthony D’Amato, Brian Dunne, Don DiLego and Mike Montali. Known for trading lead vocals and swapping instruments, the band blends harmony-rich songwriting with a playful, genre-spanning approach. Their releases include the debut The Very Best of Fantastic Cat, the 2024 album Now That’s What I Call Fantastic Cat, and Cat Out of Hell, released in 2026. Standout songs include "Fiona," "All My Fault" and "Donnie Takes the Bus."
