Pittsburgh’s Hottest Happy Hours
Check out Pittsburgh's top places for a fun time and a killer deal!
Whether you've had a long day, you're on vacation or you just plain feel like it, there's always a good reason for Happy Hour, and Pittsburgh has no shortage of them. Check out our happiest hotspots around the 'Burgh for a sip and a bite towards pure bliss!
Bigham Tavern
This Mount Washington favorite is the perfect place to stop before or after admiring the view on the Grandview Overlook. Check out their happy hour Monday – Friday from 5-7 pm for $1 off drafts, $3 domestic beers, $3 well drinks, $5 house wine and $6 Happetizers. That's five reasons not to miss out on Bigham Tavern!
Credit |
Bigham Tavern Facebook Page
Carmella's Plates & Pints
While strolling down East Carson Street in the South Side, a stop at Carmella's Plates and Pints is definitely worth adding to the schedule. This cozy spot offers 'social hour' from 4-6 pm on weekdays and Sundays for 25% off shared plates, brews, cocktails and wine on tap. You'll also find daily food specials throughout the week.
Credit |
Carmella's Plates & Pints Facebook Page
Condado Tacos
Who doesn't love a good taco? Condado is serving up some delicious creations and signature margaritas Downtown. Check out their happy hour from 6-7pm on weekdays for $5 signature margs and $1 off Cervezas and Bud Light!
Credit |
Condado Tacos Facebook Page
Industry Public House
Lawrenceville's Butler Street is full of places to shop and dine, making it easy to spend a whole day exploring. When happy hour hits, Industry Public House is the perfect place to settle in. Stop in on a weekday from 4:30-6:30 pm for $2 off draft beers, $8 featured cocktails and a variety of food specials. This warm space has lots of seating and several TVs!
Credit |
Industry Public House
City Works
Stop by City Works every weekday from 4-6 pm for $2 off all local drafts, the backyard flight, hard seltzers, wells, and much more. Happy hour food deals include half-price any bar bites and Kung Pao Cauliflower, Buffalo Chicken Dip and Queso. A spectacular atmosphere to grab a bite and drink with friends after that long work day!
Federal Galley
Four up-and-coming restaurant concepts meet an inspired bar menu of craft cocktails, wine and 20 brews at this unique dining experience. Tuesdays-Fridays you can get half off anything on their bar menu from 4:30-6:30, making this the perfect stop before a Pirates game or venturing to your next bar stop.
Coughlin's Law
Seven days, seven happy hour specials. Coughlin's Law up on Mount Washington has a new reason to visit daily, including $4 full sized drafts on Mondays, $6 mules on Thursdays and $6 crushes for Saturday and Sunday brunch.

Primanti Bros.
Sometimes, simple is best. Half off drinks at select Pittsburgh's signature sandwich shops is as easy as it gets. Plus, their happy hours tend to go late (5-7 p.m. weekdays and even later on Friday and Saturday) making this an awesome stop to carb up and save on drinks before a night on the town.
RedBeard's Sports Bar & Grill
Whether you're Downtown or up on Mt. Washington for a brew, save money at Redbeard's with their happy hour specials every Monday-Friday from 4:30-6:30. Take $3 off domestic drafts or $1 off seasonal cocktails plus $3.50 well drinks and $4 house wines to pair with their signature wings or inside out grilled cheese.
Bridges & Bourbon
Take the Instagram pic everyone will be commenting "Where was this," at Bridges & Bourbon. Their visually stunning cocktails are as amazing to look at as they are to sip. B&B's happy hour specials go Monday-Friday from 4-6:30 p.m. and include an $8 signature old fashion, $10 classic cocktails and more.
Fl. 2
You'll come to Fl. 2 for the happy hour specials Wednesday-Friday from 4-6:30 p.m., but you'll stay late into the night because of the inviting, upscale dining experience and mouthwatering menu, served family style.
