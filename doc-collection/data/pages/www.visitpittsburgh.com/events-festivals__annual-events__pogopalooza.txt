Pogopalooza 2026
Pogopalooza returns to Pittsburgh for a gravity-defying, high-flying weekend of extreme pogo competitions and community fun!
June 12-14, 2026
Across three days, athletes will flip, twist, and launch themselves sky-high using air-powered pogo sticks. Whether you’re a diehard fan or a first-timer, this family-friendly event promises nonstop action, incredible talent, and plenty of chances to jump in on the fun.

What to Expect at Pogopalooza
Friday, June 12, 2026
High Jump
Kick off the weekend with an adrenaline-charged block party in downtown Pittsburgh! Starting at 5:30 PM on 6th Street (between Penn and Ft. Duquesne Blvd), athletes will take on the High Jump competition. Expect music, energy, and sky-high drama as competitors attempt to break the world record and jump over a 12 foot high bar!
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Xpogo (@xpogo)
Saturday, June 13, 2026
Tech Throwback & Big Air Qualifiers
Head to XPOGO HQ in Wilkinsburg starting at 3 PM for a full afternoon of pogo action. Things begin with the Tech Throwback Competition, where spring pogo sticks take center stage in a showcase of precision and control.
At 5 PM, the Big Air Qualifiers crank the intensity up as athletes bounce across the custom pogo park, linking together daring tricks over ramps and rails. Only the top 10 will advance to Sunday’s finals, so the competition will be fierce.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Xpogo (@xpogo)
Sunday, June 14, 2026
Big Air Finals, Best Trick Competition, & 15 and Under Bounce Off
The grand finale starts at 3 PM with the Big Air Finals, where the weekend’s best competitors go all-in for the title of Best Pogoer in the World. With only 60 seconds per run, every flip, twist, and trick counts. At 5 PM, it’s time for the jaw-dropping Best Trick, where athletes battle the clock to land tricks the world has never seen.
Then, at 5:30 PM, it’s the Kids 15 and Under Bounce Off, a free event for young jumpers to see who can pogo the longest and win a brand-new Vurtego Slingshot Kids pogo stick!
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Xpogo (@xpogo)
Bonus fun: Throughout the weekend, enjoy tasty eats from local food vendors and try pogoing yourself in the Free Jump Area, with friendly instructors ready to help you find your bounce.
Where to Stay Near Pogopalooza Pittsburgh
With events split between Downtown Pittsburgh and Wilkinsburg, you’ve got options for how close you want to be to the action. For Friday’s downtown kickoff, stay near Market Square and walk straight to the block party. Want to be closer to the custom pogo park at XPOGO HQ? Look for accommodations in Point Breeze or Squirrel Hill— neighborhoods with quick access to Wilkinsburg and plenty of local flavor.

Your weekend in Pittsburgh doesn’t have to stop at pogo. Fill your itinerary with these activities:

Discover Local Gems in Squirrel Hill - Just minutes from Wilkinsburg, this walkable neighborhood has two leafy parks, indie bookshops, vintage finds, and a unique game store worth visiting.
