This fall promises to be the perfect time to forge lasting memories with your friends and family in Pittsburgh. The waning sun is still shining, the leaves are changing into a blazing array of color, and our community is ready to treat you like a neighbor. This month, you're encouraged to fall in love with fall, and we've got never-before-seen events designed to spark your imagination. From football games at one of the most beautiful stadiums in the country and nationally-acclaimed artists performing in new music venues to a confluence of rivers that provides the perfect backdrop to the changing season, Pittsburgh in October will make you wonder why you haven't visited sooner.

59th Annual Carnegie International
The 59th annual Carnegie International is happening now all throughout the city. Organized every four years by Carnegie Museum of Art, the Carnegie International is the longest-running exhibition of international art in North America. Step into the world of "if the word we," an homage to bringing together artistic practices that engage shared experience, circulation, and worlds in transition.
The 59th edition is the most collaborative and far-reaching to date—a clear expression of the museum’s founding commitment to the art and artists of our time, at once grounded in our locality and extending globally.
