10 Must See Attractions
Beyond the iconic skyline of the Steel City lies a diverse landscape of rolling hills, historic towns, and architectural marvels.
Forget the stress of planning, this is the must-do list that defines the Pittsburgh and Countryside experience! From soaring views to world-famous architecture and the very steel that built America, these 10 essential stops are the backbone of any unforgettable itinerary. Get ready to explore history, art, and pure engineering genius.
The Duquesne and Monongahela Inclines
📸 Get the ultimate 'Burgh selfie!
Known locally as "The Inclines," these century-old funiculars are a living piece of industrial history. Hop aboard either the Duquesne or the Monongahela Incline for a breathtaking ride up Mount Washington. At the top, you're rewarded with the absolute best panoramic view of the famous three-river confluence and the dazzling skyline. It’s more than transit, it’s the region's most iconic photo op!

The Carnegie Museums
🎨 Unleash your inner artist!
The Carnegie Museums offer a staggering cultural deep-dive. While the Museums of Art and Natural History are fantastic anchors, your essential stop is The Andy Warhol Museum. Dedicated to Pittsburgh native Andy Warhol, it’s the largest museum in North America dedicated to a single artist. Spend a day exploring Pop Art's biggest name right in his hometown.

Julie Kahlbaugh
Credit |
Julie Kahlbaugh
Gateway Clipper Fleet
⚓ See the city from a new angle!
You haven't truly seen Pittsburgh until you've cruised its rivers. Embark on a journey with the Gateway Clipper Fleet and glide along the Allegheny, Monongahela, and Ohio Rivers. Choose from historic sightseeing tours to relaxing dinner cruises. It’s the perfect, comfortable way for your group to take in the architecture and history of the city skyline.

Fallingwater
🌳 Walk through a masterpiece!
No trip to Western PA is complete without visiting Fallingwater, Frank Lloyd Wright's world-renowned home built directly over a natural waterfall. Tucked away in the beautiful Laurel Highlands, this UNESCO World Heritage Site is the ultimate fusion of design, engineering, and nature. Prepare to be inspired by architecture that truly lives with the landscape.

Arts & Education at the Hoyt
🎭 Catch a unique performance!
Head out to New Castle for this fantastic cultural hub! The Hoyt Center uses multiple historic venues to promote and present a wide array of visual and performing arts. It’s a vibrant, essential cultural stop that proves the arts thrive across our entire region. See what's on the calendar for your visit!

The Jimmy Stewart Museum
🎬 Meet a Hollywood legend (sort of)!
Visit the hometown of one of Hollywood’s most iconic actors in Indiana, PA! The Jimmy Stewart Museum is a heartwarming tribute to the star of It's a Wonderful Life. Explore exhibits covering his celebrated film career, his distinguished military service, and his life as a beloved Pennsylvania native.

Butler-Built Garage
🚌 Explore the wheels of history!
Just outside Pittsburgh near Butler, PA, discover a unique piece of American transportation history. This monthly Butler-Built garage "open house" celebrates the region’s incredible legacy in bus and transit manufacturing. If your group includes innovation or engineering enthusiasts, this stop offers fascinating insight into how Western PA kept the country moving.

Johnstown Flood National Memorial
🌊 Witness a powerful piece of the past.
Step back to 1889 at this poignant memorial in Southwestern Pennsylvania. The Johnstown Flood was one of the deadliest disasters in American history, caused by the failure of the South Fork Dam. The Johnstown Flood National Memorial site preserves the ruins and tells a powerful story of tragedy, resilience, and the birth of modern disaster relief.

Covered Bridges of Greene County
🌲 Go for the scenic route!
For pure, tranquil charm, take a detour into Greene County and discover its picturesque collection of historic covered bridges. These charming relics are perfect for a gorgeous scenic drive and offer a glimpse into a simpler time. They are particularly spectacular when framed by brilliant fall foliage!

Rivers of Steel National Heritage Area (Carrie Blast Furnace)
🔥 Stand in the shadow of giants!
Understand the sheer scale of the industry that built America. Rivers of Steel preserves the legacy of the steel industry, offering incredible tours of the towering Carrie Blast Furnace. Walking through this massive industrial landmark is a powerful, educational experience that connects you directly to Pittsburgh's famous past.
