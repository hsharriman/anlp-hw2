November 12, 2026 7:30 PM –9:30 PM
Mike Sexton (portrays Toby Keith) has been performing his tribute as the Big Dog Daddy for over 13 years. The “American Ride” shows are centered around the good time country party music of Toby Keith. You won’t want to this concert full of all Toby Keith’s biggest hits: “American Ride,” “Bullets in the Gun,” “Clancy’s Tavern,” “Hope on the Rocks,” “Drinks After Work,” and the list goes on Thursday, November 12th, 2026 at 7:30pm at The Palace Theatre- 21 W. Otterman, Greensburg, PA 15601. Tickets are just $35., $44., $49., and $75. and can be purchased online at: www.westmorelandculturaltrust.org or by calling the ticket office at (724) 836-8000. The Palace Theatre is located at 21 W. Otterman, Greensburg, PA 15601. This show is presented by Latshaw Productions.
Enjoy Toby Keith songs all evening -from his first hit “Should’ve Been A Cowboy” to his latest hit “That’s Country Bro”. You will believe you are watching and listening to the real thing. The “American Ride” show consists of only the best musical professionals, using state-of- the-art equipment, including sound and lights.
