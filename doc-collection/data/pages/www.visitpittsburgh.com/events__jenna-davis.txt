Discover Your Persona
October 28, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Jenna Davis is a country-pop singer, songwriter and actress known for her relatable storytelling and crossover presence. Her debut album, Where Did That Girl Go?, followed the single “DiCaprio,” co-written by Kelsea Ballerini, Walker Hayes and Ross Copperman. Davis also voiced the title character in M3GAN and M3GAN 2.0, and was named to Variety’s 2023 Young Hollywood Impact Report. Her 2026 performance at Jergel’s is presented by Drusky Entertainment and marks part of her first full-length headlining run.
