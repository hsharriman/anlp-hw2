October 31, 2026 12:00 AM –3:00 AM
Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets at Pittsburgh Panthers Football brings an Atlantic Coast Conference matchup to Pittsburgh, with the Panthers hosting the Yellow Jackets at Acrisure Stadium. The game features two established ACC programs competing in a Saturday college football showdown, offering visitors an energetic fall sports experience on Pittsburgh’s North Shore. Pitt’s 2026 promotional schedule identifies this matchup as the “Hail to Heroes” game, adding a special theme to the game-day atmosphere. Fans can expect traditional collegiate football excitement, team rivalries and the pageantry of major NCAA competition.
