Elementary Explorers Fall Itinerary (1-day)
The following options offer an engaging, interactive, and educational experience for young learners in the beautiful fall setting of Western Pennsylvania.
Option 1: West Overton Village – A Step Back in Time 🍂
📍 Location: West Overton Village & Museums (Westmoreland County)
Arrival & Orientation | 9:30 a.m.-10 a.m.
Meet the historical interpreters who will guide students through the day.
Guided Tour & Hands-On Activities | 10 a.m.-noon
Explore the historic 19th-century village, including the Overholt Homestead, the original distillery, and the springhouse.
Learn about daily life in the 1800s, including farming, blacksmithing, and textile production.
Participate in hands-on activities like butter churning or candle dipping (seasonal options available).
Lunch Break | noon-12:45 p.m
Enjoy a picnic lunch at the pavilion or in a designated indoor space.
Fall-Themed Agricultural Experience | 12:45 p.m.-1:30 p.m.
Learn about harvesting and food preservation from the 19th century.
Seasonal activity options may include pumpkin painting, corn husk doll-making, or apple cider pressing.
Wrap-Up & Reflection | 1:30 p.m.-2 p.m.
Q&A session with historians and a short interactive discussion on how life was different in the past.
Time for students to explore the gift shop with historical toys and books.
Departure | 2 p.m.
🔗 More Info: www.westovertonvillage.org
Option 2: Meadowcroft Rockshelter & Historic Village – A Journey Through Time 🍁
📍 Location: Meadowcroft Rockshelter & Historic Village (Washington County)
Arrival & Orientation | 9:30 a.m.-10 a.m.
Meet the guides and learn about the 16,000-year-old archaeological site, one of the oldest human habitation sites in North America!
Meadowcroft Rockshelter & Native American Experience | 10 a.m.-noon
Visit the prehistoric Rockshelter, where students can learn how early people lived.
Try out atlatl spear throwing, just like ancient hunters!
Tour the Native American Village, where students can explore a wigwam and learn about traditional tools and crafts.
Lunch Break | noon-12:45 p.m.
Picnic lunch in the historic village surrounded by the fall foliage.
19th-Century Historic Village Tour | 12:45 p.m.-1:30 p.m.
Step into a log cabin schoolhouse, where students can experience what education was like in the 1800s.
Watch a blacksmith demonstration and learn about early trades.
See an old-fashioned one-room church and other historic buildings.
Seasonal Hands-On Activity | 1:30 p.m.-2 p.m.
Participate in a fall-themed craft or historical skill such as corn grinding, candle making, or butter churning.
Departure | 2 p.m.
🔗 More Info: www.heinzhistorycenter.org/meadowcroft
