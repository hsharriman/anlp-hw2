October 25, 2026 to October 31, 2026
Nestled in the heart of the countryside, Eerie Acres offers spine-tingling fun for the whole family. Our indoor haunt and outdoor creepy cornfield are back for the 10th season! Open from dark until 10PM, navigate the barn full of horrors and escape through a path in the haunted cornfield! Throughout the season, various food trucks will be onsite, and we often have a DJ spinning the tunes. There's so much for the whole family to enjoy! This year we are featuring Helltown Brewery, Creekside Cellars Winery, and more!
Open 7PM to Dark!
