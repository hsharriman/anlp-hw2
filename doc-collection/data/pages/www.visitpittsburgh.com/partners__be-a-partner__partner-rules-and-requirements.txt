Partner Rules and Requirements
Learn more about the Partner Rules and Requirements through our Code of Ethics
As Partners of VisitPITTSBURGH, we request that you acknowledge and comply with the Partner Code of Ethics, as well as, support VisitPITTSBURGH’s mission, vision and values. Partners will also maintain the quality of the destination, agree to promote tourism and to model actions that uphold them to the highest ethical practices in business.
MISSION
Creating economic growth by promoting Pittsburgh as a leading travel destination.
VISION
Life would not be complete without visiting Pittsburgh.
VALUES
FISCAL EFFICIENCY
We manage the organization in a fiscally responsible manner.
ACCOUNTABILITY
We hold ourselves responsible for our actions to our stakeholders and community.
INTEGRITY
We act with respect, honesty and transparency in everything we do.
INNOVATION
We are dedicated to innovation and implementing best practices.
COLLABORATION
We work cooperatively as a team with our partners and stakeholders.
VISITPITTSBURGH PARTNERSHIP CODE OF ETHICS
All partners must maintain a clean and safe place of business and ensure that guests feel welcome.
All visitors and guests of partner establishments must not be discriminated against regardless of gender, race, sex, etc.
Partner establishments must provide visitors and guests with exceptional customer service to ensure customer satisfaction and repeat visits.
Partners must handle all inquiries, requests, transactions, correspondence and complaints in a timely manner, fairly and professionally and abide by all applicable federal, state and municipal laws.
Partners must treat all partners and staff of VisitPITTSBURGH in a respectful manner and support the goals and mission of VisitPITTSBURGH.
Partnership with VisitPITTSBURGH may be terminated for not adhering to and upholding the partnership ethics.
*By signing the Partnership Application, applicant/partner agrees to the partner Code of Ethics and supports VisitPITTBURGH’s mission, vision and values.
