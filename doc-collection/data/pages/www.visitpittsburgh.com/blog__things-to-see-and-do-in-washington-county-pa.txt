Things to See and Do in Washington County PA
Washington County PA is located just southwest of Pittsburgh, stretching to the Ohio border.
Washington County has a lot to offer and is perfect for a day trip during a stay in Pittsburgh. From family friendly, historic, and outdoor activities to gaming and dining, here is just some of what you’ll find in Washington County Pennsylvania.

Historic Sites and Activities
Meadowcroft Rockshelter and Historic Village
Meadowcroft Rockshelter and Historic Village is the earliest known site of human habitation in North America and can be found right here in Western Pennsylvania. At Meadowcroft, you can explore 20,000 ancient artifacts, millions of plant and animal remains, and much more.
Covered Bridges Tour
Take a driving tour through the historic covered bridges that can be found throughout Washington County. Explore the unique history of our region of the country through its beautiful countryside.
Trolley Museum
A unique stop on your tour through Washington County is the Pennsylvania Trolley Museum, which is working to keep the trolley-era alive with its collection of 50 cars. You can even, have the unique experience of operating an antique streetcar yourself!

A Night Out in Washington County
Try Your Luck
During a night out in Washington County you might find yourself at The Meadows Casino and Racetrack. Try your luck and have a blast all night. And, with a hotel onsite, you can stay a while longer.
Concert Venues
There are several outdoor and indoor venues throughout the region where you’ll find live music at your disposal. Catch one of the biggest and best summer concerts at Key-Bank Pavillion, take in a night of culture at the Washington Symphony Orchestra, or get dinner and a show at the Pepsi-Cola Roadhouse.
Performances and Experiences
With two theater companies in the region, each producing full seasons of entertainment, finding a great show option is easy for any theater lover. Little Lake Theater in Canonsburg and the Old Schoolhouse Players in Hickory are sure to keep you buzzing.
If you’d prefer to get into the action, check out one of the many escape rooms available to you! 5th Street Escape Room and Escape Room South each offer several themed escape experiences.

Dining
And no day trip is complete without a full belly from start to finish! Lots of delicious food and drink options can be found in the region.
Start with a breakfast at Bartram House Bakery and Café (and be sure to grab some macaroons for the road) or Chicco Baccello. Both offer light elegant fair, perfect for a quick bite.
Lunch options are seemingly endless, but you’ll definitely want to make a stop at Fiori’s Pizzaria in McMurray. You’ll want to write home about the pizza, and the rest of the menu will leave you satified as well.
For dinner, try something truly historic, like the Century Inn, the oldest continuously operating inn on the National Road. Find delicious fine dining, and you can even stay the night!

When you’re planning your next trip to Pittsburgh, be sure to include a short trip down the road to Washington County. Get in touch with the beauty and history of this unique region.
