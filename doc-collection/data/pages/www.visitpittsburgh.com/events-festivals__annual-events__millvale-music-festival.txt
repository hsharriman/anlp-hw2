Millvale Music Festival 2026
Wander through the streets of Millvale to celebrate the arts with two days of free live music and performances.
May 15-16, 2026
Experience the best of Pittsburgh's arts scene at the annual Millvale Music Festival. This vibrant neighborhood celebration transforms the streets of Millvale into an electrifying hub of arts and culture for two days. With over 300 musical acts, art for sale by local artists, stand-up comedy, spoken word performances, and a variety of food trucks, this free festival has quickly become a can’t miss event for locals.
Event Details
The Millvale Music Festival is a FREE festival held at different bars, breweries, outdoor spaces, and community centers in the Millvale neighborhood of Pittsburgh. This year’s festival is on Friday, May 15th and Saturday, May 16th, 2026.
Lineup
There will be over 300 local bands and musicians, visual artists, comedians, spoken word performances, and food trucks at the festival. For the most up to date lineup, visit the official Millvale Music Festival website.
Plan Your Trip
Download the App
Plan out your music festival experience by downloading the official Millvale Music Festival app in advance (App Store and Google Play). Create your own schedule, view the event map, and learn about this year’s performers and artists.
What to Bring
Millvale Music Festival is an outdoor event that will have you walking throughout the neighborhood to enjoy different performances. To ensure you have a great time, bring along these items:
a water bottle
sunglasses
sunscreen
a camera
comfortable walking shoes
Snap a great photo during the festival?
Share it with #lovePGH and #MMF25.
Parking & Transit
There are many different ways to get to the festival including driving, ride shares, biking, and public transit. Street parking will be very limited and arriving via an alternative method is highly recommended. To travel via public transit take routes that connect the 1 to Tarentum, the 2 to Mount Royal, and stops north on Evergreen.
Biking to the festival is a great way to travel! Millvale Music Festival has partnered with BikePGH since 2022 to provide extra bike racks at multiple points throughout the festival. The festival is also accessible via the Three Rivers Heritage Trail through the Millvale Riverfront Park, with multiple public parking lots along the trail.
