PGH Insiders
Passionate Pittsburghers who are experts in their craft
Pittsburgh is a city transformed on the brink of a boisterous movement, gaining significant media traction and hosting various global events in the years to come. But here's what truly makes our city extraordinary: the incredible people behind our biggest attractions and events.
They love what they do, they lift each other up, and they're passionate about sharing their craft with visitors from across the globe.
These are the voices that matter most. Since no one is better qualified to showcase our city than these local experts, we're proud to launch the PGH Insiders Program to authentically highlight all Pittsburgh has to offer.
What It Means to be a PGH Insider
As a PGH Insider, you'll be Pittsburgh's authentic voice to the world. VisitPITTSBURGH will connect you with media outlets, travel writers, and industry professionals who need genuine expertise about our city—and you'll also be featured throughout our content as a go-to source for visitors seeking true local recommendations. When a national magazine wants the real story behind Pittsburgh's food renaissance, when a family visiting needs to know the best kid-friendly spots, or when a documentary crew needs someone who truly knows our arts scene, that's where you might come in. Whether it's helping a traveler discover a hidden gem or speaking to national press, your day-to-day expertise and authentic passion will help people experience our city that only true Pittsburghers know.
Thank you to everyone who has applied to be a PGH Insider!
At this time, the application process for 2025 is closed.
We appreciate your passion for Pittsburgh and enthusiasm in sharing your Pittsburgh. If you are selected, you will be notified confidentially in November in preparation for the program launch in January 2026.
