Discover Your Persona
October 25, 2026 to December 31, 2026 12:00 PM –2:00 PM
Steel and stone rise together where Pittsburgh’s secrets echo between soaring glass towers and shadowed courthouses. Every block hides a story-most overlooked by hurried crowds.
Plug in and take control with a self-guided audio tour through the Central Business District, uncovering the city’s dramatic showdowns, courtroom whispers, and overlooked legends at your pace.
What riots once shook the U.S. Steel Tower’s foundations? Which courtroom scandal shattered reputations inside Allegheny County Courthouse? And why does an old stage at Pittsburgh Playhouse still draw the ghostly echoes of a vanished act?
Stride past the obvious into alleys where rebels once marched, linger under towering facades built from ambition, and trace forgotten footsteps that shaped a city’s destiny. Feel the pulse of Pittsburgh as history and high drama collide with modern life on every corner.
Unlock the city’s hidden heart-your Pittsburgh story starts now.
