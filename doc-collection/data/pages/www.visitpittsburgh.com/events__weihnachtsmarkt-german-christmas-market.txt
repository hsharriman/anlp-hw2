November 14, 2026 to November 15, 2026 10:00 AM –4:00 PM
Historic Harmony welcomes you to our German Christmas Market. The streets of our quaint little village will be transformed into a winter wonderland of vendors, German foods and entertainment. The air will be filled with the chill of winter, the aromatic smells of food, and the sounds of music and laughter.
November 14th: 10AM-6PM
November 15th: 12PM-4PM
Admission:
All proceeds from the Market benefit the Harmony Museum
$10.00 general admission
$5.00 for children under the age of 12
$5.00 for seniors (65 and older)
Parking and transportation will be available to the market.
Performances:
-Alpen Schuhplattlers: Saturday at 12:30 PM and 4:30 PM, and Sunday at 12 PM & 3 PM.
-Teutonia Manerchor: Saturday at 1 PM & 3 PM
-McKenzie Holt: Saturday at 2:30 PM
Come meet Santa at the Bottlebrush: A Gallery for the Arts from 1-3 PM both days!
Off-site parking will be available with shuttle transportation.
