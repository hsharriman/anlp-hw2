Discover Your Persona
October 25, 2026 to November 1, 2026 7:30 PM –6:30 PM
Beneath the bridges and the brick, Pittsburgh is alive with whispers of stories that never quite died.
This October, Liberty Magic transforms into something darker, closer, and far more personal.
In this world premiere experience, Michael steps into a more serious, story‑driven role inviting you into an intimate evening of Pittsburgh folk tales, ghost lore, and haunted history. This is not the Michael you’ve seen before.
Set against the backdrop of Halloween season, Michael weaves chilling stories of our city’s past brought to life through unusual magic, mysterious artifacts, and strange heirlooms. The experience unfolds less like a traditional magic show and more like interactive theater, where you’re not just watching the story; you’re part of it.
Liberty Magic is known for being up close, but this goes even further. Expect a shared, immersive atmosphere that feels like a late‑night storytelling circle, where the line between performer and audience is the first thing to disappear. Once that's gone, all bets are off.
"The Steel City has a haunted past. Tonight...it gets a voice."
