Restaurants Open on Monday in Pittsburgh
If you’ve ever found yourself wandering the streets of Pittsburgh on a Monday, staring longingly at “Closed” signs, you’re not alone! Many restaurants take a breather after the weekend hustle. But don’t worry! We’ve gathered some spots ready to serve you up something delicious, even on the most underrated day of the week.
Note: Hours and menu items are subject to change. Please check with businesses for the most up-to-date information and reservation availability.
Why Are So Many Restaurants Closed on Mondays?
You might be wondering why so many eateries take a break on Mondays. Well, it’s all about balancing the hustle and bustle of the weekend!
Traffic Trends: Typically, restaurants experience less foot traffic on Mondays compared to the bustling Friday, Saturday, and Sunday crowds. These prime weekend days are when folks gather for brunches, dinners, and celebrations with friends and loved ones.
The Unofficial Weekend: For many in the restaurant industry, Mondays (and sometimes Tuesdays) serve as their unofficial weekend. After working long hours during the busy weekend, staff often need a breather to recharge and prep for the week ahead.
So, while it may seem like a culinary desert on Mondays, it’s just the industry’s way of ensuring that when they are open, they can provide the best possible experience for you!
Best Places to Eat in Pittsburgh on a Monday
Spirits & Tales | 5130 Bigelow Blvd., The Oaklander Hotel, Pittsburgh
Vibe: French brasserie with amazing cocktails
What to Try: Brunch, lunch, or dinner - this place has you covered
Monday Hours: 7 AM to 10 PM (closed between 11-11:30 AM)
Eagle Food & Beer Hall | 737 Penn Ave., Pittsburgh
Vibe: Lively, perfect for catching up with friends with blues playing in the background
What to Try: Their famous fried chicken is calling your name
Monday Hours: 11 AM to 11 PM (with happy hour from 4-6 PM on Mondays)
Shorty’s Pints x Pins | 353 North Shore Dr., Pittsburgh
Vibe: Fun and quirky with duckpin bowling, shuffleboard, bocce, cornhole, foosball and pinball
What to Try: Shorty's Smokehouse Smash
Monday Hours: 11:30 AM to 12 AM
Bakersfield | 940 Penn Ave., Pittsburgh
Vibe: Warm and vibrant with a selection of over 100 tequilas and American whiskeys
What to Try: Their tagline is Tacos. Tequila. Whiskey so we say try all three!
Monday Hours: 11 AM to 11 PM
Alihan’s Coffee & Breakfast | 129 6th St., Pittsburgh
Vibe: Bright and cheerful and a good way to start your day
What to Try: The Poğaça - a Turkish bun, stuffed with spicy sausage, tomato, cheese
Monday Hours: 7 AM to 3 PM
Ritual House | 524 William Penn Place, Pittsburgh
Vibe: Chill atmosphere designed for the senses
What to Try: Lobster mac and cheese paired with their craft cocktails
Monday Hours: 4 PM to 9 PM
North Shore Tavern | 120 Federal St., Pittsburgh
Vibe: Close to PNC Park- a must stop for Pirates Fans
What to Try: Steak on a Stone
Monday Hours: 11 AM to 11 PM (Steelers Home Game Days, opens at 9 AM)
Grand Concourse | 100 W. Station Square Dr., Pittsburgh
Vibe: Located in the beautifully preserved Pittsburgh & Lake Erie Railroad Station, this restaurant is the epitome of elegant dining
What to Try: Their award-winning seafood
Monday Hours: 11:30 AM to 9 PM
Meat & Potatoes | 649 Penn Ave., Pittsburgh
Vibe: As Pittsburgh’s first Gastro-pub, this spot is refined, moody, and effortlessly cool
What to Try: Comfort food and cocktails with plenty of vegan options
Monday Hours: 5 PM to 10 PM
The Commoner | Kimpton Hotel, 620 William Penn Place, Pittsburgh
Vibe: Contemporary with an interior by New York's Mark Zeff Design Company featuring work by local artisans
What to Try: Seasonal dishes that showcase local ingredients or the brunch menu chicken and waffles
Monday Hours: 7 AM to 10:30 AM; 11 AM to 2 PM; 5 PM to 9 PM
fl.2 | 510 Market St., Fairmont Pittsburgh, Pittsburgh
Vibe: Sophisticated yet relaxed
What to Try: Family style menu paired with locally sourced spirits and brews and small batch estate wines
Monday Hours: 7 AM to 10 AM; 11 AM to 1 PM; 4 PM to 10 PM.
Hofbräuhaus Pittsburgh | 2705 S. Water St., Pittsburgh
Vibe: You’ll feel like you’ve just been transported to Germany perfect for group outings and events
What to Try: Authentic Bavarian favorites like schnitzel or bratwurst topped off with a beer!
Monday Hours: 11 AM to 10 PM
Noodlehead | 242 S Highland Ave #202, Pittsburgh
Vibe: Fun and fast-paced restaurant offering noodle dishes inspired by the street markets of Thailand
What to Try: Pad see ew or the crab rangoon
Monday Hours: 12 PM to 10 PM
Everyday Noodles | 5875 Forbes Ave, Pittsburgh
Vibe: Casual and comforting
What to Try: Their homemade noodles, beef soup or Chiang Mai Curry
Monday Hours: 11:30 AM to 2:30 PM; 4 PM to 9:30 PM
Chengdu Gourmet | 940 Penn Ave, Pittsburgh
Vibe: Great service and even better food
What to Try: Chef, Wei Zhu, has now earned his fifth nomination to the prestigious James Beard Award for Best Chef: Mid-Atlantic so you know you’ll be in good hands here with whatever you choose but we suggest the Dan Dan noodles
Monday Hours: 11 AM to 11 PM
Taiwanese Bistro Cafe 33 | 1711 Shady Ave, Pittsburgh
Vibe: Cozy, casual and quick
What to Try: Fried dumplings, pepper short ribs, and pork kimchi
Monday Hours: 11 AM to 9 PM
Kabab & Curry Restaurant & Grill | 2890 Banksville Road, Pittsburgh
Vibe: Casual and inviting serving homestyle Indian and Pakistani family recipes
What to Try: Samosa Chaat and the Chicken Tikka Masala Pizza
Monday Hours: 11:30 AM to 2:30 PM, 4 PM to 9:30 PM
Coop De Ville | 2305 Smallman St, Pittsburgh
Vibe: Fast casual with fun-for-all entertainment including vintage arcade games, pinball and more
What to Try: Delicious comfort food with a perfectly paired cocktail
Monday Hours: Coffee Bar- 7 AM to 7 PM, Kitchen Hours- 11:30 AM to 9 PM, Tiki Bar, Main Bar & Game Lounge- 11:30 AM to 11 PM (Note: 21+ on Fridays & Saturdays; All ages welcome all other times)
Alla Famiglia | 804 E Warrington Ave, Pittsburgh
Vibe: Family-style dining at its best
What to Try: Their delicious meatballs are a must
Monday Hours: 4 PM to 9 PM
Legends Eatery | 500 E North Ave, Pittsburgh
Vibe: Quaint, casual and family-friendly
What to Try: Garlic parmesan mashed potatoes or the wedding soup
Monday Hours: 4 PM to 8 PM
Tips for a Successful Monday Feast
Make Reservations: Some places can get busy, so it’s good to call ahead!
Explore Menu Specials: Many spots offer Monday-only deals so keep your eyes peeled!
Check Local Events: You might find a hidden gem with themed nights or special menus.
