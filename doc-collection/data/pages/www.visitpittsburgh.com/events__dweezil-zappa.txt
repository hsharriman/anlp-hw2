Discover Your Persona
October 31, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Dweezil Zappa is an acclaimed guitarist, songwriter and bandleader known for bringing his father Frank Zappa’s adventurous music to the concert stage. His performances blend intricate compositions, jazz-rock fusion, virtuosic guitar work and playful musicality, offering longtime fans and curious newcomers an engaging introduction to the Zappa catalog. In 2026, he is presenting DZ20: Like Father, Like Son, a concert program marking two decades of performing Frank Zappa’s music live. Expect a dynamic, technically impressive rock experience shaped by complex arrangements, sharp musicianship and Dweezil’s distinctive artistic voice.
