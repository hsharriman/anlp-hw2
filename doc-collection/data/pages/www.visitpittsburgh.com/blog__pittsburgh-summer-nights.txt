Those Summer Nights | Pittsburgh Evening Activities & Nightlife
The fun doesn't stop in Pittsburgh when the sun goes down
When it's getting late in the city but you still aren't ready to call it a night, enter our Only In Pittsburgh nightlife scene. These summer nights activities are sure to keep you outside enjoying the weather as you explore art crawls, night swims, live music and so much more under the stars.
Final Fridays on Rose Way
Enjoy one of the newest creative spaces in Pittsburgh at Final Fridays on Rose Way with The Warhol. Grab a drink and bask in the rainbow alleyway under the glowing lights, and pick up a ticket for The Warhol half off starting at 5 p.m. This free event takes place the final Friday of May-Sept. 2024 from 5-10 p.m.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by The Andy Warhol Museum (@thewarholmuseum)
Cinema in the Park
Pittsburgh parks turn into outdoor movie theatres all summer long thanks to the Dollar Bank Cinema in the Park season. Bring your blanket and a chair to these free showings at dusk across the city. There's something for all types of moviegoers, so you can bring the family to DC League of Super-Pets at Schenley Park, your friends to Top Gun: Maverick in Ormsby Park and the cinephile in your life to Cool Hand Luke at Flagstaff Hill.
Unblurred: First Fridays
Come out to Penn Ave the first Friday of the month for #Unblurred: First Fridays on Penn between the 4800 and 5500 blocks in the Bloomfield/Garfield neighborhoods. This free arts and entertainment crawl goes from 3-11 p.m. and is a great chance to meet with artists and local business owners as you enjoy painting, sculpture, dance, photography, music and spoken word performances.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Penn Avenue (@pennavenue412)
Jam on Walnut
Rock out to one of the best block parties of the summer with Jam on Walnut, an outdoor concert series that goes late into the night in Shadyside. Enjoy free live music, grab a beer and shop local businesses, with all proceeds going to help the Animal Friends Charity. Jams are scheduled for June 12, July 20 and Aug. 10 for the remainder of 2024.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Think Shadyside (@think_shadyside)
Music on the Lawn
The South Side Works is rocking every Friday from July through September for Music on the Lawn! Enjoy free music from 6-10 p.m. along with local food vendors and craft breweries giving you plenty of reasons to start your weekend on the North Lot.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by SouthSide Works (@southsideworks)
Pittsburgh Night Market
Something about shopping at night just hits different. Come to Market Square from 5-10 p.m. on select dates June 22-Oct. 26, 2024 for Pittsburgh Night Market, a pop-up shopping market and festival featuring Pittsburgh vendors showcasing their clothing, food, pet supplies, artwork, jewelry and more. Live entertainment will also be available on certain evenings.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Downtown Pittsburgh (@downtownpitt)
Moonlight Cruise Gateway Clipper
All aboard the Gateway Clipper Friday evenings through October for the Moonlight Dance Cruise & Tour, a late-night exploration of the city for night owls. Your evening comes with the best views of the city via grand riverboat along with a full-service bar, free Midnight Buffet and dance floor until the sun comes up.
