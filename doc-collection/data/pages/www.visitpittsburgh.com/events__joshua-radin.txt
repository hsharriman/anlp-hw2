Discover Your Persona
November 14, 2026 7:30 PM –10:30 PM
Joshua Radin is an acclaimed singer-songwriter known for intimate acoustic folk, gentle melodies, and emotionally candid lyrics. His music first gained broad attention through “Winter,” later featured on Scrubs, and his catalog includes fan favorites such as “Closer,” “Today,” and “I’d Rather Be With You.” With more than a billion streams, over a million albums sold worldwide, and songs featured across film, television, and advertising, Radin has built a devoted international following. His latest creative chapter includes One Day Home, a reflective 2025 album exploring love, loss, memory, and the meaning of home.
