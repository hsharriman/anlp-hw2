Pittsburgh Home & Garden Show 2026
For 10 days, explore must-see exhibits, receive expert tips, and find endless home inspiration, all in one place!
Check back for 2027 event details
Event Details
Featured Experiences
The Pittsburgh Home & Garden Show is back for its 44th year, filling over 10 acres with everything you need to refresh, renovate, and reimagine your home. With more than 1,500 exhibits, this is the ultimate marketplace for homeowners, DIYers, and design lovers. Whether you're looking for cutting-edge home tech, expert advice, or unique finds, you’ll discover it all right here!
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Pittsburgh Home & Garden Show (@pghhomeshow)
Event Details
Date: March 6-15, 2026
Location: David L. Lawrence Convention Center
Tickets: Admission starts at $10 for adults, $4 for kids age 6-12, and free for kids under 6.
Hours: Mon-Thurs 4 p.m. - 9 p.m. | Fri-Sat 10 a.m. - 9 p.m. | Sun 10 a.m. - 6 p.m.
Parking: Park all day at the Acrisure Stadium lots for just $8 and take advantage of the free continuous shuttle to the show! The shuttle runs daily, starting one hour before the show opens and continuing until one hour after closing.
Featured Experiences
Get Expert Advice from Industry Pros
Ready to tackle your next home project? Get insider tips from the best in the business! HGTV’s Steve Ford shares design insights while The Renegade Gardener, Don Engebretson, will shake up everything you thought you knew about landscaping, composting, and foraging. Be sure to bring an antique for a free appraisal by Dr. Lori!
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Pittsburgh Home & Garden Show (@pghhomeshow)
Walk Through a Fully Built Model Home
Explore a beautifully designed, 1,560-square-foot rustic farmhouse–style home by Buckhorn Showcase inside the Home and Garden Show. This open-concept layout with custom wood interiors proves that thoughtful craftsmanship makes all the difference.
Discover Tomorrow’s Trends at the Dream Home® Innovation Center
The future of home living is here! Test out smart home gadgets, energy-efficient tech that adapts to your lifestyle, and the latest in home entertainment–this interactive showcase is packed with game-changing ideas.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Pittsburgh Home & Garden Show (@pghhomeshow)
Plan your Kitchen Upgrades at The Kitchen Idea Center
Dreaming of a kitchen makeover? Explore the newest trends in cabinetry, countertops, lighting, and accessories. With hundreds of kitchen and cooking exhibits, you’ll find endless inspiration for your next renovation.
Let Kids Play, Create & Explore at The Children’s Village
Little ones can get crafty, play games, and enjoy storytime while you take a break. They can even add their crafts to a giant greeting card to brighten the day of local nursing home residents. With a cozy seating area for parents and a nursing station on-site, it’s a stress-free space for family fun.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Pittsburgh Home & Garden Show (@pghhomeshow)
Be Amazed by the Steel City LUG LEGO Display
From Pittsburgh landmarks to intricate cityscapes, this incredible LEGO display constructed by Steel City LEGO Users Group, features Pittsburgh landmarks and intricate city streets. You might even leave inspired to start your own masterpiece! is a must-see for builders of all ages.
Explore The Pennsylvania Food & Wine Festival
Sample your way through the Pennsylvania Food and WIne Festival showcasing gourmet flavors, fine wines, whiskies, and handcrafted treats. Discover unique products to take home.
Visit The Farm to Table Area
Meet small-scale farmers and food artisans dedicated to bringing fresh, sustainable ingredients to your table. Try farm-fresh products, watch demonstrations, attend talks, and learn how to incorporate more local foods into your meals. With daily programming and tastings, no two visits are the same!
Plan Your Trip
The Pittsburgh Home & Garden Show takes place in the heart of Downtown Pittsburgh, steps away from Market Square, comfortable hotels, award-winning restaurants, and top attractions—making it the perfect reason to enjoy an extended getaway. Plan your visit today and get ready for an inspiring experience!
