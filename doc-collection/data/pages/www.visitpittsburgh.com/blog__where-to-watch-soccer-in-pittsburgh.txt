Top 6 Soccer Bars in Pittsburgh
Supporters, find a favorite place to watch the big match in the Burgh
Pittsburgh has no shortage of incredible places to watch hometown teams, from giant restaurants with floor-to-ceiling TVs to dive bars that bleed black and gold. But when it comes to 'the beautiful game' of soccer, finding where to catch the next Arsenal match may be a bit more difficult.
But we did the digging for you and uncovered the top spots to watch soccer in Pittsburgh, including the official bar of our own Pittsburgh Riverhounds USL team and places open early on weekends to watch English Premier League.
Main Photo Credit: Mike's Beer Bar during the World Cup
FIFA World Cup 2026 Watch Parties
Acrisure Stadium
100 Art Rooney Ave
Get ready for a World Cup watch party at the home of the Pittsburgh Steelers! This Pennsylvania World Cup Fan Zone will offer large-screen broadcasts of FIFA World Cup 2026™ Knockout Round matches, live entertainment, and interactive soccer fan experiences — all free of charge.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by FIFA World Cup 26 Philadelphia™ (@fwc26philly)
F.N.B. Stadium
510 W Station Square Dr
Join soccer fans at F.N.B. Stadium (formerly Highmark Stadium) on June 12 & 19 for FREE watch parties featuring live U.S. matches, giveaways, food, family fun, and nonstop entertainment. From World Cup ticket opportunities to on-field contests and thousands of giveaways, it'll be a once-in-a-lifetime chance to watch the biggest soccer event in the world from Pittsburgh's own soccer hub.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by F.N.B. Stadium (@fnbstadiumpgh)
Cork Harbour Pub | 181 43rd St (South Side)
Cork Harbour is excited to be your home for every single World Cup match, capping the festivities off with a World Cup Final extravaganza on July 19!
The Pitch on Butler | 4717 Butler St (Lawrenceville)
This sports bar with a soccer twist is truly the obvious choice to take in the World Cup. Centrally located in Pittsburgh's Lawrenceville neighborhood, this spot is a great place to stop to meet fellow soccer fans.
Eleventh Hour Brewing | 3711 Charlotte St (Lawrenceville)
Eleventh Hour Brewing will be hosting World Cup watch parties throughout the entire tournament, with beer specials and food trucks ready to set you up for each match. While a select number of matches will be playing, you can ask the bartender for a specific match and they'll play it if possible.
Tom's Watch Bar | 261 North Shore Dr Suite 3 (North Shore)
Located between PNC Park and Acrisure Stadium on the North Shore, Tom’s Watch Bar is built for major sporting events and big matchdays. With massive screens, full match sound, and a high-energy atmosphere, Tom’s Pittsburgh will be one of the top watch party destinations in the city during the World Soccer Championship.
Piper's Pub
Dahn on the South Side to watch some footy n'at? Translation: Want to watch soccer in our South Side neighborhood? Piper's Pub opens 30 minutes before the first game on weekends, giving you a great place to watch the game with other supporters even for those 7:30 a.m. games. Plus, they serve up Scottish and Irish favorites including scotch eggs, boxty, cottage pie and more, plus a great local draft selection, to enjoy from brunch to dinner.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Pipers Pub (@piperspub)
The Bulldog Pub
They're Man U fans, but we won't hold that against them. In the East End neighborhood, The Bulldog Pub has a full slate of matches on every day of the week, and their Instagram does a fantastic job of showing which games are being shown so you don't miss a minute. Join their #BulldogBrunchLeague on weekends for a great menu including build-your-own breakfast sammies, breakfast pizzas and other tasty bites.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Bulldog Pub Morningside (@the_bulldog_pub)
Mike's Beer Bar
Yes, it's located right next to PNC Park in the heart of the North Shore, but Mike's Beer Bar is also the official home for Pittsburgh Riverhounds watch parties! Be sure to catch the game there when the Hounds are traveling and enjoy a pint of more than 300 local beers and menu featuring their signature steak on a stone. Mike's is also hopping for World Cup and other major soccer matches, so be sure to check their schedule.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Mike’s Beer Bar (@mikesbeerbar)
Monterey Pub
Just a short walk from Mike's, Monterey Pub in the Northside has an inviting atmosphere full of football supporters to make Premier League season a blast. They also go BIG for World Cup and other major games and tournaments throughout the year. Cheer on your team, but leave one hand free to munch on their bangers and mash, shepherd's pie and other Irish favorites on the menu.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Monterey Pub (@themontereypub)
Cork Harbour
Cork Harbour may be new to the scene in Lawrenceville, but their soccer schedule is maybe the most loaded in the city with full slates of EPL, Bundesliga, UEFA, Eredivisie and more matches. Follow their Insta for the full schedule, and don't be afraid to ask them to put your game on if you don't see it listed. Matches are best enjoyed with their cocktail menu including a hot toddy and/or irish coffee.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Cork Harbour Pub (@corkharbourpub)
The Pitch on Butler
Just down the road from Cork Harbour on Butler Street is The Pitch, a fantastic meet-up spot for all of your footy needs. The outdoor patio with a big screen is the perfect spot to catch a game and still enjoy the outdoors, or head inside to their spacious bar and indoor seating with lots of TVs broadcasting the most in-demand matches plus one of the best smashburgers and draft lists in the city.
