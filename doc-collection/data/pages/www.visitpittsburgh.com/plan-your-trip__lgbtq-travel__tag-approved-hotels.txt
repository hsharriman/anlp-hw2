TAG Approved Hotels
​Pittsburgh's many hotels offer luxury and convenience to some of the city's best cultural treasures.
Select from the many TAG Approved® hotels in the area. The TAG Approved® designation is given to hotels that meet a list of criteria including diversity training, non-discriminatory policies and recognition of domestic partnership in employee benefits.
TAG, the Travel Advocacy Group, is a collection of LGBTQIA+ welcoming accommodations that have completed a qualification process based on their employment policies, services and support for the LGBTQIA+ community.
