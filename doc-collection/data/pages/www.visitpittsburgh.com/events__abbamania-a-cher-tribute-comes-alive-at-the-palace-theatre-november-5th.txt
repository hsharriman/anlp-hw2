November 5, 2026 7:30 PM –9:30 PM
Featuring an all-Canadian cast that has been touring the globe for 18 years, this production has earned the exclusive distinction of being the only ABBA tribute to perform the cast of the Mamma Mia musical. With a focus on high-fidelity musicality, every song is performed note-for-note, exactly as the original studio recordings, capturing the lush “Wall of Sound” that made both artists global legends.
“Abbamania + A Cher Tribute” is coming to The Palace Theatre Thursday, November 5th, 2026 at 7:30pm. Tickets are only $35., $44., $49., and $75. and can be purchased online at: www.westmorelandculturaltrust.org or by calling the ticket office at (724) 836-8000. The Palace Theatre is located at 21 W. Otterman, Greensburg, PA 15601. This show is presented by Latshaw Productions.
The production offers a unique, cinematic experience by highlighting the massive hits from both Mamma Mia films, creating a seamless narrative between the two catalogs. The show reaches a high-glamour peak when the special guest tribute to Cher takes the stage, channeling the “Goddess of Pop’s” unmistakable contralto vocals and legendary stage presence. This dynamic crossover allows audiences to experience Cher’s own celebrated interpretations of ABBA’s classics alongside her own career-spanning anthems, all delivered with the authentic costumes and charisma the era demands.
Do not miss this amazing production!
