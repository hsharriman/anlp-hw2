There’s Always Something Happening at The Terminal
Discover local makers, food, fun and so much more at The Terminal
Looking for things to do in Pittsburgh? Head on over to The Terminal in the Strip District! This historic, five-block-long former produce distribution facility has been renovated into an all-day destination for shopping, restaurants, fitness options, and extraordinary experiences!
Located in the center of the dynamic Strip District neighborhood, between 16th Street and 21st Street and just one block off the famous Penn Ave., park your car in the Strip District Terminal parking lot and use the expansive building to orient yourself in the neighborhood. Head through the 17th, 18th or 20th Streets passageway to start exploring Terminal shops and experiences. In addition to an exciting collection of specialty shops and workout studios, a brewery, a winery, and a music venue, The Terminal also hosts a robust calendar of weekday and weekend special events.
Novo Asian Food Hall
NOW OPEN
Novo Asian Food Hall
Pittsburgh-based chefs execute seven ambitious concepts, all with one shared seating area, at this highly anticipated galley-style restaurant from owner Alex Tang. Expect everything from delicious seasoned fried cutlets from Kung Fu Chicken to boba and bowls of ramen from Teachana and fresh Korean baked goods from Sumi's Cakery, all in one place.
A central bar allows patrons to sip from a diverse selection of Japanese whiskeys and sake served in a traditional cedar box, or try one of the more adventurous cocktails infused with shōchū, yuzu, rice wines and more.
The Terminal Lineup: Beer, Books, Grub, Golf & A Good Time
Aslin Beer Company, Balvanera, Chipotle, City Grows, City Winery, Clean Your Dirty Face*, Envy Nail Spa, Fine Wine & Good Spirits Premium Collection, Hieber's Pharmacy, Novo Asian Cuisine Food Hall, Oddfellows Ice Cream Co., OnPar Now, PetVet365*, Playa Bowls, Posman Books, PrimoHoagies, Puttshack, Sandbox VR, Shake Shack, [solidcore], Starbucks, StretchLab, Walk Run Lift Studio.
*Coming Soon
Meet Each Week at The Terminal Saturday Markets
Every Saturday, May through October 2024, shop from a curated collection of local makers and craftspeople alongside Terminal shops and restaurants! Listen to the sounds of Pittsburgh DJs and musicians and pose for post-worthy photos with the iconic Terminal building. Each week brings new vendors, new music, and new experiences!
Save on Terminal Tuesdays
Get free 2-hour parking in the Strip District Terminal parking lot every Tuesday when you make a purchase from a Terminal shop, restaurant or experience!
Holiday Happenings
Spring, summer, fall or winter, you’ll find special seasonal celebrations at The Terminal! Hop over to snap a photo with the Easter Bunny in the spring, rev your engine at the Pittsburgh Vintage Grand Prix Car Show in June, clown around at Fall Fest in October (pictured below), and get to gifting at the Terminal Holiday Market in December. Visit StripDistrictTerminal.com to see what’s on the calendar each month!
Whatever the reason or season for your visit, you’ll find something happening at The Strip District Terminal!
