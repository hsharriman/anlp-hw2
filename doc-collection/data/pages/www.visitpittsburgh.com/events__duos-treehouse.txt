October 25, 2026 to October 30, 2026 9:30 PM –2:30 PM
Duolingo and La Escuelita Arcoiris invite caregivers and little ones (birth to 5 years) to spend time with us on Fridays. This bilingual (spanish and english) free program aims to foster healthy development for children and support the adults who care for them. The Program is held every friday from 9:30 – 11:30am and 12:30-2:30pm at the Kingsley Association (Squirrel Hill). To find out more find us on Facebook at Duo’s Treehouse.
