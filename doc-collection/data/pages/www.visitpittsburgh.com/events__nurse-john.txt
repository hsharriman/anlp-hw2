Discover Your Persona
November 5, 2026 7:30 PM –9:30 PM
Nurse John is a healthcare content creator, comedian, and internet personality known for humorous takes on nursing life, hospital culture, and the everyday realities of working in healthcare. Through relatable comedy sketches and social media videos, he has built a large following by highlighting the challenges, chaos, and humor experienced by nurses and medical professionals. His content blends fast-paced comedy, exaggerated workplace scenarios, and authentic healthcare experiences that resonate strongly with both medical workers and general audiences. With an energetic personality and sharp comedic timing, Nurse John has become one of the most recognizable online voices in healthcare entertainment and nursing culture.
