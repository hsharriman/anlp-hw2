Event Overview
The Whiskey Rebellion Dinner has become the must-attend Washington County Event. The dinner is the Bradford House's main fundraiser.
The evening includes a reception featuring passed hors d'oeuvres and a cocktail, a silent auction, and a sit down dinner.
The event details will be updated for 2026 closer to the event.
