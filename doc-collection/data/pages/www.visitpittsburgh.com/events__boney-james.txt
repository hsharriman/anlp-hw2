Discover Your Persona
November 3, 2026 5:30 PM –8:30 PM
Boney James is a four-time GRAMMY-nominated saxophonist, composer and producer celebrated for blending contemporary jazz, R&B, soul and sophisticated groove. With a distinctive tenor sound and an acclaimed career spanning more than three decades, he has become one of instrumental music’s most recognizable performers. His latest studio album, A Simple Man, is scheduled for release through Concord Records on October 2, 2026, featuring the single “I Belong To You” with Ledisi. Audiences can expect an energetic, polished performance that balances expressive melodies, smooth rhythms and soulful musicality.
