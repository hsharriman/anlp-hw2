October 23, 2026 7:30 PM –2:00 PM
Pittsburgh Ballet Theatre presents Giselle with the PBT Orchestra, a timeless ballet of love, betrayal and forgiveness. This hauntingly romantic classic follows Giselle through heartbreak and a supernatural world, pairing expressive storytelling with ethereal choreography. The production features Adolphe Adam’s sweeping score performed live by the PBT Orchestra, adding depth and immediacy to every dramatic turn. A new staging by Adam W. McKinney brings a fresh perspective to choreography by Jean Coralli and Jules Perrot, with later revisions by Marius Petipa. It’s an elegant introduction to the emotional power and enduring beauty of classical ballet.
