Historic & Architectural Charm
For the history buff, design admirer, heritage traveler, and story seeker.
Walk through living history along cobblestone alleys, Victorian row-house blocks, and grand Gilded Age mansions. Shaped by the titans, artisans, and immigrants of American industry, these neighborhoods showcase centuries of architectural craft—from ornate ironwork to preserved 19th-century streetscapes.
