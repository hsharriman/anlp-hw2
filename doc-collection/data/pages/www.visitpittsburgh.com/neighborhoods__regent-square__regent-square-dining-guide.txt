Regent Square Dining Guide
Neighborhood Craft & Casual Comfort
Regent Square’s dining scene is relaxed, independent, and easy to enjoy on foot. Along South Braddock Avenue, you’ll find craft beer classics, Pacific Northwest-inspired comfort food, neighborhood bakeries, cozy coffee houses, and casual global spots that make the area feel welcoming from morning through night.
Neighborhood Classics
These signature establishments anchor the South Braddock Avenue strip, each bringing a distinct atmosphere and its own version of inventive comfort food.
D’s Six Pax & Dogz | 1118 S. Braddock Ave.
A legendary East End institution that balances a laid-back, neighborhood hot dog joint with a world-class craft beer destination. Visitors flock here for gourmet, grilled-to-order hot dogs loaded with creative toppings, loaded fries, and the ultimate crown jewel: the walk-in "Beer Cave," housing hundreds of rare, cold-stored craft brews, sours, and imports from around the globe.
Hemlock House | 1026 S. Braddock Ave.
A moody, atmospheric neighborhood favorite with a design and menu inspired by the natural beauty of the Pacific Northwest and cozy cabin culture. Featuring warm wood paneling and vintage accents, it is the perfect spot to gather over inventive comfort plates like house-smoked meats and seasonal pasta paired with expertly balanced craft cocktails and natural wines.
Frick Park Tavern | 1100 S. Braddock Ave.
This polished yet welcoming pub right on the main avenue feels like a natural extension of the neighborhood's cozy residential streets. Known for its glowing mahogany bar, warm lighting, and elevated American comfort food, it is a go-to spot for juicy gourmet burgers, seasonal salads, fish and chips, and a crisp local pint.
Murphy’s Tap Room | 1106 S. Braddock Ave.
The quintessential, unpretentious neighborhood watering hole where locals gather to unwind. With a classic long bar, cold beers on tap, and a friendly, no-frills attitude, it offers an authentic slice of local hospitality after a long trail walk in nearby Frick Park.
Global Flavors
The neighborhood’s casual global restaurants add variety to the South Braddock dining strip, with low-key options for quick bites, BYOB dinners, and group-friendly meals.
Thai Cottage | 1109 S. Braddock Ave.
A long-standing neighborhood staple housed in a charming, intimate storefront that smells instantly of toasted peanuts, lemongrass, and rich coconut curry. Famous for its generous portions of classic Pad Thai, rich Panang curries, and crisp spring rolls, its warm BYOB policy makes it a favorite for low-key date nights and casual group dinners.
El Burro Dos | 1113 S. Braddock Ave.
A vibrant, West Coast-style taqueria that brings bright, California-inspired street food to South Braddock Avenue. Known for its colorful, low-key interior and expansive menu, it caters generously to many dietary preferences, offering everything from slow-cooked carnitas and chorizo burritos to seasoned seitan tacos, vegan queso, and loaded french-fry burritos.
Bosphorus Mediterranean Cuisine | 7600 Forbes Ave.
An inviting BYOB dining space filled with the warm aromas of Turkish spices, grilled meats, and freshly baked bread. Guests can enjoy authentic Mediterranean hospitality alongside vibrant mezze platters, tender lamb kebabs, crisp falafel, and house-made hummus finished with a pot of traditional Turkish coffee.
Cafes, Bakeries & Juiceries
When you need coffee, a pastry, or a quick afternoon reset, Regent Square has plenty of easy neighborhood stops to keep the day moving.
Madeline’s Bakery & Bistro | 609 S. Trenton Ave.
A bright, French-inspired scratch bakery that lures morning visitors from blocks away with the scent of caramelized butter and fresh sourdough. Step inside to find display cases overflowing with golden croissants, seasonal fruit tarts, delicate macaroons, and artisan loaves, all paired with high-end espresso drinks.
Biddle’s Escape | 401 Biddle Ave.
Tucked a few steps off the main avenue into the shaded residential streets, this bohemian, two-story coffee house is an East End legend. Packed with eclectic global artifacts, vintage art, and a vast outdoor deck shaded by trees, it’s a sanctuary for artists and students looking for specialized loose-leaf teas, custom espresso drinks, and local pastries.
61B Cafe | 1108 S. Braddock Ave.
A sunlit neighborhood corner cafe that functions as an everyday social hub. Featuring a popular walk-up window for park-goers and dog walkers, it serves up robust espresso drinks, light breakfast sandwiches, local ice cream, and fresh pastries in a relaxed, friendly space.
Famous Café | 1103 S. Braddock Ave.
A classic, cheery neighborhood diner spot where weekend mornings are spent over steaming mugs of coffee and plate-filling breakfast classics. Expect traditional eggs benedict, fluffy buttermilk pancakes, loaded omelets, and warm, family-style service.
Live Fresh Juicery | 1104 S. Braddock Ave.
A sleek, health-conscious bar designed for quick, revitalizing pitstops. It specializes in raw, cold-pressed fruit and vegetable juices, functional wellness shots, handcrafted acai bowls topped with fresh fruit and granola, and nutrient-dense smoothies.
Confections | 1141 S. Braddock Ave.
A sweet, charming specialty bakery dedicated to hand-crafted celebrations. Known for its artfully decorated custom cakes, gourmet cupcakes, seasonal cookies, and sweet treats, it is a staple for birthday orders and mid-day sugar cravings.
Planning Tip: A Progressive Village Evening
For a relaxed night out, treat Regent Square like a strolling dinner crawl. Start with a rare craft pint from the Beer Cave at D’s Six Pax & Dogz, wander down South Braddock Avenue for a cozy dinner at Hemlock House or Thai Cottage, and end with an easygoing nightcap at Murphy’s Tap Room.
