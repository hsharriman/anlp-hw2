Discover Your Persona
October 25, 2026 2:00 PM –4:00 PM
Learn about the fascinating life and career of photographer, author, music producer, and former CIA agent Hank O’Neal as he discusses his work featured in the exhibition Hank O’Neal: Population Not Many, as well as his celebrated portraits of artists, musicians, and other important figures. The afternoon includes light bites and a wine reception.
