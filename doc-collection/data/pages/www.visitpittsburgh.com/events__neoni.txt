Discover Your Persona
October 26, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Neoni is the sister duo of Caitlin and Sidney Powell, known for a dramatic blend of alternative pop, rock and electronic dance music. Their sound pairs powerful vocals and cinematic production with dark, hook-driven songwriting that has resonated across streaming platforms and in sync-friendly pop culture spaces. The duo’s catalog includes the albums Wars in a Wonderland, ALL MY FAVORITE MONSTERS, The Death of Daisies (Deluxe) and 2026’s How to Kill a Fairytale. Neoni’s live performance brings that bold, futuristic energy to the stage, offering fans an immersive showcase of their evolving sound.
