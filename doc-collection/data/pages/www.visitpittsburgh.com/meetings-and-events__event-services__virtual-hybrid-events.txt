Virtual & Hybrid Event Support
Pittsburgh's Business Events Team is proud to now offer extended services to support you as you plan your next hybrid event!
As our clients’ needs continue to evolve, Business Events is committed to adapting to meet these new demands and exceed expectations.
We look forward to welcoming you back to Pittsburgh for an in-person meeting. Until that time, please know that we are ready to assist in your planning needs as you move forward with hosting virtual and hybrid events.
Virtual Site Visits
Right now, travel isn’t like it once was, and we know that you still need to plan for your future meeting in Pittsburgh. We are here and equipped to show you your event venues virtually and get you familiar with Pittsburgh. We have partnered with Threshold 360 to bring Pittsburgh to you. Our interactive mapping combined with a personalized one-on-one tour will give you the information that you need to plan your event. Contact your Pittsburgh's Business Events Team member for more information.
Virtual Resources and Tools
As you continue to work through the planning process, please keep these tools and assistance in mind:
Marketing Toolkit
Take advantage of our PHOTO and VIDEO assets to help market Pittsburgh as a part of your next hybrid/virtual event and to promote Pittsburgh for your next meeting.
Pittsburgh's Business Events Team Connections
Thought Leadership from Pittsburgh’s key sectors
Local Pittsburgh entertainment to showcase during virtual coffee breaks and happy hours
Community leaders to assist in developing a Community Service Project to assist a local Pittsburgh charity and fulfill your organization’s CSR initiatives
