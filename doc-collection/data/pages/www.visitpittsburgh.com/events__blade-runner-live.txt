Discover Your Persona
November 8, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Blade Runner Live presents Ridley Scott’s acclaimed 1982 science-fiction noir on a large screen in its 2007 Final Cut edition, accompanied by Vangelis’ atmospheric score performed live by The Avex Ensemble. This film-in-concert experience immerses audiences in the rain-soaked, dystopian world of Los Angeles as Replicant Hunter Rick Deckard pursues four escaped bioengineered beings and confronts questions of identity, memory, and humanity. Produced by Avex Classics International in association with Warner Bros., the presentation combines a landmark cinematic story with an enveloping live musical performance. The film is rated R and shown in English with captions.
