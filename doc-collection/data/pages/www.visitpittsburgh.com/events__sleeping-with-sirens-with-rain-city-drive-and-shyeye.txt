October 27, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Sleeping With Sirens is a post-hardcore band that has become one of the defining acts of its generation through a combination of emotional songwriting, soaring melodies, and energetic live performances. Fronted by vocalist Kellin Quinn, whose distinctive high-register voice has become instantly recognizable within the rock community, the band rose to prominence with albums such as With Ears to See and Eyes to Hear, Let's Cheers to This, and Feel. Their music frequently explores themes of perseverance, relationships, self-discovery, and personal resilience, helping them connect deeply with fans around the world.
Joining the tour, Rain City Drive brings a modern rock sound built on powerful vocals, polished production, and a blend of alternative, post-hardcore, and hard rock influences that have earned the band a rapidly growing audience. Also appearing on the bill is SHYEYE, an emerging artist known for combining emotionally driven songwriting with contemporary rock elements, adding a fresh perspective to a lineup that showcases both established favorites and rising talent. Together, these performers create a diverse concert experience that highlights the continued evolution of modern rock while delivering the passion and intensity that fans have come to expect from the genre.
