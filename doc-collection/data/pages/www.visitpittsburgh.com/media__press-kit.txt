Press Kit
The Online Press Kit is a resource for journalists interested in experiencing and writing about the Pittsburgh region. It contains a bevy of information, including new developments, story ideas and news releases.
Backgrounders on a variety of information, including Pittsburgh's history, cultural scene and economic and environmental transformation are included, as well as facts + trivia, rankings, hotel updates and VisitPITTSBURGH-hosted press tours. Don't miss the Spotlight on Pittsburgh, the collection of great news coverage.
Request A Press Kit
If you are a member of the media and would like to request a printed Press Kit, please fill out the following form.
Tourists are encouraged to call 1.877.LOVE PGH (1.877.568.3744) or e-mail info@visitpittsburgh.com to request visitor information.
