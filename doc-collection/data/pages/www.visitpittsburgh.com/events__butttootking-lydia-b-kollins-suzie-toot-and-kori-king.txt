October 31, 2026 7:00 PM –11:00 PM
ALL NEW SHOW FOR 2026! The show that changed the face of drag is BACK!
A riotous evening starring America’s favorite couple from RuPaul’s Drag Race Season 17, Lydia B Kollins and Kori King, and their friend Suzie Toot. Don’t miss your chance to see these three make MAGIC. An all new show with all new numbers!
Buttholes, Tooters and Kingdom: we heard you loud and clear. BUTTTOOTKING is here to stay!
As always, costumes are encouraged and there will be a costume contest in every city for the best in show!
Get ready for the WILDEST night of your life; don’t say we didn’t warn you.
Meet and Greet with Lydia, Suzie and Kori is at 6:00 PM
Produced by Flip Phone Events, Fiercely You Entertainment & Michael Benedetti
