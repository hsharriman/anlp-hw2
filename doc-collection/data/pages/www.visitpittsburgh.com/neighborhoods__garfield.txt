Garfield
Driven by a fiercely independent, collaborative spirit
Garfield is a dynamic neighborhood where historic architecture meets cutting-edge creative culture. Centered along a vibrant stretch of Penn Avenue, this community has evolved into Pittsburgh’s premier playground for contemporary art, community-focused makers, and culinary innovation. Whether you are visiting to watch world-class glassblowing, browse avant-garde galleries, or dine at internationally acclaimed restaurants, Garfield delivers a bold, grassroots energy you won't find anywhere else in the city.
5 Must-See Experiences in Garfield
1. Catch the "Unblurred" First Friday Gallery Crawl
On the first Friday of every month, Penn Avenue transforms into the city's premier arts crawl, Unblurred. Galleries, boutiques, and creative spaces open late with new exhibit openings, live music, street performers, and artisan pop-ups.
2. Watch Live Glassblowing at Pittsburgh Glass Center
Step inside the Pittsburgh Glass Center, this world-renowned, open-access facility to watch incredible live glassblowing demonstrations, explore rotating contemporary glass art exhibitions, or take a hands-on introductory workshop.
3. Dine at the Acclaimed Apteka
Experience the boundary-pushing culinary scene at Apteka. This James Beard-nominated restaurant, celebrated nationally for its inventive, vegetable-forward take on traditional Eastern European comfort foods and spectacular natural wine list.
4. Get Crafty at Workshop PGH Fiber Studio
Immerse yourself in the neighborhood's maker culture by taking a creative evening DIY class at Workshop PGH Fiber Studio. or shop their retail space packed with modern craft supplies, indoor plants, and artisan-made gifts.
5. Grab a Coffee and a Pint at Trace Brewing
Hang out at this dynamic neighborhood hub! Trace Brewing serves as a high-end specialty coffee bar by day and a vibrant craft brewery by night, complete with a beautiful, string-lit outdoor courtyard hosting local food trucks.
Pro Tip: Experience the Ultimate Two-Neighborhood Stroll
While Garfield and Bloomfield have completely distinct histories and cultural identities, they are immediate geographic neighbors. You can easily experience both in a single afternoon! Head to the western end of Garfield's Penn Avenue arts corridor, make a left turn down 48th Street, and within a few short blocks, you'll cross right onto Bloomfield's bustling Liberty Avenue.
Planning to extend your day trip? Be sure to explore our companion Bloomfield Neighborhood Hub to experience Pittsburgh's historic "Little Italy."
