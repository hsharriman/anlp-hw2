November 6, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
The Pittsburgh Panthers host the Duke Blue Devils in an Atlantic Coast Conference women’s volleyball matchup at Victory Heights Arena and Sports Performance Center. The meeting brings together two prominent ACC programs in a fast-paced collegiate contest featuring powerful serves, precise setting, athletic defense, and intense net play. Pitt’s 2026 schedule identifies Duke as a conference opponent in Pittsburgh, while Duke lists the Panthers as part of its ACC road slate. It is an engaging opportunity for sports fans to experience high-level NCAA volleyball and cheer on the Panthers in a competitive conference setting.
