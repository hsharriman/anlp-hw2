Discover Your Persona
October 25, 2026 to December 17, 2026 7:00 PM –8:00 PM
When: Every 3rd Thursday, 7:00–8:00 PM
Where: Monroeville Location, L4
Dates: July 16th – December 17th (Monthly)
Sessions: 6 sessions
Ages: Adults 18+
Join us to honor the memory of your loved ones, discover valuable coping strategies, and connect with others in a safe and profoundly understanding space. Whether you are recently bereaved or navigating a longer-term healing journey, our group offers a welcoming, gentle environment to find comfort, community, and support.
Group leader, Rachel Taylor, LPC, NBCC, has been creating and facilitating support groups for the past 10 years. With her extensive experience, Rachel has developed a reputation for creating safe, deeply compassionate spaces where participants feel truly seen and heard. Her groups consistently fill up because she makes heavy topics feel manageable and accessible. Rachel specializes in grief and crisis care, bringing a warm, dynamic approach that focuses on holding space, reducing isolation, and helping individuals find a path forward after complex loss.
This Healing Hearts Support Group offers a safe space for you to connect with like-minded peers and explore your thoughts, feelings, and shared experiences call us at 412-322-2129 to get started..
