November 6, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Max Weinberg's Jukebox is a live concert experience led by legendary drummer Max Weinberg, best known for his longtime role with Bruce Springsteen's E Street Band. Unlike a traditional concert, the show allows audience members to help shape the setlist by requesting songs from an extensive catalog of classic rock favorites, creating a unique performance every night. Backed by a talented group of musicians, Weinberg delivers energetic renditions of iconic songs spanning multiple decades of rock history. Known for its interactive format, exceptional musicianship, and spontaneous atmosphere, Max Weinberg's Jukebox offers fans an engaging celebration of classic rock and live performance culture.
