Discover Your Persona
November 10, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Willi Carlisle is a spirited folksinger, songwriter and storyteller whose performances blend traditional folk, country, Americana, old-time music and sharp, compassionate humor. With banjo, guitar and a richly expressive voice, he brings vividly drawn characters and emotionally resonant tales to life, creating an experience that feels both intimate and communal. Carlisle’s forthcoming album, The Universal Bubba, expands his sound across 17 tracks and was produced with Tyler Childers, adding new depth to his distinctive musical world. This 18+ performance offers an engaging evening of heartfelt songs, memorable stories and unexpected turns.
