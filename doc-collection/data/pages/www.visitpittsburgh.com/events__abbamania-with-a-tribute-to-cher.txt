November 5, 2026 7:30 PM –10:00 PM
Abbamania with A Tribute to Cher is a high-energy concert experience that celebrates two of pop music’s most iconic acts through dazzling performances and timeless hits. Abbamania recreates the sound, style, and infectious energy of ABBA with elaborate costumes, powerful harmonies, and faithful renditions of beloved disco-pop classics. The tribute to Cher adds another layer of spectacle with glamorous stage presence, bold fashion, and performances inspired by the legendary singer’s decades of chart-topping pop and dance anthems. Together, the show delivers a nostalgic and entertaining night filled with singalong favorites, vibrant production, and the unmistakable spirit of two legendary music icons.
