Discover Your Persona
November 11, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Australian singer-songwriter Julia Jacklin is known for candid, sharply observed indie rock that explores intimacy, heartbreak, faith, and personal agency. Her acclaimed albums include the folky debut Don’t Let the Kids Win, the emotionally intense Crushing, and PRE PLEASURE, which features “I Was Neon,” “Lydia Wears a Cross,” and “End of a Friendship.” Jacklin’s latest release, The Gem, arrived in September 2026, expanding her catalog with new material while retaining her direct, expressive songwriting style.
