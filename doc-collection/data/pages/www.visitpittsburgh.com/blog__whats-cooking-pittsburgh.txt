Dive Into Flavor: Pittsburgh's Culinary Experiences
From chef-driven dishes to new restaurant offerings, Pittsburgh has built a reputation as a foodie city.
Pittsburgh has forged its legacy as a foodie city, but while you can easily go into the community to get some Pittsburgh staples, some are more of an experience than a simple sit-down restaurant. From curated multi-course meals to cooking classes from generational chefs, Pittsburgh has everything you need to feel immersed in our world-class culinary scene.
One by Spork
Where: 5430 Penn Ave (Bloomfield)
Hours: 6pm - 9pm, Thurs-Sun
One by Spork is intimate by design. 18 guests gather around the circular chef’s counter - close enough to the open kitchen to watch a sauce get finished, and ask the person who made your drink what’s in it. You’ll feel the difference between a restaurant that serves you food and one that tells a story through it. Your evening moves with intention, from a welcome in the lounge through each course. Every dish and pairing is introduced by the people who created them. Your experience is polished, personal, precise and alive.

Enrico Biscotti Co.
Where: 5430 Penn Ave (Bloomfield)
Hours: 6pm - 9pm, Thurs-Sun
Enrico Biscotti Co. is a small bakery tucked away in Pittsburgh's Strip District, but it's also so much more than that. Step into this homegrown hidden gem and get ready to learn, because you can take cooking classes for all kinds of dishes. Take your cooking skills to the next level through the expertise and minds of these beloved Italian chefs.

'Burgh Bits and Bites Food Tour
Where: Various
Hours: Various
Connecting people to communities through food, ‘Burgh Bits and Bites Food Tour is a food tasting adventure and historic walking tour of Pittsburgh neighborhoods. Enjoy a historic narrative of the area along with a sampling of cuisine from some of Pittsburgh's best small local businesses and eateries.
Tours available in the Strip District-Pittsburgh's historic market district, Lawrenceville, Northside, Brookline, South Side and Oakland. Harmony and Saxonburg tours available in Butler County.

Hyeholde Restaurant
Where: 1516 Coraopolis Heights Rd. (Moon Township)
Hours: 4:30pm - 9 pm, Tues-Sat
Established in 1937, Hyeholde is a historic Pittsburgh treasure offering farm-to-table dining in an enchanting Tudor mansion. Surrounded by lush English gardens and rich with old-world charm, it’s a beloved destination for intimate dinners, private events, and weddings. Hyeholde's scenery makes it the perfect place to enjoy a picnic, which they offer during the summer months. Designed to sweep you off your feet, you'll enjoy a delicious multi-course spread in the gardens in an experience that can only be described as whimsical.

Wigle Whiskey
Where: 2401 Smallman St. (Strip District)
Hours: 11am-7pm, Mon & Tues; 11am-9pm, Weds & Thurs; 11am-10pm, Fri; 11am-5pm, Sat; 11am-4pm, Sun
Wigle Whiskey Distillery, Restaurant, and Bottle Shop, located in the Strip District is the most awarded craft distillery in the country by the American Craft Spirits Association. This 2x James Beard nominated distillery makes whiskeys, bourbon, gin, and bottled cocktails from scratch. Step into the prohibition era with Saturday Distillery tours that include tastings and cocktails. Learn about Pittsburgh's whiskey-spiked history and Wigle's history as the first Whiskey Distillery in Pittsburgh since prohibition. Stay for a meal from the Wigle Bar & Kitchen, and leave with a bottle of Wigle Whiskey to-go from the bottle shop!
