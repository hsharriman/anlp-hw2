Discover Your Persona
November 12, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Jack White is a rock musician, singer-songwriter, and producer known for his raw guitar style, blues-driven sound, and influential role in modern garage rock revival. Rising to fame as the frontman of The White Stripes, White became celebrated for stripped-down rock arrangements, powerful riffs, and a distinctive blend of blues, punk, folk, and classic rock influences. His music is marked by inventive songwriting, energetic performances, and a commitment to analog recording techniques that give his work a gritty and authentic feel. With multiple successful bands, acclaimed solo projects, and a reputation as one of the most innovative guitarists of his generation, Jack White remains a major force in contemporary rock music.
