Preschools & Childcare
​Mister Rogers' hometown understands the importance of early childhood education and development.
The Pittsburgh region offers a variety of choices for the education and care of young children. To learn more, visit the Pennsylvania Association for the Education of Young Children, the Pennsylvania Department of Welfare and the United Way of Southwestern Pennsylvania.
External Preschools + Childcare Resources
Pennsylvania Association for the Education of Young Children
The Pennsylvania Association for the Education of Young Children (PennAEYC) works to support high quality early care and education for young children through professional development, collaboration with community resources and advocacy for the needs and rights of children, their families and the individuals who work with them.
Pennsylvania Department of Welfare
Resources for parents, business, and early education and care providers.
United Way of Southwestern Pennsylvania
As the community's fundraiser, United Way of Southwestern Pennsylvania streamlines the process of getting dollars to agencies that are impacting critical community needs. Each year, thousands of individuals in hundreds of workplace campaigns find help or give help through United Way. United Way ensures maximum community impact and optimal donor value through high standards of organizational excellence and efficiency, expertise in identifying and addressing community needs, and rigorous review and monitoring of non-profit partners.
