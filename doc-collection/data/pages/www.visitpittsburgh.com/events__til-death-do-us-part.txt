'Til Death Do Us Part For Love That Thrives in the Spotlight & Shadow.
Calling all Halloween lovers and dark romantics looking to wed! 'Til Death Do Us Part transforms the New Hazlett Theater stage into a moody, candlelit sanctuary for an unforgettable micro-wedding celebration.
Exchange vows beneath rich theatrical lighting in a gothic fairy-tale setting, then step into the lobby as newlyweds for a champagne toast, cake-cutting, and a classic Pittsburgh cookie table.
Date: Friday, October 30, 2026
Registration Cutoff: October 19, 2026
Price: $1,500 per couple (Limited to 6 couples max)
What's Included: For a full list of package inclusions—including guest capacity, floral arrangements, mood-setting decor, and our special photography experience—please visit https://www.newhazletttheater.org/weddings .
Looking for something different? If October 30th doesn't work for you, explore the rest of our Spotlight Weddings collection! Check out our upcoming Winter Wonderland event, or explore flexible, year-round options like our Ceremony on the Steps package at https://www.newhazletttheater.org/weddings
