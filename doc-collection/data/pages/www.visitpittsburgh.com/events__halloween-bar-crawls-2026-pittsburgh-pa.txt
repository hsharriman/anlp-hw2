October 30, 2026 to November 1, 2026 5:00 PM –12:00 AM
Celebrate Halloween at the best bars across South Side with welcome shots/treats, drink specials and more.
Pittsburgh's 5th Annual Halloween Bar Crawl is taking over South Side on Halloween eve.
Dress up, grab your crew and join one of Pittsburgh’s biggest Halloween nightlife events, featuring 5+ of the city’s best bars in one of its most popular neighborhoods. Costumes make the perfect icebreaker, so come ready to mix and mingle for an even more memorable Halloween experience.
🎃 One Ticket. One District. One Huge Halloween Night.
Your wristband gives you access to participating venues across the district, up to 5 complimentary welcome shots and other perks. Choose your own route, bounce from bar to bar and see where the night takes you.
🧡 Bring your friends and save 20% on group tickets of four or more!
Tickets are limited and prices go up as we sell out, so get yours early before they're gone.
MANDATORY CHECK-IN BETWEEN 5PM–8PM:
📍 Twelve Whiskey Barbecue - 1222 E Carson St, Pittsburgh, PA 15203
WHAT'S INCLUDED
🔶 Up to 5 Complimentary Welcome Shots/Treats – Redeem at the bars of your choice (limit 1 per venue)
🔶 Signature Halloween Drink Specials – Curated for the occasion
🔶 Free Cover – At all participating venues (no re-entry at some locations)
🔶 Professional Event Photography – You bring the look, we’ll capture the moment
🔶 Instagram Costume Contest – Post your look, tag us, and win
HOW IT WORKS
1. Check in between 5–8PM at Twelve Whiskey Barbecue.
2. Collect your wristband, bar map and event perks.
3. Choose your route and crawl in your own time, there is no set schedule.
4. Sip, dance, mingle, repeat. It’s your Halloween, your way.
For updated participating bars & restaurants please click on link below

COSTUME CULTURE
👻 You don’t need to go full theatrical, but this night is about the look. Make it iconic, creative, stylish - or simply bold.
📸 Post your best shot on Instagram and tag @downtowncrawlers to enter the contest. Winners will be announced the week after the crawl.
