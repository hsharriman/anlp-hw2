Where To Swim in Pittsburgh
Hot summer temps call for long days spent at the pool. Check out these swimming pools in Pittsburgh, and stay cool swimming, lounging, and splashing around.
Thrilling wave pools that kids and adults love, state-of-the-art splash parks, and everything in between make Pittsburgh’s pools a destination for every level of water comfort.

Swimming Pools
There are many lodging options in Pittsburgh with outdoor pools, including the DoubleTree by Hilton Pittsburgh Cranberry, DoubleTree by Hilton Pittsburgh - Green Tree, Hampton Inn Pittsburgh Green Tree, and Sun and Cricket Bed and Breakfast. You can also consider staying at one of the many Pittsburgh hotels with indoor pools.
Pittsburgh also has over a dozen outdoor pools to enjoy in the summertime! These public pools open for the 2026 season on Monday, June 15, and will each follow their own specific schedule. Learn more about CitiPark Pools in Pittsburgh.

Wave Pools
Just outside Pittsburgh, find a different kind of swimming adventure at the area’s wave pools. Visit the Allegheny County parks website for information about daily hours, admission, and wave pool rules.
Boyce Park Wave Pool: 200 Wave Pool Way, Monroeville, PA 15239
Settlers Cabin Wave Pool: 600 Ridge Road, Robinson, PA 15205
South Park Wave Pool: 280 Wave Pool Drive, Bethel Park, PA 15102
Splash Pads and Spray Parks
Non-swimmers who still love the water will enjoy the various spray parks around Pittsburgh. These CitiPark spray features opened for the season on May 23, 2026, and are open daily (weather permitting) from 10am-8pm through mid-September.
Before heading out for some fun, be sure to check the CitiParks Facebook page for any special events or closures.

Water Parks
Plan a full day of swimming adventures at Pittsburgh-area water parks! Enjoy swimming, lazy rivers, wave pools, water slides, and water rides. There’s something for every comfort level in the water, from young kids and beginner swimmers to thrill-seekers. Don’t forget the sunscreen!
Sandcastle Waterpark: 1000 Sandcastle Drive, West Homestead, PA 15120
Idlewild & SoakZone: 2574 Lincoln Hwy, Ligonier, PA 15658
