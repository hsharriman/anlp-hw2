Easter Events in Pittsburgh
Spring is in full bloom, and it's time to celebrate Easter!
Are you searching for the best Easter brunch in Pittsburgh, looking for the ultimate Easter egg hunt, or searching for a fun, family-friendly event? From egg-stravagant brunches to trolley rides with the Easter Bunny, check out these festive events!

Easter Egg Hunts in Pittsburgh
Easter Egg-Stravaganza (Adults Only)
Date: March 6, 2026
Time: 6:30 PM – 8:30 PM
Location: Indiana Free Library | 845 Philadelphia St., Indiana, PA
Why should kids have all the fun? This after-hours Easter egg hunt in Pittsburgh is designed for adults who still love the thrill of finding hidden treasures. Hunt through the library’s stacks, enjoy live music, sip on adult beverages, and keep an eye out for grand prize eggs.
Bunny Trolley at the Pennsylvania Trolley Museum
Dates: March 27-29 & April 3-4, 2026
Time: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM
Location: Pennsylvania Trolley Museum | 1 Electric Way, Washington, PA
Admission: $22 per person | Ages 2 & under free
Hop aboard a trolley ride through the springtime countryside and meet the Easter Bunny himself. This family-friendly event includes a festive scavenger hunt, crafts, and plenty of picture-perfect moments.

Easter Brunch in Pittsburgh
Easter Sunday Buffet at Grand Concourse
Date: April 5, 2026
Time: 9 AM - 7 PM
Location: Grand Concourse | 100 W. Station Square Dr., Pittsburgh, PA
Cost: Adults: $62 | Children 12 & Under: $29
Dine in style at one of Pittsburgh’s most iconic restaurants. Housed in the beautifully restored Pittsburgh & Lake Erie Railroad Station, Grand Concourse has a special menu with morning and evening offerings featuring fresh seafood and classic Easter brunch and dinner favorites. Reservations are strongly encouraged.
Make a Reservation
Easter Brunch in The Garden Room at the National Aviary
Date: April 5, 2026
Time: 11 AM & 12:45 PM
Location: National Aviary | 700 Arch St., Pittsburgh, PA
Celebrate Easter surrounded by lush greenery and tropical birds. Enjoy a delicious brunch spread, then hop on in to explore the National Aviary—admission is included with your brunch ticket. This popular event sells out quickly, so book your spot in advance.
Check Availability
Easter Brunch at Braddock’s Rebellion
Date: April 5, 2026
Time: 11 AM - 3 PM
Location: Braddock’s Rebellion | 107 6th St., Pittsburgh, PA
Cost: Adults: $45 | Children 6-12: $16.95 | Children 5 & Under: Free
Book a table at Braddock’s Rebellion on Easter Sunday for a brunch with something for everyone. Choose from a wide assortment of starters and entrees, such as a Denver omelet, corned beef hash, or the classic Braddock's breakfast.
Find a Table
More Easter & Springtime Events in Pittsburgh
Eggstravaganza
Date: March 28-29 and April 3-5, 2026
Time: 11 AM – 3 PM
Location: National Aviary | 700 Arch St., Pittsburgh, PA
Hop on over to the National Aviary for a spring celebration! Meet the Easter Bunny and snap photos with him! Go on an egg-cellent scavenger hunt through the Aviary’s immersive habitats. Experience a fun and engaging story time, get crafty, and so much more!
58th Annual Ukrainian Pysanky Easter Egg Sale
Date: March 29, 2026
Time: 11 AM – 4 PM
Location: Sts. Peter & Paul Ukrainian Orthodox Church | 220 Mansfield Blvd., Carnegie, PA
Admire the intricate beauty of hand-painted Pysanky eggs at this annual sale. Over 1,200 traditional eggs, Ukrainian crafts, baked goods, and authentic cuisine will be available. Plus, take a guided church tour and learn about the art of Pysanky.
Easter Weekend at Nemacolin
Date: April 3-5, 2026
Location: Nemacolin | 1001 Lafayette Dr., Farmington, PA
Celebrate Easter weekend at Nemacolin with a resort-wide adventure full of family-friendly surprises, including egg hunts, wildlife encounters, crafts, games, and more! An overnight stay or membership is required to participate.
Book Now
Royal Princess Easter Ball
Date: April 4, 2026
Time: 5 PM - 8:30 PM
Location: Wyndham Grand Hotel | 600 Commonwealth Pl. Pittsburgh, PA
Cost: $100-$300
Step into a world of elegance, magic, and wonder at the Royal Easter Ball, an enchanting spring celebration designed for families who want to create unforgettable memories together. For one spectacular afternoon, the ballroom transforms into a glowing fairytale garden where children are welcomed as honored guests and every moment feels like it belongs in a storybook.
Purchase Tickets
Easter Cruises on the Gateway Clipper
Date: April 5, 2026
Time: Various Times
Location: Gateway Clipper Fleet | 350 West Station Square Drive, Pittsburgh, PA
Come aboard and celebrate Easter with family and friends aboard a Gateway Clipper Fleet Easter Dining Cruise. Enjoy a hassle-free holiday, out on the water with the ones you love. There are options for Easter cruises, including a dinner cruise with a full bar and buffet from 12 PM - 2 PM, as well as the Bunny Family Fun cruise with morning and afternoon times that includes Bunny Hop dancing and a visit from the Easter Bunny.
Book A Cruise
The Fez Easter Dinner Celebration
Date: April 5, 2026
Time: 12 PM, 2 PM, and 4 PM
Location: The Fez | 2312 Brodhead Road , Aliquippa, PA
Cost: Adults: $42.95 | Seniors 65+ $41.95 | Children 6-12: $14.95-$16.95 | Children 5 and Under Free
Head to The Fez for their 49th annual Easter dinner, featuring chef-carving stations, soups and salads, and a large assortment of desserts. There will also be a special appearance from The Easter Bunny and plenty of photo opportunities.
Reserve a Table
Enjoy Easter brunch in Pittsburgh, participate in egg-citing Easter egg hunts, and celebrate the season with spring-inspired activities. Reserve your spot early, grab your baskets, and get ready for a wonderful holiday weekend!
