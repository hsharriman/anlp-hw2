Labor Day Weekend 2026 in Pittsburgh - The Ultimate Guide
Your three-day weekend is right around the corner. Here is the ultimate guide for how to make the most of your Labor Day weekend in Pittsburgh!
Credit |
JP Diroll
Scheduled Events
Other Ways to Celebrate
Work hard, play harder. There’s plenty of Pittsburgh fun to be had throughout the holiday weekend! Enjoy free concerts, festivals, special events and much more in the original birthplace of the Federation of Organized Trades and Labor Unions in the United States and Canada.
This Labor Day weekend is the perfect opportunity to take a respite from work and bring your family and friends to the ‘Burgh for a little R&R. Pack a chair to sit under the stars for a free outdoor concert or bring a blanket to stretch out in one of our parks. Come on over. No labor involved!
Events Scheduled for Labor Day Weekend
Acrisure Stadium Kickoff & Rib Fest
Sept. 3-Sept. 7 | Acrisure Stadium, North Shore
Enjoy mouthwatering ribs and BBQ from award-winning food vendors, free concerts, a Pitt vs. Miami kickoff game, games and rides for the kids and more.
Allegheny County Summer Concert Series at South Park
Sept. 4 | South Park Amphitheater
Experience the Allegheny County Summer Concert Series at South Park over Labor Day weekend. Enjoy a free concert as Reb Beach takes the stage at South Park Amphitheater.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Acrisure Stadium (@acrisurestadium)
HOT Jam
Sept. 4 | Pittsburgh Glass Center
The Friday that kicks off Labor Day Weekend, the Pittsburgh Glass Center will be offering a free event for guests to come into their studios and watch their talented glassblowers at work. Catch an up-close view of these artists crafting hand-blown glass pieces.
The Merry Wives of Windsor
Sept. 4 - Sept. 6 | Highland Park
Pittsburgh Shakespeare in the Parks opens their Fall season with a playful flair to the Shakespearean comedy The Merry Wives of Windsor, complete with drag queens, boisterous fun and an exquisite fall into an alternative telling of the historic tale. Performances will continue throughout the month at different outdoor parks around the city.
Pittsburgh Soul Food Festival
Sept. 4 - 6 | Stanwix Street and Liberty Avenue, Downtown
Pittsburgh's Soul Food Fest honors the rich history of culinary, butchery, and Black food businesses in Pittsburgh since 1795. The festival will take place in downtown Pittsburgh on Stanwix Street and Liberty Avenue. Enjoy delicious food from a variety of vendors across Western Pennsylvania along with:
A BBQ contest
Speakers
Local and national entertainment
Family-friendly activities
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Pittsburgh Shakespeare (@pghshakespeareparks)
My America: An Illustrated Experience
Sept. 4-7 | Heinz History Center
View the incredible illustrations of 35 artists at the Heinz History Center. Their exhibit "My America: An Illustrated Experience" will be having its final showing on Labor Day Weekend. The powerful collection features watercolors, sculptures, cartoons, and more, all depicting unique interpretations of the United States of America and the experience of living in the country.
Pirates Games at PNC Park
Sept. 4-6 | PNC Park
Support our home team as they play the Los Angeles Angels at PNC Park on Labor Day Weekend. Catch the evening games on Friday and Saturday or opt for an afternoon game on Sunday.
If you're going to the game, check out our guide to your weekend in Pittsburgh when you're not at the ballpark.
Steelers Run & Walk 5K at Acrisure Stadium
Sept. 7 | Acrisure Stadium
The Steelers Run & Walk returns to kick off the 2026 football season! The event starts at 8:00a.m. and all participants will receive a t-shirt, finisher medal, goodie bag and a chance to win autographed prizes and tickets to the home opener.
Pittsburgh Labor Day Parade
Sept. 7 | Downtown Pittsburgh
Join Allegheny-Fayette Central Labor Council for one of the country’s largest and oldest Labor Day parades! The parade will feature special guests from across the country as they celebrate the history of organized labor in our region.

Find More Ways to Get Out on Our Rivers and 10 Wet Ways to Stay Cool in Pittsburgh this Labor Day weekend!
Hike or Bike Pittsburgh
Enjoy the great outdoors with plenty of family-friendly activities in Pittsburgh! Western Pennsylvania is home to some of the most diverse landscapes in the United States.
Our steep, winding hills and flat river areas make Pittsburgh one of the best cities for cyclists. Bring your own bikes or rent from Golden Triangle Bike.
The Allegheny County Parks also offer more than 180 miles of trails for hikers and people who just want to get outside.
Check out our guide to 5 Spectacular Stops along the Great Allegheny Passage
