Must-Attend Events & Festivals: Squirrel Hill
Squirrel Hill Events Calendar: Festivals, Night Markets, and Local Traditions
Whether you’re looking to dance in the streets at a night market or celebrate a century of tradition, Squirrel Hill’s event calendar is packed with local flavor. The neighborhood is famous for its inclusive, welcoming atmosphere, making these festivals the perfect time for visitors to dive into the community spirit.
Annual Events & Festivals
Squirrel Hill Night Markets
Experience the neighborhood at its most vibrant! Held three times per season, these open-air markets feature local vendors, food, and entertainment, drawing over 800 guests per night.
Lunar New Year Parade
Celebrate Squirrel Hill's rich Asian heritage with a spectacular parade and community event that attracts hundreds of visitors to the business district.
Squirrel Hill Farmers Market
Every Sunday from May through November, join over 800 locals to shop for fresh, regional produce and artisanal goods.
Wine Walks (Mother’s Day & Holiday Editions)
Grab a glass and explore local boutiques! These popular self-guided tours allow you to sip and shop through the neighborhood's flagship stores.
JCC Big Night
One of the biggest social events of the year, this massive celebration at the Jewish Community Center hosts over 800 guests for a night of community bonding.
The Treasure Awards Dinner
A heartfelt local tradition that honors the "human treasures" of Squirrel Hill—the people who make this neighborhood so special.
Holiday Mingle & Winter Markets
December in Squirrel Hill is magical, featuring three dedicated Winter Markets and the annual "Holiday Mingle" celebration.
Seasonal & Community Traditions
Fall Fun: Get into the spooky spirit with Squirrel Hill Trick or Treating (500+ guests) or the Day of the Dead celebration.
History & Heritage: Don't miss the Neill Log House Annual Event, where you can step back in time at the oldest home in the area.
Sustainability: Every year, over 150 volunteers gather for the Earth Day/Week Clean Up to keep our historic parks and streets beautiful.
So What's New? The community is always growing! Keep an eye out for the return of CommUNITY Day and new merchant-focused sidewalk sales and pet-friendly events currently in the works for 2026!
Neighborhood Tip
If you're heading to one of the big events like the Night Markets or the Lunar New Year Parade remember that Squirrel Hill is a "walker’s paradise". While there are four public parking lots and plenty of street parking, these celebrations get busy! We recommend hopping on a PRT bus route or using the local bike lanes to arrive like a pro.
