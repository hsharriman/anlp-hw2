November 8, 2026 6:30 PM –9:30 PM
Twin Peaks: A Conversation With the Stars is a live fan experience celebrating David Lynch and Mark Frost’s landmark television series. The Pittsburgh-area edition brings together Twin Peaks cast members Ray Wise, Michael Horse and Kimmy Robertson, alongside Executive Producer Sabrina S. Sutherland and moderator Josh Eisenstadt, for an engaging discussion. Audiences can hear firsthand stories from the set, explore behind-the-scenes memories and revisit the series’ distinctive blend of mystery, drama and surrealism. Presented under license from Paramount Location-Based Experiences, the event offers fans an intimate, multimedia-style celebration of the enduring Twin Peaks universe.
