Occult, Metaphysical & Tarot Shops for your Witchy Self
Find crystals, candles, classes, Tarot readings and more to manifest
It's a good time to be a witch. Following the explosive growth in interest and practicing of Pagan, Wiccan, witchcraft and other religions and spiritualities, shops have popped up across Pittsburgh to help fulfill your metaphysical needs.
Whether you're just starting out and creating your altar, looking for guidance and readings or an expert in the field, follow our guide to some of the top occult and metaphysical shops in the 'Burgh.
Ceremonial
Although it's had other names in the past, Ceremonial, an online-only store formerly located in Point Breeze, is a must-visit for living intentionally through a large selection of ritual lifestyle and Tarot products. Carefully curated crystals and candles, a vast array of divination decks and a vast metaphysical library make this shop a standout.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Ceremonial © (@ceremonial_shop)
Hocus Pocus
The oldest occult shop in Pittsburgh has been around for decades for a reason! Enter their magickal apothecary in Oakland for everything from old world classics to the shop's own blends to spiritual bath kits to refresh your body, auras and chakras. Hocus Pocus also specializes in gemstones and crystals, sage and cleansing supplies, spell kits, tarot and oracle decks and more!
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by HOCUS POCUS (@hocuspocuspgh)
Maude's Paperwing Gallery
Sure, Maude's in Millvale sells crystals and other metaphysical products, but what they pride themselves on is community. This Queer/Woman-owned shop has fantastic LGBTQIA+ representation and tons of items to help you express yourself. Witchy tools and supplies, LGBTQIA+ art, books and accessories, hilarious greeting cards and an extended library make their offerings eclectic and incredibly unique.
Maude's Paperwing Gallery also hosts tons of inclusive events from the Grimoire Book Club to movie nights and in-person and virtual get togethers and readings.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Harold's & Maude's - Millvale's Queer Witches (@queerwitches)
The Menagerie
Styling itself as a metaphysical conclave, The Menagerie in Bellevue prides itself on an always rotating selection and smiling service. Incredibly statues and skull-sculpted crystals highlight a diverse offering of in-store items, with custom candles, Tarot readings, house cleansings and energy healing also available for sessions.
The Crow's Nest
A metaphysical supply store and community space that offers a wide variety of products and services, The Crow's Nest is a community-driven metaphysical supply store in Pittsburgh's South Hills. You can book reiki, readings, and rituals here, as well as take classes and plan special events.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by The Crows Nest (@thecrowsnestpgh)
Geology Rocks! and Minerals
When it comes to finding the best all-natural minerals, fossils, jewelry, crystals, pocket stones and more, Geology Rocks! and Minerals located right on Butler St. in Lawrenceville should be your first stop! Their experts will find you the exact stone or mineral you need, plus they're also available for crystal rentals and geology identification services.
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by Geology Rocks! Pittsburgh (@geologyrockspittsburgh)
Sparkledragon's Magical Emporium
The most fun store name to say out loud, Sparkledragon's Magical Emporium is your spiritual sanctuary. Explore their Beechview area location for handmade metaphysical jewelry, herbs, Tarot readings and other esoteric goods.
Sparkledragon also helps put together great events in the city such as the Pittsburgh Witches Ball with food, drinks, entertainment and tons of community building.
Domestic Witch
A witchy and whimsical shop that opened its doors in 2025, Domestic Witch is your newest one-stop shop for all things occult. This woman-owned business near Pittsburgh's North Hills is open Saturdays and Sundays. Step into the world of the extraordinary at this alternative boutique!
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by DOMESTIC WITCH (@domesticwitchpgh)
The Weeping Glass
The Weeping Glass is Pittsburgh's original oddities and curiosity shop, and the pride it has in that title shows. At the store in Pittsburgh's Allentown neighborhood boasts usual gifts, odd art, and revels in melancholia and sadness.
Location: 746 E Warrington Ave, Pittsburgh, PA 15210
Hours: Wednesday - Friday 1pm-7pm, Saturday 12pm-7pm, Sunday 12pm- 4pm
View this post on Instagram
A post shared by theweepingglass (@theweepingglass)
Dr. Tumblety's
A time-inspired specialty shop in Pittsburgh's Allentown neighborhood, Dr. Tumblety's hosts a wide variety of different events through several different avenues. A highlight of the shop is definitely Storyville Lounge, a speakeasy hidden in the back - if you dare to try and find it.
Location: 753 E Warrington Ave., Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania 15210
Hours: Tuesday - Wednesday 3pm-9pm, Thursday - Friday 1pm-10pm, Saturday 12pm-11pm, Sunday 12pm-6pm

A post shared by 🎩 Dr. Tumblety’s®️ (@drtumbletys)
