November 13, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Florida State Seminoles football visits the Pittsburgh Panthers for an ACC matchup at Acrisure Stadium on Friday, November 13, 2026. The game is scheduled for a 7 p.m. kickoff and will be televised on ESPN. The Seminoles and Panthers have split their most recent four meetings, including Pitt’s 34-31 win in 2025 and Florida State’s 24-7 victory in 2023. The 2026 meeting continues an Atlantic Coast Conference series featuring two major college football programs.
