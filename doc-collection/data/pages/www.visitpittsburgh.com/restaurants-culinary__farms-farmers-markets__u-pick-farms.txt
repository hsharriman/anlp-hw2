10 U-Pick Farms & Orchards
Even city folk can try their hand at farming at a u-pick farm, where you'll get some exercise as well as a great big bunch of fresh produce you pick yourself.
Looking for a u-pick farm to celebrate the fall season with pumpkin picking or apple picking? Here are 10 u-pick farms and orchards to in the Pittsburgh region where you can enjoy fresh produce and family fun.
While fall is the most popular season for picking produce, you can visit many of these farms year-round. We recommend calling ahead of your visit to check seasonal operating hours.
Farms
1. Bedner's Farm and Greenhouse
315 Coleman Road, McDonald, Pa 15057
724.926.2541
Open Apr 1 - Oct 31. Come visit the farm and take a hayride out to our produce fields to pick your own fresh vegetables. The vegetables to be picked each day will depend on crop conditions. Check our website for event dates.
2. Morris Farm
110 Slebodnik Road, Irwin Pa 15342
724.446.3521
Jul-Sep: M, W, F: 6:30pm-dark; Sa: 2-5pm
Certified organic lettuce, potatoes, tomatoes, beets, greens, eggplant, squash, zucchini, melons and more! These crops make for great vegetable picking near Pittsburgh.
3. Sarver Hill Farm
438 Old State Rte. 66, Greensburg Pa 15601
724.834.2334
This certified organic farm provides u-pick strawberries by appointment only (strawberry picking is especially popular in Pittsburgh); also offers a CSA that features beets, peppers, radishes, Chinese greens, melons, pumpkins, eggs and meats and more!
4. Simmons Farm
170 Simmons Road. McMurray, Pa 15317
724.941.1490
May-Oct: M-F: 9am-7pm; Sa: 9am-6pm; Su: 9am-5pm
Strawberries, peaches and apples seasonally, as well as cut-ur-own flowers. Best to call to verify the availability of picking at the farm. Baked goods and other specialties at market house.
5. Trax Farms
528 Trax Rd., Finleyville, Pa 15332
412.835.3246
M-Sa: 9am-8pm; Su, 9am-6pm
Call ahead for specific times to pick-your-own strawberries and blueberries throughout the growing season, from May through August, as times vary.
6. Triple B Farms
823 Berry Ln., Monogahela, Pa 15063
724.258.3557
Summer Hours: M-Fri: 9am-6pm; Sa&Su:9am-5pm
Fall Hours: M&T: 10am-6pm; W-Fri: 10am-7pm; Sa&Su: 10am-6pm
Strawberry and pumpkin picking (call to see what's in season); baked goods, candles, gifts; also farm animals for viewing and a playground.
Go pumpkin picking this fall with your family!
7. Half Crown Hill Orchard
600 N. Branch Road, McDonald, PA 15057
412.913.4981
Open weekends/Tuesdays/Thursdays & private outings during apple harvest season. There are a variety of delicious options to go home with after you're done apple picking in Pittsburgh. You might never need to see a doctor again.
8. Norman's Orchard
2318 Butler Logan Road, Tarentum, PA 15084
724.224.9491
June-Oct: T-Sa: 10am-6pm; Su: 12-5pm (or by appointment)
Featuring heirloom apples, cherries, berries and more! The harvest is waiting for you at this orchard, where the Pittsburgh pickings are ripe.
9. Soergel Orchards
2573 Brandt School Road, Wexford, Pa 15090
724.935.1743
Summer Hours (3/14 - 12/26): M-Sa: 9am-8pm; Su: 9am-6pm.
Winter Hours (12/27 - 3/13): M-Su: 10am-6pm
Call ahead for pick-your-own strawberries and blueberries; specialty is apple orchard, including Gala, Rome and Jonathan apples. Year-round market house has local meats and other goods, as well as bakery, wine shop, ice cream and more.
10. Shenot Farm & Market
3754 Wexford Run Rd, Wexford, PA 15090
724.935.2542
10am-5pm daily
Check their Facebook page for weekend pick-your-own apples, blueberries, flowers and pumpkins throughout the year. This sustainable farm and market also specializes in locally raised meats, cheese, dry goods and homemade fudge.
