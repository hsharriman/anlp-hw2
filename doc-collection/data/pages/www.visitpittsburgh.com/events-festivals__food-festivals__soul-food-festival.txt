Soul Food Festival
Savor a soulful taste of the Burgh at Pittsburgh’s can’t miss Soul Food Festival!
September 4-6, 2026
Credit |
@tomjr7
Event Details
Entertainment Schedule
Food Vendors
FAQ
Spice up your Labor Day weekend at Pittsburgh's Soul Food Festival – a mouthwatering celebration of the city's Black culinary, butchery, and food businesses.
Established in 2019, this festival honors Pittsburgh's food-service history, dating back to 1795, when Charles Richards opened the city's first Black-owned restaurant and lodge. Spend the weekend feasting on soul food dishes from more than 100 small business vendors and food service proprietors, while enjoying live entertainment and engaging activities.

Frequently Asked Questions
When is the Pittsburgh Soul Food Festival?
The 2026 Pittsburgh Soul Food Festival will be held on Labor Day weekend, September 4-6, 2026.
What activities will there be at the Pittsburgh Soul Food Festival?
In addition to food and live music, festival attendees can enjoy a BBQ contest, cultural activities, and craft vendors.
How much are tickets to the festival?
Admission to the Pittsburgh Soul Food Festival is free!
Can I bring a chair to watch the live entertainment?
Yes, attendees can bring lawn chairs to watch the music performances.
Where can I find a festival map?
An event site map can be found on the Soulful Taste of the Burgh Facebook page.

Maximize your Soul Food Festival experience by exploring everything Downtown Pittsburgh has to offer. When planning your visit, consider booking your stay at one of the many hotels near Market Square to be steps away from the festivities.
