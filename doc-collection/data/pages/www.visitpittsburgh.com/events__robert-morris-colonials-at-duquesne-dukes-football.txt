Robert Morris Colonials at Duquesne Dukes Football brings two Pittsburgh-area NCAA Division I FCS programs together for a spirited regional matchup. The Duquesne Dukes host the Robert Morris Colonials at Arthur J. Rooney Athletic Field, home of Duquesne football and a distinctive urban collegiate sports setting. Duquesne leads the all-time series 15-8, while Robert Morris won the most recent meeting in 2025, adding extra edge to this annual rivalry. Fans can expect an energetic game-day atmosphere, competitive Northeast Conference football, and a showcase of local collegiate talent.
