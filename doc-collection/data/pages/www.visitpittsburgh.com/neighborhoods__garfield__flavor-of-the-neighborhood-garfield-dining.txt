Flavor of the Neighborhood: Garfield Dining Guide
Innovation & Global Flavors on Penn
The dining scene in Garfield is a reflection of the neighborhood itself: creative, independent, boundary-pushing, and deeply community-oriented. Centered along the Penn Avenue corridor, Garfield has evolved into one of Pittsburgh’s most exciting culinary destinations.
From James Beard-nominated vegan menus and globally inspired neighborhood staples to hyper-local pizza shops and bustling craft breweries, dining in Garfield offers a progressive culinary adventure driven by passionate chefs and independent makers.

Culinary Trailblazers & Creative Concepts
Garfield’s signature restaurants are known for pushing the boundaries of traditional cuisine, making them destination dining spots for food lovers across the region.
Apteka | 4606 Penn Ave.
This James Beard-nominated restaurant is a powerhouse of Eastern European vegan cuisine and one of the city's most celebrated dining experiences. Even the most dedicated meat-eaters rave about their perfectly crisped pierogies, house-cured pickles, and vegetable-forward small plates. The space offers a chic, dimly lit vibe complemented by an exceptional, inventive natural wine and cocktail program.
Fish Nor Fowl | 5523 Penn Ave.
An upscale, contemporary culinary concept by the renowned Richard DeShantz Restaurant Group. This stylish, bi-level space offers a refined, sophisticated dining experience that reimagines modern Italian flavors. Featuring an evolving menu focused on pristine handmade pastas, artisanal small plates, and expertly crafted cocktails, it is the perfect spot for a date night or a celebratory evening out.
Neighborhood Pillars & Global Eats
These long-standing and beloved local staples deliver rich, authentic flavors from around the world and serve as essential anchors for the Penn Avenue community.
People’s Indian Restaurant | 5147 Penn Ave.
A neighborhood institution for over three decades, People's is beloved for its warm hospitality and consistently exceptional Northern Indian cuisine. Known across the city for its incredible, varied lunch buffet and a deep menu of rich curries, fragrant biryani, and fresh naan, it remains a family-owned pillar of Garfield's culinary landscape.
Spak Brothers | 5107 Penn Ave.
The ultimate neighborhood pizza and hoagie shop, Spak Brothers is a legendary cornerstone of the local community. They are famously inclusive, offering an extensive menu that caters perfectly to both meat-eaters and vegans alike. Their seitan wings, vegan Pittsburgh-style steaks, and customizable world-class slices have earned them a massive cult following.
Tram’s Kitchen | 4050 Penn Ave.
A legendary, no-frills neighborhood staple famous for delivering some of the most comforting and flavorful Vietnamese food in the city. Longtime regulars pack the tight dining room for their deeply aromatic, rich bowls of pho, crisp spring rolls, and fragrant coconut milk vermicelli dishes. (Pro tip: Be sure to bring cash!)
Local Breweries & Craft Pints
Garfield's drinking scene is closely tied to its neighborhood social fabric, offering spaces where exceptional local beverages meet community gathering.
Two Frays Brewery | 5121 Penn Ave.
Far more than just a taproom, Two Frays is a vital community hub where neighbors catch up over a fresh pint. Known for their balanced, approachable craft beers like "Jen's Porter" and a rotating selection of crisp IPAs, they provide a relaxed environment that features a regular schedule of local food trucks parked right outside.
Trace Brewing | 4312 Main St.
Situated right on the border where Garfield meets the surrounding neighborhoods, Trace is a dynamic all-day destination. By day, it functions as a high-end specialty coffee bar; by night, it transforms into a vibrant brewery and taproom. Their massive, string-lit outdoor courtyard is a neighborhood favorite, regularly hosting community events, local pop-up kitchens, and popular drag brunches.
Planning Tip: The Penn Avenue Progress
Because many of Garfield's signature spots feature specialized or limited weekly hours, planning a weekend dinner or a First Friday gallery crawl visit is highly recommended. To get a complete taste of the neighborhood, consider a "progressive" evening out: grab a craft cocktail or natural wine at Apteka, share a specialty pizza or vegan wings at Spak Brothers, and finish your night with a pint in the courtyard at Trace Brewing.
