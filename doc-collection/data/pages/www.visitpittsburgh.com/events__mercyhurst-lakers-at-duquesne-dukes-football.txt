Mercyhurst Lakers at Duquesne Dukes Football brings two Northeast Conference rivals together for a Division I FCS matchup in Pittsburgh. The Duquesne Dukes host the Mercyhurst Lakers at Arthur J. Rooney Athletic Field, creating an intimate college-football setting on the Duquesne campus. The game is part of Duquesne’s 2026 home schedule and follows a 2025 meeting in which the Dukes defeated the Lakers, 37–0. Fans can expect a spirited regional contest featuring conference competition, student-athlete talent, and traditional Saturday football atmosphere.
