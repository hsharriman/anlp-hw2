Discover Your Persona
November 13, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
A fiddler turned songwriter, Maya de Vitry brings a roots-minded touch to songs shaped by her supple vocals and candid storytelling. Before launching her solo career, she spent eight years in Lancaster trio The Stray Birds, recording four full-length albums. Her solo catalog includes Adaptations, Violet Light and The Only Moment; her 2026 album All My Faith adds new songs including “Bachelorette Lessons” and “How Bad I Wanna Live.”
