November 15, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
The Menzingers are a punk rock band known for emotionally driven songwriting, anthemic choruses, and a sound that blends punk energy with heartland rock and Americana influences. Their music often explores nostalgia, aging, friendship, heartbreak, and everyday struggles through relatable storytelling and melodic guitar-driven arrangements. Known for passionate live performances and singalong-ready songs, the band has built a devoted following within the punk and alternative rock communities. With a balance of raw emotion and high-energy musicianship, The Menzingers have become one of the defining bands in modern punk rock.
Hot Water Music is a post-hardcore and punk band recognized for gritty vocals, emotionally charged songwriting, and a melodic yet aggressive sound rooted in punk rock tradition. Their music combines driving rhythms, dual vocal harmonies, and themes of perseverance, resilience, and personal reflection that have resonated with fans for decades. Known for powerful live performances and a strong underground legacy, Hot Water Music remains one of the most influential bands in melodic punk and post-hardcore music.
Weakened Friends is an alternative rock trio known for fuzzy guitar tones, sharp songwriting, and a blend of indie rock, punk, and grunge-inspired energy. Their music balances catchy melodies with emotionally honest lyrics and explosive instrumentation that creates a raw and energetic live-show atmosphere. With a sound rooted in modern indie rock while embracing punk attitude and emotional vulnerability, Weakened Friends continues to gain attention as a rising force in alternative music.
