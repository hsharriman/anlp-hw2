November 7, 2026 2:00 PM –4:00 PM
Get ready for a magical evening at the Byham Theater as ""Matilda - The Musical - Pittsburgh"" takes the stage! This beloved tale of a brilliant young girl with a vivid imagination and a knack for mischief will captivate audiences of all ages. The Byham Theater, with its charming atmosphere and stunning architecture, provides the perfect backdrop for this enchanting performance, ensuring that every moment is filled with laughter, music, and inspiration.
Join us for a delightful journey through Roald Dahl's classic story, brought to life by a talented cast and crew. Whether you're a lifelong fan of the book or discovering Matilda's adventures for the first time, this musical promises to be a heartwarming experience. Don’t miss your chance to witness the magic—book your tickets now for ""Matilda - The Musical - Pittsburgh Byham Theater"" and prepare for an unforgettable night!
