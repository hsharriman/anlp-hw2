2026 Pittsburgh Tattoo Expo
Are you looking for your next tattoo or piercing? Artists from all over the country are in Pittsburgh for a weekend of inspiring ink.
Calling all tattoo artists and enthusiasts: the Pittsburgh Tattoo Expo. is back and better than ever.
From March 13-25, 250 artists will line the Wyndham Grand Hotel in Downtown Pittsburgh for a weekend of live tattooing, body piercing and amazing entertainment. It's a packed few days, so here's what you need to know before you go.

Want to get a tattoo or piercing?
Hundreds of artists will be at the Pittsburgh Tattoo Expo. for live tattooing, which you'll see as you walk around the booths. You might be tempted to get a tattoo yourself while you're there, but it might not be that simple.
If you want to get a tattoo or piercing at the Pittsburgh Tattoo Expo., you should see which artists are scheduled to be there and get in contact with them to get on their books before you go. You could luck out and ask an artist while you're walking around (which happened to me a few years back, but I was the very last person to get tattooed for the weekend), but chances are everyone is booked out. If that happens but you really love one of the artists and their style, ask them about getting an appointment outside of the expo.
What to do while waiting for your tattoo
The expo's location near Point State Park is ideal for killing time while waiting to get your next tattoo or piercing. Take a stroll down toward where the three rivers meet and get a breathtaking view of Mt. Washington and Acrisure Stadium.
You shouldn't get a tattoo on an empty stomach, so head to Market Square to get a bite from a place like Primanti Brother's, City Works or Las Velas. With it being St. Patrick's Day weekend, why not stick around for our parade, which is one of the largest in the country, or our plethora of Irish bars and pubs around the city?
The expo is also offering plenty of live shows through the weekend. Click here for the full schedule.
