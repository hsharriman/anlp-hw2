The Oaklander Hotel
Get the Pittsburgh experience at The Oaklander Hotel.
Exploring Pittsburgh's Oakland neighborhood is a must on any trip to the city. Whether wandering the gardens at nationally acclaimed Phipps Conservatory, catching an indie rock show at Carnegie Music Hall, or grabbing a late-night bite at the famed Primanti Brothers restaurant, it’s easy to soak it all in and experience the neighborhood’s deep-rooted history and diversity.
Even better, you can now immerse yourself in the historic charm and new-age vibe of one of Pittsburgh's most industrious neighborhoods with a stay at The Oaklander Hotel.
The Vibe
Set high above Fifth Avenue, The Oaklander stands lookout where the neighborhood unfolds. Historic elegance and modern style pair with the city’s friendly and relaxed vibe in this luxe boutique hotel located in the heart (and brain) of our Most Livable City. The Oaklander overlooks Fifth Avenue and sits conveniently at the center of the neighborhood’s landmarks, university campuses, event venues, and houses of faith.
The Space
167 guest rooms, five distinct event spaces, and a bold new brasserie on top, The Oaklander offers crisp architecture coupled with the edge of industry past set against the backdrop of the Steel City’s most historic architectural wonders, world-class museums, and iconic institutions of discovery and learning.
The Oaklander’s wedding and event spaces are situated high above the hustle and bustle of Fifth Avenue in a stunning setting, unlike any other in the city. The hotel’s event spaces have quickly become known for their sweeping views, unforgettable details, and impeccable service.
Oakland's unique charm serves as the perfect backdrop for every occasion, from intimate dinner parties in private dining and event rooms to the premier Cathedral Room for larger parties with panoramic views of the Cathedral of Learning and the historic Schenley Farms neighborhood.The Oaklander’s five distinct event spaces set the stage for an experience as iconic as the Pittsburghers for which they are named. Floor-to-ceiling windows, dazzling 360-degree views, and five-star service make every event exceptional from the start.

The Food
Spirits & Tales is the hotel's brasserie, located at the top of the building and available as in-room dining (also available in-room are curated local cocktails in the mini bar). With Chef Jessica Lewis at the helm, the brasserie’s spirit is bold with an emphasis on locally sourced everything, fresh flavors, and reimagined classics. The restaurant boasts an unforgettable backdrop for those drawn to the appeal of a good time and the desire to gather around the table.
