Ultimate Guide to the Pittsburgh Japanese Film Festival
Celebrating classic films and the most recent hits, Pittsburgh Japanese Festival unveils a multifaceted lens across different genres and time periods.
Festival Information
Top Activities
This two-week event showcases Japan’s cinematic universe with one-of-a-kind movies and hidden treasures. Sit on the edge of your seat during one of Pittsburgh’s most popular film festivals. Along with a series of fan-favorite screenings, game shows and tea tastings are some of the delightful activities at the event. Whether you’re looking for new ways to experience the arts, or scheduling a spring getaway, this annual festival offers unforgettable fun on the big screen.
Pittsburgh Japanese Film Festival Information
Dates - March 15 - 28, 2024
Location - Row House Cinema | 4115 Butler Street
Admission
Early bird Week 1 & 2 Pass (March 15 - 21 & 22 - 28,2024): $49 per person
Early bird Superfan Pass: $84 per person
Event goers purchasing their early bird tickets in advance will receive perks such as admission to all movie screenings, a reservation to specific seating, and more. For more ticket information, visit the official website of Pittsburgh Japanese Film Festival.

Top Activities During the Festival
Marvel at Breathtaking Animated Films and Iconic Stories
Step into different worlds and take in the immersive artwork of Japanese animated scenes. Hayao Miyazaki fans and Makoto Shinkai enthusiasts will enjoy the unveiling of masterful works from various anime. Catch the latest and most beloved animated stories from Japan, viewing works that are on par with movies such as Weathering with You and Your Name. Delve into Japanese culture with cult classics and nostalgic films, whether you’re a Godzilla fanatic or an avid watcher of Kurosawa-directed scenes.
Grab an Authentic Bite Before Showtime
Discover Japanese cuisine in Pittsburgh during the festival, enjoying scrumptious bites of ramen, sushi, and more. Nestled in Lawrenceville, Row House Cinema is located near an array of mouthwatering eats. Take a seven-minute stroll to Umami and enjoy mouthwatering spoonfuls of ramen in an upscale setting. Oishii Donburi is just a four-minute drive and features sensational dishes, where katsu and tempura are fried to perfection.

Admire Japanese Art Prints After the Movie
Looking for more ways to explore Japanese culture? Carnegie Museum of Art is a short 12-minute drive from Row House Cinema and features the fascinating exhibit, “Imprinting in Their Time: Japanese Printmakers, 1912 - 2022.” Wander through an array of modern artworks that reveal striking lanscapes. Including pieces from legendary artists such as Kawase Hasui and Yoshida Hiroshi, this collection of art encompasses various perspectives on Japanese culture and traditions.
