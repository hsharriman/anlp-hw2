Things to Look Forward to in 2024
There's a lot to be excited about in Pittsburgh in 2024!
Here's a few highlights
There are many adventures to be had in Pittsburgh throughout the year, and 2024 is already stock full of exciting things to look forward to. From new thrills and museums to the return of some of the city's favorite events, the 'Burgh is full of fun for everyone. Be sure to mark your calendar with this list of things to see and do in 2024.
Bracket Busting Starts Here
Experience March Madness® in the City of Champions March 21 & 23, 2024 at PPG Paints Arena! Duquesne University hosts the First and Second Rounds of the NCAA® Division I Men’s Basketball Championship right here in Pittsburgh.
Now Boarding: Iceland to Pittsburgh
Icelandair will offer European, UK and Scandinavian passengers easier routes than ever to spend time in Pittsburgh thanks to a new nonstop route connecting our city with Keflavik International Airport outside Reykjavik, Iceland. Four flights a week will arrive and depart Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Sunday starting May 2024.
Craft Beer Continues Its Dominance
Craft breweries continue to pop up in neighborhoods throughout Pittsburgh, but what will set 2024 apart is how many of our beloved brews are getting second, third and even fourth homes at expanded locations. Here's what's on tap so far:
Burgh'ers Brewing - Opening a third location in the South Side and a sprawling 13,000-square-foot fourth location in Millvale.
Necromancer Brewing Co. - Second location named The Midnight Whistler coming to Greenfield with a signature cask ale bar and elevated bites.
Allegheny City Brewing - Expanding into a standalone taproom and indoor/outdoor beer garden in Deutschtown/NorthSide.
Trace - Second location named Trace Echo in Uptown including outdoor space and a collab, Ghost Coffee Collab espresso bar and food.

50 Years of Potato Patch Fries
Don't spill your cheese sauce! Kennywood is celebrating their iconic Potato Patch french fries and their bumper car ride by transforming the latter with a total rebrand as drivers sit in fry boats powered by forks as they try to knock the toppings off of opposing drivers. It doesn't get more Pittsburgh than this.

A post shared by Kennywood (@kennywoodpark)
PiratesFest Returns (And 1 More Year for Cutch)
Always one of my favorite events of the year, PiratesFest returns Jan. 6, 2024 at the David L. Lawrence Convention Center with exclusive autograph sessions featuring current and former players, fun photo ops, lots of activities and your first crack at the team store for the season. Plus, the Pirates have signed Andrew McCutchen for another year giving us plenty of reasons to celebrate.

Barrel & Flow Fest
Named the best beer festival in the ENTIRE COUNTY in 2023, Barrel & Flow Fest returns to The Stacks at 3 Crossings Aug. 10, 2024! The Burgh will be celebrating Black-owned breweries with collaborations with Black artists from across the country along with great energy from live entertainment and art demonstrations. Tickets to this can't-miss event go on sale Feb. 1, 2024.
