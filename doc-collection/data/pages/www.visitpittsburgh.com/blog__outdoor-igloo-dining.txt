Where to Find the Best Igloo and Outdoor Dining in Pittsburgh
Looking for a spot to enjoy some of Pittsburgh’s best dining options on a chilly evening? Here’s a list of the top cold-weather outdoor patios and igloo dining options in Pittsburgh.
Ready to brave the cold? Winter lovers, this one’s for you!
Credit |
@jojo_in_pittsburgh
Igloo Dining
Heated Patios
Igloo Dining in Pittsburgh
Spirit & Tales
Address: 5130 Bigelow Blvd, Pittsburgh
Guests can savor the chill inside their own private winter igloo at Spirits & Tales, the 10th-floor restaurant at The Oaklander Hotel. This winter dining experience offers the perfect setting to enjoy drinks, share appetizers, and take in the stunning city views, all from the comfort of a cozy, heated igloo. The igloo experience is available November 14, 2025 - March 1, 2026. Make your reservation today!
Bridges and Bourbon
Address: 930 Penn Ave, Pittsburgh
From November to April, you can enjoy an intimate meal in a Bridgehouse. Enjoy a meal and drinks in this cozy, dimly lit igloo-like structure for a unique, intimate date-night experience. Reservations are required and can be made by calling 412-586-4287.
The Wine Cave
Address: 3141 Harts Run Road, Allison Park
Dine in an igloo outside of the city at the Wine Cave. From November to March, you can dine in their heated igloos, surrounded by twinkling lights and scenic views. Igloos can accommodate up to 8 people, making this an excellent option for families or small groups. Make a reservation here.

Bella Terra Vineyards
Address: 121 Sunny Ln, Hunker
About a 40-minute drive out of the city, you’ll find the gorgeous Bella Terra Vineyards. Visitors can take full advantage of their lovely grounds in their festive igloos from November 10, 2025, to March 31, 2026. With options for parties of up to 8 in their standard igloos or up to 16 in their party dome, it’s worth the drive for a fun holiday gathering with friends or family.
Heated Patios in Pittsburgh
Burgatory
Address: 342 North Shore Dr, Pittsburgh
You’ll need a warm space to enjoy a cold milkshake this winter. Take a seat on Burgatory’s heated, enclosed patio and enjoy an indulgent shake and hamburger made custom just for you.
The Porch at Schenley
Address: 221 Schenley Dr., Pittsburgh
Enjoy the enclosed patio at The Porch and take in views of Schenley Plaza while savoring seasonal flavors like a savory braised pork shank, along with their classic year-round menu items like pizza and salads.
Pizzeria Davide
Address: 2551 Penn Avenue, Pittsburgh
A favorite for locals all year-round, Pizzeria Davide is a great spot to warm up over a slice (and next to a heater!) on an outdoor patio this winter. We also recommend trying one of their hot sandwiches.
Apteka
Address: 4606 Penn Ave., Pittsburgh
Apteka is a delicious Eastern European restaurant that serves an entirely vegan menu. Even carnivores will be pleasantly surprised at the variety of entrees available and just how tasty everything is. Enjoy a meal on their outdoor, heated patio, available for walk-ins even on cold, wintery days.
La Palalpa
Address: 2224 E Carson St
Enjoy the warmth of an enclosed heated patio over a fresh bowl of salsa, sopa de tortilla, birria tacos, and tamal de elote. La Palapa is a great spot to catch some warmth this winter, whether just for a meal or for a special occasion.
