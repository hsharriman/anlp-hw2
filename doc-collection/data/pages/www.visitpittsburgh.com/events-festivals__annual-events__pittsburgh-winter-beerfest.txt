Pittsburgh Winter Beerfest
Experience the annual Pittsburgh Winter Beerfest with our guide on the official dates, craft brew-filled activities, and more.
February 21, 2026
Dates & Location
Admission
Featured Experiences
Calling all beer lovers! Every year, the craft beer community comes together and celebrates the city's thriving brewery scene at the Pittsburgh Winter Beerfest. Sample over hundreds of local beers and other libations at this can’t-miss event, where expertly brewed IPAs, ales, stouts, and lagers await. Treat your tastebuds to bubbly glimpses of Pittsburgh’s thriving beer scene from local breweries and distilleries.

Know before you go: Event experiences will vary depending on your choice of tickets. General admission includes your selection of an event session, a 5oz beer mug, and sampling of more than 200 drinks. Purchase tickets on the official website of the Pittsburgh Beerfest here.
Featured Experiences
Local Beer Tastings - Sip on a variety of refreshing beers from local and regional breweries. Whether you’re an IPA enthusiast or a porter aficionado, there is no shortage of magnificent beers at this event. Explore the complete list of beers here.
Live Entertainment - Groove to live music at the Pittsburgh Winter Beerfest, enjoying upbeat tunes and talented performers! There will also be plenty of dancing at the silent disco.
Unforgettable Activities - Participate in exciting games in between beer tastings, including the Beerfest Claw Machine!

Where to Stay
After an action-packed day of beer tasting, why not unwind in a comfortable accommodation? Check into a top-tier stay in downtown Pittsburgh, relaxing in luxurious furnishings at budget-friendly prices. Enjoy close proximity to iconic attractions, flexible services, and modern amenities.
Things to Do Before & After the Pittsburgh Winter Beerfest
Expand your beer tasting experience with one-of-a-kind activities around Pittsburgh. Elevate your palate to new flavorsome heights by exploring exquisite culinary establishments in the downtown area, whether you’re craving fried chicken sandwiches or slices of wood-fired pizza. On February 22, don’t miss watching a thrilling game of hockey between the Pittsburgh Penguins and Washington Capitals at the PPG Paints Arena. Finally, plan a night of axe throwing at places like 2420 Penn, Full Throttle Adrenaline Park, and Paradise Island Bowl & Beach.
