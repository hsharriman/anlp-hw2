Thanksgiving Dinner in Pittsburgh 2026: Restaurants Open & Meal Options
Turkey, mashed potatoes, and zero dishes to wash? Now that’s something to be thankful for. This year, plenty of restaurants are open on Thanksgiving in Pittsburgh and they’re serving up festive feasts you won’t want to miss.
From hearty buffets to cozy sit-down meals and easy take-home options, you’ll find the perfect Thanksgiving dinner in Pittsburgh waiting for you and the best part: no cooking required! Bring your appetite and let the ‘Burgh do the holiday cooking for you!
Note: Restaurant availability, hours and menus are subject to change. Please check with individual businesses for the most current schedules and offerings.
Restaurants Open on Thanksgiving in Pittsburgh
Skip the kitchen chaos! These Pittsburgh restaurants open on Thanksgiving are ready to serve up a full plate of holiday comfort. Whether you’re in the mood for a classic turkey spread, a fancy feast with a view, or a laid-back bite that still feels special, the ‘Burgh’s got your Thanksgiving dinner covered.
Grand Concourse
If there’s ever a place that feels like Thanksgiving, it’s the Grand Concourse. Set inside the beautifully restored Pittsburgh & Lake Erie Railroad Station, this South Shore favorite hosts a legendary Thanksgiving buffet each year turkey, trimmings, and all the cozy vibes that make you forget you didn’t cook a thing.
The Capital Grille
Known for its white-tablecloth experience, The Capital Grille delivers a Thanksgiving meal worth dressing up for. Their special holiday menu typically includes slow-roasted turkey, brioche stuffing, and housemade cranberry relish. Reservations are a must here! It’s a local favorite for stress-free holiday dining.
LeMont Restaurant
Head up Mount Washington for a Thanksgiving with a view. LeMont pairs its elegant holiday buffet with sweeping skyline views that make the mashed potatoes taste even better. Dress up, bring the family, and toast to another beautiful Pittsburgh Thanksgiving. And don’t skip the Viennese Table. It’s dessert heaven with mini pastries, tortes, cakes, bananas Foster, fruit, and plenty of pumpkin and apple pie.

fl.2 at Fairmont Pittsburgh
If you’re craving something classy but still cozy, fl.2 delivers. Their Thanksgiving menu is served à la carte and includes favorites like slow-roasted turkey with brown butter mashed potatoes, green bean casserole, and citrus-herb jus. For couples or small groups, the Thanksgiving Plate for Two ($89) is the perfect shareable feast.
And if you’d rather celebrate at home, fl.2 also offers “Thanksgiving To Go”, with boxed and ready-to-reheat meals prepared by their award-winning culinary team.
The Commoner
The Commoner has become a staple gathering spot for locals and travelers alike for holiday cheer and returns this Thanksgiving with a special holiday buffet with seatings between 1pm through 7pm on Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, November 27, 2025. Reservations can be made through OpenTable.
Prepare for a buffet menu highlighting holiday classics with The Commoner's signature twists, including Truffled Prime Rib, Roasted Garlic and Herb Organic Turkey, Brioche Stuffing, Maple Glazed Sweet Potatoes, and more.

Mitchell’s Fish Market
Another go-to for Thanksgiving dining, Mitchell’s offers a prix-fixe traditional turkey dinner with soup or salad, roasted turkey breast, gravy, stuffing, and a choice of mashed or sweet potatoes. Dessert options include pumpkin pie, apple pie, crème brûlée, or turtle cheesecake.
You can also order a full Thanksgiving dinner to-go, complete with turkey, sides, salad, bread, and pie. Reservations and pre-orders are highly recommended. Find more info here.
Benihana Pittsburgh
Looking for a Thanksgiving that’s a little…different? Benihana stays open on the holiday, offering its signature teppanyaki-style dining where chefs slice, dice, and grill your meal right at the table. It’s a fun, social way to celebrate the day (and the kids love it). You can also earn rewards through their loyalty program for future visits. Reserve your table here.

Salem’s Market & Grill
For those looking for Halal options, Salem’s Market offers fresh and frozen turkeys, including locally raised Amish selections. Their meats are hand-cut and always high-quality, making them a favorite for Pittsburgh’s diverse Thanksgiving tables.

Black Radish Kitchen
Black Radish brings big flavor to side dishes. Their Thanksgiving Sides Package covers the entire spread: mashed potatoes, green bean casserole, bleu cheese bread pudding, cranberry-orange relish, and gorgeous salads. A dream for anyone who wants to cook…nothing. Explore the menu here.
Bravo Italian Kitchen
For those who want a little Italian flair with their turkey, Bravo typically welcomes Thanksgiving diners with a mix of traditional fare and favorites from the regular menu. You’ll find roast turkey and stuffing alongside pasta dishes and hearty Italian sides. Find more info here.
Pane è Pronto
Known for its Italian-inspired Thanksgiving meals, Pane è Pronto offers a full feast that serves two or four. Expect thick-sliced porchetta, cacio e pepe mashed potatoes, sausage-stuffed dressing, cauliflower casserole, and Italian cookies for dessert. It’s the perfect blend of comfort and flavor… Pittsburgh style. Find details here.
Cooked Goose Catering
Hosting a crowd? Cooked Goose Catering can handle that. Their holiday catering packages include whole or sliced turkey with all the fixings. It’s perfect for big family gatherings or office parties. Order online here.
Mediterra Café
Mediterra Café keeps things simple and delicious with family-style side dishes for pickup, including candied yams and roasted Brussels sprouts. Orders are available at their Lawrenceville, Mt. Lebanon, and Sewickley locations. Check out the catering menu here.
Tips for a Smooth Thanksgiving in the ‘Burgh
Reserve early. Prime times fill fast, especially at buffets and skyline-view spots. OpenTable’s Thanksgiving page is a good pulse-check for who’s taking reservations on Nov 27, 2025. OpenTable
Confirm menus and hours. Some places serve holiday prix fixe only. Others offer the full menu plus turkey. Websites and Instagram updates are your friend.
Order deadlines matter. To-go packages often close preorders the week before. Watch each vendor’s pickup window and reheating instructions.
Have a backup plan. If your first choice sells out, scan resort buffets or steakhouse holiday menus, which often keep extra capacity
Whether you want a white-tablecloth feast, a mountain-view buffet, or a heat-and-serve dinner that makes your kitchen smell amazing, Thanksgiving 2025 dinner options are looking delicious. If you find a new spot taking reservations or offering a great to-go package, drop us a note so we can add it for fellow ‘Burgh food fans.
