Outdoor Adventure & Skyline Views
For the trail walker, kayaker, skyline photographer, and fresh-air enthusiast.
Three rivers, dramatic hillsides, and expansive green spaces make Pittsburgh an urban outdoor haven. Kayak past iconic downtown bridges, pedal miles of riverfront trails, or ride historic inclines up to breathtaking cliffside vistas. Perfect for travelers who love to explore on foot or on two wheels.
