New in the 412 in 2025: New Businesses, Events, and More in Pittsburgh
Everything new in one place
Pittsburgh is constantly changing and evolving, a tradition that started when our steel mills turned to technology hubs and beyond. We welcome the new while celebrating where we've come from and looking forward to where we go from here.
Here's everything new that came or is coming to Pittsburgh this year:
FOOD & DRINK
Whisper - Opened March 2025
Wine and dessert bar in Downtown Pittsburgh
Dad's Dog & Burger - Opened April 2025
Classic diner food offering hot dogs, burgers, fries, onion rings and more in Bloomfield
AVVA - Opened May 2025
Modern Mediterranean cuisine in Aspinwall
Capozoli's Trattoria Ristorante
Italian restaurant in the South Side
Riverlife's Shore Thing - Opened July 2025
Floating platform on Pittsburgh riverfront off of the North Side
Hey Babe - Opened July 2025
Craft cocktail-driven bar in East Liberty
Palm Palm - Opened July 2025
Restaurant with California, South American-inspired menu in Shadyside
The Finch Distillery - Opened July 2025
Rooftop bar and restaurant between Station Square and the South Side Flats
Tapville Social - Opened August 2025
Located in the heart of the Strip District, Tapville Social is known for its tech-forward, guest-focused dining experience, signature self-pour tap wall, handcrafted cocktail flights, and modern American cuisine.
Spot of Coffee - Opened August 2025
This new coffee shop in East Liberty is hidden in an alleyway behind Kelly's Bar and Paris 66.
Grist House Command - Opened September 2025
What do you get when you mix a brewery with a Cold War-era building? The answer is Grist House Command, located in a former Nike Missile Command Center in Collier Township.
Poulet Bleu - Opened October 2025
French restaurant focused on bringing the essence of Paris to Pittsburgh's Lawrenceville neighborhood
Camino Cocteleria and Cocina - Opened October 2025
Located in Bloomfield, this restaurant is modern Mexican with street soul with a laidback vibe.
F&F Pizzeria - Opened October 2025
This Brooklyn pizza spot brings its New York flavor to Mount Lebanon.
Foxhole Tavern - Opened November 2025
This bar, right in the heart of Aspinwall, features a cozy interior and some of the best steak you'll ever have.
Creative Chem Co. Coffee & Coworking - Opened December 2025
This former chemical plant in Bloomfield is now one part coffee shop, one part coworking space, and one part maker space.
