October 25, 2026 to November 1, 2026
Play All Day. Fright All Night.
Get into the Halloween spirit at Kennywood’s Phantom Fall Fest, where family-friendly fall fun meets nighttime thrills and scares. Voted the #4 Best Theme Park Halloween Event in the country by USA TODAY 10BEST, Phantom Fall Fest offers something for everyone on select weekends through November 1.
During the day, families can enjoy 30+ rides, seasonal entertainment, trick-or-treating, fall food and the new Fall Fest Tasting Experience, featuring seasonal dishes, beer and wine samplings. As the sun goes down, Kennywood transforms for Fright All Night with seven haunted attractions and four scare zones—all included with regular park admission.
Whether you're looking for a family-friendly Halloween experience, a night of haunted attractions with friends, or a classic fall day at one of Pittsburgh's most iconic amusement parks, Phantom Fall Fest brings it all together in one unforgettable Halloween tradition.
Come for the fall fun. Stay for the scares.
