October 28, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Radiator Hospital is the Philadelphia indie-rock project led by singer-songwriter Sam Cook-Parrott. The project is recognized for emotionally direct lyrics, catchy power-pop melodies and a lo-fi, home-recorded sensibility. Its notable releases include Something Wild, Torch Song and the sixth album, Distorting Time. Standout songs include "Our Song" and "Cut Your Bangs," which capture Cook-Parrott’s blend of anxious, heartfelt songwriting and energetic guitar-driven arrangements.
