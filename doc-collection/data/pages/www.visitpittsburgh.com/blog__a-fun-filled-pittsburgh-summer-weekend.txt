A Fun-Filled Pittsburgh Summer Weekend
If you're planning a summer getaway, July 16-19 is the perfect weekend to visit Pittsburgh. From the nation's only vintage street race to the country's top pickle festival and family-friendly sporting events, the city is packed with unforgettable experiences.
Whether you’re here for the food, the racing, the festivals, or just to soak in the summer vibes, this is a weekend you won’t want to miss! There’s something happening in every neighborhood, catering to a variety of interests. Follow along for a weekend itinerary to make the most of your time in the Steel City!

Friday | July 17, 2026
America’s Mile & North Shore Eats
To start the weekend off, join us for America’s Mile. This race-day celebration creates lasting memories for everyone while honoring the incredible legacy of our country. The event features a variety of races tailored to participants of all ages, abilities, and needs. From the Move Mile for participants of all paces and abilities, to the Elite Mile featuring professional runners, there is something for everyone. This event will begin at P3R’s headquarters and finish on West General Robinson Street. After the race, keep the celebration going at Mike’s Beer Bar for the official post-race party!
Don't just watch the fun, be a part of it! Register for America’s Mile today, score 25% off with code PGH25, and we'll see you at the starting line (and the post-race party)!
If you’re exploring the North Shore, stop by North Shore Tavern for casual bar bites, Fig & Ash for elevated modern American dishes, or Burgatory for their famous burgers, shakes, and cocktails. These spots are some of Pittsburgh’s best, offering a wide range of flavors and all just a short walk from the end of the race.
Saturday | July 18, 2026
A Pittsburgh Pair: Picklesburgh & Riverhounds Soccer Game
Named USA Today’s #3 Specialty Food Festival in the country, Picklesburgh proves that pickles deserve four days of praise! Picklesburgh is a must for those who indulge in the true culinary masterpiece: the pickle.
Celebrated from July 16-19, this four-day event takes over Downtown Pittsburgh and the Sister Bridges: the Roberto Clemente Bridge and the Andy Warhol Bridge. Sample a variety of pickle-inspired foods and drinks, from pickle cocktails and pickle pizza to fried pickles and other pickle-themed treats. Browse dozens of pickle vendors, enjoy themed activities, and even watch (or join!) a pickle juice drinking contest.
While pickles are the highlight of the event, it’s no big dill if you aren’t a pickle fan. The festival also features live music, cooking demonstrations, and other activities tailored for eventgoers of all ages.
Come join us and share your love for pickles in the greatest city!
Picklesburgh takes place on Pittsburgh's iconic Three Sister Bridges.
Are Pickles really not your thing? The Riverhounds take on Louisville City at 12pm. This game features an AMO Bobblehead Giveaway, making it a popular game for those who want a tangible keepsake of Riverhounds memorabilia. The game will take place at F.N.B. stadium.
Prefer horsepower to pitch lines? The legendary Pittsburgh Vintage Grand Prix is already in full swing over at Schenley Park! Saturday features a massive International Car Show with thousands of vehicles on display, qualifying races, and family-friendly festival activities. Check out our ultimate PVGP event guide to view Saturday's full schedule of events.

Sunday | July 19, 2026
Two Global Phenomenon: Vintage Car Finds and FIFA Fan Zone
Cap off an incredible weekend of racing with Sunday's main event at the Pittsburgh Vintage Grand Prix. Celebrating its 44th anniversary, this week-long festival culminates in the official Race Day on Sunday at Schenley Park (July 18-19).
As the nation's largest vintage street race—and the only one staged on public roads—you can catch spectacular qualifying races and international car shows on Saturday, leading up to Sunday's feature races. For a complete hour-by-hour breakdown of both days, look at our comprehensive PVGP event guide.
The Vintage Grand Prix is the nation’s largest vintage street race, and the only one that is staged on public roads.
Enjoy the show and take it all in, browse various tents filled with sponsors and exhibits, including the Peoples Gas Kids tent, and explore the full day of food, drinks, vendors, and activities that put you amidst the action.
After exploring the display of stunning vintage vehicles on Sunday, join fellow Pittsburghers in another global marvel: the FIFA World Cup Watch Party!
Acrisure is also hosting the free FIFA World Cup Fan Zone event taking place on the field and in the stands from 2pm-6pm, with the FIFA World Cup Championship starting at 3pm. This experience will include live music, youth soccer clinics, festival-style foods, activities for the whole family to enjoy, and a thrilling match you won’t want to miss.
City Information & Getting Around
Planning ahead can help you make the most of your weekend. Be aware of road closures associated with festival events and allow extra travel time when moving between destinations.
For transportation and parking information:
⚠️ Read Our Official Pittsburgh July 2026 Construction & Navigation Guide for step-by-step detours around the I-376 Parkway East closure, Picklesburgh, and America's Mile race routes.
Check local road closure updates before heading downtown. Visitors coming to Pittsburgh this July should be aware of planned closures on I-376 near the Squirrel Hill Tunnel, which may affect travel times. For the latest traffic conditions, detours, and closure information, visit 511PA.
Use ParkPGH to view real-time parking availability in Pittsburgh garages.
Consider taking the T Light Rail for convenient access to downtown and nearby event locations.
If driving isn't ideal, consider using rideshare services to avoid parking and congestion.
With multiple events drawing large crowds throughout the city, it's a good idea to arrive early at each destination to allow time for traffic, parking, and security screenings.
SportsPITTSBURGH Supported Events Also Happening this Weekend:
Hoop Group Steel City Jam Fest | David L. Lawrence Convention Center
Hoop Group Pitt Summer Jam Fest is back at the David L. Convention Center for an exciting weekend of basketball action! Over eight age groups will compete, showcasing their skills across 45 courts as college coaches look for their next top recruit.
AC Baseball: 2026 Louisville Slugger Wood Bat W.S. II | Variety of Venues
The 15th annual Slugger Wood Bat World Series is one of the largest wood bat tournaments in the Northeast. With over 90 teams hosted annually, It’s a high‑energy weekend of baseball on Pittsburgh’s turf and grass fields, complete with awards for standout teams and athletes from 13U through Highschool. This tournament is hosted at a variety of venues in Monroeville, Butler, and Washington
AC Baseball: 2026 ACS Showdown in Steel Town | Monroeville & Pittsburgh
The ACS Showdown in Steel Town returns to Pittsburgh July 16–19 for its 9th annual softball showcase, bringing together top 12U–18U teams for a high-level competition. Taking place across the county, it’s a top opportunity for teams and individuals to showcase their skills across the region, with venues located in Monroeville and Pittsburgh
Continental Chess Association | Courtyard Marriott University Center Hotel
2026 CCA Pittsburgh Open: The 27th annual Continental Chess Association Open is coming to Pittsburgh from July 17-19 at the Courtyard Marriott University Center Hotel for a thrilling competition with $13,000 in guaranteed prizes.
