Autumn in Pittsburgh
Forge Your Fall Adventure in the Steel City
There just aren’t words to describe the beauty of fall in Pittsburgh. The season begins with summer temperatures cooling down, then the picturesque fall foliage slowly spreads throughout the rolling hills that make up the city’s landscape. The hues of the skyline will spark your imagination from the second you step foot into the city, setting the scene with the perfect backdrop for exploring our three rivers, rolling hills, and walkable neighborhoods.

What to Pack
The good news is that fall in Pittsburgh is full of colorful trees and warm temperatures, perfect for outdoor exploration. However, because our rivers and evening breezes can bring the cold after dark, dressing smart is the key to a great trip.
When packing, there are a few things you should be sure to grab:
A Jacket is a Must: Despite the warm temperature sticking around, the air will definitely cool down. A jacket, even if it's light, is essential to a trip to Pittsburgh in the autumn.
Comfortable Walking Shoes: Pittsburgh is incredibly walkable, from the North Shore trail to historic neighborhood business districts. Bring durable sneakers or supportive sandals.
Sun Protection is Essential: Don't forget your sunglasses, sunscreen, and a rimmed hat or baseball cap to stay comfortable during afternoon ballgames or river cruises.
Break Out Those Sweaters: We know you've been itching to bundle up in your favorite sweater, so take this as your sign to finally break it out for the season. And while you're at it, get those fuzzy socks out too.
Autumn Weather Overview
Pittsburgh autumns are the perfect mix of warm and cool, which means no matter your weather preference, you'll find it here during your trip.
September: Average High 78°F (25°C) | Average Low 57°F (14°C)
October: Average High 65°F (18°C) | Average Low 45°F (7°C)
November: Average High 53°F (12°C) | Average Low 35°F (2°C)
Things to Do
Whether you want to maximize your time in the waning sunshine or want to cozy up away from the brisk air, a Pittsburgh summer offers the perfect mix of experiences.
Enjoy the Views from the Inclines: Take a ride on the Duquesne Incline for breathtaking panoramic views of the city. Hop on a bike and ride the 24+ miles of riverfront trails and beyond – the Great Allegheny Passage is a must in the fall for biking enthusiasts. Or, book a walking tour with ‘Burgh, Bits & Bites Food Tour or Walk the Burgh Tours to discover more reasons why Pittsburgh is so special.
Explore Pittsburgh's Spooky Scene: Plan a visit before Halloween and explore the spookier side of Pittsburgh with haunted attractions like Zombies of the Corn and Hundred Acres Manor. Families can partake in the Halloween fun, too, with ZooBoo at Pittsburgh Zoo & Aquarium.
Take in the Best of Pittsburgh's Sports: Fall also is a fantastic time of year for sports fans. Join Steelers Nation at Acrisure Stadium for a one-of-a-kind experience cheering on the Pittsburgh Steelers. From fall festivals to food festivals, Three Rivers Beer Week, world-class theater experiences and more, visitors are sure to find an entertainment option that fits their interest.
