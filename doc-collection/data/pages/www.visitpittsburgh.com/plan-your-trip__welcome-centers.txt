Welcome Centers
Visit any one of our VisitPITTSBURGH Welcome Centers in the Greater Pittsburgh region.
To connect with a Visitor Services Representative, and request hours of operation, please call
877-LovePGH (877-568-3744) or email info@visitpittsburgh.com. You can also interact with our friendly AI neighbor to help plan for your upcoming visit.
Welcome & Information Centers
NOW OPEN!
Pittsburgh International Airport
Welcome to the Steel City! Whether you've just touched down or are waiting to greet a loved one, the VisitPITTSBURGH Welcome Center is officially open and ready to kickstart your adventure.
📍Find Us
Our sleek new home is located in the Landside Terminal, conveniently situated right near the Arrivals Tunnel.
🙋How can we help you?
We’ve upgraded the visitor experience to be more intuitive and helpful than ever. Stop by to enjoy:
Personalized Service: Chat with our friendly onsite representatives for local "insider" tips. The Center is staffed at select hours and will change with demand.
Interactive Tech: High-tech touchscreens allow you to browse at your own pace.
The Classics: Grab traditional publications, restaurant guides, and physical city maps.
Comprehensive Info: Get the latest on local attractions, upcoming events, and the best places to stay.
🗺️Beyond the City Limits
For travelers looking to explore the beauty of the Keystone State, we’ve teamed up with VisitPA to offer new electronic kiosks designed to help visitors seamlessly navigate regional destinations, scenic road trips, and hidden gems throughout Pennsylvania.
Visit us today and make the most of your Pittsburgh experience!
Senator John Heinz History Center
Located in the heart of the historic Strip District, our Senator John Heinz History Center location offers visitors a unique opportunity to blend trip planning with a deep dive into the region’s rich heritage. Whether you are looking for a dinner recommendation in the neighborhood or want to understand the roots of the Steel City, our team is here to help.
📍Find Us
Senator John Heinz History Center
1212 Smallman St., Strip District - in the heart of Pittsburgh's historic Strip District
⏰Hours & Availability
Open Daily: 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.
Holiday Closures: New Year’s Day, Easter, Thanksgiving, and Christmas.
🙋How can we help you?
Experience the same high-standard service and resources found at our airport location, set within Pennsylvania’s largest history museum:
Expert Guidance: Our onsite representatives are ready to help you craft the perfect Pittsburgh itinerary.
Modern Discovery: Use our interactive touchscreens to explore current events, hidden gems, and local dining.
Travel Essentials: Pick up traditional publications, maps, and detailed guides to the city’s 90 unique neighborhoods.
A Journey Through Time: Start your visit with a tour of the museum to see how the people and events of the past have shaped the modern Pittsburgh you see today.
🚶‍➡️Explore the Neighborhood
After visiting the Welcome Center, you’re perfectly positioned to explore the Strip District, Pittsburgh’s famous market district filled with local coffee roasters, international grocers, and iconic eateries.
Stop by today to plan your stay while surrounded by the history that made Pittsburgh great!
