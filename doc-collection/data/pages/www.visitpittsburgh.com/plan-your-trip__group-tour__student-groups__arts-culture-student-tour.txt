Arts & Culture Student Tour (3-days)
Day 1: Performing Arts & Theatre
Lincoln Park Performing Arts Center (Beaver County) 🎭 | 10 a.m.-12:30 p.m.
Kick off the tour with a live performance or workshop at the Lincoln Park Performing Arts Center in Midland, PA. This renowned arts institution offers top-tier student productions in theatre, music, and dance in a state-of-the-art theater.
Lunch Stop🍽️ | 1 p.m.-2 p.m.
Enjoy lunch at a local restaurant or picnic at a nearby park.
Little Lake Theatre (Washington County) 🎭 | 3:30 p.m.-5:30 p.m.
Next, visit Little Lake Theatre in Canonsburg, a charming theater-in-the-round
known for its intimate setting and student-friendly productions. Groups can participate in a talk-back session with actors or engage in a behind-the-scenes tour.
Hampton Inn Greensburg 🌙
Travel to Greensburg and check in at Hampton Inn Greensburg, offering comfortable accommodations and a convenient location for the next day's activities.
Day 2: History & Fine Arts
The Palace Theatre (Westmoreland County) 🎭 | 9 a.m.-10:30 a.m.
Start the day with a guided tour or student performance at The Palace Theatre, a beautifully restored 1926 venue in downtown Greensburg. This historic stage has hosted everything from Broadway-style productions to concerts.
Westmoreland Museum of American Art 🎨 | 10:45 a.m.-12:30 p.m.
Explore The Westmoreland Museum of American Art, featuring collections from colonial to contemporary art with a special focus on regional artists. Student-friendly workshops and guided tours are available.
Lunch Break 🍽️ | 12:30 p.m. - 1:30 p.m.
Grab lunch at a nearby café or enjoy a group meal at a local restaurant.
Flight 93 National Memorial (Somerset County) 🕊️ | 3 p.m.-5 p.m.
Pay tribute to history at the Flight 93 National Memorial, a powerful site honoring the passengers and crew of United Flight 93. Students can explore the Visitor Center, Memorial Plaza, and the Tower of Voices, gaining a deeper understanding of this significant event.
Courtyard by Marriott Monroeville 🌙
Return to Allegheny County and stay at the Courtyard Marriott Monroeville, providing a convenient location for the final day’s adventure.
Day 3: Classic Pittsburgh Fun
Kennywood Amusement Park (Allegheny County) 🎢 | 10 a.m.-3 p.m.
End the trip with a thrilling day at Kennywood, one of America’s most historic amusement parks. Students can enjoy classic roller coasters like the Phantom’s Revenge, the Jack Rabbit, and the Thunderbolt, as well as cultural heritage rides like the Old Mill and Noah’s Ark.
🍽️ Lunch Inside Kennywood
Enjoy famous Potato Patch fries or other classic amusement park fare.
