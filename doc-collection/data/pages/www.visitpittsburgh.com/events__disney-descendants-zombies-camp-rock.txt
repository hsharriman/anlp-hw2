October 28, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Disney Descendants, Zombies & Camp Rock brings together three popular Disney music franchises filled with energetic performances, catchy songs, and themes of friendship and self-expression. Descendants follows the children of famous Disney villains as they navigate identity, acceptance, and life beyond their parents’ legacies through high-energy pop performances and colorful choreography. Zombies adds a fun blend of music, dance, and supernatural comedy while celebrating individuality, teamwork, and breaking social barriers in a vibrant school setting. Camp Rock captures the excitement of young musicians chasing their dreams through upbeat pop-rock songs, inspiring performances, and stories centered around confidence, creativity, and finding your voice.
