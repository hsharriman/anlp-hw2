Join us for our 33rd Halloween season. Castle Blood combines a traditional haunted house, with a theatrical experience, and some escape room inspirations as well, in a 1 hour show like no other.
Maximum of 8 per group. If you purchase less than 6 tickets you may be asked to welcome others into your group.
Special notice, we are no longer open Sunday Nights. We are open Sunday afternoons for our family matinee shows.
