Discover Your Persona
November 16, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Chad Damiani brings a distinctive clown-comedy perspective shaped by years of physical storytelling and improvisation. A writer and performer, he toured and taught clown improv with his team Jetzo, drawing on an unusual earlier chapter: five years working for World Championship Wrestling. Damiani also created the “STAND UP AND CLOWN” podcast and live show, guiding comedians through their first clown performances, and has appeared on “America’s Got Talent,” “Dave” and “The Joe Schmo Show.”
