Discover Your Persona
November 4, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Judy Collins is a legendary folk singer-songwriter known for her crystal-clear vocals, poetic songwriting, and influential role in the American folk music revival of the 1960s. Her music blends folk, pop, and traditional ballad influences while often exploring themes of love, social awareness, introspection, and human connection. Collins became widely respected for interpreting songs by iconic songwriters and for helping introduce audiences to many influential artists throughout her long career. With decades of acclaimed performances, timeless recordings, and a graceful stage presence, Judy Collins remains one of the most celebrated voices in folk and contemporary acoustic music.
