Celebration of Diversity in Pittsburgh
Pittsburgh’s diversity and 90 vibrant neighborhoods make our region an inclusive event and meeting hub
Pittsburgh is historically known as a melting pot of cultures and lifestyles. Settled by hard-working immigrants, our vivid neighborhoods are alive with unique voices. Discover how Pittsburgh’s diversity can enhance your next meeting or event in our city!

3. Diverse Communities
Visitors looking for an authentic Pittsburgh experience need look no further than Pittsburgh's 90 neighborhoods, each one unique and distinct. Take Homewood, a historically Black and culturally vibrant community bursting with culture, history, brilliant art and so much more. The Homewood Experience, in partnership with Neighborhood Allies and the Homewood Community Development Collaborative, brings the positivity and pride of Homewood to visitors while also catering to its current residents. From the Artwalk, a curation of public art offerings, to the Homewood Walk of Fame and thriving small businesses, the Homewood neighborhood is a great itinerary builder for all visitors.
