Pittsburgh Panthers football is the college football program representing the University of Pittsburgh, known for its rich history, passionate fanbase, and long-standing tradition within major college athletics. The Panthers have produced numerous legendary players, championship seasons, and memorable moments that helped shape the history of college football. Playing home games at Acrisure Stadium, the program is recognized for intense rivalries, strong defensive traditions, and a proud connection to Pittsburgh’s blue-collar sports culture. With a legacy that includes national championships, Hall of Fame talent, and decades of competitive success, Pittsburgh Panthers football remains one of the most respected programs in collegiate athletics.
