Discover Your Persona
October 25, 2026 to February 21, 2027
Fractures explores what happens when familiar ways of seeing begin to break apart. Across a range of media, the artists in this exhibition transform landscapes, memories, materials, and everyday objects into works that resist easy interpretation. Fragments overlap, perspectives shift, and what first appears recognizable gradually transforms into something else. These works invite viewers to linger in moments of uncertainty, where meaning is continually reconstructed through observation and experience.
Presented in partnership with Associated Artists of Pittsburgh and Radiant Hall.
Artwork credit: James Tobin, Chromatic Ruin – Machine Dreams, 2025. Digital photography collage on metal
