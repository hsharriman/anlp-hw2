Rock, Reggae & Relief
Get ready for a summer of rhythm and goodwill with Pittsburgh's Rock, Reggae and Relief festival!
August 8, 2026
This festival on August 8th in Downtown Pittsburgh, combines world-class music with community support. Hosted by the Piatt Family Foundation, this year’s beneficiary is Cafe Momentum, a nonprofit dedicated to empowering justice-involved youth. Join us for a season of beats, unity, and positive change in the heart of Pittsburgh!
Rock, Reggae & Relief
Event Details
The Rock, Reggae & Relief Festival is on Saturday, August 8, 2026 at in Downtown Pittsburgh, covering Forbes Avenue and Market Square. The day of the event, the area is blocked off from the rest of the city, creating a unique concert ground known as the “Urban Oasis”.
Tickets
The festival features three different admission options including General, VIP Gold and VIP Platinum, plus an add-on option: "Kingston Town" UB40 Meet & Greet Experience. To find more details and to secure your tickets, visit the Rock, Reggae & Relief Festival website.
