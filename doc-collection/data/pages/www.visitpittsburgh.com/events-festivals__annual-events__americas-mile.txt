🚨 2026 America's Mile Has Been Canceled 🚨
We are disappointed to share that the 2026 in-person America's Mile has been canceled. The health and safety of everyone involved will always be our top priority.
Due to Air Quality Index (AQI) levels remaining at unhealthy and hazardous levels, the 2026 America's Mile in-person event scheduled for Friday, July 17 has been canceled.
Participants can look for an email with registration options. Please contact info@p3r.org with any additional questions.
America's Mile
America's Mile Awaits...
July 17, 2026
Join us for America's Mile, a spectacular race event open to all, set against Pittsburgh's breathtaking North Shore. Choose your challenge from diverse heats like the inclusive MOVE Mile, the brave Heroes' Mile, and the competitive Elite Mile (just to name a few of your options!), catering to every age and ability.
Register, train, and then take to the start line to create your moment in this national celebration. Cross the finish line and keep the energy high at the Mile 1.1 post-race party. Ready to write your story? America's Mile awaits.
When & Where
America's Mile will take place in Pittsburgh's North Shore neighborhood. The race begins at P3R's headquarters near Acrisure Stadium and finishes on West General Robinson Street.
Race Info & Registration
America's Mile is Pittsburgh's signature one-mile challenge event. Fourteen years in the making, America's Mile transcends the conventional race, turning every step into a narrative of personal triumph and communal celebration.
Registration for America's Mile is available online
The reimagined event will feature a variety of heats catering to different ages, abilities and competitive spirits, ensuring there's a place for everyone at the start line. Highlights include:
The MOVE Mile: A welcoming race for participants of all abilities and aspirations.
The Kids of STEEL Mile: A fun and encouraging race for children 13 and younger, alongside their guardians.
The Heroes’ Mile: A tribute heat for our nation's valiant servicemen and women.
Additional heats include the Masters Mile, the Unstoppable Mile, the RUN Varsity Mile, and the exclusive Elite Miles, alongside a Virtual Mile option for remote participants.
The event will offer a prize purse of $34,200 with cash prizes for the top five runners in the Pro Mile, Unstoppable, and Masters.
Plan Your Visit
Make the most of your time during America's Mile by exploring Downtown Pittsburgh or other exciting Pittsburgh neighborhoods. Discover fun things to do throughout the city and be sure to experience our culinary scene before attending a performance.
