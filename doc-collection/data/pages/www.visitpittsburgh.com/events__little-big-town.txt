October 25, 2026 6:30 PM –9:30 PM
Little Big Town is a Grammy Award-winning country music quartet renowned for its rich four-part harmonies, sophisticated songwriting, and ability to blend traditional country roots with contemporary influences. Consisting of Karen Fairchild, Kimberly Schlapman, Phillip Sweet, and Jimi Westbrook, the group has spent more than two decades building one of the most respected catalogs in modern country music. Their breakthrough success came with hits such as “Pontoon,” “Girl Crush,” “Boondocks,” and “Better Man,” songs that showcased both their vocal chemistry and willingness to explore a wide range of musical and lyrical themes. Throughout their career, Little Big Town has earned acclaim for tackling stories of love, heartbreak, resilience, and everyday life with authenticity and emotional depth, helping them maintain a lasting connection with audiences across generations. Known for polished live performances and exceptional musicianship, the quartet continues to be one of country music’s most influential and enduring acts, celebrated for both its artistic growth and commitment to vocal excellence.
