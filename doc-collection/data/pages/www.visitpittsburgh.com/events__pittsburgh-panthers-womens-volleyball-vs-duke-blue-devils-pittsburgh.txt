November 6, 2026 7:00 PM –9:00 PM
Get ready for an electrifying showdown as the Pittsburgh Panthers take on the Duke Blue Devils in a thrilling match of the Pittsburgh Panthers Women's Volleyball vs. Duke Blue Devils - Pittsburgh! Join us at the Petersen Events Center, where the energy is palpable and the atmosphere is electric. Fans will be on the edge of their seats as these talented teams battle it out for victory, showcasing their skills and teamwork in front of an enthusiastic crowd.
The Petersen Events Center is the perfect backdrop for this exciting sports event, with its vibrant ambiance and passionate supporters. Whether you’re a die-hard volleyball fan or just looking for a fun night out, this match promises to deliver unforgettable moments. Don’t miss your chance to be part of the action—grab your tickets now and cheer on your favorite team!
