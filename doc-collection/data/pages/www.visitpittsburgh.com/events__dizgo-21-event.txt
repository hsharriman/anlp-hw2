Discover Your Persona
November 13, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Dizgo is a Bloomington, Indiana quartet known for an exploratory blend of jamtronica, funk, soul, psychedelic rock and improvisational live performance. The group combines soulful vocals, analog synths, dynamic guitar work and deep grooves to create shows that can feel equally danceable, immersive and spontaneous. Their current music includes “Grand Ol’ Time” and “Exit 59,” while their 2024 studio album, *Melt*, captures the band’s collaborative, groove-focused approach. Pittsburgh audiences can catch Dizgo at Thunderbird Café & Music Hall in a 21-and-over concert setting.
