October 30, 2026 8:00 PM –9:30 PM
Cross the road comedy & Bougie bitch comedy are collaborating to bring you a spooktacular night stand up!
We’re back!
Bougie Bitch Comedy and Cross the Road Comedy are coming together to bring you one spooktacular night of standup. Boo-HAHA is a Halloween-themed comedy show featuring headliner Jenny Lopez along with a stellar crew of spirited Pittsburgh comics.
Local comic and artist Chicken (of 1flychicken creations) is producing her first show and launching Cross the Road Comedy with the help of Bougie Bitch Comedy at the site of their first show together, the Dormont V.F.W. How sweet!
Costumes are not required but strongly encouraged. Come and pre-game Halloween with us. We can’t wait to get spooky with yinz!
Thank you for your continued support of our comedy dreams!
Headliner: Jenny Lopez (Arcade Comedy, Next Comedy, Don’t Tell Comedy)
Host: Meghan “Chicken” Tutolo (Next Stop Comedy, Roast Battle League, Don’t Tell Comedy)
Featuring:
Kayla Leto-Marks (debut)
Virginia Alvino (Arcade Comedy)
Jo Crawford (Arcade Comedy Theater, Don’t Tell Comedy)
Jared Figel (Next Stop Comedy, Don’t Tell Comedy)
