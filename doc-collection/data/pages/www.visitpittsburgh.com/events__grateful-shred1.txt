November 1, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Grateful Shred is a collective of accomplished musicians dedicated to celebrating the music and improvisational spirit of the Grateful Dead through vibrant, guitar-driven performances. Known for their exceptional musicianship and deep appreciation of the Dead’s catalog, the group delivers faithful interpretations while embracing the spontaneity and exploration that define jam-band culture. Their concerts feature intricate harmonies, extended instrumental passages, and a relaxed yet energetic atmosphere that appeals to both longtime Deadheads and newer audiences. By combining technical skill with a genuine love for the music, Grateful Shred creates an engaging live experience that honors the enduring legacy of the Grateful Dead.
