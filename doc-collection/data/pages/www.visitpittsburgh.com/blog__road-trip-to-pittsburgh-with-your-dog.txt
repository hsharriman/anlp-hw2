Rediscover Pittsburgh on a Roadtrip With Your Dog
We invite you and your favorite furry pup friends to experience Pittsburgh!
We all know Pittsburgh is the perfect destination for family fun, but what about fun for the fur-kids? We’re talking about your dog… or dogs! Pittsburgh is ready to welcome you and your dog to explore and rediscover the city like never before.
Pet-Friendly Hotels
Planning on spending the night in Pittsburgh with your pup? You’re in the right city! Pittsburgh offers a variety of unique hotels with enhanced health and safety measures in place for you to travel safely. Explore options all over the city including Downtown hotels like the Kimpton Hotel Monaco Pittsburgh or Fairmont Pittsburgh. Or venture out into Pittsburgh’s 90 unique neighborhoods at hotels like the Hotel Indigo Pittsburgh East Liberty and the HYATT House Pittsburgh-South Side.
Know Before You Go
Hotels may have restrictions or fees associated with your dog’s stay. Prior to your arrival, confirm with the hotel staff that your dog will be staying and that all rules are followed. Don’t forget to pack your pup’s essentials!
Restaurants & Breweries
A trip to Pittsburgh isn’t complete without indulging in the city’s delicious food scene! Enjoy breakfast, lunch, dinner or sweet treats with your furry friend by your side. Outdoor and pet-friendly seating is available at a variety of restaurants including Walter’s Southern Kitchen, serving up tasty BBQ and soul food in Lawrenceville. Or, stop by the outdoor patio at the North Shore’s Federal Galley for craft cocktails and a taste from a new restaurant concept from up and coming chefs.

For the beer lovers, Pittsburgh’s dynamic craft beer scene is very welcoming of dogs! Craft breweries like Grist House Craft Brewery, Allegheny City Brewing, The Leaning Cask Brewing Company and Southern Tier are all pup-friendly and even have outdoor areas for you to play with your pals.
Know Before You GoBefore you head out for a meal or drink, make sure your dog can socialize or is well-behaved in new spaces. Keep your dog’s well-being in mind and avoid staying out too late or going to a place with loud music. Be sure to grab your pup’s leash before heading out!
Parks & Outdoor Adventure
With 165 parks in the City of Pittsburgh, there’s no shortage of green spaces for your outdoor adventure! Pittsburgh has a wide range of regional and state parks to neighborhood parklets, including many different off-leash areas.
Walk your dog through Point State Park, the city’s 36-acre state park located in Downtown, and discover “The Point,” the confluence of Pittsburgh’s iconic three rivers. Cross one of Pittsburgh’s notable bridges for a walk along the North Shore Riverfront Trail and Park. And, if you and your dog like to hike, or even bike, check out Frick Park and the Montour Trail for a range of easy, intermediate and hard trails.
If you and your pup are ready for a water adventure, check out Venture Outdoors for an unforgettable time on Pittsburgh’s rivers. Dog-friendly kayak rentals are available through Kayak Pittsburgh with three different location options. When making a reservation, be sure to leave a note that your dog will be coming along.

Know Before You Go
Pittsburgh is a very walkable city, and it’s easy to walk across bridges, through neighborhoods and connecting trails. Map out your walk before heading out to make sure you get the most of your adventure. Check out a full list of off-leash areas. Make sure you bring doggie waste bags and extra water!
Shopping
Need to restock on dog gear while you’re in town? Check out these awesome locally owned stores to satisfy all your dog necessities and more! Stop by Pittsburgh’s Petagogy located in Shadyside for healthy, natural foods and treats along with fun and innovative pet toys and accessories. Wagsburgh is a specialty food and pet supply store on the North Side guaranteed to make your dogs tail wag. Locally owned and operated Lawrenceville Pet Supply is a neighborhood gem offering trusted products and advice for pets.
We can’t wait to see you and your furry friend in Pittsburgh soon! Be sure to tag us in your dog-friendly photos and use #LovePGH for a chance to be featured in our marketing materials.
