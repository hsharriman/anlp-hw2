November 5, 2026 7:30 PM –9:00 PM
Former CIA counterterrorism officer, whistleblower, author, and Lawrence County native John Kiriakou is coming home. Join Forward Lawrence on Thursday, November 5, at the Scottish Rite Cathedral in New Castle for a compelling keynote on Leadership & Ethics, featuring stories and insights from Kiriakou’s extraordinary career in American intelligence and beyond. The evening marks a special homecoming: the last time Kiriakou stood on the Scottish Rite Cathedral stage was when he walked across it for his high school graduation. Now, he returns to that same stage to share his story with his hometown and audiences from across the region. Public keynote tickets start at just $35.
