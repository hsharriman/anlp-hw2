Discover Your Persona
October 30, 2026 6:00 PM –10:00 PM
Something wicked is washing ashore for Devil’s Night.
On Friday, October 30th, The Plaza at North Shore transforms for a one-night-only Devil’s Night experience, where the line between the living and the dead begins to disappear. Your ticket unlocks a night of sinister encounters with never-before-seen characters, killer photo opportunities, and twisted surprises dreamed up by the demented minds behind ScareHouse. Keep your eyes open…creatures of the night are waiting for you. Encounter a mysterious creepy mermaid, be amazed by fire and freak show performers, and have your fortune revealed by a tarot reader.
With devilish scares, unexpected encounters, and surprises that will keep you guessing all night long, this is Devil’s Night like you’ve never experienced it before.
*Haunted food & beverages will satisfy your appetite for the supernatural and will be available separately for purchase.
