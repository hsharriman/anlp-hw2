November 9, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Mountain Grass Unit is a fast-rising bluegrass ensemble that has earned attention for its impressive musicianship, youthful energy, and fresh approach to traditional American roots music. The band blends the foundational elements of bluegrass—lightning-fast picking, tight harmonies, and acoustic instrumentation—with influences drawn from jam bands, folk, and contemporary Americana, creating a sound that appeals to both longtime bluegrass fans and newer audiences. Known for dynamic live performances, the group frequently stretches songs into inventive improvisational passages while maintaining the precision and craftsmanship associated with classic bluegrass traditions. Their growing reputation on the festival circuit has been fueled by a commitment to authentic musicianship and a willingness to explore new creative directions within the genre. As they continue to build a national following, Mountain Grass Unit is helping introduce a new generation of listeners to the enduring appeal of acoustic string music while carving out a distinctive identity of its own.
