Pittsburgh Independent Film Festival
Discover a weekend of bold storytelling, nonstop screenings, and conversations with the filmmakers redefining independent cinema.
Event Details
Schedule
Previous Winners
Submit a Film
Watch raw, unfiltered cinema up close at the Pittsburgh Independent Film Festival, where micro-budget and no-budget films take center stage. Held each June inside the Parkway Theater’s 45-seat cinema, this highly-anticipated film festival screens 12-hours of indie films each day giving you a front-row seat to stories you won’t find anywhere else. Between screenings, join casual conversations with filmmakers in the onsite gathering space, where creative exchange happens naturally and often leads to lasting connections.
Event Details
When & Where
The Pittsburgh Independent Film Festival takes place June 26-28, 2026 at the Parkway Theater & Film Lounge, located at 644 Broadway Avenue in McKees Rocks, PA. Just a short drive from downtown Pittsburgh, this intimate venue provides a relaxed and welcoming space for screenings, discussions, and filmmaker networking.
Tickets
Tickets for the 2026 Pittsburgh Independent Film Festival are available now at pghindie.com, with options for one-day, two-day, or full weekend passes. All tickets can be picked up at the theater's will call window during the event.

Previous Winners
Winning or even screening at the Pittsburgh Independent Film Festival can be a turning point for indie creators. In just the past two years, 20 films have gone on to secure distribution deals after appearing at the festival. Explore the archive of winners and see what kinds of projects are making an impact.

Submit a Film to the Pittsburgh Independent Film Festival
Submissions are now open for the 2026 Pittsburgh Independent Film Festival through FilmFreeway.com. Whether you're submitting a short, feature, or experimental film, the platform includes all key dates and deadlines to help you plan ahead. This is your chance to share your work with an engaged audience and connect with fellow indie creators.
