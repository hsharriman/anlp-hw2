Rush to Crush Cancer
Pedal for a cure in Pittsburgh’s Rush to Crush Cancer.
May 16-17, 2026
This annual event benefits the UPMC Hillman Cancer Center, offering five in-person courses and a virtual option to bring people and communities together for a common cause. The goal is to inspire patients, survivors, medical professionals, and everyone who has been touched by cancer to get one mile closer to achieving a life without cancer with every mile. Each ride (and the family-friendly walk) takes participants through the beautiful neighborhoods around Pittsburgh.

Dates & Course Info
Dates
May 16-17, 2026
The Short Course (8 Mile)
This ride kicks off on Sunday, May 17 at 9am from Hazelwood Green. It’s a flat course that travels through the city and finishes in SouthSide Works.
Route Map
Brian Shanahan 15-Mile Ride
An extended version of the short course, this ride kicks off at the same place and time, also finishing at SouthSide Works after traveling a bit more uptown.
Route Map
30-Mile Ride
This point-to-point ride starts at Central Elementary School at 8am on Sunday, May 17. It’s an intermediate ride with rolling hills and open roads, finishing in SouthSide Works.
Route Map
60-Mile Ride
Similar to the 30-miler, this ride also kicks off at Central Elementary school on Sunday, but starts two hours earlier at 6am. This is a challenging ride where you’ll see the best of the hilly terrain of Pittsburgh and the surrounding area to the north.
Route Map
PNC Mile to Crush Cancer Walk
This 1-mile walk serves as the weekend’s opening ceremony on Saturday, May 16 at 7pm. It is free, family-friendly, and open to the public. The fun kicks off in SouthSide Works.

Where To Stay
There are tons of comfortable accommodations in Pittsburgh. If you’re looking for nearby options to the courses, check out Hyatt House South Side, SpringHill Suites Southside Works, or Hotel Indigo Pittsburgh University-Oakland.
FAQ
How do I register?
Once you’ve selected your event, you can click here to register.
How much is it to register?
The 1-mile walk is free to everyone to participate, and bike races start at $30 for 12-18 years old and $55 for 19+ years old.
Can I rent a bike and participate?
Yes! Unlimited Biking is offering bike rental options and free helmets with convenient pick-up and drop-off options for race participants. Please book your rental in advance.
Are there restrooms, medical support, and other assistance along the course?
Yes. All bike courses include full medical and mechanical support, rest stops, and SAG assistance, with optional shuttle service on the 30-mile and 60-mile courses.
Are there fundraising requirements?
Yes, and every dollar raised helps to make a difference in cancer research. Each registration has its own fundraising goal, but you are welcome to raise as much as you would like beyond that goal. For tips on maximizing your impact, click here.
What does the winner get?
This is a community ride, not a race! No prizes are involved, just the sense of community and inspiration that comes with banding together for a good cause.
