Pittsburgh International Airport
Pittsburgh is conveniently located within 500 miles of nearly half the U.S. population and less than a 90-minute flight from nearly half of North America's population.
With more than nine million passengers annually, Pittsburgh International Airport gets people to Pittsburgh and brings Pittsburghers where they need to go, offering flights to 62 nonstop domestic and international destinations on 14 commercial carriers and more than 170 daily flights.
Flying across the Atlantic? We’ve got you covered thanks to our nonstop flights through British Airways and Icelandair.
Flying in and out of our city has never been easier thanks to Pittsburgh International Airport’s new terminal project. Well on its way, this landmark renovation is to be completed in 2025—improving the passenger experience from arrival to departure. Find your next flight at flypittsburgh.com.
Pittsburgh Takes Flight: International Recognition
We’re on a mission to reflect the re-emergence of our region as a player on an international stage, and people are noticing. In 2024, Pittsburgh International’s efforts were recognized by:
Fast Company Magazine’s Most Innovative Companies, Transportation Category
Air Transport World Magazine’s Hall of Fame Award
Future Travel Experience’s Airport Pioneer Award
As Pittsburgh continues to reinvent itself, the airport is proud to be a key component of the region’s overall success. With more routes, more airlines, and more options, Pittsburgh International Airport can’t wait for you to visit.
Airport Transportation
Ride Share:
Transportation Network companies ZTrip, Uber and Lyft are now authorized to pick up and drop off passengers at Pittsburgh International Airport. These popular ride-share services are accessed through smartphone or tablet apps. Pick up is at the designated area outside of Door #4 on the commercial curb.
By Taxi:
One-way fare between Pittsburgh International Airport and Downtown is about $40. Upon arrival at the airport, proceed to the landside terminal, lower level, exit through the "commercial" doors and head toward the taxi stand area on the curb where cabs are waiting.
Checker Cab: (412) 664-5600
People's Cab: (412) 441-3200
zTrip: (412) 777-7777
By Bus:
Pittsburgh Regional Transit 28X Airport Flyer
The fare between the airport and Downtown is $2.75 each way. (Note: Exact change is required.) Passengers are responsible for handling their bags when getting on and off the vehicle. Tipping the bus driver is not permitted.
