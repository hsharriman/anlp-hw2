October 17, 2026 7:00 PM
Miami RedHawks visit the Robert Morris Colonials for the Pennsylvania leg of a two-game, nonconference home-and-home series. The NCAA Division I men’s hockey matchup is part of Robert Morris’ 34-game 2026–27 schedule and marks the Colonials’ home opener against Miami (Ohio) at Clearview Arena. Miami competes in the National Collegiate Hockey Conference, while Robert Morris plays in Atlantic Hockey America.
