Discover Your Persona
October 25, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Creed Fisher is a West Texas singer-songwriter known for blending traditional country with modern elements and hard-rock influence. His music draws on working-class themes, patriotism and personal experience. Fisher’s notable releases include the albums Old School, which reached No. 3 on the iTunes Country Albums chart, and Hellraiser, which debuted at No. 2. His catalog also includes the songs “I’ll Keep Drinkin’,” “Ain’t Scared to Bleed” and “Fairies Wear Boots,” a cover of the Black Sabbath classic.
