November 7, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Lee Emi will perform live at the Thunderbird Café & Music Hall in Pittsburgh on Saturday, November 7, 2026.
The singer-songwriter (formerly of the West Michigan band The Crane Wives) is touring in support of her solo project, delivering her trademark indie-pop-rock sound.
Special Guest: Cal in Red is scheduled to open the show.
