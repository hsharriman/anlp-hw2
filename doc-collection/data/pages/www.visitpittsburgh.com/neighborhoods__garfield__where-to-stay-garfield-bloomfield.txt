Where to Stay: Garfield & Bloomfield
While this area is famous for its residential charm and independent shops, it offers a variety of ways to stay local. From modern extended-stay hotels to a wide array of unique short-term rentals, visitors can easily find a home base that fits their style.
Neighborhood Boutique & Nearby Stays
Hyatt House Pittsburgh/Bloomfield/Shadyside | 5335 Baum Blvd.
Located on the edge of Bloomfield, this is the most central hotel for exploring the area. It offers an upscale, extended-stay experience with homelike amenities including studio and one-bedroom suites with fully equipped kitchens, complimentary breakfast, and an indoor pool.
With a stay at Hyatt House, you can walk straight into the heart of Bloomfield's shopping district in under ten minutes.
The hotel offers a complimentary shuttle service that typically runs within a three-mile radius, perfect for getting to the heart of the Penn Avenue arts district or the shops on Liberty Avenue without needing to move your car.
The Maverick by Kasa | 120 S Whitfield St.
Located in nearby East Liberty, this historic building (formerly a YMCA) offers modern, apartment-style boutique rooms that mirror the creative energy of Garfield.
Hotel Indigo Pittsburgh East Liberty | 123 N Highland Ave.
A boutique hotel that celebrates the neighborhood’s industrial and theatrical history with unique design touches and a vibrant bar scene.
TRYP by Wyndham Pittsburgh/Lawrenceville | 177 40th St.
Situated in a converted vocational school just minutes away, this hotel features a rooftop bar with some of the best views of the city skyline.
Planning Tips
If you choose a hotel in East Liberty or Lawrenceville, you can easily access the neighborhoods via Pittsburgh's PRT bus lines (like the 88 or 87, which run directly down Penn and Liberty Avenues) or take a quick, five-minute rideshare to your first destination.
If you stay at the Hyatt House on Baum Boulevard, you can walk straight into the heart of Bloomfield's shopping district in under ten minutes.
Book Early for Events: Because of the area’s popularity during events like Little Italy Days and Unblurred: First Fridays, lodging fills up quickly. If you're planning to visit during these peak times, booking at least 2–3 months in advance is recommended.
Parking Considerations: Parking in these neighborhoods is primarily a mix of metered street spots and residential permit zones. On Liberty Avenue and Penn Avenue, look for the multi-space parking kiosks; these usually have a two-hour limit during business hours. For those attending large events, the Bloomfield Public Parking Lot (located near Liberty and Taylor St) is a centrally located off-street option.
