Winter in Pittsburgh
Looking for cozy winter vibes, old world European markets and fireworks-filled festivals to kick off the holiday season? Look no further than Pittsburgh!
Whether you’re looking for a fun, easy holiday getaway, unique shopping or the quintessential sports weekend, we’ve got you covered. We make planning your trip easy with the list of events, activities and hotels below.

What to Pack
Must-See Winter Events + What to Do
Embrace the season, enjoy the winter weather and make plans to visit The UPMC Rink at PPG Place in the heart of Downtown Pittsburgh – just steps away from Market Square. While you're in the area, head over to the Cultural District for the Peoples Gas Holiday Market, your one-stop shop for your holiday wish list. And be sure to be in town for our annual Light Up Night, which kicks off the holiday season the week before Thanksgiving.
Take in one of several light shows happening around the region, including Kennywood's Holiday Lights, Phipps Conservatory and Botanical Gardens' Holiday Magic, Glowland in Oakland, Pittsburgh Botanic Garden's Dazzling Nights, and Pittsburgh Zoo and Aquarium's Wild Illuminations, just to name a few.

Warm up at one of Pittsburgh’s eclectic, cozy coffee shops like De Fer Coffee & Tea or Grim Wizard. Or, grab a delicious steaming bowl of soup at Smiling Banana Leaf, Patti’s Pasticceria or EatUnique Café.
Not a fan of cold weather? No worries! There are plenty of indoor attractions and activities to explore. Spend the day enjoying the visual arts at renowned museums like the Mattress Factory – a must-see contemporary art museum.
In town for the holidays? Pittsburgh dazzles with holiday lights and events!

Outdoor Activities
Hit the slopes at Boyce Park for all your tubing, snowboarding, and skiing needs!
Take a day trip to Seven Springs Mountain Resort. Or, grab a sled and zip your way through the many hills in Pittsburgh city and Allegheny County parks.
If you're feeling like getting out of the city, there are plenty of other spots to go ice skating besides PPG Plaza. Head to Schenley Park, North Park, South Park, or any of the local indoor rinks, strap on your skates, and take a few laps.
Get Your Game On
Even when it gets cold, Pittsburgh's sports teams stay heating up.
The Pittsburgh Steelers bring in thousands of fans from all over the country during every game at Acrisure Stadium. Grab your Terrible Towels and cheer on the Black and Gold for a win. And while you're in the North Shore, visit the Steelers Hall of Honor Museum and head over to the Great Lawn to visit the official 2026 NFL Draft Countdown Clock.
Just like penguins thrive in the cold, the Pittsburgh Penguins will be playing all season long. Visit PPG Paints Arena and see this storied franchise through at least April.
And if you're a baseball fan, visit the Clemente Museum and learn about humanitarian and baseball legend, Roberto Clemente.

How to Pack
There’s no way around it, winter in Pittsburgh is bound to be chilly and often snowy as well. The good news is that with a chill in the air comes all the fun and festivities of the winter season. When packing, there are a few things you should be sure to grab.
Dress in Layers! The city, especially Downtown, naturally warms up during the day. Dress in layers so that you can easily remove articles of clothing as you explore during the day, then bundle up again in the evening.
One of the highlights of the winter season in Pittsburgh is the UPMC Rink at PPG Place! If you are a serious skater, be sure to pack your ice skates. Don’t worry, you can always rent a pair if that’s more your style.
Even if you plan to keep you walking to a minimum, it’s always a good idea to have the right shoes. Boots that can handle getting wet or snowy will make exploring much easier, and more fun!
