Discover Your Persona
October 25, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Kendl Winter and Palmer T. Lee make up The Lowest Pair, an Americana duo whose signature pairing of two banjos gives their songs a distinctive rootsy sound. Their close harmonies and nimble picking meet thoughtful songwriting across albums including 36¢, The Sacred Heart Sessions and The Perfect Plan. Their 2026 release, Always As Young As We’ll Ever Be, expands their acoustic palette while retaining the duo’s intimate, song-centered approach.
