Discover Your Persona
November 14, 2026 8:00 PM –11:00 PM
Yaelokre is the musical storytelling project of Icelandic-Filipino singer-songwriter and multimedia artist Keath Ósk. Built around the illustrated, diary-turned-storybook world of “Meadowlark,” the project follows the Lark, a quartet of young minstrels, through folk-inspired songs and character-driven mythology. Yaelokre gained broad attention with “Harpy Hare,” followed by releases including “And the Hound,” “Hartebeest,” and the “Composing Colentine” EP. Ósk’s work combines songwriting, illustration, and fairytale world-building into a distinctive contemporary folk presentation.
