November 8, 2026 2:00 PM –4:00 PM
Join us for an unforgettable evening at the Byham Theater in Pittsburgh, where the magic of childhood imagination comes to life in ""Matilda - The Musical - Pittsburgh."" This enchanting production tells the story of a brilliant young girl with a vivid imagination and a thirst for adventure, as she navigates the challenges of her unkind surroundings. With its captivating performances, stunning choreography, and delightful music, the atmosphere at the Byham Theater will transport you into Matilda's whimsical world, making it a perfect outing for families and theatre lovers alike.
Don't miss your chance to experience the joy and inspiration of ""Matilda - The Musical - Pittsburgh."" Gather your friends and family, and prepare for a night filled with laughter, heartwarming moments, and a celebration of the power of imagination. Secure your tickets now and be part of this extraordinary theatrical journey!
