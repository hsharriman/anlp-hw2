Discover Your Persona
October 29, 2026 5:30 PM –8:30 PM
Periphery is a progressive metal band known for technical musicianship, intricate guitar work, and a modern sound that blends djent, metalcore, progressive rock, and electronic influences. Their music features complex rhythms, soaring melodies, heavy breakdowns, and emotionally layered songwriting that explores themes of technology, isolation, personal struggle, and self-discovery. Fronted by dynamic vocals and driven by highly technical instrumentation, Periphery has become one of the defining bands in contemporary progressive metal. Known for explosive live performances and innovative production, the group continues to push the boundaries of modern heavy music through precision, creativity, and genre experimentation.
