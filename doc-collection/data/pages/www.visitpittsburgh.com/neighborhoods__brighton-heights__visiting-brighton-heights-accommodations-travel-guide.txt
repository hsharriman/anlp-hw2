Visiting Brighton Heights: Accommodations & Travel Guide
While Brighton Heights itself is a purely residential and green sanctuary with no commercial hotels within its immediate borders, its exceptional geographic location makes visiting incredibly simple. Located just north of the city center, you can stay in premier accommodations nearby and commute into the neighborhood in minutes.
🏨 Where to Stay: Close-By Selections
North Shore Hotel Hub (3-4 Miles Away)
Staying on the nearby Pittsburgh North Shore keeps you incredibly close to Brighton Heights while placing you steps away from Acrisure Stadium, PNC Park, and the Andy Warhol Museum.
The Priory Hotel | 614 Pressley St.
A historic, 42-room boutique hotel nestled in a beautifully restored former Benedictine monastery. It offers a unique historic charm that mirrors the architectural spirit of Brighton Heights.
Residence Inn by Marriott Pittsburgh North Shore | 574 W General Robinson St.
Excellent for families or extended stays, featuring spacious suites and incredibly fast transit access heading north up the river.
Hyatt Place Pittsburgh North Shore | 260 North Shore Dr.
Modern, reliable, and conveniently situated right off the main transit corridors connecting Downtown to the upper Northside neighborhoods.
Downtown Pittsburgh Hub (4-5 Miles Away)
For a full city experience, stay in the heart of Downtown Pittsburgh and utilize the direct public transit lines to travel straight up into Brighton Heights for an afternoon of outdoor exploration.
The Industrialist Hotel | 405 Wood St.
A stylish choice celebrating Pittsburgh’s classic trade history, located right in the center of the city.
The Atterbury Hotel (formerly Renaissance ) | 107 6th St.
Housed in the historic 1906 Fulton Building right on the riverfront, this newly debuted hotel features a breathtaking skylit atrium. It is a stunning match for architecture lovers visiting for the Brighton Heights House and Garden Tour.
Omni William Penn Hotel | 530 William Penn Pl.
A historic, grand landmark hotel perfect for architecture lovers who plan on taking the Brighton Heights House and Garden Tour.
🚌 Getting Around: Transit & Parking
Traveling to and traversing Brighton Heights is convenient whether you are utilizing city transit, driving a personal vehicle, or exploring on foot.
Public Transportation
The neighborhood is thoroughly mapped and connected by Pittsburgh Regional Transit (PRT). Visitors can hop on three primary bus lines, the 13, 16, and 17, all of which provide consistent, direct routes connecting Brighton Heights to the North Shore and Downtown corridors.
Pedestrian Walkability
Once you arrive, the neighborhood boasts wide, continuous pedestrian pathways and sidewalks. It offers a highly secure and comfortable environment for recreational walkers, joggers, and families.
Visitor Parking
If you are traveling by car, ample free public parking is readily available via street parking throughout the neighborhood's residential and commercial avenues.
Community Safety
Peace of mind comes easy during your stay; Brighton Heights maintains a stellar safety record on the Northside and works closely with the local officers of Pittsburgh Police Zone One to ensure a welcoming environment for all visitors.
