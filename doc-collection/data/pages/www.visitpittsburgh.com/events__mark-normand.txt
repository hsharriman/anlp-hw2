Discover Your Persona
August 27, 2026 7:00 PM –6:30 PM
Mark Normand is a stand-up comedian known for his rapid-fire joke writing, sharp observational humor, and clever takes on everyday social situations. His comedy style blends self-deprecation, wordplay, and quick punchlines, earning him a reputation as one of the most respected joke craftsmen in modern stand-up. Normand has built a devoted following through comedy specials, podcast appearances, and relentless touring, showcasing a comedic voice that is both relatable and refreshingly unfiltered. Known for his energetic delivery and ability to find humor in life's awkward moments, Mark Normand continues to be a standout figure in contemporary comedy.
