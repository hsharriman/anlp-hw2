Discover Your Persona
November 14, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Zoltan Kaszas is a stand-up comedian known for sharp observational humor, dry wit, and relatable stories drawn from everyday life and cultural experiences. His comedy often explores family dynamics, social awkwardness, relationships, and immigrant upbringing through a conversational style that feels both personal and approachable. Kaszas has built a strong following through viral stand-up clips, crowd-friendly performances, and an ability to turn ordinary situations into clever comedic material. With laid-back delivery and smart storytelling, Zoltan Kaszas has become a rising favorite in modern stand-up comedy.
