Discover Your Persona
November 15, 2026 6:00 PM –9:00 PM
T. Murph is a Chicago-born stand-up comedian, actor, and writer known for energetic storytelling, sharp observations, and a distinctly relatable point of view. He has appeared on Hulu’s Woke, Saturday Night Live, The Tonight Show with Jimmy Fallon, and Comedy Central, establishing himself as a versatile performer across television and live comedy. His current Why Am I Raising Adults Tour showcases his candid, high-energy approach to material about modern adulthood, relationships, family, and everyday frustrations. Audiences can expect an engaging stand-up performance from a rising comedy voice with a strong connection to contemporary culture.
