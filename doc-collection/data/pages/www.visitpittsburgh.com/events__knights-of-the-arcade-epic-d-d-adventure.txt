October 25, 2026 to December 26, 2026 9:00 PM –10:30 PM
A live Dungeons & Dragons-inspired comedy show
Join Knights of the Arcade for another hilarious D&D comedy adventure! One of Pittsburgh’s longest running and best-selling comedy shows! A recurring cast of characters are joined by special guests and led by their maniacal dungeon master. Where they’re going, who they fight, and if they ultimately succeed is decided upon live dice that are rolled and projected on the theater wall.
Featuring a rotating cast of charismatic heroes and mischievous villains, this is a night of comedy like no other. Past shows have included thrilling dragon battles, daring rescues, and a healthy dose of audience participation. Held in a casual, welcoming atmosphere, Knights of the Arcade promises to be a critically acclaimed crowd-pleaser for gamers, geeks, and comedy lovers alike. Prepare for magic, mischief, and a night you'll never forget!
"Best Nerd Fantasy Come to Life" by Pittsburgh Magazine
"A neo-geek wet dream" – Pittsburgh City Paper
Runtime: 75 minutes
Tickets $15 / $10 college students
Ages 16 & up
BYOB 21+
This event will occur every last Saturday of the month from May 30, 2026 to Dec 26, 2026.
