November 12, 2026 1:00 PM –11:00 PM
Alternative pop artist Sombr will bring his "You Are The Reason Tour" to Pittsburgh on Thursday, November 12, 2026, at 7:00 PM at PPG Paints Arena.
The extensive North American fall arena run highlights the 21-year-old singer-songwriter's moody, bedroom-pop textures and dark alternative undertones. The tour features prominent guest support from multi-award-winning superstar Dove Cameron and indie-pop singer Hannah Jadagu.
Doors at 6:00 pm. Show starts at 7:00 pm.
