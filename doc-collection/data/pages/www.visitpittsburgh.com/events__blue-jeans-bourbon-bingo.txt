November 6, 2026 6:00 PM –10:00 PM
The name says it all!
Grab your favorite pair of jeans and come on out for the most fun you can pack into one evening. Great food. Great drinks. Casual dress code. A chance to win some money. What more do you need!
Get ready to yell B-I-N-G-O for VisAbility Pittsburgh on Friday, November 6, 2026, at the UPMC Club in Acrisure Stadium for the 7th Annual Blue Jeans, Bourbon & Bingo event! The fun begins at 6:00 PM with our signature cocktail, a fantastic selection of Italian wines, and an exciting silent auction.
Come enjoy a relaxed and memorable night filled with delicious food, great music, the finest beverages, and plenty of chances to win cash prizes. Whether you’re joining us with friends, family, colleagues, or clients, Blue Jeans, Bourbon & Bingo offers a lively and welcoming atmosphere for all.
Most importantly, your support helps individuals who are blind, visually impaired, or have related disabilities build independence, financial stability, and meaningful community connections. We would be delighted to have you join us for this special evening.
For more information or any questions, please contact Leslie Montgomery at lmontgomery@visability.org or 412.368.4072. We hope to see you there!
