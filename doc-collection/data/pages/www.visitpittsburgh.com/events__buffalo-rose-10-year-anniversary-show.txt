Discover Your Persona
November 7, 2026 7:00 PM –10:00 PM
Buffalo Rose is a singularly spectacular six-piece band from Pittsburgh, PA. Overflowing with the bold and unfettered joy of their inimitable blend of “Theatre Kid Americana,” a Buffalo Rose show is a celebration of creative wonder; a sparkling sonic delight. Their live show is where their energy fully shines, and songwriting is their lodestar. The band’s recorded catalog is full of vibrantly emotive and methodically arranged songs, sure to thrill the mind and charm the heart. Come join Buffalo Rose as they celebrate their 10th year as a band in their hometown at Mr Smalls Sanctuary.
