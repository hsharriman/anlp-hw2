Arts Pass
All graduate and undergraduate CMU students have free access to eight Pittsburgh venues with their Carnegie Mellon ID. In order to be eligible for free access, you must be enrolled in classes and your primary affiliation must be “student” (this applies for students to be enrolled in classes over the summer).
At this time, faculty, staff and sponsored guests, in addition to recent CMU graduates, are not eligible for the Art Pass program.
Please be sure to check the museums' websites before planning your trip to learn the most up-to-date information regarding current hours of operation and ticketing procedures.
PSO Go is a student discount program from the Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra, designed to make live symphony music accessible to CMU students. Get your first Pittsburgh Symphony concert ticket free. PSO Go is a free student ticket program offering access to more than 80 concerts this season, including BNY Classics, PNC Pops, and Highmark Holiday Pops, with $15 tickets after your free first visit. Registration takes under a minute. Sign up: Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra. View eligible concerts here: PSO Go

Planning Your Visit
Carnegie Museum of Natural History, Carnegie Museum of Art, The Andy Warhol Museum & Kamin Science Center
Free general admission is available for CMU students with a valid student ID. Students will be asked to present their student ID card upon entering the museum.
All faculty-led groups and docent-led tours must be pre-registered by reaching out to each museum’s respective program registration office.
Carnegie Museums of Art and Natural History | 412-622-3131 | oakadmissions@carnegiemuseums.org
Kamin Science Center | 412-237-3400 | CustomerService@KaminScienceCenter.org
The Andy Warhol Museum | 412-237-8300 | information@warhol.org
Visit the Carnegie Museums of Pittsburgh website for more information.
Mattress Factory
Free admission for CMU students is available on a walk-up basis. CMU students are asked not to purchase their ticket online; instead, they should check in at the admissions desk upon arrival. Please bring your valid CMU student ID with you to gain free entry to Mattress Factory.
Visit the Mattress Factory website for more information.
National Aviary
Free admission for CMU students is available for visitors on a walk-up basis. CMU students are asked not to purchase a timed ticket but to present their CMU ID card to museum staff upon arrival. Please bring your valid student ID with you for your discount and entry.
Visit the National Aviary website for more information.
Phipps Botanical Gardens
CMU students may visit Phipps during their normal business hours without prior booking. Please have your student ID ready when you approach the admissions desk. CMU students who wish to book their tickets in advance may do so by emailing tickets@phipps.conservatory.org or calling 412-622-6914. Please bring your valid student ID card when visiting Phipps
Visit the Phipps ticketing website for more information.
Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra
Your first ticket is FREE through the PSO Go program. All students are eligible for the program and must register to PSO Go to receive their first free ticket. Any tickets after your first free one are $15 each, up to 2 per concert. Concerts include PSO Classics, Pops, and Holiday Pops, plus select movies and specials — around 80 concerts to choose from throughout the year. Notifications go out via a weekly newsletter each Monday/Tuesday when tickets go on sale.
For students who enjoy the symphony and would like to attend more concerts, please check out the PSO Go+ membership program. This program provides one ticket to all of the concerts for the season at only $50 per student. You may see this program offered through your PSO Go newsletters so be on the lookout and upgrade if you would like to!
For more information, please visit the Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra.
Heinz History Center, Fort Pitt Museum & Meadowcroft Rockshelter
Free admission for CMU students is available for visitors on a walk-up basis. CMU students are asked not to purchase a timed ticket but to present their CMU ID card to museum staff upon arrival. Please bring your valid student ID with you for your discount and entry.
Visit the Heinz History Center website for more information.
