Carnegie Mellon University is the exclusive higher education partner of The Tony Awards
CMU recognizes the transformative power of theatre education and has had a longstanding impact on Broadway, leading to an exclusive higher education partnership with The Tony Awards. The two organizations partnered to create the Excellence in Theatre Education Award, celebrating the teachers who turn passion into performance.
Over a Decade of Partnership
Since 2015, Carnegie Mellon University has partnered with The Tony Awards to celebrate the impact of education in the theatre arts. As part of the collaboration, CMU and The Tony Awards jointly created the Excellence in Theatre Education Award, which honors kindergarten through high school (K-12) theatre educators for their dedication to inspiring the next generation of theatre kids.
