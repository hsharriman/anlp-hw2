Postal Services
Students who live on campus can ship items directly to their SMC mailbox using the appropriate address format. If you cannot pick up a package during business hours, you may request your package be moved to our parcel pickup lockers. Please note that we are unable to participate in student academic projects at this time.
Please make sure to use the SMC address on the student's SIO.
We are open Monday - Friday 9:00am to 5:00pm.
The Package Pick-up Window will close for staff lunch from 11:00am to 12:00pm.
The CMU Postal Service will close at 2:00pm on Friday September 4, 2026 and will be closed on Monday September 7, 2026. We will resume normal business hours on Tuesday September 8, 2026.
Get help sending mail to and from Carnegie Mellon University. Postal Services is CMU's full-service United States Postal Service (USPS) mailing and shipping facility.
Learn more about our incoming and outgoing mail services and policies. We also offer additional services, such as money orders, stamps and mailing supplies.
