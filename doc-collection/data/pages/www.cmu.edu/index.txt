A Historic Investment to Build a New University Model
CMU Miami will be a new campus organized around societal challenges, designed to prepare future leaders for a rapidly changing world while accelerating research and discovery in areas of critical importance to society.
With this gift, Carnegie Mellon will strengthen the university in Pittsburgh, extend its reach in Miami, and help demonstrate a new model for how research universities can serve their country.

CMU Celebrates America250
How our history is woven into the fabric of the nation.
From developing lifesavers like Kevlar and pioneering the foundation of Wi-Fi to standing at the forefront of AI-enabled scientific discovery, Carnegie Mellon’s evolution exemplifies the American experience.
As the nation marks its semiquincentennial, CMU is hosting a yearlong slate of America250 programming. Together, we will reflect on our founding documents, explore untold stories of our past, and engage with the profound questions that will shape our human-centered future.

The University That Invented the Field.
The origins of AI at CMU date back to 1957. Two of the university’s professors — Allen Newell and Herbert Simon — are considered among the godfathers of AI.
CMU’s research and robotics institute launched in 1979 – the first robotics institute at a U.S. university.
In 1988, CMU announced the first college in the world devoted solely to computer science.
CMU awards the first undergraduate degree in AI.
