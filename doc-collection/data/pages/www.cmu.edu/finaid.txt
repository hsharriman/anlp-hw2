CMU Pathway Program
The CMU Pathway Program, which starts with the 2025-26 academic year, provides access to a tuition-free or loan-free CMU undergraduate education to students who qualify. Learn more about the program.
My Student Aid
My Student Aid (MSA) is your personalized financial aid portal where you can find the status of your application, upload documents and review your financial aid offer. Learn how to navigate MSA.
