ICA Pittsburgh
The ICA Pittsburgh supports experimentation that expands the notions of art and culture, providing a forum for engaged conversations about creativity and innovation.
STUDIO
The Frank-Ratchye STUDIO for Creative Inquiry is a laboratory for atypical, antidisciplinary and interinstitutional research at the intersections of arts, science, technology and culture.
Research & Creative Practice
The College of Fine Arts aspires to be recognized as the definitive international hub of arts, architecture and design for education, research and practice in the 21st Century.
Our passionate and inspirational world-class faculty, staff and students regularly produce extraordinary new culture, knowledge, techniques and technologies.
