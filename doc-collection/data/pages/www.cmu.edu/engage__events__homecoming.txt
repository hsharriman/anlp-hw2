Homecoming
Welcome home Tartans! Carnegie Mellon's annual Homecoming Weekend is happening now, Oct. 23-24!
Alumni, parents and guests can join students, faculty and staff for a weekend of exciting events like Spirit Day, the 76th Annual Alumni Awards and the Homecoming tailgate Activities and football game.
Weekend Highlights
Homecoming Spirit Day
Friday, Oct. 23, 1-4 p.m. | The Cut
Kick off Homecoming with a zipline, free food, games, giveaways and plenty of Tartan spirit on The Cut.
76th Annual Alumni Awards
Friday, Oct. 23, 6:30-9 p.m. | Kresge Theatre, CFA
Celebrate extraordinary Tartans making an impact around the world at an inspiring evening honoring CMU alumni and their achievements.
Tailgate Activities
Saturday, Oct. 24, 11 a.m.-4 p.m. | Legacy Plaza
Enjoy food trucks, student performances and family-friendly fun, plus connect with fellow Tartans at the Pittsburgh Network Watch Tent!

Frequently Asked Questions
The annual Homecoming celebration at Carnegie Mellon is an opportunity for CMU community members to share their pride, reconnect with campus, celebrate alumni and cheer the Tartans on to victory!
Where should I park on campus?
Parking is available in the East Campus, Dithridge and Gates garages on a first-come, first-served basis. You can reserve a spot via the ParkMobile app and scan the QR code at the garage entrance. Reservations are recommended but not required. Free parking is available in Morewood Lot on a first-come, first-served basis on Saturday and Sunday. A full list of parking locations is available on the visitor parking website.
Am I able to see who else is registered to attend?
Yes, you are able to view a list of registrants, if they did not opt out of the listing during their registration.
What is Homecoming Spirit Day? Who is invited?
The entire CMU community is welcome to attend, but the event is geared toward students. Spirit Day takes place on the Friday of Homecoming Weekend and includes activities including zipline, balloon artists and games, plus giveaways and food freebies while supplies last.
Where should I stay near campus?
While we do not hold room blocks during Homecoming Weekend, we offer a list of local hotels near campus.
Where should I eat on campus?
Many events throughout the weekend will have refreshments or food. Additionally, Carnegie Mellon has numerous dining concepts where you can purchase a meal on campus Homecoming Weekend. Visit the Dining website for more information about locations and hours.
What else should I see while I’m visiting Pittsburgh?
Don’t miss the chance to explore all that Pittsburgh has to offer when you’re in the area during Homecoming Weekend. You can find restaurants, events and attractions at Visit Pittsburgh.
What should I wear for the weekend?
Casual attire is appropriate for most events throughout the weekend, unless otherwise noted.
When are the University Bookstore be open?
The University Bookstore is located on the first floor and lower level of the Cohon University Center. The University Bookstore hours are:
Thursday through Saturday: 10 a.m.-5 p.m.
Sunday: closed
Is Wi-Fi available?
All guests can access the CMU Guest Wi-Fi with the code GPLM3BL6. It is valid from October 22 to 25.
How do I contact CMU Police?
For emergencies or to report a crime, contact the Carnegie Mellon University Police at 412-268-2323.
