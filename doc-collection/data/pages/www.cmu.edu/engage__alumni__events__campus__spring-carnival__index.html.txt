Spring Carnival
For more than 100 years, Spring Carnival has celebrated CMU's traditional mainstays like Booth, Buggy, the Kiltie Band concert and Scotch'n'Soda performances.
Thousands of students, alumni, faculty, staff and friends came together to enjoy this oh-so-CMU tradition April 9-11, 2026.

Tartans on the Rise
Tartans on the Rise recognizes recent alumni who are transforming the planet through artistic expression, scientific exploration, entrepreneurial spirit and visionary innovations. Learn more about the 2026 honorees.
Order of the May
The Order of the May celebrates individuals whose consistent support inspires others to make a similar impact. The ceremony recognizes those who reach 25 years of consecutive giving.
Reunion Weekend at Spring Carnival
Reunion Weekend celebrates friends new and old at exclusive events and opportunities during Spring Carnival. This year recognized undergraduate classes ending in 1s and 6s, Half Century Tartans (Classes of 1975 and prior), as well as the Classes of 1966 and 2025.
