Office of the Vice Provost for Faculty
Supporting Faculty Success and Excellence
At the heart of academic excellence is a thriving, supported and engaged faculty. The Office of the Vice Provost for Faculty champions the success, growth and well-being of our faculty at every stage of their academic journey, directly advancing the university’s academic mission. Our work encompasses faculty development and recognition, tenure reviews, academic personnel policy and more.
Celebrating 10 Years
We recently celebrated our 10-year anniversary, marking a decade of championing faculty and postdoc success, growth and well-being across all stages of academic careers. Since its establishment, the Office of the Vice Provost for Faculty has supported CMU’s academic mission by advancing faculty development and recognition, guiding tenure and promotion processes, and strengthening academic personnel policies to foster a vibrant and inclusive faculty community. Read more about the celebration and the history of the office in our OVPF 10 Year Anniversary Report [pdf].
