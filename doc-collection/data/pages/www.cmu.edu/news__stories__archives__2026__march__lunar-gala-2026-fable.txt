Lunar Gala 2026 — FABLE
Annual student-run fashion show celebrates its 30th anniversary

Carnegie Mellon University's annual Lunar Gala(opens in new window), a student-run fashion show which began in 1996, returned for its 30th year in CMU's Weigand Gym on March 15. This year's theme was FABLE, "an anthology of the fantastical worlds that color our identities, stepping through imaginations, and memories across people, times, and spaces." Featuring 17 fashion lines(opens in new window) created by CMU students, the show highlights artistry in design, modeling, production and dance performance.
This year's producers were Katherine Wang, a senior studying music(opens in new window) and business administration(opens in new window), and Yui Ishibiki, a senior studying chemical engineering(opens in new window).
Originally inspired(opens in new window) by the Taiwanese Student Association’s Lunar New Year tradition of wearing new clothes, it has evolved to become a unique platform to display innovative and avant garde fashion.
