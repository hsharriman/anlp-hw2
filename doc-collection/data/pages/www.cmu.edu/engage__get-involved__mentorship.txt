No Substitute for Experience
As a Carnegie Mellon alum, you have the opportunity to share your career and life experiences and hard-won knowledge by mentoring current students.
These positive experiences foster relationships between students and alumni and set our students up for future success. Mentoring opportunities exist across campus, at the university, school, college and department levels. Explore more about sharing your advice and experiences with the next generation of Tartans.
