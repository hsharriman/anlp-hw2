Business Support Services
Business Support Services provides financial information, planning and support to CMU's administrative and operational departments, including campus design, campus services, facilities, finance, human resources, IT, legal, risk and the university's police. Services include:
Budgeting and forecasting
Financial reporting
Procurement services
Travel coordination
Year-to-date variance analysis
