Emerging Leaders
A long-standing CMU tradition for first-year students, Emerging Leaders (EL) promotes the development of leadership skills through the lens of community engagement and social action.
For more information about the Emerging Leaders program, check out our Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ), or contact Justin Pearl via email or at 412-268-8704.
To learn more, join us for an information session on December 2nd, 12:00-1:00pm. Lunch included!

Nomination
