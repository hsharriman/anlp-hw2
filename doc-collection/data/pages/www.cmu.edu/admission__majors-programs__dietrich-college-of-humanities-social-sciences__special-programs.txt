Special Programs
This is the kind of place that prides itself on mixing fields and merging expertise every day — so naturally, we’ve designed programs to let you do the same. With a humanities scholars program or an inter-college degree s, you’ll get an interdisciplinary experience that prepares you for an interconnected world.
Program Options
Humanities and the Arts
Bachelor of Humanities and Arts
If you’re interested in both the humanities and the fine arts, this is the kind of program for you. As a Bachelor of Humanities and Arts (BHA) student, you’ll have the opportunity to work at the intersection of your academic and creative fields and to explore the connections between your chosen disciplines. The program also provides enough flexibility for you to broaden or deepen your concentrations, and to explore other areas of interest through minors or additional majors.
Humanities Scholars Program
In this rigorous but rewarding interdisciplinary program, you’ll join a vibrant community of scholars who take special seminar courses together, live together in a designated residence hall, and conduct humanities research in Carnegie Mellon’s rich interdisciplinary environment. In your fourth year, you’ll culminate your academic journey in a capstone research project and a research seminar with your Humanities Scholars Program (HSP) cohort.
