Interdisciplinary Programs
Carnegie Mellon thrives where disciplines collide, pushing boundaries and making important discoveries in the intersections between arts and sciences, expertise and exploration. So it’s no surprise that several of our programs just can’t be confined to one department. Instead, they exist as interdepartmental programs, spanning two or more areas within the college, and uniting their strengths to prepare students for the world beyond Carnegie Mellon.
