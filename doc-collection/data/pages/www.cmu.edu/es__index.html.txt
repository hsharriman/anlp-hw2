Academic Department & Faculty Services
Division of Enrollment Management
Providing high-quality service and support to academic and administrative units.
The Division of Enrollment Management is dedicated to providing Carnegie Mellon faculty and staff with exceptional administrative services to support advising, registration processes, program management, course and classroom scheduling, grading and final exams, and more.
Other administrative services offered include Student Services Suite (S3) development and maintainance, Student Data Warehouse services, matters regarding student financial aid and billing, and matters involving International Education.
