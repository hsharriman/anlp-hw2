Online Graduate Certificates
Lead the way. Wherever you are.
Carnegie Mellon University offers online graduate certificate programs in artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning, data science, software engineering and other high-demand fields. Designed for working professionals, CMU’s flexible, graduate-level programs bring the university’s renowned research, faculty expertise and interdisciplinary approach to learners around the world.
Taught Live by CMU Faculty
All courses are taught by CMU faculty from our nationally-ranked School of Computer Science, College of Engineering, Heinz College of Public Policy, and Dietrich College of Humanities & Social Sciences.
Designed for Working Professionals
Weekly, live-online sessions in the evening (ET) and asynchronous coursework you can finish on your own time, at your own pace.
Engaging Educational Experiences
Feedback, labs, real work. Built for real results that go far beyond recorded lectures. Active learning sessions will help you master key concepts so you can immediately apply them at work.
Highest Quality, Backed by Research
Carnegie Mellon University's online, graduate-level certificates are taught by world-class faculty and combine live-online classes with flexible, self-paced learning. These programs are not lighter versions of our on-campus offerings; they deliver the same high standards, depth, and academic excellence that define a CMU education.
Programs are shaped by CMU’s pioneering role in learning science and years of research into what makes online learning most effective.
Courses follow proven instructional design methods to help you stay focused, absorb material efficiently, and apply it immediately.
Every course is developed and reviewed by faculty from across disciplines to ensure it’s relevant, clear, and practical.
Content is regularly updated based on student feedback and learning data to keep your experience engaging and valuable.
