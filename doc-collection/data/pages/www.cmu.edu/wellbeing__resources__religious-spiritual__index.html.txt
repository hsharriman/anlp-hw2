Religious & Spiritual Life at CMU
Religious and Spiritual Life (RSL) offers programs and initiatives that cross traditional religious boundaries in order to increase our students' knowledge of and appreciation for the full diversity of the world's religious and spiritual traditions. RSL's mission is to support all students along the spectrum of religious and spiritual practice and exploration by:
Supporting individual religious commitment through programs, events, resources and space
Facilitating connections of individuals and communities of faith, on and off campus
Encouraging cooperation among and between religious, secular and worldview communities
Promoting spiritual exploration and wellness for the whole Carnegie Mellon University community
Promoting religious literacy for all members of the Carnegie Mellon community

Interfaith Leadership Development
In our diverse world, every career path may lead us to work with people from a variety of different religious, spiritual, and secular worldviews. The Interfaith Leadership Development Program is an opportunity to develop cross-cultural competency and build skills that prepare you to be an effective interfaith leader in your community and particular field of work. The program is housed on the Tartan Connect platform and is a choose-your-own-adventure style, identifying in-person, hybrid, and virtual choices to grow in understanding and leads to a badge that you can post on LinkedIn or other networking sites.  Participants can begin at any point in the academic year and progress at their own pace. This program will officially become available on Tartan Connect by the first day of the classes in fall, 2026. Join the Interfaith Leadership Development group on Tartan Connect to register. Email the Program Director of Religious and Spiritual Life with any questions.

Academic Religious Accommodations
Carnegie Mellon University is committed to creating an inclusive and welcoming campus environment and does not discriminate with respect to access to its educational programs and activities on the basis of religion. Students may request religious accommodations from their instructors or professors.
how to request an accommodation as a student
With straightforward requests for accommodation, such as an absence due to a religious or spiritual holiday, students should contact their professor or instructor in advance to seek accommodations.
For more complex requests, such as modification to the curriculum or significant schedule changes, the Student Religious Accommodation Request Form [pdf] is a helpful tool to articulate nuance.
Employees (staff/faculty) may request religious accommodations by submitting an Employee Religious Accommodation Request Form [pdf] to Amelia Kephart in Disability Services.
Recommended Religious Accommodation Language for Syllabi
We recommend that instructors include this language on their syllabi.

Students may request religious accommodations from their instructors or professors. With straightforward requests for accommodation, such as an absence due to a religious or spiritual holiday, students should contact their professor or instructor in advance to seek accommodations. For more complicated requests, such as modification to the curriculum or significant schedule changes, the Student Religious Accommodation Request Form [pdf] is a helpful tool to articulate nuance.
Option Two | (More personalized)
Academic Religious Accommodations
Carnegie Mellon University is committed to creating an inclusive and welcoming campus environment and does not discriminate with respect to access to its educational programs and activities on the basis of religion. Please let me know if you need an accommodation for a religious holiday, preferably at the start of the semester or at least one week in advance. If you need additional modifications to the curriculum or significant schedule changes, please use the Student Religious Accommodation Request Form [pdf].
Questions? The Program Director of Religious and Spiritual Initiatives is available to assist faculty, staff and students who have questions or concerns related to religious accommodations.

Housing Needs
Shabbat Keys are available by request for most University Housing facilities. To request a key, please email the office of Religious and Spiritual Initiatives or call Housing Services at 412-268-2139.
Housing Services does not offer any housing communities dedicated to a specific religious affiliation, is not able to provide accommodations such as single rooms or private bathrooms for religious reasons, and is unable to consider requests for roommates of similar religious beliefs.
