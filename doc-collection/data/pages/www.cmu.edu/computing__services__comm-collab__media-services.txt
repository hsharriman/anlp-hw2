Media Services
​Video and audio production and post-production expertise, including full-service support of AV needs for on- and off-campus conferences and events.
Event Support Services
Professional audio-visual technology and technical support for conferences and events on- and off-campus. Includes full-service operation of audio, video, and projection equipment.
Video Production Services
Fee-based video production and post-production services for your event, course, or marketing needs. Includes live streaming, hosting, and media conversion services.
