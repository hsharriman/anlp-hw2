Celebrating CMU’s past, present and future
From Nov. 6-8, the Tartan community came together at Homecoming Weekend to celebrate 125 years of Carnegie Mellon University. In addition to traditional festivities like Spirit Day and the football game, CMU125 at Homecoming Weekend featured special themed events and opportunities for Tartans of all ages to celebrate this remarkable moment in CMU history.

President's Lecture Series: 125th Anniversary Kickoff featuring Luis von Ahn
Launched in 2023 by President Farnam Jahanian, the President’s Lecture Series engages and inspires the CMU community with thought-provoking presentations from renowned scholars and leaders. This special installment featured alumnus and CEO and co-founder of Duolingo Luis von Ahn.

75th Annual Alumni Awards
Now in its 75th year, the CMU Alumni Awards recognizes Carnegie Mellon alumni who have had a positive impact on their industries, their communities, their alma mater and people around the world. During this double anniversary year, the Alumni Awards shined brighter than ever.

CMU125 at Homecoming Weekend
Each year, Homecoming Weekend provides the CMU community with the opportunity to reconnect with each other and the university and enjoy being Tartans together. This year, Homecoming activities included a celebration of CMU’s 125-year legacy of community, innovation and impact.
