Dietrich College of Humanities and Social Sciences
English
Technologies may change around you, but the wisdom you’ll earn through your English degree will endure. CMU English graduates know how to articulate their value, and our outcomes reflect it. The reason CMU English graduates become successful doctors, authors, venture capitalists, lawyers, and CEOs is because English has some of the most flexible and skill-rich majors you can choose. The capabilities you’ll build here – judgment, persuasion and thinking innovatively across contexts – don’t get automated. You'll develop skills employers actively seek: distilling complex information quickly, writing persuasively for diverse audiences, researching with precision and telling stories that move people to action. Whether your path leads to media, law, technology, nonprofits or fields you haven't yet considered, an English degree gives you the adaptable tools to succeed.
English Majors and Minors
Choose the path that fits you best. Browse all current English curriculums and courses.
Creative Writing
Bachelor of Arts
Minor
In this program, you’ll develop talents in writing fiction, poetry, screenplays and creative nonfiction. While studying with faculty members who are skilled writers themselves, you’ll read a wide range of literature and genres, sharpen your critical and verbal skills, better explore the resources and potential of imagination and develop a professional attitude towards writing. Extracurricular activities and a variety of internships available on and off campus will give you valuable experiences for planning both your professional and academic futures. Your verbal and storytelling skills will prepare you for careers in writing, content strategy, brand communications, publishing, TV and film, UX, advertising and public relations.
Film and Visual Media
Bachelor of Arts
Minor
Do you want to spend a semester interning in Hollywood? Film and Visual Media majors have the opportunity to intern at studios, agencies and production companies — and study directly with working industry professionals — through CMU's Semester in Hollywood program. Back on campus, our community of accomplished screenwriters, filmmakers, film scholars, computational humanities instructors and visual communication researchers gives you a rigorous foundation in how stories are structured, how images shape culture and how to create and critically analyze moving-image work. Skills and experiences in storytelling, visual literacy, audience analysis, and media production translate directly to careers in entertainment, content strategy, documentary filmmaking and user experience (UX).
Literature and Culture
Bachelor of Arts
Minor
This program trains you to do what few people can: read complex situations quickly, identify what's really going on, and write about it persuasively. By studying literature across genres, media, and historical periods, including some of humanity’s greatest literary achievements, you'll sharpen the analytic, argumentative, and empathetic skills that apply in virtually any professional context and that employers across industries actively seek. Students in Literature and Culture are grounded in reading and trained for leading.
Professional Writing
Bachelor of Arts
Minor
This flexible program prepares you to create clear and effective communication for diverse audiences and real-world contexts. You will engage in intensive practice in writing, editing and revising, grounded in rigorous study of rhetoric, argument and genre — skills that remain foundational as communication platforms and professional contexts continue to evolve. By combining analytical insight with applied experience, you will learn to adapt your writing to new and emerging contexts, preparing you for diverse careers in writing and communication, as well as for advanced study in fields such as law, medicine, and rhetoric and writing.
Technical Writing
Bachelor of Science
Minor
This program prepares you to generate clear and effective communication that translates complex scientific and technical information for diverse audiences. Whether your interests span writing and STEM fields, or you want to complement a STEM major with the communication skills industries demand, this program offers an individually tailored path with tracks in technical communication and science and medical communication. You will engage in intensive practice in writing, editing and user-centered design with technical and scientific coursework. This interdisciplinary curriculum positions you for a field defined by ongoing shifts in media, tools and communication practice — with career paths spanning technical writing, scientific communication and UX writing, among others.
Additional Minor Options
Humanities Analytics
As AI and data reshape every industry, the most valuable question isn't can we do this — it's should we, and why. The Humanities Analytics (HumAn) minor trains you to answer both. You'll develop the quantitative fluency and analytical depth to work with data not just technically, but critically — learning to analyze, digitize, question, quantify and visualize the full range of human cultural production: printed books, manuscripts, historical records, fan fiction, art, music and film. Drawing on traditions in philosophy, literature and history, the program prepares you to bring humanistic judgment to data-driven environments and computational tools to humanistic questions — exactly the combination that industries increasingly say they need. Graduates are positioned for any career (technology, law, advocacy, publishing) in which the ability to think across the humanities-technology divide is the competitive edge.
