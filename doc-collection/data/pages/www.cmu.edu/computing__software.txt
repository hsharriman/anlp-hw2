University Software and Licensing Management
Navigating software licenses has become easier with new features such as user-managed software titles, ISO Data Classifications, and transparent pricing.
Your Front Door to Software Needs
We want to make sure you have the tools you need to get your work done. Some software at CMU is acquired through software licenses managed by the university. Some enable all faculty, staff, or students to use the software at no cost. Others limit who is eligible to use the software or have a fee. Each licensed product has its eligibility criteria; not all products are licensed at all locations.
University Software and Licensing Management
University Software and Licensing Management is a partnership among the University Contracts Office (UCO), Procurement, Eberly Center, the University Bookstore, and Computing Services.
What's Available?
See what titles are available. Some titles are free of charge, some limit eligibility or have a cost associated with them, and some have university approval or guidance for use.
