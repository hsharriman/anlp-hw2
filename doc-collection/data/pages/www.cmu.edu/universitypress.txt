Our New Releases
Rock Arrangement
The first English translation of Chinese poet Yan An's award-winning collection. Translated by Chen Du and Xisheng Chen.
My Mother's House
An updated translation of Colette’s revolutionary 1922 autobiographical collection. Translated by Carol Mastrangelo Bové.
From the Diary of Madame Mao
A translation of Venezuelan poet María Teresa Ogliastri's collection exploring the authoritarian psyche through Mao Zedong's wife. Translated by Yvette Neisser and Patricia Bejarano Fisher.
The City-Women
This debut play follows what happens after two nuns fall in love in seventeenth-century Latin America. Written by Mallorie Sievert.
The Half of It
A play that uncovers queer history by telling the story of forgotten pioneer drag artist Bert Savoy. Written by Domenick Scudera.
That's Where You Come In
A poetry collection meditating on the twin experiences of love and loss. Written by John Skoyles.
Year of the Labyrinth
A poetry collection that threads together myth, ritual, fatherhood and climate collapse. Written by Brian Sneeden.

And still counting.
Since our founding in 1972, we've published Pulitzer Prize-winning poets and Poets Laureate, started an academic imprint that draws from Carnegie Mellon's institutional expertise, and even expanded into new areas like tabletop game publishing.
