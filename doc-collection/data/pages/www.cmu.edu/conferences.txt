Conference & Event Services
Your Headquarters for Effortless Events
One-Stop Shop
Conference & Event Services takes pride in delivering professional and organized events. With over 40 years of combined event planning experience, we are your one-stop shop for catering, room reservations, summer campus housing, hotel accommodations, audio/visual, parking, web registration and many more customized services. Our staff works closely with you to ensure your next conference, meeting, summer camp or event is specially tailored to accommodate your needs.
We have established strong relationships with campus service providers and local businesses (i.e., hotels, transportation, rental companies, etc.) who work with us to create a unique event. To ensure a seamless experience, we are your single point of contact throughout the planning process.
Book your next event with confidence, and you will be able to focus on the program while we take care of all of the details. Your effortless event begins with Conference & Event Services.
