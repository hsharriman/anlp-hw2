Core@CMU
The newly enhanced 99-101 Core@CMU course empowers students with a competitive edge that extends far beyond graduation into a lifetime of professional growth and global impact. It is designed around the Core4 essential skills:
Information & Data Literacy
Communication & Presentation
Intercultural & Global Literacy
Collaboration & Teamwork
As generative AI becomes increasingly ubiquitous, the Core4 competencies are more important than ever. Throughout this course, students consider how AI can support their work while developing the judgment, communication, collaboration, and intercultural understanding that remain uniquely human strengths.
This foundational course is an essential first step in a student's educational journey at CMU, setting them up for academic, personal, and professional success regardless of their field of study.
